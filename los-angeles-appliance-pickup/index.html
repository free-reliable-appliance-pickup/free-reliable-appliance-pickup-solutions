Los Angeles Appliance Pickup & Removal

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and qualifying multiple-appliance loads in Los Angeles. Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Free pickup is qualification-based and depends on condition, location, access, local demand and available route coverage.

Appliances We Review

Working washers and dryers are priority categories. Refrigerators and freezers should be described as currently cooling or not cooling. Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may also qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Los Angeles Access Details

Los Angeles pickups can involve apartments, elevators, gated buildings, narrow halls, limited parking and loading restrictions. Include the floor level, stairs, elevator availability, gate instructions and removal path. Clear appliance photos and an exact ZIP code help us review the request before a route is confirmed.

Prepare Your Pickup Request

Send the appliance type, working condition, address or ZIP code, photos and access information. Pickup is confirmed only after eligibility and route availability are reviewed.
