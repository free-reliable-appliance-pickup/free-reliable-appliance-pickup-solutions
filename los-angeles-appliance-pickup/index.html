What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Send the appliance condition, city or ZIP code, photos and access details. Pickup must be confirmed based on the load, location, access and available coverage before scheduling.

Washer & Dryer Pickup in Los Angeles

Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets receive priority. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer heats and tumbles. Include the fuel type and describe each machine separately, including leaks or error messages.

Refrigerator, Freezer, Stove & Oven Pickup

Qualifying refrigerators, household freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens can also be submitted for review. For refrigerators and freezers, tell us whether both compartments maintain temperature and send an interior photo. For cooking appliances, identify gas or electric and describe the working condition of the burners and oven.

Pickup Access

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upstairs, basement or outside. Include stairs, narrow doors, tight halls and gates.

Los Angeles Appliance Disposal Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our pickup, City of Los Angeles residents with eligible LASAN curbside service can review the city's metal appliance pickup program. LASAN lists refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, air conditioners and water heaters among accepted household metal appliances and asks residents to schedule before collection.
