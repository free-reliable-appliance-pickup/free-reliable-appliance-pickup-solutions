Los Angeles Appliance Removal Options by Property Type

Los Angeles appliance removal is not one single service. The best route depends on whether the appliance is reusable, whether the address receives City residential collection, whether the property is multifamily, and whether the request is commercial or requires inside carry-out.

Working or Reusable Appliances

Use our pickup review for qualifying washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges that still work or may have practical reuse value. Send photos, condition, exact address, stairs, gates, elevators and parking details before pickup is confirmed.

Single-Family City Collection

LA Sanitation publishes no-extra-cost metal-appliance collection for eligible residents with City curbside collection. Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, air conditioners and water heaters are among the listed items. City scheduling and preparation rules apply.

Multifamily & Apartment Buildings

Los Angeles also operates bulky-item collection for eligible multifamily customers through its waste-service system. Building managers should confirm which hauler serves the property and whether the appliance must be staged in a designated collection area.

Commercial or Difficult Carry-Out

Commercial appliances are not accepted through the City's ordinary residential bulky-item program. A business, renovation project, difficult interior carry-out or nonqualifying broken appliance may need a private licensed removal provider instead of our free reuse-focused pickup.

Property managers, landlords and recurring turnover accounts can also review our landlord and property-manager appliance pickup guide. For nonqualifying appliances, compare the alternatives in our appliance disposal and recycling guide.

Los Angeles Appliance Pickup vs. City Metal-Appliance Collection

Los Angeles residents have more than one legitimate way to remove an appliance. Our service reviews qualifying working and reusable appliances for pickup, while the City of Los Angeles also provides a public metal-appliance collection option for eligible curbside customers. Choosing the right option depends on the appliance condition, where it is located, and whether reuse is practical.

When our pickup review may fit

A working washer, dryer, refrigerator, freezer, stove or range may be useful for reuse, resale or repair. Send photos, tested functions and access details so we can review the appliance before a pickup is confirmed.

When the City option may fit

LA Sanitation states that eligible single-family curbside customers can request large metal and household-appliance pickup at no extra cost. Its accepted list includes refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, air conditioners and water heaters. The City lists 1-800-773-2489 as its 24-hour Customer Care Center for this service.

Preparation matters

For our service, appliances should be safely disconnected before pickup. For the City program, LA Sanitation instructs customers to schedule ahead and provides specific curbside preparation rules for refrigerators and freezers.
