Los Angeles Appliance Pickup & Removal

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and qualifying multiple-appliance loads in Los Angeles. Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Free pickup is qualification-based and depends on condition, location, access, local demand and available route coverage.

Pickup is confirmed after review. Submit the exact Los Angeles address or ZIP code, appliance condition, photos and access details before scheduling.

Appliances We Review

Washers & Dryers

Working laundry appliances and sets are priority categories. Tell us whether washers fill, drain and spin and whether dryers tumble and heat.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the appliance is currently plugged in and maintaining temperature. Interior and exterior photos are helpful.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Gas and electric cooking appliances may qualify. Include whether burners and the oven operate normally.

Multiple Appliances

Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. List the condition of every appliance separately.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Los Angeles Access Details

Los Angeles pickups can involve apartments, elevators, gated buildings, narrow halls, limited parking and loading restrictions. Include the floor level, stairs, elevator availability, gate instructions and removal path. Clear appliance photos and an exact ZIP code help us review the request before a route is confirmed.

Send the appliance type, working condition, exact address, ZIP code, photos and access information. Pickup is confirmed only after eligibility and route availability are reviewed.
