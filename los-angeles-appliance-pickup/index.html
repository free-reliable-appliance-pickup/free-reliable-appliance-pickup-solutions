Los Angeles Appliance Pickup vs. City Metal-Appliance Collection

Los Angeles residents have more than one legitimate way to remove an appliance. Our service reviews qualifying working and reusable appliances for pickup, while the City of Los Angeles also provides a public metal-appliance collection option for eligible curbside customers. Choosing the right option depends on the appliance condition, where it is located, and whether reuse is practical.

When our pickup review may fit

A working washer, dryer, refrigerator, freezer, stove or range may be useful for reuse, resale or repair. Send photos, tested functions and access details so we can review the appliance before a pickup is confirmed.

When the City option may fit

LA Sanitation states that eligible single-family curbside customers can request large metal and household-appliance pickup at no extra cost. Its accepted list includes refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, air conditioners and water heaters.

Preparation matters

For our service, appliances should be safely disconnected before pickup. For the City program, LA Sanitation instructs customers to schedule ahead and provides specific curbside preparation rules for refrigerators and freezers.
