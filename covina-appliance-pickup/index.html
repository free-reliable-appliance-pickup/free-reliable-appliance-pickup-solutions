Residential Garages, Rear Access & Rental Properties

Covina pickups may begin in an attached garage, laundry room, kitchen, apartment or rear unit. Explain whether the crew must use a side gate, shared driveway, alley, stairs or a long path to the truck. For inside refrigerators, include doorway clearance and any tight turns.

Laundry and Kitchen Replacement Requests

When replacing a washer and dryer, describe each machine separately and submit the pair together. For a kitchen replacement, tell us whether the refrigerator cools, whether the freezer holds temperature, and whether stove burners and the oven operate. Photos of the complete appliance help prevent delays.

Important Pickup Limits

Minor-repair appliances may be considered after accurate disclosure. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Submitting the request starts a review and does not guarantee free pickup. We confirm condition, exact address, access and route availability before scheduling.
