How Covina Appliance Pickup Works

1. Send appliance details

Tell us the appliance type, tested condition and brand or model when known. Clear exterior and interior photos make the review faster.

2. Describe the removal path

Include garage or interior location, floor level, stairs, gates, side-yard or alley access, parking limits, doorway restrictions and carrying distance.

3. Confirm the local route

We review condition, access, local demand and current 909 route availability. Free pickup is confirmed only after the request qualifies.

Covina Address, ZIP Code & Access Review

Covina mailing addresses commonly use ZIP codes 91722, 91723 and 91724. Because some properties with a Covina mailing address can be outside the incorporated city, use the exact street address and ZIP rather than the mailing city alone so the request stays on the correct local route.

Pickup access can range from attached garages and standard driveways to apartments, rear units, shared driveways and alley approaches. Include floor level, stairs or elevator, gates, parking or loading limits, narrow doors and carrying distance.

What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Fully working and reusable major appliances receive the strongest consideration. Minor-repair appliances may be reviewed when current local demand and route capacity support the pickup. For mixed loads, tell us the condition of every machine rather than describing the whole load as working.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Submitting a request begins a review; it does not create a guaranteed appointment. Read the full qualification rules before submitting a heavily damaged or nonworking load.
