City of Orange Bulky-Item Alternative for Nonqualifying Appliances

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused route, the City of Orange has a separate residential bulky-item program through CR&R Environmental Services. The City states that each residential account is entitled to four bulky items per collection, six times per year—up to 24 bulky items annually—and appliances are included.

Residents must reserve a collection date with CR&R and then follow the City's set-out instructions for the regular trash day. Multi-family properties with bin service may also have bulky-item service available. This municipal program is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup, so confirm current eligibility and preparation rules directly with CR&R.
