Fontana Refrigerator Pickup Review

A working refrigerator is a strong candidate for review. A single refrigerator that no longer cools does not automatically qualify for free pickup through our reuse-focused route.

Fontana requests commonly come from single-family homes, garages, side-yard access and newer residential areas. Tell us if the appliance is inside, in a garage, behind a gate, or requires a long carry from the home to the loading area.

Cooling condition

Tell us whether both the fresh-food and freezer sections are currently cooling normally and whether the refrigerator is plugged in and testable.

Photos that help

Send the front, interior, model label and any visible damage. Side-by-side, French-door and larger units should include approximate width when known.

Removal access

Mention stairs, narrow doors, gates, interior removal and how close the loading vehicle can reach the appliance.

Fast review: Text clear photos to 909-375-6685 and include your Fontana ZIP code plus the condition details above.

How This Fontana Request Works

1. Send condition + photos

Start with what works, clear photos and the ZIP code. You do not need to wait until every access detail is known to start the review.

2. We review qualification

We check condition, access, current demand and Inland Empire route availability. Free service is not guaranteed until the request is accepted.

3. Confirm pickup details

If the request qualifies, the pickup details and removal access are confirmed before the appointment.

Prepare the Refrigerator Safely

Keep the refrigerator intact. Do not cut refrigerant lines or remove the compressor. Empty food and loose shelves before an accepted pickup, and tell us if doors may need to be removed for clearance.

Do not move a heavy appliance alone if doing so is unsafe. Photos of the appliance in its current location can help us evaluate the removal path first.
