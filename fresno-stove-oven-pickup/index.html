Fresno Stove, Range & Oven Pickup Review

For cooking appliances, the most useful details are fuel type, tested functions, completeness and safe disconnection status. Working burners and a working oven make a range much stronger for reuse than a unit with missing controls, broken glass or major electrical/gas issues.

Gas ranges

Tell us whether burners and oven operate and whether the gas line has already been safely disconnected.

Electric & induction

Report which elements work, whether the oven heats correctly and whether controls/display work.

Wall ovens

Built-in units require extra access planning. Send dimensions and safe-disconnection status before pickup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can one working stove or range qualify for free pickup in Fresno?

Yes. A fully working and reusable stove or range can be reviewed as a single appliance. Acceptance depends on condition, access, local demand and route availability.

Do you take gas and electric ranges?

Both can be submitted. Tell us the fuel type and which burners, oven functions and controls work.

Will you disconnect a gas range?

Do not assume gas-line disconnection is part of pickup. Tell us whether the appliance is already safely disconnected and use a qualified person for gas work when required.

Can a broken oven be picked up free?

A single nonworking stove, range or oven does not automatically qualify for free pickup. Submit the actual condition and photos for review.

What access details should I send?

Include floor level, stairs, doorway width, parking distance and whether the appliance is already pulled out and safely disconnected.
