Qualification Rules

Fully working appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of major appliances work. Minor repair needs may be considered. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
