Appliance Pickup Service Terms

Last updated: September 20, 2026. Free Reliable Appliance Pickup provides a request-and-review service for qualifying appliance pickup. Submitting a form, calling, texting or appearing in a listed service area does not by itself guarantee free pickup, an appointment or a specific pickup time.

Qualification and Coverage

Each request is reviewed using appliance type and condition, exact location, access, current local demand and available direct or partner coverage. Fully working and reusable major appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A single nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify for free pickup. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances, and microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Accurate Request Information

Please provide accurate appliance condition, location, floor level, stairs, elevator, gates, parking and other access information. If a machine has a known defect, leak, error code, missing part or other problem, describe it rather than marking the appliance fully working.

Pickup Providers and Scheduling

A confirmed request may be handled by a direct operation or an independent local pickup professional participating in the network. Coverage and scheduling can change with route capacity, provider availability, appliance demand and the information supplied with the request. A pickup is scheduled only after the request has been reviewed and explicitly confirmed.

Safe Access and Disconnection

Customers should provide a reasonably safe removal path and disclose stairs, narrow doors, difficult carries or building rules before confirmation. Do not disconnect gas appliances, perform electrical work, move an unsafe heavy appliance or alter refrigerant lines merely to prepare a request. Gas, electrical, plumbing, built-in appliance or regulated refrigerant work should be handled safely by an appropriately qualified person when required.

Nonqualifying Appliances

If a request does not qualify for this reuse-focused free pickup service, the customer may need to use a municipal collection, utility program, retailer take-back service, recycler or other appropriate disposal option. Those outside services are separate organizations with their own requirements.
