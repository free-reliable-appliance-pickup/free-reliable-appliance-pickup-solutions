Appliance Pickup Service Terms

Submitting a request does not guarantee free pickup or a scheduled appointment. Each request is reviewed for appliance condition, location, access, current demand and available service or partner coverage.

Qualifying fully working appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances, and microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Please provide accurate appliance condition, location, stairs, floor level and access information. Pickup is confirmed only after the request has been reviewed.
