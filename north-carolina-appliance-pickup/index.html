Customers can submit washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges, ovens and qualifying multi-appliance loads. We review the exact city or ZIP code, appliance condition, reuse potential, stairs and access before any pickup is confirmed.

North Carolina Requests Follow Distinct Metro and Regional Paths

Charlotte, the Triangle, the Triad and the coast are different appliance markets. This statewide intake keeps those signals separate. A request near Raleigh or Durham is not treated as if the same pickup professional can automatically cover Charlotte, Greensboro, Winston-Salem, Fayetteville or Wilmington.

Urban and multi-property requests

Apartment turns, rental replacements, senior communities and property managers should list the number of units, appliance types, property addresses and loading restrictions. Repeated inventory at one site is evaluated differently from a one-item household request.

Household reuse requests

Working refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers and ranges are the strongest candidates. Send tested condition and photos before the appliance is disconnected when practical and safe.

Qualification is based on condition plus local coverage

A fully working major appliance may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when about 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repair needs can be reviewed, but one completely nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify. The statewide form is free to submit, while pickup itself is confirmed only after an available local route or partner accepts the details.

Access information prevents bad matches

Include floor level, stairs, elevators, gates, narrow doorways, parking or loading restrictions and carrying distance. For a refrigerator or freezer, report cooling. For laundry equipment, state each machine’s condition separately. For a stove or range, identify gas or electric and whether it has been safely disconnected.

North Carolina regions are shown because we want to measure demand there, not because a permanent branch exists in every listed city. Qualified submissions guide recruiting for local buyers, repair/refurbishment businesses, resellers and pickup operators. Microwaves are not accepted by themselves, and dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

North Carolina Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can I submit even if my city is not listed above?

Yes. The form accepts requests from anywhere in North Carolina. The regions above are demand groups, not a complete list of cities with guaranteed coverage.

Will someone call me immediately?

Your request must first be reviewed for appliance condition, location, access and available local coverage. A pickup is not confirmed until those details match an available route or participating local partner.

Can landlords or businesses submit several appliances?

Yes. Property managers, apartments, senior communities, hotels, installers, retailers and other organizations can describe one-time or recurring appliance replacement loads in the same request.
