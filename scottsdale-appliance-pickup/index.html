What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Fully working appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify. Dishwashers require two or more major working appliances, and microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Pickup Access
