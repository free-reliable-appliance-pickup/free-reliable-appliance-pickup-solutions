Questions

Can any resident in the metro get free appliance pickup?

The request form accepts inquiries, but no free removal can be guaranteed without an eligible appliance and available local partner or route.

What machines qualify?

Working and reusable refrigerators, freezers, stoves, washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are prioritized.

Why do ZIP and stairs matter?

Travel distances and equipment access affect whether a local operator can accept the job.
