Which refrigerators and freezers qualify?

Fully working refrigerators and freezers receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of major appliances work. A single nonworking refrigerator or freezer generally does not qualify for free pickup. Availability and eligibility are confirmed after reviewing your request.

Prepare for refrigerator or freezer pickup

Tell us whether the unit cools or freezes properly, the approximate size, whether it is emptied and accessible, and if stairs or narrow doorways are involved. Do not damage or cut refrigerant lines.

What if your appliance still works?

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers and stoves may qualify for free pickup in the Salem area. Call 503-868-4455 to discuss the appliance and whether a local pickup partner can accept it. You can also text photos or use the request form.

If your appliance does not qualify for free pickup, ask about other options such as selling it locally, checking donation acceptance requirements, or arranging removal through another provider. We do not promise paid removal or donation placement.
