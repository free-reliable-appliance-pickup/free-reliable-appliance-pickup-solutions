How Single-Washer Pickup Review Works

1. Describe the washer

Identify front-load or top-load, working condition, drain and spin results, and the known problem if any.

2. Share photos and access

Send a whole-machine photo, the controls or model label, and the pickup path from laundry room or garage to the vehicle.

3. Check your local pickup option

We review the address, reusable value, local demand and available partner routes. If a free pickup is possible, the operator confirms the details.

4. Confirm before moving anything

Do not assume same-day service. Confirm disconnection responsibilities and whether stairs or difficult access change the terms before scheduling.

Questions About Free Washer Pickup

Can I request just one washer?

Yes. A single working washing machine can be submitted for review. You do not need to supply a dryer or a matching set.

Do you pick up a washer that does not spin?

Explain the symptoms. Some repair-focused routes may consider it, but not all machines or ZIP codes qualify for free pickup.

Does free washer pickup mean free everywhere?

No. Sending a request is always free; the actual pickup must be accepted based on condition, address, access, local demand and current operator availability.

What if I have a washer and dryer together?

Use the dedicated free washer and dryer pickup guide and list both machines, including any stacked or gas dryer connections.
