Montana Appliance Reuse and Recycling Guidance

Working major appliances may be reusable; nonworking appliances often require special scrap-metal or white-goods processing. Montana Department of Environmental Quality manages the solid-waste and recycling programs, and refrigerator or freezer refrigerants must not be vented or removed by unqualified handlers. Do not cut sealed refrigerant lines or disconnect hardwired/gas appliances yourself.

See Montana DEQ recycling resources, its solid waste overview and US EPA refrigerant disposal rules. Always confirm municipal facility acceptance, handling and fees directly.
