Find the Right Appliance Pickup Page

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup reviews requests for major household appliances such as washers, dryers, refrigerators, stoves, ranges, ovens and freezers. Browse the state, regional and city pages below for pickup details and local contact information.

Use the most specific page that matches your area. Each location page explains the local coverage focus, the appliances we prioritize and how to submit a pickup request. A location appearing on this site does not by itself guarantee free pickup. Every request is reviewed for appliance condition, access, local demand and available pickup or partner coverage.

Working appliances receive priority. Appliances that need a minor repair may still be considered, and mixed loads can qualify when most of the major appliances are working. Standalone microwaves are not accepted. Dishwashers are considered only when they are included with two or more major working appliances.

What to Include With Your Request

For the fastest review, include the appliance type, whether it works, your city or ZIP code, the floor or pickup location, stairs, and any tight-access details. Photos are helpful because they show the appliance style, visible condition and whether special moving access may be needed.

Pickup can often be reviewed for appliances located in a garage, driveway or inside a home, but access matters. Narrow hallways, stairs, door removal, upper floors and other moving conditions should be described before scheduling. Providing accurate information helps avoid delays and lets the pickup team determine whether the request qualifies before a trip is arranged.
