How Coverage Works

Coverage is organized around real operating areas, broader regional hubs and partner-supported markets rather than a separate ranking page for every city. A city or state appearing on this site does not guarantee free pickup. Every request is reviewed for appliance condition, access, local demand and available route or partner coverage.

Working appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of major appliances work. Standalone microwaves are not accepted, and dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
