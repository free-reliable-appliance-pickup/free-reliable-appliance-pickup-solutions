How Free Pickup Is Reviewed

Working appliances receive priority. Eligible mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the load is working. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances, and microwaves only with another qualifying major appliance.
