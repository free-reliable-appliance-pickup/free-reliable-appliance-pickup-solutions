How Our Coverage Works

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup reviews requests for major household appliances such as washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens. Coverage is organized around real operating areas, broader regional hubs and partner-supported markets rather than a separate ranking page for every city.

A city or state appearing on this site does not guarantee free pickup. Every request is reviewed for appliance condition, access, current local demand and available pickup or partner coverage.

Working appliances receive priority. Appliances needing a minor repair may still be considered, and mixed loads may qualify when most major appliances are working. Standalone microwaves are not accepted. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

What to Include With Your Request

Include the appliance type, whether it works, your city or ZIP code, pickup location or floor, stairs and any tight-access details. Photos are helpful because they show appliance style, visible condition and possible moving-access issues.

Pickup can be reviewed for garage, driveway and inside-home locations, but access matters. Narrow hallways, stairs, door removal, upper floors and similar conditions should be described before scheduling.
