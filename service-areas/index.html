How Our Nationwide Network Works

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup is organized as one nationwide appliance pickup request network. Customers submit appliance condition, photos when available, access details and city or ZIP code through the central request system. We review the request before any pickup is promised.

1. Submit one request

Tell us what appliances you have, whether they work and where they are located.

2. Central review

We review condition, access, local demand and current coverage to determine whether the request may qualify.

3. Local fulfillment

In established direct markets, a company crew may handle the pickup. In partner-supported markets, an available local appliance pickup operator may be matched with the request.

Important: submitting a request or seeing a state or region listed does not guarantee free pickup. Pickup is confirmed only after the request is reviewed and direct or partner coverage is available.

Direct Operations and Local Partners

Our network is designed to expand through real local fulfillment rather than creating a separate virtual location in every city. Local partners may include appliance pickup operators, appliance resellers, repair and refurbishment businesses, recyclers and haulers that can evaluate and handle qualifying appliances in their service areas.

This structure lets customers use one recognizable request process while the actual pickup is handled locally. Businesses interested in serving approved areas can learn about the Appliance Pickup Partner Program.

How Coverage Works

Coverage is organized around real operating areas, broader state hubs and partner-supported markets rather than a separate ranking page for every city. Every request is reviewed for appliance condition, access, local demand and available route or partner coverage.

Working appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of major appliances work. Standalone microwaves are not accepted, and dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
