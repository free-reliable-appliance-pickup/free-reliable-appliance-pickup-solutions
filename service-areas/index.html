Pickup Qualification

Working appliances receive priority. Fully working qualifying appliances may receive free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Standalone microwaves are not accepted, and dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
