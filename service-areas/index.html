How Our Pickup Network Works

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup uses one request network with local operating areas, selected city pages, regional hubs and partner-supported markets. Customers submit appliance condition, photos when available, access details and city or ZIP code. We review the request before any pickup is promised.

1. Choose your area

Use a local city page when one is available or start with the appropriate regional or state hub.

2. Submit one request

Tell us what appliances you have, whether they work and exactly where they are located.

3. Local review

We review condition, access, demand and current route or partner coverage before pickup is confirmed.

Important: a city page is a request and service-information page, not a promise that every appliance or every address receives free pickup. Qualification and route availability still apply.

Pickup Qualification

Working appliances receive priority. Fully working qualifying appliances may receive free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Standalone microwaves are not accepted, and dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
