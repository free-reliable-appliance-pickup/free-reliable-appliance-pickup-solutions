How California Pickup Is Reviewed

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens. Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Free pickup is not automatic and is confirmed only after the appliance condition, exact location, access, local demand and available route or partner coverage are reviewed.

This page is the California hub. We organize local information by meaningful operating regions instead of maintaining a separate search page for every city.

Send useful details first: appliance type, working condition, city or ZIP code, floor level, stairs, access restrictions and clear photos.

What Usually Qualifies?

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Appliances needing a minor repair may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only when included with two or more major working appliances.

Access and Condition Details Matter

For refrigerators and freezers, tell us whether the unit maintains temperature and include interior and exterior photos when possible. For washers, tell us whether they fill, drain and spin. For dryers, include whether they heat and tumble and whether the unit is gas or electric.

For inside pickup, describe stairs, basements, upper floors, narrow hallways, door removal, elevators, gated access and parking restrictions before scheduling. Accurate information helps avoid unnecessary trips and lets the pickup team determine whether the request can be handled safely.
