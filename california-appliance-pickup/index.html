What Usually Qualifies?

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Appliances needing a minor repair may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup under the general California qualification rules.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Selected washer-and-dryer routes may have different repair-focused review rules; use the dedicated laundry pages above for those markets.

Access and Condition Details Matter

For refrigerators and freezers, tell us whether the unit maintains temperature and include interior and exterior photos. For washers, tell us whether they fill, drain and spin. For dryers, include whether they heat and tumble and whether the unit is gas or electric.

For inside pickup, describe stairs, basements, upper floors, narrow hallways, door removal, elevators, gated access and parking restrictions before scheduling.
