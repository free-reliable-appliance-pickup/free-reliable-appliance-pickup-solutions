Westminster Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused route, the City of Westminster identifies Midway City Sanitary District as the local bulky-item provider. The City's current FAQ says the District provides a monthly bulky-item service for some appliances and furniture and collects two such items from each household approximately every 30 days.

This municipal service is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup. Confirm the specific appliance, scheduling and curb-placement rules directly with the District before setting anything outside.

Westminster CA Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup automatically free in Westminster?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based. We review condition, exact Westminster, CA address, safe access, local demand and route availability before confirming service.

Can one working appliance qualify?

Yes. One working refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer, stove, range or oven can be submitted for review.

Can you pick up from an apartment or gated property?

Those requests can be reviewed. Include floor level, stairs/elevator, gate entry, loading or parking restrictions and carrying distance.

What if the appliance is broken?

A single completely nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for our free reuse-focused route. The local bulky-item option above may be a better fit for an end-of-life appliance.

Is this the page for Westminster, Colorado?

No. This page is only for Westminster, California. Use the Westminster, Colorado page for Colorado addresses.
