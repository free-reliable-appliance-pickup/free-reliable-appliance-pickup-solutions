What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

A fully working major appliance is the strongest fit. Minor-repair appliances may sometimes be considered depending on the item and current demand. A single completely nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup by itself.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only when included with two or more major working appliances. Exact qualification also depends on access and route availability.

Send the exact address or ZIP, floor level, stairs or elevator, parking or loading restrictions, building-entry details, gates, hallway or doorway limitations and carrying distance. Clear access photos are especially useful for larger refrigerators, ranges and laundry equipment.

Westminster Apartment, Gate & Parking Details

For apartments and multi-unit properties, include the floor, elevator availability, stair flights, building-entry instructions and parking or loading distance. For houses, tell us whether the appliance is in the garage, driveway, first floor, upstairs or another location and whether gates or narrow side access affect the route.

Landlords and property managers may submit multiple units at once. Grouping appliances in an accessible garage or vacant unit can simplify review, but every pickup still requires confirmation.
