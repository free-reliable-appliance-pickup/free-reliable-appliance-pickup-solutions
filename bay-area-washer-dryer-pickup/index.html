Bay Area Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Is pickup automatically free throughout the Bay Area?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based and depends on condition, exact location, access, route distance and current coverage.

Which city page should I use?

Use the dedicated city page that matches the actual pickup address. If your city is not listed, use this hub and enter the exact city, ZIP code and address.

Can one working washer or dryer qualify?

Yes. Single working machines can be reviewed, while complete working sets generally receive stronger consideration.
