Appliances We Review in Baldwin Park

Washers & Dryers

Report washer fill, drain and spin and dryer tumble, heat and gas/electric type. Complete working sets receive strong priority.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Include inside and outside photos, whether the unit is plugged in and whether it holds temperature.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and whether burners, controls and oven functions operate normally.

Multiple Appliances

List each appliance separately. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances; microwaves only with another qualifying major appliance.

Baldwin Park Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can one working appliance qualify?

Yes. A single working washer, dryer, refrigerator, freezer, stove or range can be reviewed.

Can inside pickups be reviewed?

Yes. Include floor level, stairs, gates, doorways and carrying distance.

What if it needs repair?

Minor-repair appliances may sometimes qualify depending on condition, age, parts demand and current route capacity.
