What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working major appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Every request is reviewed for appliance condition, exact city or ZIP code, access, current demand and available route or partner coverage before a pickup time is confirmed.

How Central Coast Routes Differ

Monterey Bay

Santa Cruz, Watsonville, Monterey, Seaside and Salinas requests may involve dense streets, apartments, hills, limited parking or longer travel between communities.

San Luis Obispo County

Paso Robles, San Luis Obispo, Arroyo Grande and nearby requests can involve longer north-south routing and rural or hillside access.

Santa Barbara County

Santa Maria, Lompoc, Santa Barbara and Goleta requests may require advance planning for route distance, gates, property access or multi-unit scheduling.

Ventura County

Ventura, Oxnard and nearby communities can be reviewed for route availability with complete appliance and access details.

Appliances We Review

Washers

Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins, and mention leaks, noise or error codes.

Dryers

Identify gas or electric and tell us whether the dryer tumbles and heats normally.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Include an interior photo and tell us whether the appliance is currently plugged in and cooling or freezing correctly.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Gas and electric cooking appliances may qualify. Tell us whether burners and oven functions operate normally.

Access Details Matter on Coastal Routes

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first-floor room, upstairs apartment, condominium, storage area or business. Include stair flights, elevator availability, steep driveways, gates, narrow halls, loading rules and parking restrictions.

For large refrigerators or tight kitchens, send a photo of the exit path if door removal may be needed. For apartment or condo pickups, include the floor level and whether the building requires scheduled elevator or loading access.

Best first message: city/ZIP + appliance list + working condition + clear photos + floor/stairs + access notes + total number of appliances.

Landlords, Property Managers & Multiple Appliances

For rental turnovers, multi-unit properties or several appliances at one location, list each appliance separately with its condition. Multiple qualifying appliances can make a longer Central Coast route more practical. Tell us whether the appliances are together, in different units or at separate nearby addresses.

For property-management requests, include gate codes, loading instructions, contact-on-site details and any time restrictions that could affect pickup access.

Photos Help Avoid Unnecessary Trips

Send one full exterior photo of each appliance, an interior photo for refrigerators and freezers, and a model-label photo when available. Accurate photos and condition details help us decide whether an appliance appears reusable and whether a longer route is practical before anyone travels.

Central Coast requests use 310-774-4304. Service hours are Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM. Calling, texting or submitting the form begins the review; service is confirmed only after qualification and route availability are checked.

Central Coast Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is pickup free everywhere on the Central Coast?

No guarantee is made by location alone. Free pickup is qualification-based and depends on condition, route availability, access and current demand.

Can I submit a request from Santa Cruz or Monterey?

Yes. Submit the city or ZIP code, appliance details, photos and access information so Monterey Bay route coverage can be reviewed.

Can I submit from San Luis Obispo, Santa Barbara or Ventura?

Yes. These areas can be reviewed through this regional page. Longer routes require complete information before scheduling.

What if my appliance does not work?

A nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repair needs may be considered, and eligible mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.
