Central Coast Regional Coverage

This regional hub can review requests from Santa Cruz, Monterey, Salinas, San Luis Obispo, Santa Maria, Santa Barbara, Ventura and nearby communities. We group these communities into one useful regional resource rather than producing separate near-duplicate city landing pages.

Central Coast distances and route schedules vary considerably. Exact ZIP code, appliance condition, photos and number of appliances help determine whether pickup is practical.

What Makes a Request Easier to Review?

Condition details

Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Tell us whether washers fill/drain/spin, dryers heat/tumble, and refrigerators or freezers maintain temperature.

Photos and model

Clear exterior, interior and model-label photos help us review size, condition and reusability before arranging a longer route.

Access information

Tell us about hills, driveways, gates, stairs, floor level, elevators, narrow halls, parking and loading restrictions.

Route-Based Service

Coastal geography makes route planning especially important. A request near Santa Cruz or Monterey may be reviewed on a different route from San Luis Obispo, Santa Barbara or Ventura. Multiple qualifying appliances, flexible timing and complete information can make a longer route easier to coordinate.

Best first message: city/ZIP, appliance list, condition, photos, floor/stairs and access details.

Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance alone generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

California Contact

Central Coast requests use 310-774-4304. Service hours are 8:00 AM–8:00 PM daily. Contact starts a review; free pickup is not confirmed until condition, access and route availability are evaluated.
