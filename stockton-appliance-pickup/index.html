What Qualifies for Free Pickup in Stockton?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Working and reusable washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. Each request is reviewed for appliance condition, Stockton location, access, current demand and available pickup coverage before scheduling.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Stockton Appliance Pickup & Removal

Submit a pickup request when you are replacing a household appliance, clearing a rental, managing a move-out or removing reusable equipment from a home or business. Tell us exactly what you have and whether each appliance works.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Sending complete condition and access details helps us determine whether the load qualifies before a pickup time is confirmed.

Appliances We Review

Washers

Top-load and front-load washers. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether it has leaks or error codes.

Dryers

Electric and gas dryers, including matching laundry sets. Tell us whether the dryer heats and tumbles and identify the fuel type.

Refrigerators

Standard, side-by-side, bottom-freezer and French-door refrigerators. Include an interior photo and whether both sections maintain temperature.

Freezers

Chest and upright freezers in reusable condition. Tell us whether the unit freezes properly and include an interior photo.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may qualify. Describe whether the burners and oven operate normally.

Stockton Pickup Access Details

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upstairs, basement, apartment laundry area or business. Include the floor level, number of stair flights, narrow doors, tight hallways, gates and parking limitations.

If an appliance must pass through a small doorway or a refrigerator door may need to be removed, mention that before scheduling. For apartments or managed properties, include elevator, loading-zone or building-access requirements.

Stockton Areas We Review

We review qualifying requests throughout Stockton, including Downtown Stockton, Brookside, Spanos Park, Lincoln Village, Weston Ranch and surrounding Stockton neighborhoods. Send your ZIP code and full pickup address so coverage can be confirmed for the specific location.

For nearby Central Valley requests outside Stockton, send the city and ZIP code first. Coverage varies by route and available pickup capacity.

Photos and Information to Send

For the fastest review, send one full photo of each appliance, a model-label photo when available, the working condition of every item, your Stockton ZIP code and the access details. For a multi-appliance load, list each appliance separately rather than describing the group as a single condition.

Call or text 310-774-4304, Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM. Pickup is confirmed only after the appliance load, location and access have been reviewed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is appliance pickup free in Stockton?

Qualifying working appliances and eligible mixed loads may receive free pickup after condition, access, location, demand and coverage are reviewed.

Do you pick up washers and dryers?

Yes. Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are priority categories.

Do you pick up refrigerators and freezers?

Qualifying refrigerators and household freezers can be reviewed. Send photos and confirm whether the unit maintains temperature.

Can I submit several appliances at once?

Yes. List every appliance and its working condition. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work.

How do I request pickup?

Use the online request form or call or text 310-774-4304 with appliance condition, Stockton ZIP code, photos and access details.
