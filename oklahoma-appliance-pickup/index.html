How Free Appliance Pickup Requests Work

  1. Tell us which appliances work, the number of items and any defects.
  2. Provide ZIP, photo availability, floors, stairs, loading access and your contact number.
  3. We review reuse potential and check whether an accepting local operator is available.
  4. Any appointment and any optional paid quote require separate confirmation.

Working appliances and mostly working sets are prioritized. Nonworking items are reviewed case by case. Dishwashers may be considered with two or more other working major appliances; microwaves alone are not collected.

Independent Oklahoma Disposal Alternatives

Oklahoma City municipal bulky waste collection has separate rules for qualifying residential accounts and cooling units. Tulsa city bulky waste pickup charges its own collection fee and accepts listed appliances. Neither program is affiliated with Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.
