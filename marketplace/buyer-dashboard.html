Individual pricing

Protected market rules apply. Availability depends on territory assignment and overflow status.

Buyer matching preferences

Approved buyers will be able to choose appliance types, condition, service radius, cities/ZIPs, commercial interest and maximum opportunity price.

Matching principle: do not send scrap refrigerator opportunities to a buyer who only wants working washer/dryer sets.

Performance & priority

Priority can consider successful pickups, prompt customer contact, expired offers, cancellations, no-shows and complaints. Reliable buyers can receive higher priority and become candidates for monthly or protected territories.

Wallet & payments

The planned wallet lets a buyer preload funds and claim opportunities quickly. The production version will only activate after a payment provider and webhook verification are connected; no fake payment flow is enabled on this preview.
