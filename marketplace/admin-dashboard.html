Territory controls

Each market can be classified as Protected, Open or Territory Available. Protected partners can receive first priority, while overflow can be released to the marketplace when allowed.

Safety rule: private customer contact data is never part of the public opportunity feed.

Buyer quality controls

Track response time, completed pickups, passes, expirations, cancellations, no-shows and customer complaints. Strong operators can move up in priority; poor performers can lose access.
