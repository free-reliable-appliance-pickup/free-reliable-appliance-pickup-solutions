Core operating rules

Payment before unlock. New buyers never receive private customer information before payment.

Prompt contact required. Once customer details unlock, the buyer is expected to contact the customer quickly and arrange pickup.

Truthful urgency. Buyers are told when an opportunity is also being offered to other qualified providers. Claimed opportunities lock immediately.

Coverage-aware customer messaging. Customers receive different expectations depending on whether the market has strong, limited or no current provider coverage.
