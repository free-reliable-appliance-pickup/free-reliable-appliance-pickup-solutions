Request Refrigerator & Freezer Pickup

Enter your pickup location and appliance details. You do not need to find a local phone number first.

Submitting does not guarantee free pickup. Appliance qualification, access, local demand and available partner coverage must be confirmed.

What Helps Your Request Qualify?

Tell us whether the refrigerator or freezer cools properly, whether it is empty, and whether there are stairs, narrow doors or other access restrictions. Keep doors secured safely if the appliance is stored.

Fully working major appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Pickup is reviewed case by case; we cannot guarantee service in every ZIP code.

How the Nationwide Request Network Works

1. Submit the appliance type, condition and pickup address. 2. We review qualification and local availability. 3. If coverage is available, pickup arrangements can be confirmed.
