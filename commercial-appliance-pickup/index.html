Commercial & Recurring Appliance Pickup

Commercial appliance removal is different from a one-item household request. The useful details are the equipment count, condition, site type, loading access, cadence and destination requirements. We review working and reusable appliances first and coordinate requests through available local coverage.

Multifamily & Apartments

Apartment turnovers can produce washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers and ranges from multiple units. Include unit numbers, elevators, stairs, loading areas and whether the appliances are staged or still installed.

Property Managers & Landlords

For recurring replacements, list each appliance separately and identify what was tested. Working or reusable equipment receives priority for qualification-based pickup.

Businesses & Installers

Retailers, installers, offices, hospitality sites, warehouses and service businesses can submit one-time or recurring appliance-removal requests. Include site contacts, loading constraints and expected volume.

Senior Living & 55+ Communities

Retirement communities, independent living, assisted living and senior apartments can submit one-time or recurring appliance replacement requests. See the senior living pickup program.

Independent Local Pickup Network

Commercial requests are routed through independent local pickup professionals where coverage is available. This page does not represent a company-owned local branch in every market. Each site, appliance load, access plan and current provider availability are reviewed before service is confirmed.

One Site, Multiple Sites & Recurring Projects

One Site

List every appliance, condition, floor or unit, loading access and onsite contact for the single address.

Multiple Sites

One program inquiry can cover multiple properties, stores or facilities. List each site or service area clearly with its appliance flow and access rules so route review stays accurate.

Recurring Work

For ongoing turnovers or replacement programs, include expected frequency, markets, appliance categories and approximate monthly volume. Each actual pickup still requires current load and access details.

Photos That Speed Up Commercial Review

Send clear photos of each appliance or staged group, plus loading areas, stairs, elevators, docks, gates or narrow pathways when they affect removal. For refrigerators and freezers include cooling status; for laundry equipment include basic test results; for ranges identify gas or electric service.

Text California commercial appliance photos to 909-375-6685.

Equipment We Review

Laundry Equipment

Washers, dryers and complete laundry sets. Include electric/gas dryer type, testing status and whether equipment is stacked or installed in a laundry room.

Refrigeration

Refrigerators, stand-alone freezers and qualifying commercial refrigeration equipment. Describe cooling performance and whether refrigerant-containing equipment is still intact.

Cooking Appliances

Ranges, stoves and ovens. Identify gas or electric service, approximate size and whether burners and oven functions work.

Free pickup is not automatic. A broken or difficult commercial appliance may require a paid removal, recycling or disposal provider. We do not promise free service until the actual load, condition, access and route are reviewed.

Commercial Appliance Pickup Questions

Do you guarantee free commercial appliance removal?

No. Free pickup depends on appliance condition, load mix, access, exact location and available local coverage. Working and reusable appliances receive priority.

Can you review multiple appliances from one property?

Yes. List every appliance and its condition. For multiple units or properties, separate the appliance list by site or apartment so the route can be reviewed accurately.

Can property managers submit recurring requests?

Yes. Recurring apartment turnovers and replacement projects can be reviewed. Include expected frequency, appliance categories and the cities or properties involved.

What about broken commercial appliances?

Submit the details if the appliance may have repair or reuse value. If it does not qualify, a municipal, recycler or paid removal provider may be a better fit.
