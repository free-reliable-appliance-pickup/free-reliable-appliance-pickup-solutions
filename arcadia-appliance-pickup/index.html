What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Our free service is focused on major appliances with reuse value. A fully working appliance is the strongest fit. Minor repair may sometimes be considered. One completely nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup by itself. Mixed loads can be reviewed when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only when they are included with two or more major working appliances. Pickup is never automatically guaranteed; condition, access, local demand and route availability are reviewed first.

Arcadia Pickup Details That Help Us Schedule

For refrigerators and freezers, mention whether the appliance is still plugged in and cold. For laundry machines, describe the last tested cycle. If doors may need to be removed, a gate must be opened, or the appliance has to travel through a narrow hallway, include that information before scheduling.

Landlords and property managers can submit several units at once. If appliances are already grouped in a garage, driveway or vacant unit, say so. Clear access information can make the review much faster.
