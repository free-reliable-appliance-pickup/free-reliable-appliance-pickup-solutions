Which appliances might qualify?

Working first

Usable washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers and stoves receive priority for potential reuse; condition, location and acceptance still determine pickup details.

Condition and access matter

Share exterior and interior photos, machine tests, floor, stairs, driveway, door widths and the real ZIP. Nonworking units require individual review.

Free is conditional

A possible paid removal quote is optional and requires your separate agreement. An inquiry is never a scheduled pickup or charge.

How to submit a conditional request

  1. Describe each appliance: List type, quantity, what currently works, model or dimensions and any damage.
  2. Show the removal path: State actual street, city, ZIP, floor, stairs, doorway limits and access for carrying.
  3. Wait for operator confirmation: We review whether an accepting local provider and any pickup terms can be verified. A form submission does not book an appointment.
  4. Accept only approved terms: Free pickup requires acceptance; any paid alternative is a separate quote requiring your consent.

Independent appliance recycling or removal alternatives

The City of Columbus bulk collection guide states that eligible non-refrigerated appliances may be collected by appointment through 311, while refrigerators and freezers are excluded from that curbside program. The city's rules are separate from private appliance reuse pickup.

Ohio EPA advises that appliances can often be recycled through appropriate scrap or district programs and that refrigerants require professional recovery. Review Ohio EPA appliance disposal guidance and check with the local solid-waste district before transporting equipment. These recycling providers are independent of our conditional reuse pickup request.

Retailer haul-away, licensed recycling and municipal bulky-item collection may have different pickup details and charges. These programs are independent from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup; do not mistake public drop-off for a confirmed private pickup or puncture refrigerant lines.
