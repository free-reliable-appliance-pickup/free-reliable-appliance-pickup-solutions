Which used appliances may qualify?

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, stoves and freezers, especially working sets and multi-appliance loads, receive priority for possible reuse. Broken or isolated items, second-floor moves and difficult access need an individual review. An optional paid removal quote requires separate customer consent.

Separate official disposal options

For residents who cannot secure a private reuse pickup, some municipalities and licensed facilities offer independent options. Richmond's collection information includes a $50 city appliance collection fee; Virginia Beach's bulky-item instructions describe its separate municipal scheduling and appliance rules. Neither program is operated or booked by this site.

The Virginia Department of Environmental Quality's white-goods guidance explains that appliances may contain refrigerants and other regulated fluids. Ask the local government or recycler about accepted items and fees. This information concerns separate disposal programs, not a pickup route we operate.

Send a Virginia Appliance eligibility request

A form submission asks us to review a possible appliance pickup and does not create an appointment or confirm free service. Send the exact pickup ZIP and access details. Photos can be shared after the request if a current photo link is not available yet.

Privacy: Details are sent through Formspree for review and may be shared with a prospective independent pickup operator. Read our Privacy Policy. Never provide payment or ID documents in this form.
