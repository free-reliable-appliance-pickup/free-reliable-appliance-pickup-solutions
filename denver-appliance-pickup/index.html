Denver Appliance Pickup & Removal

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup provides pickup and removal for qualifying household appliances in Denver and nearby active service areas. We review each request individually before confirming pickup.

If you have a refrigerator, dryer, stove, range, freezer or qualifying washer, send us clear photos along with the appliance condition, Denver ZIP code and exact location of the appliance.

What Appliances May Qualify?

Refrigerators

Qualifying household refrigerators including side-by-side, French-door, top-freezer and bottom-freezer models may be considered.

Washers

Select top-load and front-load washing machines may qualify. Tell us whether the washer is fully functioning, needs repair, or is not working.

Dryers

Qualifying electric and gas dryers may be considered. Include clear photos and explain the current condition.

Stoves & Ranges

Qualifying electric and gas stoves, ranges and ovens may be considered.

Freezers

Qualifying upright and chest freezers may be considered.

Microwaves

We do not normally take microwaves by themselves. A microwave may be considered when it comes with another qualifying major appliance.

Denver Pickup Requirements

Before we confirm pickup, please provide:

  • Clear photos of the appliance
  • Appliance type and brand when known
  • Whether it is fully functioning or needs repair
  • What is wrong with it if repair is needed
  • Denver address, city and ZIP code
  • Exact location of the appliance
  • First floor, second floor, third floor, basement, garage or outside
  • Whether stairs are involved
  • Information about narrow hallways, doorways or difficult access
