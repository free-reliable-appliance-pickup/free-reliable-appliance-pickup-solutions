Denver Appliance Pickup & Removal

Submit washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges for pickup review in Denver. Working and reusable appliances receive priority, and freezers are an especially useful category in Colorado.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Appliance condition, location, building or property access, local demand and available coverage are reviewed before scheduling.

Appliances We Review

Washers

Working top-load and front-load washers are a priority. Tell us whether the machine fills, drains and spins, and describe leaks or error messages.

Dryers

Working electric and gas dryers, including laundry sets. Tell us whether the dryer heats and turns, its fuel type and the condition of each machine in a set.

Refrigerators

Standard, side-by-side, bottom-freezer and French-door refrigerators.

Freezers

Working chest and upright freezers are a priority in Colorado. Tell us whether the freezer holds its temperature, its approximate size and whether it is in a basement, garage or inside the home.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may qualify.

What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Qualifying fully working appliances may receive free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Denver Pickup Access

Include the floor level, stairs, elevator access, narrow doors or hallways, gates, parking or loading restrictions, and whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, house, apartment or commercial property. Photos help us review the request faster.

Denver and Aurora Pickup Coverage

Our Colorado pickup area centers on Denver and Aurora, with requests reviewed approximately 35–45 miles around the metro area. This is a coverage guideline, not automatic approval: send your address or ZIP code so we can confirm the route and pickup conditions.

Nearby requests may include Lakewood, Arvada, Westminster, Thornton, Centennial and Parker. Check the Colorado service page for broader coverage, or contact the Colorado team at 720-604-7498.

What to Send the Colorado Pickup Team

Send one clear photo of each appliance, its brand and model when known, and whether it is fully working, needs repair or does not work. For a refrigerator or freezer, tell us whether it maintains its temperature; for laundry sets, describe each machine separately.

For basement or apartment pickup, include stairs, elevator arrangements, doorway clearance and parking or loading restrictions. For multiple appliances, list the condition of every item so we can review the working share of the complete load.

Call or text between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM, Monday through Sunday. We review the information and confirm eligibility and an available pickup time before scheduling.
