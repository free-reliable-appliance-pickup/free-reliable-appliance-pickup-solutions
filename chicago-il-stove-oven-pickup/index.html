Chicago kitchen installations affect safe removal

A range in an older Chicago apartment may sit inside a narrow galley kitchen with an angled hallway, while a condo unit may require a scheduled service-elevator move. Measure the stove's width and height, the narrowest doorway and any steps. Indicate if the cooking appliance is part of a remodel, still bolted in, or positioned behind a tight countertop opening. A fitted built-in oven needs additional planning and may not qualify.

Report the actual working tests

For gas ranges, report which burners ignite and whether the oven heats and controls respond. For electric ranges, state whether coil or glass-top burners operate and whether the oven reaches temperature. Mention broken knobs, shattered ceramic glass, missing grates, oven-door damage or unusual gas odors. A fully working, reusable range is generally more suitable for evaluation than a dangerous or incomplete unit.

Gas and electrical disconnection rules

Never disconnect a gas range or hardwired electric oven without qualified assistance. If an appliance is still installed, note that explicitly and tell us who will perform the safe disconnection. A pickup decision must account for the connection type, access limits and whether a local operator is actually available.
