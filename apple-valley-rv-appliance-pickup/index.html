Air-Conditioning Units

Already-removed, intact RV air-conditioning units can be submitted for review. We do not open refrigerant systems or remove rooftop units from the RV.

Safety & Disconnection Requirements

Pickup does not include RV repair or utility disconnection. We do not disconnect propane lines, remove propane cylinders or fuel tanks, perform electrical or gas work, drain fuel systems, open refrigerant systems, or cut built-in equipment out of an RV. Equipment must already be safely disconnected and accessible. Use a qualified RV technician when needed.

Apple Valley RV Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can you pick up an RV propane refrigerator?

Yes, it can be submitted after it has been safely disconnected and removed from the RV.

Can you take an RV furnace, heater or water heater?

Removed units can be reviewed. We do not disconnect gas, propane or electrical systems.

Can you pick up an RV generator?

Portable or removed generators can be reviewed when safely prepared for transport. Do not include loose fuel containers or propane cylinders.

What other RV items can I submit?

Removed ranges, ovens, cooktops, microwaves, compact laundry units, freezers and other removable RV appliances can be reviewed. Send photos when the item is unusual.
