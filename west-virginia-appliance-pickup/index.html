How Free Appliance Pickup Requests Work

  1. Describe your used appliance, whether working, and any defects.
  2. Share photos, exact ZIP, location, stairs and safe access.
  3. We check whether an independent local operator accepts the ZIP and item.
  4. Only after acceptance can terms and a date be confirmed. Optional paid service requires a separate customer-approved quote.
