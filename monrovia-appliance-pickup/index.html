What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Fully working and reusable major appliances receive the strongest consideration. Minor-repair appliances may be reviewed when local demand and route capacity support the pickup. For mixed loads, list the condition of every appliance instead of describing the whole load as working.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Submitting a request begins a review; it does not create a guaranteed appointment. Review the full qualification rules before submitting a heavily damaged or nonworking load.
