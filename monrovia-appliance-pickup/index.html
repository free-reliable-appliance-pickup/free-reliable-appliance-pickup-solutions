Monrovia Appliance Pickup for Homes, Rentals & Garages

Monrovia is a priority market in our Pasadena-to-Fontana service corridor. We review pickup requests for reusable major appliances from houses, apartments, rental units, garages and property-management turnovers. The appliance condition matters, but the removal path matters too.

Some Monrovia homes have older layouts, exterior steps, detached garages or longer carries from the appliance to the street. Foothill properties can also involve sloped driveways or stair approaches. How Free Pickup Qualification Works

Free pickup is intended for major appliances with reuse value. Fully working items are the strongest fit, and minor repair may sometimes be considered. A single completely nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup by itself.

For multiple-appliance loads, we review the group together. A load may qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. Microwaves must come with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Monrovia Access Information to Include

If the appliance is inside an older house, tell us about narrow doorways, porches, interior turns and stairs. If it is in a detached garage, describe driveway or alley access and whether a truck can get close. For apartments, include floor level, elevator information, hallway access and parking restrictions.

Property managers can submit several units together. Grouping working appliances in a garage or accessible area can make evaluation and removal simpler, but we still need each appliance condition listed separately.

Monrovia Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can you review a refrigerator in a detached garage?

Yes. Tell us whether it is cold and working, and describe how close a pickup vehicle can get to the garage.

What if the washer is upstairs?

Include the number of flights, stair width, landings and any tight turns. Upstairs pickup requires access review.

Can landlords submit several appliances?

Yes. List each item and its condition so the whole load can be evaluated correctly.
