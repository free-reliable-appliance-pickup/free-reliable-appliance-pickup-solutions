Free appliance pickup in Columbus: what qualifies?

We review working refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers and stoves for free pickup with an available local partner. Please tell us if an appliance is not working. For mixed loads, share the condition of each appliance so we can confirm whether the pickup qualifies before scheduling. Free pickup is not guaranteed for every appliance or address.

Have a washer and dryer set, refrigerator, or freezer? Use the specific Columbus service pages below, or call 614-874-3407 to discuss your pickup.

How is this different from city bulk collection?

Columbus also offers municipal bulk collection for qualifying residents and certain appliances without refrigerants. City collection generally requires a scheduled curbside placement. Our service instead reviews appliance condition, photos and access details with an independent pickup partner. Confirm any inside removal, stairs, availability and cost before pickup.

For the municipal option, see the City of Columbus bulk collection information.

What we review for pickup

Tell us whether the appliance powers on and performs its normal functions. For refrigerators and freezers, confirm whether they cool or freeze properly. For washers, tell us whether they fill, drain and spin; for dryers, whether the drum turns and heats. If unsure, choose Unknown in the form.

Working single appliances and multi-appliance loads may qualify. Nonworking appliances or loads containing mostly nonworking items may not qualify for free service. Dishwashers are only considered alongside at least two other major working appliances; microwaves are not accepted alone.
