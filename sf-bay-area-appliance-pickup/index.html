What Helps Us Review a Pickup

Working condition

Tell us exactly what works. For washers include fill, drain and spin; for dryers include heat and tumble; for refrigerators and freezers include cooling status.

Clear photos

Exterior, interior and model-label photos help determine condition, size, age and whether an appliance may be reusable.

Building access

Include floor level, stairs, elevator availability, narrow halls, gates, loading restrictions, parking and whether doors may need removal.

Bay Area Access Can Change the Pickup Plan

San Francisco and other dense communities can involve limited curb access, apartment stairs, elevators and loading restrictions. Peninsula and South Bay requests may involve condominium rules or scheduled loading areas. East Bay and North Bay requests can involve hills, garages, gates and longer routes.

For large refrigerators, send a photo of the exit path if the doorway or hallway is tight. For stacked laundry units, tell us whether the units can be separated. For garage or driveway pickup, mention slopes, gates, alleys or long carries.

Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance alone generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Landlords, Rentals and Property Managers

For rental turnovers or multi-unit properties, list every appliance separately with its condition and unit location. Tell us whether all items will be staged in one garage or whether access is needed to several units.

For multiple addresses, use the message field to identify which appliances are at each property. Mixed loads are still reviewed for working percentage, access and route availability before scheduling.

Bay Area Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free throughout the Bay Area?

Only qualifying requests receive free pickup. Condition, location, access, demand and available route or partner coverage are reviewed before confirmation.

Can I request pickup from an apartment or condo?

Yes. Include the floor, stair flights, elevator information, loading restrictions and parking details.

Does a broken appliance qualify?

A nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify. Minor repairs may be considered, and qualifying mixed loads may be accepted when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.
