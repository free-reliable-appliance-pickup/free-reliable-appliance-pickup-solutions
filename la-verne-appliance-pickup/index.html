La Verne Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can I get free appliance pickup in La Verne?

Yes. A fully working or reusable major appliance can be submitted for review. Acceptance depends on condition, exact address, access and current route availability.

Do broken appliances qualify?

A completely nonworking appliance by itself normally does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs or a qualifying mixed load may still be reviewed.

Can you pick up from gated or hillside properties?

They can be submitted, but gate access, slope, stairs, carrying distance and safe removal conditions must be reviewed before pickup is confirmed.

How soon can appliance pickup be arranged in La Verne?

Timing depends on qualification and current local route availability. Send complete photos and access details first so the request can be reviewed accurately.
