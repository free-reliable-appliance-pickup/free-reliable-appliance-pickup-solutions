How Nevada Pickup Is Reviewed

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens. Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Free pickup is qualification-based and is confirmed only after appliance condition, exact location, access, local demand and current route or partner coverage are reviewed.

Send useful details first: appliance type, working condition, city or ZIP code, floor level, stairs, access restrictions and clear photos.

Priority Nevada Request Regions

Las Vegas Metro

Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas and nearby communities may submit requests for review. Coverage is determined from the exact pickup location and available route rather than from a separate landing page for each city.

Reno Metro & Northern Nevada

Reno, Sparks and nearby northern Nevada communities may submit requests. Longer-distance requests depend more heavily on appliance condition, load size and available route or partner coverage.

Other Nevada locations can also be submitted for review. Listing a region here does not guarantee free pickup before the request is evaluated.

Appliance Condition Details

Washers & Dryers

Tell us whether washers fill, drain and spin, and whether dryers heat and tumble. Include gas or electric for dryers.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the appliance maintains temperature and include exterior and interior photos when possible.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Include fuel type, working condition and any known burner, oven or control problems.

What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Access and Scheduling

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upstairs, basement or outside. Include stairs, elevators, gates, narrow doors, tight hallways, parking restrictions and any door-removal concerns before scheduling.

Accurate photos and access information help determine whether the request can be handled safely and efficiently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is every Nevada appliance pickup free?

No. Free pickup depends on appliance condition, location, access, local demand and available coverage.

Are washers and dryers a priority?

Yes. Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are priority categories.

Can I submit several appliances?

Yes. List each major appliance and its condition in one request so the complete load can be reviewed.
