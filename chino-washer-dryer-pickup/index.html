Chino Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Can one working washer or dryer qualify?

Yes. A single working washer or dryer can be reviewed. Complete working sets generally receive stronger priority.

What condition details should I send?

For a washer, report power, fill, drain and spin. For a dryer, report power, tumble, heat and whether it is gas or electric.

Do stairs or gates matter?

Yes. Include floor level, stairs, gates, parking, narrow doors and carrying distance so access can be reviewed before pickup is confirmed.
