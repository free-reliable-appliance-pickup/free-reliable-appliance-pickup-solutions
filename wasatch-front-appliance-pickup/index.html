Wasatch Front Coverage

This regional hub reviews requests from Salt Lake City, West Valley City, Sandy, Draper, Lehi, American Fork, Orem, Provo, Layton, Ogden and nearby communities. These locations are organized as one connected regional market rather than separate near-duplicate city pages.

Coverage depends on appliance condition, exact location, access, local demand and available route or partner capacity. Listing a community does not guarantee free pickup.

What Helps With Pickup Review?

Condition

Tell us whether washers fill, drain and spin; dryers heat and tumble; refrigerators/freezers maintain temperature; and stoves have working burners and oven.

Photos

Exterior, interior and model-label photos help determine size, condition and reusability before a route is scheduled.

Access

Report stairs, basements, upper floors, elevators, gates, narrow halls, garages and whether appliance doors may need removal.

North-South Route Planning

The Wasatch Front stretches across several connected metro areas, so exact ZIP code matters. Salt Lake-area requests may be grouped differently from Utah County or Ogden-area requests. Multiple qualifying appliances should be listed together so the complete load can be evaluated.

Best request: city/ZIP + appliance list + condition + photos + floor/stairs + access.

Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance alone generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
