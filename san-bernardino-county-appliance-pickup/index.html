San Bernardino County Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free in San Bernardino County?

It can be free for qualifying fully working appliances or eligible mixed loads after condition, location, access and route availability are reviewed.

Do broken appliances qualify?

A single nonworking appliance does not qualify under the general free-pickup rules. Minor repair needs may be considered, especially as part of a qualifying mixed load.
