Free Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

We review qualifying major-appliance pickup requests across San Bernardino County. Working and reusable washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens receive the strongest priority. Free pickup is not automatic; each request is reviewed for condition, exact location, access, local demand and current route availability.

Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs can be considered in some cases, while a single nonworking appliance does not qualify under the general free-pickup rules.

Best request: send the appliance type, working condition, city and ZIP, floor level, stairs, gates or loading restrictions, and clear photos.

Appliance Types We Review

Washers & Dryers

Working single machines and complete laundry sets receive strong priority. Include whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the unit cools or freezes correctly and include interior and exterior photos when possible.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

State whether the burners and oven operate and whether the unit is gas or electric. Appliances must be safely disconnected before pickup.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Inside Pickup, Apartments & Gated Properties

Tell us if the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first-floor unit, upstairs apartment, condo, business or storage area. Include stair flights, elevator availability, gate instructions, parking or loading restrictions, narrow hallways and doorway concerns. For large refrigerators, a photo of the exit path can help prevent a failed trip.

San Bernardino County Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free in San Bernardino County?

It can be free for qualifying fully working appliances or eligible mixed loads after condition, location, access and route availability are reviewed.

Do broken appliances qualify?

A single nonworking appliance does not qualify under the general free-pickup rules. Minor repair needs may be considered, especially as part of a qualifying mixed load.
