Bakersfield Free Pickup Qualification

Working and reusable major appliances receive priority for free pickup in Bakersfield. Fully working appliances may qualify, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances are working. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Condition is only one part of the review. We also check the Bakersfield pickup location, access, current demand and available route coverage before confirming service.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Appliance Pickup for Bakersfield Homes & Properties

Requests can come from homeowners replacing appliances, renters preparing a move, landlords clearing a unit, property managers handling turnovers, or businesses removing reusable household-style equipment. Send the complete appliance list rather than only the largest item.

Photos make the review faster. A full appliance photo, model-label photo when available, working-condition details and clear access information help us decide whether the pickup qualifies before scheduling.

Major Appliances We Review

Washer Pickup

Top-load and front-load washers. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and note leaks, noise or error codes.

Dryer Pickup

Gas and electric dryers, including matching washer-and-dryer sets. Identify the fuel type and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Refrigerator Pickup

Top-freezer, bottom-freezer, side-by-side and French-door refrigerators. Include an interior photo and tell us whether both sections stay cold.

Freezer Pickup

Chest and upright household freezers. Include an interior photo and confirm whether the freezer maintains freezing temperature.

Stove & Range Pickup

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may qualify. Tell us whether the burners and oven operate normally.

Multiple Appliances

For garage cleanouts or property turnovers, list every appliance and the working condition of each item.

Bakersfield Areas We Review

We review qualifying requests across Bakersfield, including Downtown Bakersfield, East Bakersfield, Stockdale, Seven Oaks, Laurelglen, Riverlakes and surrounding neighborhoods. Send the exact street address and ZIP code so route coverage can be checked for that location.

Selected requests from nearby Kern County communities can also be submitted for review. Coverage outside Bakersfield varies by route, appliance load and current availability.

Inside, Garage & Upstairs Pickup Access

Tell us where the appliance is located: driveway, garage, kitchen, laundry room, first floor, upstairs apartment, storage area or business. Include stair flights, gates, narrow hallways, doorway concerns, elevator availability and parking or loading restrictions.

For refrigerators, mention if the unit is wider than the exit doorway or may need its doors removed for safe passage. For apartments or managed properties, include any gate code, loading-zone or scheduled-access requirements before a pickup time is confirmed.

What to Send With a Bakersfield Request

Send one full photo of each appliance, the condition of every item, the Bakersfield ZIP code and access details. A model-label photo is useful when available. Refrigerators and freezers should include an interior photo and cooling status.

Call or text 310-774-4304, Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM. Pickup is scheduled only after the request has been reviewed and coverage confirmed.

Bakersfield Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free in Bakersfield?

Qualifying fully working appliances and eligible mixed loads may receive free pickup after condition, access, location, demand and route coverage are reviewed.

Do you pick up a broken appliance by itself for free?

No. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work.

Do you pick up washers and dryers?

Yes. Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are priority categories.

Can you review appliances inside a home or apartment?

Yes. Include the floor level, stairs, doorway and hallway information so access can be reviewed before scheduling.

How do I request pickup?

Use the online request form or call or text 310-774-4304 with appliance photos, condition, Bakersfield ZIP code and access details.
