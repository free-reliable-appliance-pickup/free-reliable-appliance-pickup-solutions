Privacy Information

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup uses the information submitted through the pickup request form to review and respond to appliance pickup requests. Request information may include your name, phone number, email address, pickup location, appliance details, access information, message and any photos or photo details you provide.

The website uses Formspree to process form submissions. Information submitted through that form is also subject to Formspree's service and privacy practices. Do not submit passwords, payment-card numbers, government identification numbers or other unrelated sensitive information through the pickup form.

If you have a question about information associated with a pickup request, contact the service number for your region on our Contact page.
