What to include in your Spokane request

We review electric and gas ranges, freestanding stoves and ovens. Please describe burner and oven operation, dimensions and any stairs or tight doors. Gas or hardwired connections must be safely disconnected by a qualified person before removal.

Send the ZIP code, photos, appliance location (garage, driveway, apartment or inside), floor level, stairs, parking and any carrying restrictions. Requests from Spokane Valley, Liberty Lake and nearby communities may be reviewed individually; listing them does not guarantee service.

Free pickup eligibility

Fully working appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of major appliances work. Nonworking appliances alone generally do not qualify. Pickup and any free service must be confirmed after review.
