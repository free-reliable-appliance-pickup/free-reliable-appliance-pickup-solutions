Jurupa Valley Appliance Pickup & Removal Review

Jurupa Valley connects the Riverside and Inland Empire route clusters, with a mix of residential neighborhoods, larger lots, rentals and business properties. We review qualifying washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and multi-appliance loads.

Send the exact address, appliance type, operating condition and access details first. Working major appliances receive priority because free pickup depends on reuse or resale potential rather than standard junk-hauling service.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup.

Appliances Reviewed in Jurupa Valley

Washers & Dryers

Describe whether the washer fills, drains and spins. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether the unit tumbles and heats.

Refrigerators

Include interior and exterior photos, size and current cooling status. Say whether the unit is plugged in and cold at the time of the request.

Freezers

Chest and upright freezers may be reviewed when freezing performance, dimensions and moving access are clear.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric cooking appliances may qualify when the burners, oven functions and controls operate normally.

Long Driveways, Gates & Property Access

Jurupa Valley pickups can involve long driveways, side-yard access, gates, detached garages and properties where the truck cannot park directly beside the appliance. Tell us the distance from the appliance to the loading area, whether gates are wide enough, whether pets or locked access need coordination and whether there are stairs or uneven surfaces.

Inside pickups should include doorway width and tight-turn information when a refrigerator or range is close to the available clearance. Accurate access details help us evaluate the route before confirming service.

Jurupa Valley Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our service, Jurupa Valley publishes a separate residential bulky-item pickup program through Burrtec Waste. The city currently lists refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers and other household appliances among eligible bulky items and states that qualifying residents can schedule limited curbside collections each year.

This city/Burrtec program is independent from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup. Residents should review current scheduling, item-count and preparation requirements with the city or Burrtec before placing an appliance at the curb.

Mixed Loads, Microwaves & Dishwashers

When several appliances are being replaced at one property, submit the complete group together. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work, but every item must be described accurately.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

A submitted request starts review only. Acceptance, route availability and pickup timing must still be confirmed.

Jurupa Valley Appliance Pickup FAQs

Can a working refrigerator qualify?

Yes. Send photos and tell us whether both compartments are cooling correctly.

Can you review a pickup from a detached garage or long driveway?

Yes. Explain the path, gate width, distance to the truck and any uneven surfaces or access restrictions.

Do you take a nonworking dishwasher by itself?

No. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.
