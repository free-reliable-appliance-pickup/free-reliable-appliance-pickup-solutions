Jurupa Valley Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Jurupa Valley sits between Riverside, Eastvale and the western Inland Empire, so city labels and postal geography can be misleading. We use the actual pickup address, appliance condition and access details before confirming a route.

Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Fully working refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves and ranges can be strong requests. Minor-repair appliances and mixed loads can also be reviewed when the overall request supports the trip.

Free pickup is qualification-based. A single completely nonworking appliance generally does not qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Jurupa Valley ZIP Geography

Major Appliances We Review

Refrigerators

Report whether the refrigerator is plugged in and cooling. Send exterior, interior and model-label photos when available.

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether the drum turns and heats.

Freezers

Chest and upright freezers can be reviewed when they hold freezing temperature and are empty and accessible.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and describe burner, oven and control operation.

Access in Jurupa Valley

Requests may involve garages, larger lots, side-yard access, apartments, mobile-home communities, gates or longer carries. Tell us how close a pickup vehicle can get, whether the appliance is disconnected, and whether stairs, narrow doors or uneven paths affect removal.

For several appliances at one property, list every item and condition together so the complete load can be reviewed as a package.

Jurupa Valley Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for reuse-focused pickup, the City of Jurupa Valley states that Burrtec provides residents with up to two curbside bulky-item pickups per calendar year at no charge, with up to six bulky items per request. Current city guidance lists refrigerators, washers, dryers, water heaters and dishwashers among bulky-item examples.
