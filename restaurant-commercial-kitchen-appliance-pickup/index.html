Who This Program Is For

Restaurants, food-service operators, commissaries, hospitality kitchens and commercial facilities can submit renovation, replacement, closure or recurring equipment flow for review.

Working and reusable equipment receives priority. Free pickup is qualification-based and is confirmed only after equipment condition, access, location, load mix and current route availability are reviewed.

Common Repeat Work

Kitchen remodels

List movable refrigerators, freezers, ranges, ovens and other appliance equipment separately from fixtures and construction debris.

Refrigeration replacements

Reach-ins, undercounter units, freezers and other movable cold equipment should include dimensions, cooling status and access path.

Facility upgrades

Back-of-house equipment changes can be reviewed by phase to reduce interference with operating hours.

Closures & resets

One-time restaurant resets or closures can be reviewed when appliance condition, utility status and loading access are clear.

Build a Clear Commercial Pickup Scope

Equipment

List appliance type, count, condition, dimensions when relevant, whether it is disconnected and whether photos are available.

Cadence

State whether the work is one-time, weekly, monthly, seasonal, turnover-based or tied to a renovation phase.

Access

Include stairs, elevators, docks, gates, parking, service windows, staging areas and any vehicle or appointment restrictions.

Closeout

Tell us whether your team needs photos, a pickup confirmation, work-order reference, signature or other completion record.

Site & Access Details to Include

Include equipment dimensions, condition, utility/disconnect status, door and aisle widths, stairs/elevators, loading zone or dock, operating blackout windows, grease/food-cleaning status and the onsite manager authorized to release equipment.

California Priority Markets

Current California priority coverage includes Los Angeles, San Gabriel Valley, Inland Empire, Orange County, Riverside/San Bernardino corridors and Coachella Valley. Other U.S. locations can also submit for review.

Restaurant & Commercial Kitchen Appliance Pickup FAQ

What restaurant or kitchen equipment can be reviewed?

Movable refrigerators, freezers, ranges, ovens and other qualifying appliance-type equipment can be reviewed when condition, size and access are provided.

What kitchen access details matter most?

Include utility or disconnect status, dimensions, doors and aisles, loading access, operating-hour restrictions and whether equipment is clean and ready to move.

Can remodel or closure projects be reviewed?

Yes. Renovations, replacements, closures and reset projects can be reviewed as one-time or phased appliance pickup work.
