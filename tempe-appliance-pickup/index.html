What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Fully working appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. A nonworking appliance alone does not qualify. Dishwashers require two or more major working appliances, and microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Pickup Access

Include floor level, stairs, gates, narrow doors, tight halls and whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upstairs or outside.
