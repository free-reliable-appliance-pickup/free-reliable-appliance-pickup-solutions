Condition and pickup requirements

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers and stoves are prioritized for possible reuse. A matched laundry set or mostly-working group may be more attractive to an operator. Nonworking appliances, stairs, long carry-outs and gas hookups require individual review. Paid pickup is a separately approved option, not an automatic charge.

Independent official appliance disposal alternatives

Nashville municipal alternative: Davidson County's official convenience-center bulky-items guidance lists major household appliances and up to one bulk metal item for eligible residents without charge, subject to current requirements. This is resident drop-off, not a pickup operated by our business.

Knoxville municipal alternative: the City's bulky waste guidelines say refrigerated appliances are not eligible for curbside bulky collection unless refrigerant is professionally recovered and the appliance tagged. The municipal transfer station describes separate resident and fee rules. Do not remove refrigerant for a private reuse inquiry.

The Tennessee solid-waste guidance on white goods identifies refrigerants and other regulated appliance materials that require careful handling for recycling and disposal. Local government collection, retailer haul-away and private recyclers are independent of this conditional appliance reuse request.

These city and state programs have their own conditions and do not confirm pickup through our website. Leave refrigerants intact for appropriate professional handling.
