What to Send for Faster Review

Washer condition

Tell us whether it powers on, fills, washes, drains and reaches final spin. Mention leaks, error codes or loud bearing noise.

Dryer condition

Tell us whether it powers on, tumbles and heats and whether it is gas or electric when known.

Access details

Include stairs, elevators, gates, garages, parking restrictions, narrow paths and carrying distance.

Photos

Send clear front photos of both machines and model/serial labels when available.
