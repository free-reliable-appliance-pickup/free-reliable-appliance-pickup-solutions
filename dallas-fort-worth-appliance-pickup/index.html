DFW pickup questions

Is free pickup available in all DFW cities?

No. City pages accept eligibility requests; free pickup depends on appliance condition and an available partner or route.

Why separate Dallas and Fort Worth?

Different pickup corridors and operators may handle different cities and ZIP codes.

What appliances are prioritized?

Working reusable washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and full sets are prioritized.
