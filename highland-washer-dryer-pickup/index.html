Highland Washer & Dryer Pickup Review

Highland is primarily residential, so pickup requests commonly come from garages, interior laundry rooms, rental turnovers and homes where access can vary from a short garage roll-out to a longer carry through gates, side yards or interior doorways.

Working washers, dryers and complete sets receive the strongest consideration. A single working machine can also be reviewed. Repairable or nonworking equipment is considered only when current demand and route capacity make pickup practical.

For faster review: include clear photos, tested functions, gas or electric dryer type, floor level, stairs, gates, driveway or parking access and the distance from the machine to the loading point.

Highland Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Can one working washer or dryer be reviewed?

Yes. Single working machines can be reviewed, while complete working sets generally receive stronger priority.

Should I include access photos?

They are useful when gates, stairs, slopes, narrow doorways or longer carries are involved.

Can nonworking equipment qualify?

Sometimes, depending on condition and current local demand, but free pickup is not guaranteed for nonworking equipment.
