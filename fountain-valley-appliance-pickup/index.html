Single-Story Homes, Garages & Rental Turnovers

For garage or driveway pickups, tell us whether a truck can approach the loading area and whether vehicles will need to be moved. For indoor appliances, include hallway width, doorways, thresholds and any steps between the appliance and exit.

Property managers handling a turnover can submit several appliances together. Listing each item separately helps us calculate the working percentage and decide whether the overall load qualifies.

What We Commonly Review

Washer and dryer sets

Describe complete-cycle operation and any leaks, noises or heat issues. Matching or mixed-brand sets can both be submitted.

Refrigerators and freezers

Confirm that the unit cools properly. Photos of the exterior, interior and model label are helpful.

Ranges and ovens

Tell us whether burners and oven functions work and whether the appliance is gas or electric.

Microwave & Dishwasher Rules

A microwave is accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. A dishwasher is considered only with two or more major working appliances. Unknown-condition or repair-needing items may be reviewed, but they are not automatically eligible for free pickup.

A request becomes a scheduled pickup only after we confirm eligibility and route availability.

Fountain Valley Bulky-Item Alternative
