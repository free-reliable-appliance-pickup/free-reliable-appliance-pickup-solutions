Fountain Valley Appliance Pickup Review

Qualifying working and reusable major appliances can receive free pickup in Fountain Valley after review. We review appliance condition, the exact Fountain Valley address or ZIP, property access, local demand and current central/coastal Orange County route availability before confirming free pickup.

For refrigerators and freezers, send exterior and interior photos plus cooling status. For washers, report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, report tumble and heat. For stoves or ranges, tell us whether the burners and oven work.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Fully working appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when about 80% of the major appliances work. One nonworking appliance alone generally does not qualify.

How Fountain Valley Pickup Review Works

1. Send photos and condition

Show each appliance clearly and describe what has been tested. Include the exact pickup address or Fountain Valley ZIP 92708.

2. Describe the removal path

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway or interior room and include steps, thresholds, doorway limits, gates and carrying distance.

3. Confirm route availability

We review the request against current Fountain Valley, Huntington Beach, Costa Mesa and nearby Orange County routes before confirming service.

Fountain Valley Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup, the City of Fountain Valley publishes a separate large-item program through Republic Services. The current city page states that residential customers can schedule bulky-item pickup up to four times per year with up to ten items per pickup. Household appliances listed as qualifying items include refrigerators, dishwashers, water heaters, clothes washers and dryers, ovens and other major appliances.

Commercial and multi-family customers have different terms, so confirm the current rules directly with Republic Services. This municipal program is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup and does not change our qualification rules.
