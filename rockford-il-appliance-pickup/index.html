Report the condition of each machine

A working washer/dryer set is a good candidate to evaluate when the washer fills, drains and spins and the dryer tumbles and heats. For refrigerators or upright freezers, tell us whether each compartment cools and whether doors, seals, shelves and controls are intact. On a used gas or electric range, report both burner and oven performance. Missing parts and damaged surfaces matter; do not describe untested units as working.

Refrigerators, stairs, and multiple-appliance requests

Before any potential collection, measure cellar stairs and doorway turns, disclose whether the machine is disconnected and accessible, and provide pictures of the appliance inside and outside. For multiple units from a rental turnover, list each separately and state how many are fully working. A nonworking item does not automatically qualify for free removal.

Independent appliance recycling and disposal information

Rockford's city bulk-item collection has its own fees and handling requirements. Rockford collection restrictions for refrigerators and freezers

The City of Rockford's official bulk collection rules say appliances containing refrigerant must have certification attached by a licensed refrigeration technician showing that the refrigerant was removed before municipal collection. Do not cut refrigerant lines or attempt recovery yourself. For collection eligibility or possible charges, residents can contact Rock River Disposal at 815-965-2489, as listed in the city's waste-service information. This is independent public guidance, not a Free Reliable Appliance Pickup service or route.

When requesting a potential reuse pickup from us, include whether the refrigerator still cools, if both doors seal, any missing shelves, and whether the machine is in a garage or must pass basement stairs. We review reusable units first; a submitted Rockford request is not an accepted appointment.

Eligibility details

Working appliances that may be suitable for reuse are prioritized. Broken units are evaluated case by case and may not qualify alone. Dishwashers are considered only with at least two other qualifying working major appliances; microwaves are not usually standalone pickup items.
