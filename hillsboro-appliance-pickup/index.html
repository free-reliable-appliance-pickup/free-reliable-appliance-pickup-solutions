Hillsboro Appliance Pickup With Condition Reviewed First

Hillsboro is a priority west-side market in our Portland coverage. We review working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges for possible free pickup. The strongest requests explain both the appliance condition and the route from the appliance to the pickup vehicle.

A single completely nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repair situations may be considered, and mixed loads can be reviewed when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. That keeps the service focused on reuse while still allowing some repairable equipment to be considered.

Helpful before scheduling: send a full appliance photo, tested working details, floor level, stairs and the exact pickup address.

Major Appliances We Review in Hillsboro

Washers

Tell us whether the machine fills, drains and spins. Mention leaks, unusual noise, door-lock issues and any known error codes.

Dryers

State whether the dryer tumbles and heats and whether it is gas or electric. Working washer-dryer pairs are especially useful to submit together.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the unit is currently plugged in and holding temperature. Interior photos help show shelves, bins and overall condition.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and describe burner, oven and control operation. Note whether the unit is already disconnected.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Garages, Apartments, Townhomes and Longer Carries

For Hillsboro pickups, include whether the appliance is in a garage, kitchen, laundry room, apartment, townhome or outside storage area. List stairs, elevators, narrow doors, hallway turns and the distance from the appliance to legal loading or parking.

For refrigerators, mention kitchen islands, door removal or tight corners that may affect the exit path. For stacked laundry, tell us whether the machines are still installed in a closet or alcove. If several appliances are being removed from a rental turnover, list each item and its working condition separately.

Hillsboro Bulky-Item Collection Is a Paid Backup Option

If an appliance does not qualify for our free reuse-based pickup, the City of Hillsboro publishes bulky-item collection charges through its franchised garbage and recycling system. The City also directs residents to Washington County recycling resources for oversized items and recommends contacting the assigned hauler for special pickup availability.
