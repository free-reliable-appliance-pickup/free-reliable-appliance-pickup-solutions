Major Appliances We Review

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges are the main categories. Fully working appliances are the strongest fit for free pickup because the service is reuse-based. Minor repair may sometimes be considered. A single completely nonworking appliance does not qualify on its own.

For several appliances, a mixed load may qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. Microwaves must come with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.
