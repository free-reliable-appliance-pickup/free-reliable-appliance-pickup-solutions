San Jacinto Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

This is the San Jacinto-specific page for general major-appliance pickup requests. We review qualifying refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves, ranges, ovens and mixed household appliance loads.

Fully working appliances receive the strongest priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Best request: send clear photos, tested condition, exact address or ZIP code, floor level, stairs, gates, driveway or parking details and carrying distance.

San Jacinto Pickup for All Major Appliance Types

Refrigerators & Freezers

Report whether each compartment maintains temperature and include exterior, interior and model-label photos when possible.

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report fill, drain and spin. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether the machine tumbles and heats.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

State fuel type and whether burners, controls and oven functions operate normally.

Multiple Appliances

List each item separately so a strong working load is not confused with a single broken appliance.

San Jacinto Residential & Route Access

Pickup conditions can vary between established residential streets, newer subdivisions, apartments, garages and properties closer to the valley edge. Exact access details are important for heavy appliances.

Include stairs, narrow doorways, side gates, driveway length or grade, curb or parking restrictions and carrying distance. For an interior refrigerator or range, send photos of tight turns before scheduling.

San Jacinto Waste-Service Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup, the City of San Jacinto states that CR&R Environmental Services handles trash, recycling, organics and customer-service requests including bulky-item pickups. That municipal waste-service path is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.

Residents should confirm current bulky-item rules and scheduling directly with the City or CR&R before placing an item out.

Qualification Limits

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances. Minor-repair appliances can be submitted honestly for review but are not automatically accepted.

Submitting a request begins review; it is not a confirmed pickup until condition, location, access and route availability are accepted.
