# Big-Box Appliance Last-Mile / Warehouse Channel — Southern California — 2026-10-06

## Goal

Identify the actual last-mile appliance delivery operators, cross-dock terminals, and logistics warehouses that handle major-appliance deliveries for large retailers such as Home Depot, Lowe's and Costco.

This is a business-development channel for official pickup/reuse partnerships. Do not ask individual drivers to divert retailer-controlled haul-away appliances. Once a delivery company accepts a haul-away unit, ownership, recycling, disposal and chain-of-custody rules may apply. Contact operations/terminal/vendor management instead.

### Costco policy caution
Costco says its delivery team hauls away one like appliance per delivered appliance and recycles the used haul-away item under its program.
Therefore:
- do not target drivers for informal appliance diversion;
- approach Costco Logistics / reverse logistics / sustainability / depot management for an authorized reuse or recovery program;
- a direct reuse partnership may be harder than with independent installers because Costco already has a formal recycling chain.

## The business proposition

"We operate Free Reliable Appliance Pickup and a growing nationwide pickup-partner network. We are interested only in an authorized program for working/reusable appliance take-outs. If your delivery network receives refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges or freezers during replacement deliveries, we can review approved loads for reuse/pickup in markets where your current process allows it. We do not want to interfere with existing recycling contracts or ask drivers to divert company-controlled property."

## Why this channel matters

A single last-mile terminal can touch hundreds of appliance deliveries per day. Even if only a small approved percentage can be routed for reuse, this could become a major recurring appliance-supply source and a nationwide partner-network feed.
