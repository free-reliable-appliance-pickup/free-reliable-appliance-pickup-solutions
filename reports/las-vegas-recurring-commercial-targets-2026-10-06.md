## Recurring-service pitch

Offer a simple vendor workflow:
1. Property manager/facility sends photos or a list of appliances.
2. Include property address, unit/floor, stairs/elevator/gate/loading access, condition and preferred pickup window.
3. We review which appliances/loads qualify for free pickup and route only accepted jobs.
4. Working/reusable appliances receive priority.
5. Mixed loads can qualify when the majority of the load is working.
6. Do not promise blanket free pickup for nonworking-only loads.
