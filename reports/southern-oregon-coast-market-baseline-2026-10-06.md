## Southern Oregon inland priority

### Tier A — Medford / Grants Pass

**Medford**
- U.S. Census 2025 population estimate: 86,494.
- Large enough to justify monitoring as a future standalone city-owner candidate.
- Natural hub for Central Point, Jacksonville, Phoenix, Talent and Ashland-area routing.

**Grants Pass**
- Owner-occupied housing rate: 57.0%.
- Median owner-occupied home value: $382,600.
- 16,552 households in the 2020–2024 Census estimate period.
- Strong candidate as the second Southern Oregon owner after Medford if finalized search demand appears.

Recommended market architecture:
1. Medford as the primary Southern Oregon city candidate.
2. Grants Pass as the second primary candidate.
3. Ashland as a high-quality support/candidate market.
4. Central Point / Jacksonville / Phoenix / Talent as support areas unless independent finalized demand appears.
5. Roseburg as a separate I-5 corridor candidate, not folded into Medford.

### Tier B — Roseburg

Roseburg should be monitored separately because it sits between Eugene and the Rogue Valley. Promote only if finalized query evidence appears; do not create a page simply to fill the map.

## Oregon coast priority

### Tier A — Central Coast: Lincoln City / Newport

**Lincoln City**
- 2025 population estimate: 10,117.
- Owner-occupied rate: 59.3%.
- Median owner-occupied home value: $412,700.

**Newport**
- Owner-occupied rate: 58.2%.
- Median owner-occupied home value: $441,400.
- 4,997 households in the 2020–2024 Census estimate period.

Why this cluster ranks first for coast monitoring:
- Two meaningful coastal population centers close enough to function as one practical central-coast corridor.
- Housing values are strong enough to make reusable-appliance supply attractive.
- Monitor both city intent and property-management / vacation-rental style commercial demand, but do not claim or publish specific commercial relationships without evidence.

- Waldport
- Yachats
- Toledo

**Coos Bay**

- Owner-occupied rate: 60.1%.
