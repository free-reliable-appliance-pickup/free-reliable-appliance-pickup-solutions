# Multifamily Renovation Pickup Targets — Orange County + Inland Empire — 2026-10-06

## Target definition

Focus on companies that physically renovate apartment units, execute unit turns/make-readies, or replace appliances during repositioning projects.

We are NOT offering:
- appliance maintenance;
- appliance repair;
- general handyman service;
- ongoing maintenance contracts.

We ARE offering:
- pickup/removal of qualifying appliances removed during renovations;
- pickup from vacant units during turnover;
- removal of appliances left by tenants;
- batch pickup when multiple units are renovated;
- reusable/working appliance routing before disposal.
