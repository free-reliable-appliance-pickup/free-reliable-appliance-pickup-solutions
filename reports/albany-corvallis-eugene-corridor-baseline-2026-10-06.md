## Official local alternatives / facts for future use

### Lane County / Eugene / Springfield
Lane County publishes current 2026–2027 transfer-station rules. Refrigerant-containing appliances are accepted for a fee; the current listed appliance fee is $25, and the County explicitly recommends reuse for newer models before disposal.

Lane County also lists curbside/recycling providers serving Eugene, Springfield, Junction City, Coburg, Creswell, Cottage Grove and surrounding communities.

The City of Eugene multifamily-recycling resource points residents/property managers to reuse/recycling options including St. Vincent de Paul.

St. Vincent de Paul Lane County states that free donation pickup is available in Eugene, Springfield and surrounding communities, subject to approval and item condition. It accepts refrigerators, freezers, ranges, washers and dryers, but not dishwashers.

### Corvallis / Benton County
Benton County lists:
- Republic Services of Corvallis
- Coffin Butte Landfill
- appliance acceptance with and without Freon for a fee
- large-appliance / special-pickup service through Republic Services

This gives a verified local fallback for future Corvallis content if a city-owner page is later justified.

### Albany
City of Albany materials list large appliances in the city's Recycle Roundup / Big Pickup ecosystem and identify Republic Services as the local recycling/disposal contact.

Use current official City of Albany sources again before publishing any future Albany-specific fee, event-date or program detail.

## Promotion rule for a new city owner

A new indexable Albany, Corvallis, Springfield or other city page is considered only if at least one of these is true:
1. repeated finalized GSC city-modifier impressions are landing on a less-relevant regional/sibling page;
2. the city query produces meaningful impressions/clicks over more than one finalized observation window;
3. live organic results plus GSC show the regional page cannot concentrate the city intent;
4. a real local operating/partner advantage exists that can support materially unique city content.

Do not promote a city page merely because a competitor has one.
