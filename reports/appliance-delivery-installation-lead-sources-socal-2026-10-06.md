## Operating model

For any installer/delivery partner:
1. Their driver/dispatcher sends photo(s) of the old appliance.
2. Include brand/type, working condition, age if known, ZIP/address and access.
3. We immediately decide whether it is a fit for our own pickup team or a local network partner.
4. Working/reusable appliances get priority.
5. Nonworking-only loads are not automatically free.
6. Partner does not need to become an appliance-maintenance provider.
7. We do not interfere with their installation job; we only handle the outgoing appliance.

## Why this channel matters
