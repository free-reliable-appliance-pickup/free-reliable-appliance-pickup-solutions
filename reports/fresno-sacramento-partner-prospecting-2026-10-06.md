## Partnership / vendor workflow

- Property manager sends photos, address/ZIP, condition, floor/stairs/elevator/gate and preferred timing.
- We review whether the appliances qualify before confirming pickup.
- Working/reusable units receive priority.
- Mixed loads can be reviewed when the majority of the load is working.
- Repair companies can hand off customers who decline repair.
- Used appliance stores can specify brands, age, condition and load sizes they want.
- No blanket promise of free pickup for nonworking-only loads.
