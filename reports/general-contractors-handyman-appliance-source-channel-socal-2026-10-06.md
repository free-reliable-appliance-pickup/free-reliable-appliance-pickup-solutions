## Exact partnership pitch

"We only handle the outgoing appliances. When your crew remodels a kitchen, turns an apartment, upgrades a rental unit or prepares a vacant property, send us photos of the refrigerator, washer, dryer, range, freezer or other qualifying major appliance before disposal. If it is working/reusable or the load qualifies, we can arrange pickup."

## Important qualification rules

- Do not promise all appliances are free.
- Working/reusable units receive priority.
- Mixed loads can qualify when most of the load is working.
- Dishwashers only with 2+ major working appliances.
- Microwaves not alone.
- No maintenance, repair or contracting services offered.
- Do not interfere with the contractor's project schedule; pickup must be coordinated around their jobsite access.
