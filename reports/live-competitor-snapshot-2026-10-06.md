3. neighborhoods/ZIPs where genuinely useful
4. appliance-specific guides
5. FAQs
6. commercial/recurring path

8. simple request flow

The parts not to copy:
- blanket “always free” promises
- unsupported same-day guarantees
- noisy/misaligned city lists
- claims of local crews/branches that are not verified
- thin city × appliance expansion merely for page count
