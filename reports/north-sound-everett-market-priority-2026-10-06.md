# North Sound Market Priority — Everett / Snohomish County — 2026-10-06

## Decision

Everett is a **high-priority North Sound market** and should be the first standalone North Sound city candidate reviewed after the Puget Sound corridor experiment reaches its evidence gate.

Do **not** change Everett indexability during the active Puget Sound regional-depth experiment. First measure whether the strengthened regional owner begins earning Everett/Lynnwood/Snohomish County impressions.

## Market evidence

### Housing / turnover
Current external market evidence:
- Everett median home sale price: about **$595K**
- Everett homes sold in Aug 2026: **268**

- Snohomish County median sale price: about **$723K**

- Snohomish County owner-occupied rate: **68.2%**
- Snohomish County median owner-occupied home value: **$696K**

Interpretation:
- Large housing base
- High owner-occupancy
- Strong home values
- Active housing turnover
- Good fit for reusable refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges, freezers and multi-appliance move/remodel loads

## Future decision rule

At/after the Puget Sound experiment gate:
1. Pull finalized GSC for Everett, Lynnwood, Snohomish County and North Sound modifiers.
2. Protect the Puget Sound regional owner if it begins ranking well.
3. If Everett produces meaningful city-modifier demand but the regional owner stays weak, test **Everett first** as the standalone North Sound city page.
4. If Lynnwood shows similar demand, test Lynnwood separately only after Everett/region ownership is clear.
5. Do not bulk-index all North Sound city pages.
6. Any standalone page must contain meaningful city-specific access, neighborhood, disposal/alternative, commercial and request information; no thin city templates.
7. Keep truthful partner-coverage language and current qualification rules.

## Why Everett first

Everett combines:
- strong housing values and turnover
- large Snohomish County catchment
- meaningful owner-occupied housing base
- visible exact-city competitor investment
- practical geography/access differences
- commercial/industrial and multifamily demand potential

This makes Everett the strongest North Sound city to test independently if the regional owner alone is not enough.
