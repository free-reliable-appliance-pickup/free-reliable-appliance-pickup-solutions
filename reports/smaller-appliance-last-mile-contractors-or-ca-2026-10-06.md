## What to ask

We want the business/operations side, not individual drivers.

Ask:
- Which retailer/account do your appliance routes serve?
- Where is the local appliance staging/cross-dock warehouse?
- Who controls haul-away appliances after replacement delivery?
- Is there an approved reuse/recovery path for working or reusable units?
- Can Free Reliable Appliance Pickup become an authorized pickup/reuse partner?

## Safety / ownership rule

Never encourage drivers to take or divert appliances without company authorization. Retailer/carrier haul-away units may be company-controlled property or assigned to recycling/reverse-logistics contracts.
