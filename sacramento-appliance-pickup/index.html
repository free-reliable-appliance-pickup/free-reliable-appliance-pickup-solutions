What Qualifies for Free Pickup in Sacramento?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Working and reusable washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. Every request is reviewed for appliance condition, Sacramento location, access, current demand and available pickup coverage before scheduling.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Sacramento Appliance Pickup & Removal

Submit a request when you are replacing a household appliance, clearing a rental, preparing a move-out or removing reusable equipment from a home or business. Tell us exactly what you have and whether each appliance works.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Sending complete condition, photo and access details helps us determine whether the load qualifies before a pickup time is confirmed.

Appliances We Review

Washers

Top-load and front-load washers. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether it has leaks or error codes.

Dryers

Electric and gas dryers, including matching laundry sets. Tell us whether the dryer heats and tumbles and identify the fuel type.

Refrigerators

Standard, side-by-side, bottom-freezer and French-door refrigerators. Include an interior photo and whether both sections maintain temperature.

Freezers

Chest and upright household freezers in reusable condition. Tell us whether the unit freezes properly and include an interior photo.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may qualify. Describe whether the burners and oven operate normally.

Multiple Appliances

List each appliance separately with its working condition so the complete load can be reviewed accurately.

Sacramento Pickup Areas

We review qualifying requests throughout Sacramento, including Downtown and Midtown Sacramento, Land Park, Pocket-Greenhaven, Natomas, East Sacramento and surrounding neighborhoods. Send your exact ZIP code and pickup address so coverage can be confirmed for the specific location.

Requests from nearby Sacramento-area communities can also be submitted for review. Route availability varies, so include the city, ZIP code and complete appliance list before scheduling.

Apartment, Rental & Property Access

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upstairs apartment, condo building, storage area or business. Include the floor level, stair flights, elevator availability, narrow doors, tight hallways, gates, loading restrictions and parking limitations.

For rental turnovers or multiple-unit properties, list each appliance and its condition separately. If building management requires a loading reservation or specific access instructions, include those details with the request.

Photos and Information to Send

For the fastest review, send one clear full photo of each appliance, a model-label photo when available, the working condition of every item, your Sacramento ZIP code and the access details. For refrigerators and freezers, include an interior photo and tell us whether the unit maintains temperature.

For washers, tell us whether the machine fills, drains and spins. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether it heats and tumbles. Call or text 310-774-4304, Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is appliance pickup free in Sacramento?

Qualifying working appliances and eligible mixed loads may receive free pickup after condition, access, location, demand and coverage are reviewed.

Do you pick up washers and dryers?

Yes. Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are priority categories.

Do you pick up refrigerators and freezers?

Qualifying refrigerators and household freezers can be reviewed. Send photos and confirm whether the unit maintains temperature.

Can I submit several appliances at once?

Yes. List every appliance and its working condition. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work.

How do I request pickup?

Use the online request form or call or text 310-774-4304 with appliance condition, Sacramento ZIP code, photos and access details.
