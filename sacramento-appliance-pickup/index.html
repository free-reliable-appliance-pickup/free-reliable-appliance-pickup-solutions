We pick up qualifying major appliances from Sacramento homes, rentals and businesses. Working and reusable refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges, ovens and mixed major-appliance loads receive priority. Washer- or dryer-only requests should use the dedicated Sacramento washer & dryer pickup page.

Sacramento Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free in Sacramento?

It can be free for qualifying fully working appliances or eligible mixed loads after condition, exact location, access and route availability are reviewed.

Do you pick up broken appliances?

A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repair needs or qualifying mixed loads may be considered depending on local demand.

Free Pickup vs. City Appliance Collection

Sacramento residents have a dedicated public appliance and e-waste collection program in addition to our qualification-based pickup review. Our service prioritizes appliances with reuse or repair value; the City program is a separate curbside collection option for eligible residential customers.

Our reuse-focused review

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges can be reviewed with photos, condition and access details before a route is confirmed.

City appliance appointments

The City of Sacramento states that its residential collection customers can schedule two free appliance/e-waste appointments per calendar year, with up to two appliances collected per appointment. The City directs customers to schedule through 311, the Sac 311 app or 916-264-5011.

Placement and multifamily rules differ

The City directs appliance customers to place accepted items in the street, visible and easy to access, no earlier than 24 hours before the appointment. Tenants in multifamily properties with five or more units that are not serviced by City solid waste are directed to their landlord, property manager or commercial hauler. Our reuse-focused request is separate and should include the actual interior or garage access before anything is moved.
