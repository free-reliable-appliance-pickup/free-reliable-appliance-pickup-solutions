Denver Washer & Dryer Pickup Review

Denver washer and dryer pickup requests are reviewed through the established Denver–Aurora route. Complete working sets receive especially strong consideration, but single working washers or dryers can also be useful when the machine is accurately tested and accessible.

For washers, report fill, wash or agitation, drain and spin. For dryers, identify gas or electric service and report power, tumble and heat. If a machine shows an error code, leaks or makes unusual noise, include that before pickup is scheduled.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets receive priority. A single completely nonworking machine does not automatically qualify; repairable units are reviewed only when condition, local demand and the current route support pickup.

What to Send for a Faster Laundry Pickup Decision

Washer Test

Tell us whether the washer powers on, fills, washes or agitates, drains and completes the final spin. Mention leaks, unusual noise, error codes or a door-lock problem.

Dryer Test

Identify gas or electric service and tell us whether the dryer powers on, tumbles and heats. If it is a ventless or combination unit, include that in the request.

Photos

Send a clear full photo of each machine and a model-label photo when practical. For stacked units, show the full installation so height and removal access are clear.

Removal Path

Include floor level, stairs or elevator, narrow doors, hallway turns, gates, parking or loading limits and the carrying distance to the pickup vehicle.

Denver Laundry Access & Local Route Details

Denver laundry removal can be simple in a garage or first-floor utility room, but older homes, basements, downtown apartments and multifamily buildings can create different access problems. Tell us the floor, stair flights, tight turns, door width, elevator availability, loading rules and distance to legal vehicle access.

For property managers or apartment turnovers, list all washers and dryers at the same address in one request and identify the condition of each machine. For stacked laundry, show the full installation and tell us whether the units are already disconnected.

Washer & Dryer Requests Only

This page is specifically for washers, dryers, laundry centers and washer/dryer sets in Denver. For refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges, ovens or mixed major-appliance loads, use the general Denver appliance pickup page.

If you have several laundry machines from a rental turnover or property refresh, list each machine separately and identify which units are fully working, need repair, do not work or are unknown. Grouped working equipment at one address is easier to evaluate than several incomplete requests.

Denver Laundry Pickup Qualification

A working washer, working dryer or complete working set can be submitted for free-pickup review. Minor repair needs may be considered when you describe the issue accurately. If you are submitting a mixed load, list every major appliance so we can review the working share of the complete load.

Do not describe a machine as working unless the key functions have actually been tested. Honest condition details help prevent wasted trips and make it easier to match the request to the correct local route.

Denver Local Reuse or Disposal Backup

If a washer or dryer does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup, Denver Solid Waste Management offers a separate free appliance collection by appointment for residents it services. The City's accepted-appliance list includes washers and dryers, and Denver says appointments can be scheduled through 311 or 720-913-1311.

Denver's normal Large Item Pickup program specifically excludes appliances, so residents should use the City's appliance-collection program rather than setting laundry machines out for a regular large-item day.

Denver Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQs

Can one working washer or dryer qualify?

Yes. Single working machines can be reviewed, and complete working sets receive especially strong consideration.

Do you review basement pickups?

Yes. Include stair count, turns, doorway clearance and the carrying distance from the laundry area to the truck.

Does the City of Denver take appliances on normal Large Item Pickup days?

No. Denver lists appliances as excluded from normal Large Item Pickup and provides a separate appliance-collection program for eligible residents.
