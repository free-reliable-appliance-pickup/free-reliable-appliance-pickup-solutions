Who This Program Is For

Reverse-logistics teams, retailers, delivery companies, installers and regional return programs can submit home, store, warehouse or consolidation-site appliance flow for review.

Working and reusable equipment receives priority. Free pickup is qualification-based and is confirmed only after equipment condition, access, location, load mix and current route availability are reviewed.

Common Repeat Work

Old-for-new routes

Describe where replaced appliances originate, whether they are disconnected and how each stop is released.

Regional returns

Consolidate working, repairable and damaged equipment by condition so pickup options can be reviewed accurately.

Trade-in programs

List appliance category, customer/store source, expected volume and the handoff record your team needs.

Multi-site recovery

Sites can be grouped into one program when locations, cadence and exception rules are clearly defined.

Build a Clear Commercial Pickup Scope

Equipment

List appliance type, count, condition, dimensions when relevant, whether it is disconnected and whether photos are available.

Cadence

State whether the work is one-time, weekly, monthly, seasonal, turnover-based or tied to a renovation phase.

Access

Include stairs, elevators, docks, gates, parking, service windows, staging areas and any vehicle or appointment restrictions.

Closeout

Tell us whether your team needs photos, a pickup confirmation, work-order reference, signature or other completion record.

Site & Access Details to Include

Include origin sites, route or order references when used, appliance condition, stairs/parking/access at customer stops, consolidation rules, dock appointments, normal and peak weekly volume, and any photo/signature requirements.

California Priority Markets

Current California priority coverage includes Los Angeles, San Gabriel Valley, Inland Empire, Orange County, Riverside/San Bernardino corridors and Coachella Valley. Other U.S. locations can also submit for review.

Reverse Logistics Appliance Pickup FAQ

What reverse-logistics appliance streams can be reviewed?

Old-for-new replacement routes, consolidated customer returns, trade-ins and multi-site recovery programs can be submitted for review.

What identifiers should be included?

Include route, order or project references when available, appliance condition, photos, pickup access, destination or consolidation instructions and expected cadence.

Can recurring reverse-logistics routes be reviewed?

Yes. Weekly, monthly, seasonal or project-based reverse-logistics flows can be reviewed when the service area and normal volume are defined.
