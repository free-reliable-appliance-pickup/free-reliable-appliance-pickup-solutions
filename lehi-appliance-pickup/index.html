Lehi Appliance Pickup & Removal

Submit washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges for pickup review in Lehi. Working and reusable appliances receive priority.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Condition, access, demand and available coverage are reviewed first.

Priority Appliances

Washers, dryers and laundry sets are priority categories. Refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens may also qualify.

What Qualifies?

Fully working appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when about 80% of the appliances work. A nonworking appliance alone does not qualify. Dishwashers require two or more major working appliances, and microwaves only with another qualifying major appliance.

Pickup Access

Include floor level, stairs, narrow doors, gates and whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upstairs or outside.
