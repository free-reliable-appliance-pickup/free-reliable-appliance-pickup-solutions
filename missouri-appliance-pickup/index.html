Which major appliances may qualify?

Fully working refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers and stoves are prioritized for potential reuse. Nonworking units need individual review. Dishwashers are considered only when paired with at least two other working major appliances. Provide accurate condition details, photos and stairs.

Independent appliance disposal alternatives

Municipal recycling, bulky-item services and private haulers are independent options. Ask the city or county agency about resident eligibility, fees and refrigerant handling. A local directory is not proof our business has an accepting Missouri operator.
