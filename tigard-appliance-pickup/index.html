Tigard Appliance Pickup for Working & Reusable Appliances

Tigard requests are reviewed as part of our Portland-area operating route. We focus on appliances that can be reused, resold, repaired economically or handled through an approved local route. Working laundry equipment is especially useful, and complete washer-and-dryer sets should be submitted together whenever possible.

Free does not mean every item qualifies. A fully working major appliance may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for our free service.

What We Need Before Scheduling in Tigard

Working condition

Tell us what works today. For washers, mention filling, draining and spinning. For dryers, tell us whether the drum turns and the unit heats.

Pickup location

Garage and driveway pickups are usually simplest. For inside pickup, include floor level, stairs, narrow halls, doorways and elevator information.

Photos and size

Send a full appliance photo plus the model label when available. Refrigerator and freezer requests should include an interior photo and cooling condition.

Appliance Types We Review in Tigard

Washers and dryers: working top-load, front-load, electric and gas units are priority categories. Refrigerators and freezers: tell us whether the fresh-food and freezer compartments maintain temperature. Stoves and ranges: include whether the unit is gas or electric and whether burners and oven functions work.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only when included with two or more major working appliances.

Tigard Route & Access Planning

We review addresses in Tigard and nearby Portland-metro routes, including connections toward Beaverton, Tualatin, Sherwood and Lake Oswego when route capacity is available. Exact coverage is confirmed from the address rather than a broad mileage promise.

Tigard pickups can involve apartments, townhomes, garages, storage areas and single-family homes. If a truck cannot park close to the appliance, describe the walking distance, gate, slope, loading zone or building rules before scheduling. For stacked laundry equipment, say whether the machines are already separated.

When a Tigard Appliance Does Not Qualify

The City of Tigard directs residents to their garbage hauler for bulky-item collection and participates in the Washington County Cooperative Recycling Program. If your appliance is not eligible for our free pickup, confirm current bulky-item collection options, fees and preparation rules with the City or your assigned hauler.

Tigard Appliance Pickup Questions

Can I request pickup from an apartment?

Yes. Include the floor, elevator availability, loading access and any management requirements.

Can a minor-repair appliance qualify?

Possibly. Describe the problem accurately and send photos. Minor repairs may be considered, but approval is not automatic.

Do you review multiple appliances at once?

Yes. List every appliance and its condition. Mixed loads are reviewed as a complete load.
