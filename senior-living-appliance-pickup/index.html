Built for Communities That Replace Appliances Repeatedly

Senior housing operators often manage many units under one property team. Unit turnovers, modernization projects, maintenance replacements and community-wide upgrades can create a steady need to move appliances. Our review process is designed to collect the details that matter before a pickup is scheduled.

55+ & Active Adult Communities

Submit appliances removed during resident turnovers, upgrades or planned replacement projects. Include quantity, condition and whether equipment is staged for removal.

Independent & Assisted Living

Senior Apartments & Retirement Properties

Appliances We Review

Washers & Dryers

Individual machines and matching sets. Tell us whether they run through basic cycles and whether gas dryers are safely disconnected before pickup.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Include whether the unit cools/freezes correctly, approximate size, and whether it is empty and ready to move.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Identify gas or electric service, working condition and whether the appliance has already been safely disconnected.

Qualification matters. Free pickup is not automatic for every load. Working and reusable appliances receive priority; broken, incomplete or difficult-access items may require another removal or recycling option.

One Community, Multiple Communities, or a Recurring Program

One Property

Provide the property name, exact address, onsite contact, appliance count and access plan.

Multiple Properties

Tell us the cities or ZIP codes, number of locations and approximate appliance volume. Each actual pickup address is reviewed separately.

Recurring Replacements

Choose weekly, monthly, turnover-based or project-based frequency and estimate the number of appliances expected during a normal month.

Information That Speeds Up Review

For senior-living properties, the fastest requests include the community type, number of locations, appliance inventory, condition, floor level, stairs/elevator access, loading area, gated-entry instructions, onsite contact and photos. If a maintenance or renovation team has a planned replacement schedule, include the target start window and expected frequency.

For a large upgrade, send photos of staged appliance groups plus a few close photos showing condition. For refrigerators and freezers, include cooling status. For laundry equipment, include whether a basic cycle was tested.

Senior Living Appliance Pickup Questions

Can a senior community request pickup repeatedly?

Yes. One-time and recurring requests can be reviewed. For recurring work, include expected frequency, approximate monthly appliance count and the properties or cities involved.

Can qualifying working appliances be picked up free?

Yes, qualifying appliances may be eligible for free pickup. Condition, load mix, access, exact location and route availability are reviewed before service is confirmed.

Do you take several appliances from the same property?

Yes. List every appliance and its condition. For large loads, photos and a staged inventory help determine whether the load qualifies and what equipment is needed.

Do you work with property managers and maintenance teams?

Yes. Requests can be submitted by property managers, maintenance staff, community administrators, renovation teams or other authorized site contacts.
