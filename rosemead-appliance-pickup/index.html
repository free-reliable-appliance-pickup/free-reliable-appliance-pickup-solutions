Working refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. Free pickup is qualification-based and depends on condition, exact address, access, local demand and current route availability.

Multi-Item Loads

List each major appliance and condition separately. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
