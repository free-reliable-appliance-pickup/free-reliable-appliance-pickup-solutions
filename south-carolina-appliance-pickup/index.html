What may qualify for free pickup review?

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and matching laundry sets are priority reuse candidates. Multiple mostly working items may be evaluated together; nonworking and difficult-access items need individual review. A paid quote is optional and requires separate approval.

Official recycling guidance and independent alternatives

State recycling rules: The South Carolina Department of Environmental Services explains that large household appliances must be recycled in South Carolina and directs residents to county collection locations. Public recycling is different from private appliance reuse pickup and does not establish a free truck service through this website.

Grand Strand recycling alternative: The Horry County location directory identifies collection centers accepting large appliances, including refrigerators, washers and dryers. Residents should confirm hours, accepted items and any limits before traveling. This is a public drop-off program, not our private pickup.

Greenville County recycling alternative: Greenville County lists large metal appliances at Residential Waste and Recycling Centers and Twin Chimneys Landfill, not at all unattended neighborhood bins. This is separate from a private reuse pickup request.

South Carolina public appliance recycling is an independent disposal option, not proof of private pickup service. Preserve refrigerant systems and follow qualified gas-disconnection practices.
