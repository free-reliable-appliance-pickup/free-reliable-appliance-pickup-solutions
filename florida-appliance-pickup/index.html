Florida Requests Are Matched by Metro, Property Type and Access

South Florida, Orlando, Tampa Bay, Jacksonville and Southwest Florida are tracked as separate demand markets. A submission in Miami or Fort Lauderdale does not imply that the same local pickup route serves Orlando or Tampa. The statewide form lets us measure those areas independently while keeping one clear qualification process.

Condo, gated and managed-property details matter

Florida requests often need more than an appliance type and ZIP code. If the pickup is from a condo, apartment, senior community or gated property, include floor level, elevator requirements, gate instructions, loading access and any property scheduling rules. For a house, note whether the appliance is in a garage, kitchen, laundry room or outside and whether the removal path has stairs or narrow doors.

Cooling appliances need current condition information

For a refrigerator or freezer, tell us whether it is plugged in and holding temperature now. Photos of the interior, exterior and model label are useful. Working washers, dryers, stoves and ranges should also include basic tested functions. Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup when local partner coverage exists; mixed loads may qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances work.

A single completely nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify for free pickup. Microwaves must accompany another qualifying major appliance, and dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

How Florida demand becomes local coverage

We compare qualified requests by metro area instead of claiming statewide crews. Repeated South Florida demand can support one partner territory, while Orlando, Tampa Bay, Jacksonville or Southwest Florida may justify separate partners. Pickup requests are reviewed against actual local operator availability; the existence of a city guide does not establish active pickup coverage.

Property managers, hotels, senior-living communities, installers and retailers with recurring replacement appliances should include estimated frequency and volume. Those repeat sources help us evaluate whether a local Florida pickup route can be supported. Until a request is accepted, statewide intake is not a guaranteed appointment.
