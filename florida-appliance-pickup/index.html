What Makes a Strong Pickup Request?

Working Condition

Tell us what works now. Working and reusable major appliances receive priority because they are more useful to local buyers, resellers and refurbishment partners.

Clear Location

Provide the exact city, ZIP code and street address so the request can be matched against real local demand and future partner territories.

Access Details

Report stairs, floors, elevators, gates, narrow doors, loading restrictions and whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway or inside the property.

Photos & Appliance List

Clear exterior/interior photos, model information and a complete list of appliances make qualification and partner matching more useful.

Free Pickup Qualification in Florida

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs can sometimes be reviewed, but a single completely nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are not accepted by themselves. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

How Florida Requests Help Build Future Partner Coverage

Every form submission gives us a real demand signal: city, ZIP code, appliance category, condition and access. When one area begins producing enough qualified requests, we can recruit appliance buyers, resellers, repair/refurbishment businesses and pickup operators for that territory.

This lets the partner network grow around real customer activity instead of claiming statewide crews that do not exist yet. Qualified local professionals interested in Florida can apply through the partner program.

Florida Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can I submit even if my city is not listed above?

Yes. The form accepts requests from anywhere in Florida. The regions above are demand groups, not a complete list of cities with guaranteed coverage.

Will someone call me immediately?

Your request must first be reviewed for appliance condition, location, access and available local coverage. A pickup is not confirmed until those details match an available route or participating local partner.

Can landlords or businesses submit several appliances?

Yes. Property managers, apartments, senior communities, hotels, installers, retailers and other organizations can describe one-time or recurring appliance replacement loads in the same request.
