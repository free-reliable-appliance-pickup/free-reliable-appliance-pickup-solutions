What Qualifies for Free Pickup in Oakland?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. Oakland requests are also reviewed for property or building access, street or loading conditions, current demand and available route coverage before scheduling.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Major Appliances We Review

Washers

Top-load and front-load washers. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and mention leaks, noise or error codes.

Dryers

Gas and electric dryers, including laundry sets. Identify the fuel type and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Refrigerators

Top-freezer, bottom-freezer, side-by-side and French-door refrigerators. Include an interior photo and cooling status.

Freezers

Chest and upright household freezers. Include an interior photo and confirm whether the freezer maintains temperature.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may qualify. Tell us whether the burners and oven operate normally.

Multiple Appliances

For property turnovers or garage loads, list each appliance separately and give the working condition of every item.

Apartment, Hillside & Property Access

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, kitchen, laundry room, first floor, upstairs apartment, condo or business. Include stair flights, elevator availability, narrow halls, gates, steep or difficult access, and parking or loading restrictions.

For managed properties, include any move-out hours, loading-zone requirements or building access instructions. For large refrigerators, send a photo of tight turns or doorways along the exit route.

Oakland Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free in Oakland?

Qualifying working appliances and eligible mixed loads may receive free pickup after condition, access, location, demand and coverage are reviewed.

Do you pick up a broken appliance by itself for free?

No. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work.

Can I request pickup from an upstairs apartment?

Yes, you can submit the request. Include floor level, stair flights, elevator information and doorway or hallway concerns so access can be reviewed.

Do you review refrigerators and laundry sets?

Yes. Working refrigerators, washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are priority categories.

How do I request pickup?

Use the online request form or call or text 310-774-4304 with appliance photos, condition, Oakland ZIP code and access details.
