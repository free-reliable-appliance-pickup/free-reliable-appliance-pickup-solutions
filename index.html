Why Working Appliances May Receive Priority

Our pickup model is designed to give qualifying appliances another opportunity for reuse, repair, refurbishment, parts recovery or recycling when practical. Because of that, appliances that are fully working, repairable or complete may be easier to match with an available pickup route.

That does not mean a non-working appliance cannot be submitted. We review each request individually based on appliance type, condition, location, access and current local demand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is appliance pickup really free?

Qualifying appliance requests may receive free pickup. Approval depends on appliance condition, photos, location, access, local demand and current pickup availability.

What appliances do you pick up?

Common requests include washers, dryers, washer-and-dryer sets, refrigerators, stoves, ranges, freezers, dishwashers and other qualifying major household appliances.

Do you pick up washers and dryers?

Yes. Qualifying washers, dryers and washer-and-dryer sets are priority pickup categories in many of our service areas.

Do I need appliance photos?

Clear photos are strongly recommended. They help us verify appliance type, condition, size and possible access issues before someone drives to the pickup location.

Do you pick up broken appliances?

Working, repairable and non-working appliances can be submitted for review. Pickup is not guaranteed and depends on the individual request and local availability.

Do you pick up standalone microwaves?

Standalone microwaves are generally not accepted. A microwave may be considered when included with another qualifying major appliance.
