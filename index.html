About Free Reliable Appliance Pickup

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup provides appliance pickup, removal and recycling services in active service areas throughout California, Oregon, Washington, Arizona and Colorado.

Our goal is to make appliance removal simple and convenient. If you have a qualifying appliance that is working and in acceptable condition, you can send us the details and photos so our team can determine whether the appliance qualifies for free pickup.

Pickup must be confirmed before a scheduled visit. Availability can vary by city, appliance type, condition, access and current demand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is appliance pickup really free?

Qualifying appliances may be picked up at no charge. Pickup is subject to appliance condition, location, access, demand and availability. We must confirm eligibility before scheduling.

What appliances do you pick up?

We primarily consider washers, dryers, refrigerators, stoves, ranges, freezers, dishwashers and other major household appliances.

Do I need to send photos?

Yes. Photos are strongly recommended because they help us evaluate the appliance and pickup conditions before confirming service.

Do you accept broken appliances?

Free pickup is primarily intended for qualifying appliances in working or acceptable condition. Send photos and details so we can determine whether your appliance can be considered.

Do you pick up appliances from apartments?

Apartment pickups may be possible depending on access, stairs, elevators, parking and other removal conditions. Please provide these details with your request.

Do you pick up microwaves?

We generally do not accept standalone microwaves. A microwave may be considered when included with another qualifying major appliance.

When are pickups available?

Our general service hours are Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Actual pickup times depend on availability and confirmation.
