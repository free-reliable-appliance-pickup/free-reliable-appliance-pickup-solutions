Appliance Pickup Services

Washer Pickup

Top-load and front-load washing machines are one of our highest-priority appliance categories.

Dryer Pickup

Qualifying electric and gas dryers may be accepted for free pickup.

Washer & Dryer Pickup

Washer and dryer sets are especially welcome. Matching sets are not required.

Refrigerator Pickup

Qualifying top-freezer, bottom-freezer, side-by-side and French-door refrigerators.

Stove & Range Pickup

Qualifying electric and gas stoves and ranges may be considered.

Freezer Pickup

Upright and chest freezers may qualify depending on condition and local demand.

Dishwasher Pickup

Dishwashers may qualify depending on condition, accessibility and location.

Multiple Appliances

Have several appliances? Submit them together in one pickup request.

Reuse & Recycling

Appliances may be recovered for reuse, refurbishment, parts recovery or recycling.

Microwaves: Standalone microwaves are generally not accepted. They may be considered when picked up with another qualifying major appliance.
