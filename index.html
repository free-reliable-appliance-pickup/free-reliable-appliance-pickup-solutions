Frequently Asked Questions

Is appliance pickup really free?

Qualifying appliances can be picked up for free. Each request is reviewed based on the appliance, condition, location and pickup circumstances.

What appliances do you pick up?

We commonly consider refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves, ranges, ovens and other major household appliances.

Do you pick up appliances that do not work?

Some non-working appliances may qualify. Tell us the appliance condition when you submit your request so we can evaluate it.

Do you pick up multiple appliances?

Yes. If you have multiple appliances, provide the types, condition and pickup location in your request.

Do you serve outside the cities listed?

Possibly. Service availability depends on the location and pickup circumstances. Send us your city and ZIP code so we can check.
