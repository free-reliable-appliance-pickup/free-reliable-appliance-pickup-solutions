Major Appliance Pickup Services

Washer Pickup

Top-load and front-load washing machines are a priority pickup category.

Dryer Pickup

Qualifying electric and gas dryers may be picked up from accessible locations.

Washer & Dryer Sets

Matching or mixed-brand washer and dryer sets are welcome for review. Please send photos of both machines.

Refrigerator Pickup

Qualifying full-size refrigerators, including common top-freezer, side-by-side and French-door models.

Stove & Range Pickup

Qualifying electric and gas stoves and ranges may be considered.

Freezer Pickup

Qualifying upright and chest freezers may be available for pickup.

Dishwasher Pickup

Dishwashers may qualify depending on condition, access and local demand.

Multiple Appliances

Have several major appliances? Include all of them in one request and upload clear photos.

Reuse & Recycling

Qualifying appliances may be removed for reuse, recovery or recycling.

Microwaves: standalone microwaves are generally not accepted. A microwave may be considered with another qualifying major appliance.
