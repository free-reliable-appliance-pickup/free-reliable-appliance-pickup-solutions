Washington Appliance Pickup & Removal

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup helps Washington residents request removal of qualifying major household appliances. Send us the appliance type, condition, photos and pickup location so the request can be reviewed before service is confirmed.

Washers and dryers are a major pickup priority. Working appliances and washer-and-dryer sets may be especially useful for reuse, resale or refurbishment.

Washington Appliance Pickup Services

Washer Pickup

Qualifying top-load and front-load washing machines may be picked up depending on condition, location and availability.

Dryer Pickup

Electric and gas dryers may qualify for pickup, especially when they are complete and reusable.

Washer & Dryer Sets

Washer and dryer sets are a high-priority category. Matching sets are welcome but are not required.

Refrigerator Pickup

We review qualifying top-freezer, bottom-freezer, side-by-side and French-door refrigerators.

Stoves & Ranges

Qualifying electric and gas stoves and ranges may be considered for pickup.

Freezers

Upright and chest freezers may qualify based on condition and local demand.

Dishwashers

Dishwashers may be considered depending on condition, accessibility and local availability.

Multiple Appliances

Removing more than one major appliance? Include all appliances in the same request.

Reuse & Recycling

Appliances may be directed toward reuse, refurbishment, parts recovery or recycling.

Microwaves: Standalone microwaves are generally not accepted. They may be considered when included with another qualifying major appliance.
