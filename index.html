About Free Reliable Appliance Pickup

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup helps homeowners, renters, landlords, property managers, businesses and other customers arrange pickup and removal of qualifying major appliances.

Our goal is to make appliance removal simple and convenient while helping usable appliances stay in circulation whenever possible.

Depending on the appliance and its condition, appliances may be reused, recovered, recycled or otherwise responsibly handled.

Important: Free pickup is subject to qualification, location and availability. Please send clear photos of the appliance and provide your city, ZIP code and pickup-location information before service is confirmed.

How Free Appliance Pickup Works

Step 1 — Send Your Information

Complete the request form and provide your name, phone number, city, ZIP code, address and information about the appliance.

Step 2 — Send Clear Photos

Photos help us determine the appliance type, condition and whether it may qualify for pickup.

Step 3 — We Review the Request

We review the appliance, location, access conditions and service availability.

Step 4 — Confirm Pickup

If the request qualifies, we coordinate a pickup time and provide any additional instructions needed.

Step 5 — Appliance Pickup

Our pickup team arrives at the agreed location and removes the qualifying appliance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is appliance pickup always free?

No. Free pickup depends on the appliance, condition, location, access and current availability. Every request is reviewed before pickup is confirmed.

What appliances do you pick up?

We primarily consider washers, dryers, refrigerators, stoves, ranges, freezers, dishwashers and other major household appliances.

Do you pick up broken appliances?

Some non-working appliances may be considered, but free pickup is not guaranteed. Send photos and details so we can review the request.

Do you pick up microwaves?

We generally do not provide pickup for a standalone microwave. A microwave may be considered when included with another qualifying major appliance.

What information should I provide?

Please provide clear appliance photos, your city, ZIP code, exact pickup address, floor information, stairs and any access details.

Do you pick up appliances from apartments?

Apartment pickups may be possible depending on access, stairs, elevators, parking and other conditions. Please provide these details with your request.

What are your hours?

Pickup requests are accepted Monday through Sunday, generally from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, subject to availability.
