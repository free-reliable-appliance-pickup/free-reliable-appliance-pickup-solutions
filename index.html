Appliances We Pick Up

We review qualifying requests for major household appliances. Free pickup depends on condition, location, access, route availability and local coverage.

Refrigerators

Working refrigerators and qualifying units with minor repair needs.

Washers & Dryers

High-demand laundry appliances, including qualifying sets and individual units.

Stoves & Freezers

Ranges, ovens and freezers are reviewed based on condition and route availability.

Other Appliances

Microwaves are not accepted alone. Dishwashers generally require two or more major working appliances.
