Free Appliance Pickup

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup helps customers request pickup of qualifying major household appliances in active service areas across the United States.

Pickup availability depends on appliance condition, location, access, route availability and local partner coverage. Free pickup is not guaranteed for every appliance or address.

Appliances We Pick Up

We review requests for refrigerators, washers, dryers, stoves and ranges, freezers, ovens and qualifying appliance loads. Microwaves are not accepted alone. Dishwashers generally require two or more major working appliances with the pickup.

Service Areas

Our growing network includes established and developing service areas in California, Oregon, Washington, Arizona, Colorado, Nevada, Utah, Idaho and additional U.S. markets.

How Free Pickup Works

Send your appliance details, condition, city or ZIP code, access information and photos when available. We review the request and confirm whether a qualifying pickup route is available.
