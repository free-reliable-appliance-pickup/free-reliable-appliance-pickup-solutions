What We Review in Riverside

Laundry Appliances

For washers, report whether the machine fills, drains and spins. For dryers, identify gas or electric and say whether it tumbles and heats. Submit complete sets together.

Refrigerators

Include current cooling status, approximate size and photos. If the unit is still plugged in, say so. An interior photo and model label are useful when available.

Freezers

Upright and chest freezers can be reviewed. Tell us whether the appliance maintains freezing temperature and whether it is empty and accessible.

Cooking Appliances

Gas or electric stoves, ranges and ovens can be submitted. Describe whether burners, oven functions and controls work normally.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

City of Riverside Access Details Matter

A garage pickup, upstairs apartment and interior kitchen removal require very different handling. Tell us where the appliance is located and describe the exit path. Include stairs, elevators, gates, long carries, narrow doors, parking limits or loading-zone rules. For refrigerators, mention whether doors may have to be removed to clear the path.

Landlords and property managers can submit several appliances together. List the condition of each item and whether they are located at one property or several units. For a mixed load, the working percentage is evaluated across the major appliances being submitted.

Riverside Appliance Pickup vs. City Bulky-Item Service

Riverside residents can compare our qualification-based appliance pickup with the City of Riverside's public bulky-item program. The two options are different, which is useful when a working appliance may still have reuse value or when the City's curbside rules do not match the item.

Our reuse-focused review

We review qualifying washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges based on condition, access, photos and current route demand. A useful working appliance may be routed for reuse instead of being treated only as bulky waste.

City of Riverside curbside option

Riverside Public Works states that residents can schedule up to two bulky-item appointments per year, with up to five items per appointment. Its curbside accepted-appliance list includes stoves, washers and dryers.

Refrigerators and freezers

Riverside's curbside bulky-item page lists appliances containing Freon, including refrigerators and freezers, as not accepted through that curbside program and directs residents to other refrigerator-recycling information.
