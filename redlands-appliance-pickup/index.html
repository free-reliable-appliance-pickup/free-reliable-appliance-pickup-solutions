Older Homes, Long Driveways & Interior Carry Distance

Redlands has a mix of older homes, newer subdivisions and properties where the appliance may be far from the curb. Tell us whether the unit is in a detached garage, rear area, kitchen, laundry room or upper floor. Long driveways, gates and narrow side access should be mentioned before scheduling.

Appliances We Prioritize

Washers & dryers

Working laundry machines are a strong priority. Describe normal cycle operation, leaks, noise and heating performance.

Freezers & refrigerators

Cooling performance is important. Include interior condition and note any missing shelves, cracked drawers or door-seal problems.

Ranges & ovens

Gas and electric cooking appliances may qualify when the working condition and disconnection status are clear.

Standalone microwaves are not accepted. Dishwashers are considered only when they come with two or more major working appliances. Minor repair issues can be submitted honestly for review, but approval depends on the full load and current demand.
