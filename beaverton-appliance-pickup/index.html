Beaverton Appliance Pickup for Working and Reusable Units

Our Beaverton review focuses on major appliances with reuse value: washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges. A clear description of what works is important because our free service is not a blanket junk-removal promise.

A completely nonworking single appliance does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repair situations may still be considered, and mixed loads can be reviewed when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. Submit honest condition details so we can decide before anyone drives to the property.

Faster review: include tested functions, appliance photos and access notes in the first request.

What to Tell Us About Each Appliance

Washer

Tell us whether it fills, agitates or tumbles, drains and spins. Note leaks, loud bearings, door or lid problems and error codes.

Dryer

Say whether it tumbles and heats and whether it is gas or electric. Include the washer too when both machines are available.

Refrigerator / Freezer

State whether it is currently plugged in and cold. Show the inside so missing shelves, bins or damage are visible.

Stove / Range

Identify gas or electric, describe burner and oven operation and tell us whether the unit is already disconnected.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Beaverton Condos, Apartments and Managed Properties

For apartment and condo pickups, list the floor, stairs, elevator access, hallways, loading area and any building time restrictions. For gated or managed properties, explain where the driver can park and whether management must approve the move.

For landlords and property managers, a grouped turnover can be easier to review when all appliances are listed together with condition. If several working machines are staged in one garage or maintenance area, say so. For refrigerators inside occupied units, note tight kitchen turns or doors that may need to be removed for clearance.

If your appliance does not fit our free reuse model, the City of Beaverton maintains garbage, recycling and non-curbside recycling information, including tools to identify the assigned hauler and find recycling options for items that do not belong in regular carts.

Photos, Parking and Removal Path Details Matter

Send a full exterior image and, for refrigerators and freezers, an interior image. A model-label photo is useful when easy to access. For washers and dryers, describe the actual cycle functions rather than only saying the machines look good.

Beaverton Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can you review appliance pickup from a condo?

Yes. Include the floor, elevator or stairs, parking and any building move restrictions before scheduling.

Can I submit several appliances at once?

Yes. List each major appliance and its condition. Mixed loads are reviewed based on the working percentage and route.

What if the appliance is broken?

A nonworking single appliance does not qualify for free pickup, but the City provides recycling and hauler resources for other options.
