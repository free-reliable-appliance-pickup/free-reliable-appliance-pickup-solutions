Major Appliances That May Qualify

We review working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges. Fully working appliances are the strongest fit for free pickup because the service focuses on reuse. Minor repair may sometimes be considered. A single completely nonworking appliance does not qualify by itself.

Mixed loads may qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Driveway, Stair and Doorway Details

For a hillside or sloped property, tell us whether a pickup vehicle can approach safely and how far the appliance must be carried. Mention exterior steps, gate width, side-yard access, interior stairs and narrow doorways. For large refrigerators, doorway measurements can be especially useful.

Landlords and property managers can submit multiple appliances together. List the condition of each unit and where the appliances are staged.
