Free Appliance Pickup in Houston, TX

Is free appliance pickup available in Houston?

Request-only market: The form is open for Houston inquiries, but free pickup is not available or guaranteed at every address. We confirm condition, access, local pickup partner or route, and any possible charges before scheduling. Please do not leave appliances outside until an appointment is confirmed.

Houston-area requests can span very different travel corridors. Please identify which side of town the appliance is on and whether the carrier can park near a garage, driveway, apartment loading area or elevator. In a large metro, access and distance can determine whether a free route is practical.

Working and reusable major household appliances receive priority. A nonworking unit may be reviewed case by case, especially when combined with other qualifying working appliances.

Which appliances can you submit?

Priority items

Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, washer-and-dryer sets, and stoves or ranges. Tell us whether a unit runs normally and include the make, model and approximate age if known.

Requests needing additional review

Single nonworking appliances, hard-to-access loads and smaller items are not automatically eligible for free collection. Dishwashers are normally reviewed alongside two or more working major appliances; microwaves are not accepted alone.

Three steps before a pickup is confirmed

  1. Describe the appliances: say which work, provide sizes and note cooling, heat, wash or spin functions.
  2. Provide the exact location and access: city, ZIP, stairs, floor level, gates, parking, and whether appliances are disconnected.
  3. Confirm the offer: we check reuse potential and route/partner availability, then any pickup date or fee is agreed before work takes place.

Submit your Houston appliance pickup request
