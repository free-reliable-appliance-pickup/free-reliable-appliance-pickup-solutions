Last updated: 2026-10-11

California determines the page-quality bar; established Oregon operations are the next deployment; later states inherit the quality bar only with verified coverage and differentiated local information.

## One-page gold acceptance checklist

For every **eligible, indexable** city page: one H1; one self-canonical; title/description matched to that city's actual request intent; accurate phone or form-only intake; reachable fast mobile request; appliance condition and access questions; real, correct appliance images; useful unique local removal/disposal information with official references; honest qualification and routing; links to parent state/region and relevant supporting service pages; no repetitive keyword lists. The general city page owns broad pickup; laundry/service pages own only their distinct appliance intent.

Measure finalized Search Console query × page × device data, live city-query visibility, form/phone leads, eligibility, completed pickups and legitimate referring domains. Rank #1 is the ambition, not a guarantee. **Do not** count research as deployment, drafts as outreach, or outreach as earned backlinks.

## Verified Gold Model (VGM): mandatory organic + competitor ranking evidence

**Reusable instruction:** "Use VGM" means **verify current state → measure our organic visibility and actual competitors → diagnose the page/query problem → make one evidence-gated change → test source and customer paths → verify production separately → measure organic outcomes and qualified leads → report proof and open limitations**.

This **extends** the California Gold Model; it does not replace established market truth, the SEO experiment registry, or the change-control policy. Competitor intelligence and organic search performance are **required inputs to every discretionary ranking decision**. Operational corrections that protect customers, lead routing, accurate facts or security may proceed without an organic baseline, with the reason documented.

### Mandatory priority-market scoreboard

Record separately for each market and search intent:

| Field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Market and page owner | Actual city/state, intended canonical URL, target service (general appliance vs washer/dryer etc.), verified operational coverage |
| Query and search context | Exact search phrase; date; mobile/desktop; search engine; target city or geolocation when supported; organic results distinguished from Maps/local pack, AI, paid and image results |
| Our ranking visibility | Rank and **actual ranking URL** from repeatable SERP tracking, or `not found to measured depth` / `not measured` (never invent a rank or treat "not found" as position zero) |
| Competitor landscape | The actual competing domains, **ranking URLs**, positions and relevant useful page differences on the same query, device and location; include TakeMyAppliance, Appliance Pickup Now and Fast Free Appliance Removal *when present*, not by assumption |
| Finalized Google Search Console | Query × page × device clicks, impressions, CTR, average position and comparison period, with exported source and end date; position is an impression-weighted search metric, not a guaranteed live rank |
| Search intent / page ownership | General-city vs specialty URL matches; cross-city or own-page cannibalization **supported by actual query/URL evidence**; no duplicate-page diagnosis from URL patterns alone |
| Customer outcomes | Qualified organic forms, attributed phone calls, partner acceptance and completed pickups when instrumentation supports it; mark unknown attribution explicitly |
| Decision and follow-up | Confirmed defect vs hypothesis; one permitted action, previous experiment/PR, baseline, owner approval when required, rollback, observation date and post-change verdict |

**Minimum query set for primary cities:** "free appliance pickup [city]", "appliance pickup [city]", "appliance recycling [city]", "free washer and dryer pickup [city]" (on the relevant laundry owner page). For "near me" queries, measure **only** with a supported fixed search location and clearly report that location; never publish one supposedly universal near-me rank.

**Priority order:** Rancho Cucamonga → Upland → Claremont → La Verne → San Dimas → Pomona; then Fontana and adjacent Inland Empire and San Gabriel Valley owners; Portland → Salem → Vancouver, WA; Seattle/Tacoma; Denver/Aurora; other established markets as coverage and data allow. This is workflow priority, **not** a statement of their current Google positions.

### Evidence and decision gates

1. **Baseline before ranking experiments:** Capture the latest available 28 *complete, finalized* GSC days per query/page/device, preferably 90 days for sparse data, plus a comparable current dated SERP/competitor snapshot. State sample sizes, scope and lag. If GSC is inaccessible or there are too few observations, mark the evidence **unavailable/inconclusive** and do **not** perform a discretionary SEO ranking rewrite.
2. **Compare like with like:** Competitor position snapshots must use the same query, date, region/location, device and result category as our own observations. Separate paid/Maps/AI/image visibility from traditional organic ranking. Keyword.com tracked ranks are not interchangeable with GSC average position or third-party estimated traffic.
3. **Diagnose rather than guess:** Inspect the winning *page* and the intended owner page, query intent, title/snippet, actual helpful content, valid local/service claims, mobile contact experience, reputable linking and relevant technical signals. A competitor ranking above us is **evidence of a gap to investigate**, not proof that copying its layout, changing a title or adding pages will improve rankings.
4. **One bounded change:** Check `data/seo-experiments.json` and historical PRs, record baseline and rollback, then change only a necessary variable family. No speculative city-page proliferation, wholesale rewrites or cannibalization redirects.
5. **Independent verification:** Differentiate source commit, CI, merge, public deployment, Google indexing, organic ranking trend and genuinely received qualified leads. Require tests on changed code and a public-page check. Test form *receipt* with authorization rather than inferring delivery from a click.
6. **Measure after an observation window:** Respect the active experiment registry and finalized GSC delay; compare the same query/page/device before versus after (and an unchanged comparable market if possible). Record `improved`, `unchanged`, `regressed` or `inconclusive`, with counts and alternate explanations. Do not promise a top-1/2 position or treat volatile SERP snapshots as proof of causality.

**Report format every time:** (a) exact URLs/queries and date, (b) observed facts and original evidence source, (c) competitors outranking us **only if observed**, (d) what changed with PR/commit or **no change**, (e) what was tested and live-verified, (f) organic GSC/tracker measurements if available, (g) lead impact if attributable, (h) unknowns, rollback and next review date.

## Practical lessons consolidated from videos

- **GSC:** Improve high-impression/low-CTR pages and relevant queries at positions 7–20; diagnose the exact query's ranking URL before changing it.
- **Competitors:** Compare actual content and search intent, reviews/FAQs, relevant published backlinks and honest Google Business categories with public competitors. PlePer/GMB Spy may help GBP research, but categories must accurately describe the business; do not change profiles just to mimic competitors.
- **Technical first:** Diagnose 404s, duplicate/cross-city intent, canonicals, schema, image quality and broken internal links before expansion. Avoid mass noindex/rewrite actions without measured evidence.
- **Authority:** Earn real relevant business, supplier, landlord, retailer, reuse/recycling and referral mentions. Avoid low-quality bulk directories, paid ranking links and fake listings.
- **Conversion and trust:** Accurate free-pickup qualification, mobile call/text, accessible forms, real pictures, service alternatives and genuine business experience matter more than vanity page counts.
- **Network:** Appliance Deal Flow USA recruits and verifies partner/operators; customer-facing Free Reliable Appliance Pickup content remains separate and reflects verified service capacity.

## Original California quality architecture (retained)

## Primary scoreboard

The primary SEO scoreboard is live organic visibility for the searches real customers use. Search Console is supporting evidence, not a substitute for live visibility.

For each priority market track both:
1. Explicit-city queries: "free appliance pickup + city", "appliance pickup + city", "appliance recycling + city", "free washer and dryer pickup + city".
2. Localized generic queries from the market when the search method supports location: "free appliance pickup", "appliance pickup", "free appliance pickup near me", "appliance pickup near me", "appliance recycling near me", "free refrigerator pickup near me", "free washer and dryer pickup near me".

Do not claim a universal "near me" position because results vary by location, device, and Google context.

## Architecture

Use this hierarchy consistently:

National homepage
→ national intent owners
→ state / major-region hubs
→ county / metro hubs where useful
→ priority city general page
→ dedicated city washer/dryer page only where commercially important
→ appliance-specific local pages only where real search demand justifies them

Examples:
- / → /appliance-removal/ → /california-appliance-pickup/ → /inland-empire-appliance-pickup/ → /rancho-cucamonga-appliance-pickup/
- /washer-dryer-pickup/ → /inland-empire-washer-dryer-pickup/ → /rancho-cucamonga-washer-dryer-pickup/

The homepage must not become a directory of hundreds of cities. Full city inventories belong on /service-areas/ and regional hubs.

## National homepage rules

Purpose: own broad generic intent such as free appliance pickup, appliance pickup near me, appliance removal near me, and connect users to local coverage.

Keep:
- One H1.
- Clear pickup/removal promise.
- ZIP/location availability path.
- Major appliance categories.
- Simple request process.
- Nationwide/priority hub links.
- A short set of proven priority cities.
- Commercial program entry point.

Avoid:
- Dozens of mini-regional sections.
- Hundreds of city links.
- Repeating the same "near me" explanation in multiple sections.
- Treating every city as equally important from the homepage.

Target: focused page comparable in scope to strong nationwide competitors, not a giant city directory.

## Regional hub rules

Regional/state hubs should:
- Explain the real network/coverage model.
- Link to the strongest city pages in that region.
- Link to relevant national intent pages.
- Carry the longer city list that does not belong on the homepage.
- Provide unique regional details, service logic, commercial opportunities, and local alternatives.
- Avoid duplicating another regional hub word-for-word.

Examples: Inland Empire, San Gabriel Valley, Orange County, Riverside County, San Bernardino County, Phoenix Metro, Oregon, Colorado.

## City general-page rules

The city general page is the primary owner for:
- free appliance pickup + city
- appliance pickup + city
- appliance removal + city
- appliance recycling + city
- general mixed-appliance intent

It should include:
- clear H1 and concise title/meta
- real qualification rules
- request form / phone where appropriate
- appliance categories
- access details
- true local neighborhoods / ZIP context
- local disposal or municipal alternative
- commercial / landlord use where relevant
- FAQ based on real customer questions
- real photos when available
- links upward to the correct regional hub and national intent pages
- links to the city washer/dryer page if it exists

Do not create several local pages that all compete for the same broad city query.

## Washer/dryer rules

The national washer/dryer page owns broad national laundry intent.

Regional washer/dryer hubs connect the national page to priority city laundry pages.

A city washer/dryer page should own only laundry intent, such as:
- free washer pickup + city
- free dryer pickup + city
- free washer and dryer pickup + city
- washer dryer removal + city

Do not let city washer/dryer pages compete for the city's broad appliance pickup query.

## Refrigerator/freezer/stove local specialty pages

Do not create or emphasize these by default in every city.

Use them only when:
- Search Console/live SERP evidence shows real appliance-type demand,
- the page has genuinely useful distinct content,
- the city general page remains the owner of broad local appliance intent.

Existing specialty pages should point back to the main city page and should not be the main internal-link target for broad queries.

## Internal-link authority

Homepage:
- national intent pages
- state/region hubs
- a short list of strongest priority cities

State/region hubs:
- city pages
- relevant appliance-type hubs
- adjacent hubs when useful

City general page:
- parent regional hub
- national intent pages
- dedicated city washer/dryer page where applicable
- local specialty pages only as supporting resources

Specialty page:
- main city page
- matching national appliance-type page
- parent region if useful

Do not place giant city lists on every page.

## Live SERP decision rules

Before a substantial change:
- check the exact target query live when possible
- identify which page/domain currently wins
- inspect why that result matches intent
- compare our intended owner page
- make one evidence-backed change

After changes:
- do not rewrite the same page every day
- allow crawl/indexing time
- watch live position and Search Console trend
- change again only when evidence supports it

## Authority outside the site

On-page work alone is not enough.

Build legitimate outside signals through:
- Facebook business/page posts pointing to the correct local page
- verified Google Business Profiles only for real eligible locations
- partner/business mentions
- local citations
- property-manager / senior-living / installer / retailer partnerships
- real community/business references
- useful content that can earn links naturally

Never create fake GBP locations or misleading local business citations.

## Expansion rule

Do not mass-produce another large batch of pages until the gold model shows measurable improvement in live organic visibility.

When the model is working:
1. propagate structure to the next strongest city cluster,
2. preserve genuine local uniqueness,
3. keep homepage/state/region/city ownership clear,
4. validate live visibility after deployment,
5. repeat.

## Safety / quality guardrails

Never:
- keyword stuff
- fabricate reviews
- fabricate addresses or local presence
- create doorway pages with only city-name swaps
- noindex/delete large page groups without evidence
- make paid-tool purchases automatically
- make irreversible mass changes without review

The goal is a nationwide search network with concentrated authority, clear intent ownership, real local usefulness, and measurable live organic growth.
