Tacoma Pickup Requests Are Reviewed for Local Partner Coverage

This is a Tacoma-specific request page inside the Washington pickup network. It does not represent a permanent Tacoma branch or guarantee that a truck is available every day. The form gathers the exact location, appliance condition and access details so we can check whether a Tacoma or Pierce County appliance buyer, reseller, repair business or pickup professional can review the request.

Fully working refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves and ranges may qualify when a suitable local partner is available. Minor repair needs can sometimes be reviewed. Mixed loads can be considered when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single appliance that is completely nonworking does not automatically qualify for free pickup.

Confirmation matters: do not treat a submitted form as a scheduled appointment. Keep reusable appliances protected until you receive direct acceptance and pickup details.

Tacoma Hills, Basements and Older-Home Access

Tacoma appliance removal can involve steep streets, basement utility rooms, side-yard stairs and older door openings. Those details should be included before a partner accepts the request. A refrigerator on a level garage floor is a different job from the same refrigerator in a basement with a narrow stair turn.

Basement laundry rooms

State the number of steps, stair width, landings and whether a washer or dryer is still connected. For gas dryers, do not disconnect equipment solely to qualify for pickup; tell us the current installation instead.

Steep or limited vehicle access

Describe driveway slope, alley access, street parking limitations and carrying distance. If a pickup vehicle cannot safely stop close to the appliance, that can change whether a free route is practical.

Doors, porches and side gates

Send photos when large appliances must pass through older doorways, porches, fenced side yards or tight turns. Measurements are especially useful for French-door refrigerators and wide ranges.

What Makes a Strong Tacoma Appliance Request

Condition should be specific rather than simply “used” or “old.” For refrigerators and freezers, report whether the appliance holds temperature. For washers, describe fill, drain and spin. For dryers, say whether the drum turns and the machine heats. For stoves and ranges, identify gas or electric and which burners and oven functions work.

Send exterior photos and, for cooling appliances, interior photos when practical. Include the model label if it is easy to reach. For multiple appliances, list each item and condition separately so the working percentage can be evaluated correctly.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Tacoma Call-2-Haul Is a Separate City Program

Customers who do not have a reusable appliance candidate may have an official City of Tacoma option. The City says residential Solid Waste customers can schedule two curbside Call-2-Haul pickups or self-hauls per year, with the service cost built into residential rates. The current program allows up to three large items per pickup, and its accepted large-item examples include refrigerators, washers and dryers.

The account holder schedules the service through the City. The City also publishes separate rules and fees for multifamily and commercial customers, so apartment or business customers should not assume the residential terms apply. Tacoma instructs customers to place approved curbside items out by 7 a.m. and only set out the items disclosed when the appointment was scheduled.

Official Tacoma resource: City of Tacoma Call-2-Haul · Call-2-Haul scheduling: 253-573-2468. Verify current eligibility, limits and appointment availability directly with the City.

Tacoma Apartments, Rentals and Recurring Appliance Loads

For a rental turnover, apartment property, senior community, installer route or appliance business, tell us whether the request is one-time or recurring. Include how many appliances are available at one address, which units work, whether they are already staged, and whether the property has loading, elevator or gate rules.

Recurring Tacoma and Pierce County supply can help justify stronger partner coverage because a local buyer or refurbisher can evaluate predictable inventory rather than a single long-distance pickup. Commercial or multifamily requests still require qualification; this page is not a promise that every recurring load will be accepted.

Tacoma Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup automatically free in Tacoma?

No. It is free to submit a request, but free pickup is confirmed only when the appliance qualifies and an available Tacoma or Pierce County partner accepts the request.

Can I submit appliances from a Tacoma basement or steep property?

Yes. Include stair count, basement access, narrow doors, grade, carrying distance and photos so the access can be reviewed before a local partner accepts the job.

What official Tacoma option exists if my appliance is not a reuse candidate?

City of Tacoma residential Solid Waste customers can use the Call-2-Haul program under current eligibility rules. The City says eligible residential customers receive two curbside pickups or self-hauls per year, with appliances accepted among the large-item categories.
