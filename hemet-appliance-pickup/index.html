Hemet Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

This page is the Hemet-specific destination for general appliance pickup and removal requests. We review working and reusable refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves, ranges, ovens and qualifying multi-appliance loads.

Free pickup is not automatic. We review the exact address, appliance condition, access, current local demand and route availability before confirming a pickup. Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Send first: clear appliance photos, tested working condition, exact address or ZIP code, floor level, stairs, gates, parking or loading limits and carrying distance.

Hemet Pickup for All Major Appliance Types

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the appliance is currently cooling or freezing. Interior, exterior and model-label photos help with size and condition review.

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report fill, drain and spin. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether the machine tumbles and heats.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

State whether burners, controls and oven functions work and whether the appliance is gas or electric.

Mixed Loads

Multiple-item loads can be reviewed together. Describe each appliance separately so the working percentage is clear.

Hemet Homes, Rentals & Pickup Access

Hemet requests can come from single-family homes, apartments, rental turnovers, mobile-home communities, garages and business properties. The route review depends on the actual removal path, not only the city name.

Include stairs or elevator, driveway or alley access, gates, parking restrictions, narrow doors and carrying distance. If a refrigerator or range is inside, photos of tight turns or doorways can prevent a failed trip.

Qualification Limits

A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup. Minor-repair items may be reviewed when current local demand supports them. Microwaves are not accepted alone. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Submitting a request starts the review. It does not guarantee free pickup or a specific arrival time until the request is accepted.

Hemet Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup, the City of Hemet publishes a separate bulky-item program through its contracted waste provider. The City lists washers and dryers among common bulky items and describes resident pickup allowances and scheduling requirements.

The municipal program is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup. Confirm current eligibility and fees directly with the City or its waste provider before setting an appliance out.
