Property Managers, Senior Communities & Recurring Replacements

Managers, landlords, maintenance teams and senior-community staff can submit repeat appliance replacement needs. Send item count, working condition, building or unit access, staging details and expected cadence.

What Helps a Pickup Qualify

Send clear photos, model information, tested condition and exact access details. Mixed loads may qualify when most major appliances are working or reusable. A single broken appliance may require another hauling or recycling option.

Valley Pickup Planning by Property Type

San Fernando Valley requests vary by neighborhood and property layout. Single-family homes may have garage or side-yard access, while apartments and managed buildings may require elevator reservations, loading-zone timing or manager approval. For faster review, include the exact neighborhood, appliance location, whether the unit is disconnected, and any gate, stair or long-carry details.

For recurring replacements, group appliances by building or property whenever possible and identify which units are fully working. That helps route reusable refrigerators, laundry sets, freezers and ranges efficiently across north, central and east Valley stops without treating every request as a separate haul-away job.
