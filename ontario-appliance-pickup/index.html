The fastest way to know whether an Ontario appliance may qualify is to tell us what it actually does. Ontario Access & Pickup Planning

Access information prevents wasted trips. If the appliance is already in a garage or driveway, tell us whether the pickup vehicle can get reasonably close. If it is inside, describe the floor level, stairs and any narrow doors or turns. Large refrigerators sometimes require door removal, and laundry appliances can be difficult when installed in closets or stacked configurations.

For apartment, condo or commercial-property requests, include gate access, elevators, loading zones and parking restrictions. For rental turnovers, list each appliance and unit location. If several appliances are being replaced at once, the complete load can be reviewed together rather than treating every item as a separate request.

Do not move a heavy appliance unsafely just to make it easier to accept. Give us accurate access details first.

What to Send With Your Ontario Request

Include a clear full-appliance photo, exact pickup address and ZIP code, and the working condition. For a refrigerator or freezer, say whether it is plugged in and cooling. For a washer, tell us whether it fills, drains and spins. For a dryer, tell us whether it tumbles and heats. Describe leaks, noises, error messages or cosmetic damage honestly.

Then explain access: garage, outside, first floor, upper floor, stairs, elevator, narrow hallway, gate or long carry. These details help us decide whether the appliance and route are a practical match.

Ontario Appliance Pickup vs. City Bulky-Item Collection

Ontario residents can compare our qualification-based appliance pickup with the City's Integrated Waste bulky-item service. Our route prioritizes appliances that still have reuse or repair value, while the City program is a separate public disposal option for eligible residents.

Ontario public option

The City of Ontario states that single-family residents may schedule up to four bulky-item appointments per calendar year. Its bulky-item page lists appliances among accepted items and limits curbside pickup to five large items per appointment.

Use reuse when practical

If the appliance still works, reuse-focused pickup can preserve more value than treating it only as bulky waste. If it does not qualify for our route, Ontario's public program may be the better path.
