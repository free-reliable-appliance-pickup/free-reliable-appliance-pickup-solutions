Ontario Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can I get free appliance pickup in Ontario?

Yes. A fully working or reusable major appliance can be submitted for free-pickup review. Acceptance depends on condition, exact location, access and current route availability.

Do you pick up broken appliances for free?

A completely nonworking appliance by itself normally does not qualify. Minor repairs or a qualifying mixed load may still be reviewed.

Can landlords or property managers submit several appliances?

Yes. List every appliance, its condition, the property address and unit/floor/access details. Use separate requests for separate properties.

How fast can pickup happen?

Timing depends on qualification and local route availability. Send photos, condition and access details first so the request can be reviewed accurately.
