Azusa Appliance Pickup Review

Azusa is part of the eastern San Gabriel Valley route centered on the 909 regional team. Homeowners, renters, landlords and property managers can submit working and reusable major appliances for qualification review.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup.

Appliances We Review in Azusa

Washers & dryers

Working laundry machines are a priority. Describe fill, drain, spin, tumbling and heating performance for each appliance.

Refrigerators & freezers

Tell us whether both compartments maintain temperature and include clear exterior, interior and model-label photos.

Stoves, ranges & ovens

Gas and electric cooking appliances may be reviewed when operation, fuel type and removal access are clearly described.

Foothill Access, Driveways & Multi-Level Homes

Azusa properties may have sloped approaches, narrow driveways, detached garages, exterior steps or apartment access restrictions. Describe the safest path from the appliance to the loading area, including gates, stair flights, elevators and parking. Do not relocate a working unit into a harder-to-reach area before review.

Photos and Operating Details That Help

For refrigerators and freezers, include exterior and interior photos and say whether the unit maintains temperature. For washers, report fill, drain and spin operation; for dryers, report tumbling, heat and fuel type. A model-label photo helps identify size and configuration.

Important Pickup Limits

Minor-repair appliances may be considered after accurate disclosure. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Submitting the request starts a review and does not guarantee free pickup. We confirm condition, exact address, access and route availability before scheduling.

Azusa Appliance Pickup FAQs

What appliances can I request in Azusa?

We review qualifying washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and multiple-major-appliance loads.

Can you review an appliance from an upstairs apartment?

Yes. Include the floor, stairs, elevator and loading access so the removal can be evaluated before scheduling.

Are microwaves accepted alone?

No. A microwave is accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.
