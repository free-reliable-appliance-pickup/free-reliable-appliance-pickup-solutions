Send first: exact Phoenix ZIP/address, clear photos, working condition, brand/model if known, floor/stairs and access details.

What We Need to Know

Cooling

Tell us whether the refrigerator is plugged in and holding normal food-safe temperature.

Inside & Outside Photos

Show shelves, drawers, doors, handles and exterior condition.

Access & Size

Include approximate width, doorway clearance, stairs and whether the unit is in a kitchen, garage or outside.

Free Pickup Is Qualification-Based

Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Minor repair needs may be considered when local reuse demand supports the route. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify under the standard free-pickup rules.

Current Phoenix Utility Rebate Note

Some older Phoenix appliance-recycling pages still mention refrigerator recycling rebates. SRP currently states that its former refrigerator recycling program is no longer available. Because utility programs can change, check the current utility program before relying on an old rebate amount.

City of Phoenix Alternative

The City of Phoenix lists washers, dryers, stoves, refrigerators and freezers among appliances accepted through its appliance recycling options; scheduled curbside pickup is fee-based. If your appliance is still working or reusable, submit it here first for free-pickup review.
