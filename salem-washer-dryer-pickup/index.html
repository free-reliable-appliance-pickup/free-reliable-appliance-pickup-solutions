Salem Washer & Dryer Pickup Review

Salem washer and dryer requests are reviewed through the established Salem-area route. A working set in a garage is different from machines in a second-floor rental, a West Salem hillside home or an older central Salem house with steps and tight turns.

Tell us what you actually tested. Washers should be described by power, fill, wash or agitation, drain and spin. Dryers should include gas or electric service, power, drum movement and heat. If you do not know the condition, say that rather than guessing.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets receive priority. A single completely nonworking machine does not automatically qualify; repairable units are reviewed only when condition, local demand and the current route support pickup.

What to Send for a Faster Laundry Pickup Decision

Washer Test

Tell us whether the washer powers on, fills, washes or agitates, drains and completes the final spin. Mention leaks, unusual noise, error codes or a door-lock problem.

Dryer Test

Identify gas or electric service and tell us whether the dryer powers on, tumbles and heats. If it is a ventless or combination unit, include that in the request.

Photos

Send a clear full photo of each machine and a model-label photo when practical. For stacked units, show the full installation so height and removal access are clear.

Removal Path

Include floor level, stairs or elevator, narrow doors, hallway turns, gates, parking or loading limits and the carrying distance to the pickup vehicle.

Salem Laundry Access & Local Route Details

The City of Salem recognizes 17 neighborhood associations, and laundry access can vary across those areas. West Salem is in Polk County and often includes hillside access, so driveway slope, steps and carrying distance can matter. Central Salem homes may have basements or older doorways, while Lancaster-area and newer multifamily requests can involve exterior stairs, shared parking or apartment access.

Common Salem ZIP codes include 97301, 97302, 97304, 97305, 97306 and 97317. Use the exact street address because a Salem mailing address alone is not enough to determine the correct local route.

Washer & Dryer Requests Only

This page is specifically for washers, dryers, laundry centers and washer/dryer sets in Salem. For refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges, ovens or mixed major-appliance loads, use the general Salem appliance pickup page.

If you have several laundry machines from a rental turnover or property refresh, list each machine separately and identify which units are fully working, need repair, do not work or are unknown. Grouped working equipment at one address is easier to evaluate than several incomplete requests.

Salem Laundry Pickup Qualification

A working washer, working dryer or complete working set can be submitted for free-pickup review. Minor repair needs may be considered when you describe the issue accurately. If you are submitting a mixed load, list every major appliance so we can review the working share of the complete load.

Do not describe a machine as working unless the key functions have actually been tested. Honest condition details help prevent wasted trips and make it easier to match the request to the correct local route.

Salem Local Reuse or Disposal Backup

If a washer or dryer does not qualify for our pickup, Marion County's Salem-Keizer Recycling & Transfer Station at 3250 Deer Park Dr. SE is a public disposal option. Marion County currently lists $5 per appliance without refrigerant, which covers typical washers and dryers, and public hours of 8 a.m.–5 p.m. seven days a week.

Mixed loads may be charged at the garbage rate, so confirm the current rules before transporting laundry machines with other waste.

Sometimes. Describe the exact issue and what still works; free pickup is not guaranteed for nonworking equipment.
