Texas Cannot Be Treated as One Appliance Pickup Route

Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, Austin, San Antonio and El Paso are separate operating markets. The statewide form is designed to discover demand inside each of them without suggesting that one truck covers Texas end to end. Exact city and ZIP information determines which market should receive the request.

Metro-specific demand is more useful than a statewide total

A cluster of working appliances in DFW helps identify demand and assess whether a pickup route can be supported without making a service promise. Austin and San Antonio can develop independently, and El Paso must be evaluated as its own market. We keep those signals separate so future service decisions are based on realistic travel and customer volume.

What Texas appliance owners should send

For refrigerators and freezers, include cooling status and clear interior/exterior photos. For washers, report fill, drain and spin; for dryers, tumble and heat. For a stove or range, state gas or electric and which functions operate. Include the exact address, stairs, floor, gates, parking or loading limits and carrying distance.

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup when local coverage exists. Mixed loads can be considered when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single completely nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify. Microwaves need another qualifying major appliance, and dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Texas recurring sources can support efficient pickups

Apartment operators, senior communities, hotels, installers, appliance stores and property managers should identify whether replacements happen repeatedly and whether several appliances are stored at one property. A dependable stream of reusable inventory can be more useful for developing a local network than isolated statewide leads.

This page accepts Texas requests before every market has a confirmed local pickup arrangement. Submission is free; pickup is confirmed only after condition, access and an available local route are reviewed. That distinction lets us expand into Texas without making a service promise we cannot yet support.

Texas Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can I submit even if my city is not listed above?

Yes. The form accepts requests from anywhere in Texas. The regions above are demand groups, not a complete list of cities with guaranteed coverage.

Will someone call me immediately?

Your request must first be reviewed for appliance condition, location, access and available local coverage. A pickup is not confirmed until those details match an available local pickup route.

Can landlords or businesses submit several appliances?

Yes. Property managers, apartments, senior communities, hotels, installers, retailers and other organizations can describe one-time or recurring appliance replacement loads in the same request.
