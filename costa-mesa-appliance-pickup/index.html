Costa Mesa Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Costa Mesa requests are reviewed through our Orange County network. We prioritize working appliances and useful multi-appliance loads, then check the address and removal path before confirming a route. Homeowners, renters, landlords, businesses and property managers can submit appliance details for review.

For searches such as appliance pickup, appliance removal, appliance recycling, washer pickup, refrigerator pickup or appliance removal near me in Costa Mesa, send the exact address or ZIP, appliance condition and loading/access details so the current 909 route can be reviewed.

Free pickup is conditional. Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for our free pickup.

What Makes a Strong Costa Mesa Request?

Accurate condition

Tell us what works today. For laundry equipment, describe the cycle. For refrigerators and freezers, state whether the appliance maintains temperature.

Photos

Send a full exterior photo and model-label photo when available. Interior photos are useful for refrigerators and freezers.

Access

Include floor level, stairs, elevators, gates, narrow doors, parking restrictions and the distance from the appliance to the loading area.

Homes, Apartments & Multi-Family Properties

For single-family homes or small multi-family properties, describe the appliance's current location and any limited parking or alley access. For larger multi-family or commercial properties, include property-management or loading requirements. Costa Mesa uses different waste-service arrangements depending on property type, so accurate address and building details are important.

For move-outs and replacement projects, submit the complete group of appliances together. We evaluate the entire load rather than assuming every individual item qualifies.

Appliance Categories & Limits

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and qualifying multi-appliance loads. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

A submitted form is not a confirmed appointment. We schedule only after the condition, access and Orange County route are approved.

Costa Mesa Waste-Service Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our free service, the City of Costa Mesa states that residential waste collection for single-family homes and properties with four or fewer units is administered by the Costa Mesa Sanitary District, while larger multi-family and business properties use approved franchise haulers.
