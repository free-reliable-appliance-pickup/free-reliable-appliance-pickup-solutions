Upland Stove, Range & Oven Pickup Review

Working cooking appliances receive priority. Include width, gas/electric/induction type, burner operation, oven heat, control condition and whether the appliance is already safely disconnected.

Gas ranges

Report burner and oven function. Do not disconnect a gas line unless it can be done safely by an appropriate person.

Electric & induction

Tell us which cooktop zones and oven functions were tested and whether the power cord is present.

Professional-style ranges

For Viking, Wolf, Thermador, KitchenAid and similar units, include width, weight/access details and model information.

Upland Removal Access

Historic Downtown and older homes can have narrow doorways or steps; North Upland properties may have longer or sloped driveways. Apartments and condos should include gate, elevator, parking and loading details.

Upland ZIPs 91784 and 91786 use the same qualification review, but exact address and access determine route planning.

Replacement-Day Review

If a new range is arriving, send the outgoing appliance’s working condition before the delivery team removes it. Store haul-away can be convenient, but a working appliance may have reuse value and may qualify for the free route.
