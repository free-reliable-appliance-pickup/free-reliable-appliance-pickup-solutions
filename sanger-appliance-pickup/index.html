What Can Qualify for Free Pickup in Sanger?

Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered, but a single nonworking general appliance does not normally qualify by itself.

Sanger Bulky-Item Pickup vs. Our Appliance Route

Sanger residents also have a separate municipal bulky-item option. The City of Sanger bulky-item pickup program says eligible residents may receive two bulky-item pickups per year, with up to three items per pickup; listed examples include washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers and stoves. The city also lists weight, loading and flatbed-size requirements.

Our service is different: it is a qualification-based appliance pickup route focused on working, reusable and certain repairable appliances. Customers can call or text 310-774-4304 and send appliance photos for route review. If the appliance does not qualify here, the city program may be another option, subject to the city’s current rules.

Sanger Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can you review inside pickup?

Yes. Send floor level, stairs, elevator, doorway, gate and parking details before scheduling.

Can I submit one working appliance?

Yes. A single working refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer, stove or range can be reviewed. Route availability still matters.

What happens to reusable appliances?

Depending on condition and local route capacity, reusable appliances may be directed toward reuse, repair, resale or an appropriate recycling path rather than disposal-only handling.
