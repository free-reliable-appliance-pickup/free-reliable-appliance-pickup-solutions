What can I submit?

Freestanding gas ranges, electric ranges and some wall ovens can be reviewed. Tell us the fuel type, approximate size and whether burners or elements, controls and the oven work.

What condition receives priority?

Working burners or elements, a functioning oven, intact knobs and controls, solid doors and clean reusable condition increase the chance of qualification. Cosmetic wear can be acceptable when the appliance still works.

What access details matter?

Tell us whether the range is in a kitchen, garage, upstairs unit or another location. Include stairs, narrow halls, floor level and whether the appliance is disconnected and ready to move.

How should gas ranges be prepared?

Gas appliances should be disconnected safely when required. Do not loosen gas fittings unless qualified. Tell us whether the gas line is already disconnected.

What happens after I submit?

We review whether the appliance is gas or electric, which functions work, overall condition, access and local demand. Clear photos of the front, controls and cooking surface help the review. If pickup is available, make sure the appliance is safe to move. Gas connections must be handled properly, and the pickup path should be free of loose items and obstacles.

Free Pickup Is Qualification-Based

Fully working appliances receive priority. A single nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the load is working. Microwaves are accepted only with another major qualifying appliance, and dishwashers only with two or more major working appliances.
