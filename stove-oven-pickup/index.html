Looking for Stove, Range or Oven Pickup Near Me?

Replacing a working stove, range or oven? Send its fuel type, working condition and pickup address. Tell us whether it is freestanding, slide-in or built into cabinetry. Gas, electric, induction and dual-fuel appliances can be submitted for review, with removal arrangements confirmed before scheduling.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Working and reusable cooking appliances receive priority. A single nonworking stove or oven does not qualify for free pickup.

Cooking Appliances We Review

Electric ranges

Report whether all burners or elements heat, the oven reaches temperature, and the display and controls work.

Gas ranges

Tell us whether burners ignite and the oven heats normally. Mention damaged grates, missing caps, ignition issues or control problems.

Induction and smooth-top ranges

Report cracked or chipped glass, error codes and whether each cooking zone responds correctly.

Wall and double ovens

Built-in appliances require more access information. Tell us whether the unit has already been safely removed from cabinetry and is ready to move.

How to Describe Working Condition

For electric ranges, test each burner or element, oven, broiler and controls when safe. For gas ranges, report normal ignition and heating only if the appliance can be operated safely. Mention error codes, missing knobs, damaged racks, broken handles, cracked cooktops, weak flames or oven-temperature problems.

For premium or commercial-style ranges, include the width, approximate weight, number of ovens and full removal path. Heavy 36-inch or larger ranges may require additional handling equipment.

Gas and Electrical Safety

Do not loosen or disconnect gas fittings, hardwired electrical connections or built-in appliances unless you are qualified to do so. If a gas range has already been safely disconnected, say so in the request and include a rear-connection photo when practical.

If gas odor is present, stop using the appliance and contact the appropriate utility or qualified professional. Pickup planning should never require unsafe testing or gas-line work.

Measurements and Removal Access

Include appliance width, depth and height and tell us whether it is freestanding, slide-in or built-in. Mention stairs, narrow hallways, kitchen islands, tight turns, floor transitions, gates, elevator access and parking or loading restrictions.

For apartment or rental turnover requests, list each cooking appliance separately and identify which unit or floor it is located in.

Pickup Preparation

Remove cookware, racks you plan to keep and personal items, and clear the path to the exit. Tell us whether the appliance is already safely disconnected. Do not move a heavy range into a harder-to-access location before pickup is confirmed.

Built-In Ovens and Large Ranges

For a wall oven or double oven, include the model number, dimensions and whether it remains installed in cabinetry. For a large range, photograph the route from the kitchen to the loading area. Confirm removal arrangements with the team before moving the appliance or arranging installation work.

Stove & Oven Pickup FAQ

Can a single working stove qualify?

Yes. A fully working stove or range can be reviewed as a single appliance. Condition, access, local demand and route availability still determine acceptance.

Will you disconnect a gas range?

Do not assume gas disconnection is part of pickup. Tell us whether the appliance has already been safely disconnected, and use a qualified person for gas-line work when required.

Can a broken range be picked up free?

A single nonworking range does not qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Free Pickup Rules

Fully working appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Submitting a request does not guarantee free pickup. Appliance condition, access, location, local demand and route availability are reviewed before scheduling.
