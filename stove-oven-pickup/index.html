What Can I Submit?

Freestanding gas ranges, electric ranges and some wall ovens can be reviewed. Tell us the fuel type, approximate size and whether burners or elements, controls and the oven work.

What Condition Receives Priority?

Working burners or elements, a functioning oven, intact knobs and controls, solid doors and clean reusable condition increase the chance of qualification. Cosmetic wear can be acceptable when the appliance still works.

Access and Safe Preparation

Tell us whether the range is in a kitchen, garage, upstairs unit or another location. Include stairs, narrow halls, floor level, tight turns and whether the appliance is already disconnected and ready to move.

Gas appliances must be handled safely. Do not loosen or disconnect gas fittings unless you are qualified to do so. Tell us whether the gas line has already been properly disconnected.

How to Describe Stove or Range Condition

Electric ranges

Tell us whether all burners or elements heat, whether the oven reaches temperature and whether the display and controls work. Mention cracked glass cooktops, missing knobs, damaged racks, broken handles or error codes.

Gas ranges

Say whether the burners ignite and whether the oven lights and heats. Mention ignition clicking, weak flames, damaged grates or missing burner caps. Do not test or disconnect gas connections in an unsafe way.

Wall ovens

Some wall ovens can be reviewed, but built-in removal is more complex. Tell us whether the unit is already removed from cabinetry and ready to move, and include photos of the installation and access path.

Pickup Preparation

Remove cookware and personal items and make the path to the exit clear. Tell us about stairs, narrow doorways, kitchen islands, tight turns and floor transitions. Pickup crews should not be expected to perform unsafe gas-line work.

Free Pickup Is Qualification-Based

Fully working appliances receive priority. A single nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the load is working. Microwaves are accepted only with another major qualifying appliance, and dishwashers only with two or more major working appliances.
