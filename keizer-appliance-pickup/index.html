Keizer Appliance Pickup Review

This page is for major-appliance pickup requests at Keizer addresses. Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves, ranges and mixed major-appliance loads can be reviewed for qualification before a route is confirmed.

Washer-, dryer- and laundry-set-only requests should use the dedicated washer & dryer pickup guide.

How Keizer Pickup Review Works

1. Send appliance details

Include appliance type, tested condition, photos and whether the unit is already disconnected and movable.

2. Describe the access

Tell us about floor level, stairs, doors, driveway or parking access, gates and carrying distance.

3. Confirm qualification and route

Free pickup is qualification-based. Pickup is confirmed only after condition, access and current route availability are reviewed.

Keizer Appliance Qualification

Working and reusable major appliances are the strongest candidates for free pickup. Minor-repair situations may be considered, and mixed loads can be reviewed when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

See the free-pickup qualification rules.

Keizer Homes, Rentals and Access

Garages, driveways, apartments, stairs, narrow doors and long carries can change the handling plan. For rental turnovers or multi-appliance requests, list each appliance separately and explain whether the units are staged, connected or still inside the property.

For refrigerators, include interior and exterior photos and say whether the unit is holding temperature. For washers and dryers, describe basic tested functions and whether the dryer is gas or electric.

Official Paid Backup: Salem-Keizer Transfer Station

If an appliance does not qualify for our free reuse-based pickup, Marion County's Salem-Keizer Recycling and Transfer Station accepts appliances for a fee. Marion County publishes separate rates for appliances with and without refrigerant and explains the applicable handling requirements.

Check current Marion County transfer-station appliance fees and rules.
