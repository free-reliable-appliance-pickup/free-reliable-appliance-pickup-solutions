Keizer Appliance Pickup Connected to Our Salem Route

Keizer is part of the Salem-area route we are strengthening for local search and real pickup demand. We review working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges for possible free pickup, with condition and access checked before scheduling.

A completely nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for our free service. Minor repair situations may be considered, and mixed loads can be reviewed when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. If you have several appliances, list each one separately so the working percentage is clear.

Working laundry equipment is especially useful: tell us whether washers fill, drain and spin and whether dryers tumble and heat.

Major Appliance Categories We Review

Washers & Dryers

Submit pairs together when possible. State whether the washer completes a cycle and whether the dryer heats; also identify gas or electric dryers.

Refrigerators

Tell us whether the refrigerator is currently plugged in and holding temperature. Include exterior and interior photos.

Freezers

Chest and upright freezers can be good reuse candidates when they freeze correctly and are empty and accessible.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and describe burner, oven and control operation. Tell us whether it is already disconnected.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Keizer Homes, Garages, Rentals and Stairs

Tell us where the appliance is located before scheduling. A unit in a garage with driveway access is different from one inside a kitchen, upstairs unit or basement. List exterior steps, interior stairs, narrow doors and long carries.

For rental turnovers, identify which appliances are working and whether they are already disconnected or staged. For refrigerators, mention doorways and kitchen turns. For stacked laundry, explain whether the machines are still installed in a closet or tight alcove.

Salem-Keizer Transfer Station Is an Official Paid Backup

If an appliance does not qualify for our free reuse-based pickup, Marion County's Salem-Keizer Recycling and Transfer Station accepts appliances for a fee. The County currently publishes separate rates for appliances without refrigerant and appliances designed to contain refrigerant, and explains that refrigerant handling must be performed professionally.

Keizer Appliance Pickup FAQ

Do you take a broken refrigerator for free?

A completely nonworking refrigerator by itself does not qualify. Use the Marion County transfer-station option when reuse is not a fit.

Can I submit a washer and dryer together?

Yes. Working laundry sets are a priority. Tell us what you tested on both machines.

Can you pick up from a garage?

Garage pickups are often straightforward when a truck can get close, but condition and route availability still need review.
