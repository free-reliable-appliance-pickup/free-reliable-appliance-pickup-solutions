Free Pickup Qualification in Duarte

Free pickup is aimed at major appliances with reuse value. Fully working appliances are the strongest fit. Minor repair may sometimes be considered. One dead appliance by itself does not qualify. If you have several appliances, the load may qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working.

Microwaves are not accepted alone. Dishwashers are reviewed only when included with two or more major working appliances. Pickup remains subject to condition, access, local demand and route availability.

Help Us Review the Removal Path

Measure or describe narrow doorways when a refrigerator or range may be a tight fit. Mention stairs, landings, elevator access, parking restrictions or a long carry from the appliance to the street. If a unit is already outside or in a garage, say so because that can simplify the review.

Landlords and property managers can submit multiple units in one request. List each item separately so working and nonworking appliances can be evaluated as one load.
