Upland Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Upland residents may search for free appliance pickup, appliance removal, appliance recycling, washer pickup, refrigerator pickup or appliance pickup near me. We review those requests using the exact pickup address, appliance condition, removal access and current 909 route availability.

Upland is part of our priority Inland Empire cluster. We review working and reusable major appliances before scheduling, with extra attention to the complete load and the path from the appliance to a safe loading area.

Free pickup depends on qualification. Fully working appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when about 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup.

What to Tell Us About the Appliance

Washer or dryer

Describe the full cycle, heat or spin condition and any leak or error. Complete working laundry sets receive priority review.

Refrigerator or freezer

Confirm whether the appliance is currently cooling. Send an interior photo and mention missing shelves, drawers or door damage.

Stove or range

Include gas or electric type and whether the burners and oven are working. Tell us if the appliance is already disconnected.

Upland Pickup Access

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upper floor, basement or outside. Include steps, narrow doorways, alleys, gated access or parking limitations. For apartment and condominium requests, include elevator and loading-zone information.

Multiple-appliance requests should list the condition of every item. One detailed request is more useful than separate forms that do not show the full load.

Important Limits

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Unknown-condition or repair items may need additional information before a decision can be made.

We confirm a pickup only after the appliance, address, access and available route have been reviewed.
