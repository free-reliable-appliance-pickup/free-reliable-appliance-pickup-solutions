About refrigerator pickup in Columbus

What qualifies for refrigerator pickup?

Working refrigerators that cool properly receive priority. Tell us the refrigerator type, approximate size, and whether it maintains a safe cooling temperature. If it needs repair, describe the problem and include photos so a local partner can review the request.

Include your ZIP code, floor level, stairs, and access details. Free pickup is subject to qualification and partner availability. Never cut refrigerant lines or dismantle the sealed cooling system.

How to request refrigerator pickup
