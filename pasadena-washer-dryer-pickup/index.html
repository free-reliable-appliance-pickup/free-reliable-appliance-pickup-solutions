What We Review Before Confirming Pickup

Washer condition

Tell us whether it fills, agitates or tumbles, drains and spins. Mention leaks, loud bearing noise or error codes.

Dryer condition

Tell us whether it is gas or electric, whether the drum turns and whether it produces heat.

Access

Pasadena requests may come from houses, apartments, garages or multi-unit properties. Include stairs, elevators, tight doors and parking restrictions.

Set completeness

If the washer and dryer match, submit both in the same request. Include hoses, cords or pedestals only if they are part of the set.

Pasadena Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Can one working washer qualify?

It may. A single working washer or dryer can be reviewed, although complete working sets receive especially strong priority.

Can you pick up from an apartment?

Apartment requests can be reviewed. Include floor level, elevator availability, stairs, hallway or doorway limitations and loading access.

Should I disconnect the machines first?

Tell us whether the appliances are already disconnected. Do not move or disconnect anything unsafely just to submit a request.
