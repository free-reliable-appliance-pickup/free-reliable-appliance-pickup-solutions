What to Test Before Requesting Sacramento Laundry Pickup

Washer

Report whether it powers on, fills, washes or agitates, drains and reaches final spin. Mention leaks, error codes, bearing noise, lid-lock or door-lock problems and water remaining in the tub.

Dryer

Identify gas or electric service and report whether it powers on, tumbles and heats. Mention no-heat problems, control issues, unusual noise or a door that will not latch.
