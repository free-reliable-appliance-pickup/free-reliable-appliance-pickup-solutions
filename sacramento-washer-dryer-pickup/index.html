Sacramento City Appliance Pickup vs. Our Laundry Route

The City of Sacramento appliance and e-waste program currently gives residential collection customers two free appliance/e-waste appointments per calendar year and allows up to two appliances per appointment. Washers and dryers are listed as accepted appliances.

The City requires appliances to be placed in the street, visible and easy to access, and says tenants in multifamily properties with five or more units that are not served by City solid waste should work with the landlord, property manager or commercial hauler. Commercial appliances are not accepted by the City program.

Our route is different: it reviews working and reusable laundry equipment for pickup from the property, including apartment and property-management requests when access and route conditions can be handled. Customers should submit photos and access details before moving a machine outside.
