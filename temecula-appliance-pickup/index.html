Major Appliance Categories We Review

Washers & Dryers

Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. Identify gas or electric dryers.

Refrigerators

State whether the refrigerator is currently plugged in and holding temperature. Include exterior and interior photos.

Freezers

Chest and upright freezers can be good reuse candidates when they freeze correctly and are empty and accessible.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric, describe burners, oven and controls and tell us whether the appliance is already disconnected.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Garage Access, Gated Communities and Interior Pickups

Temecula pickups can range from an easy garage handoff to an interior removal through a gated or managed property. Tell us whether a gate, code, guard or HOA procedure affects access. Do not place private gate codes in a public message; provide necessary details through the request process.
