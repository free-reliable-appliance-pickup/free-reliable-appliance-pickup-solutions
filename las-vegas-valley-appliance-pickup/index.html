What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working major appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Every request is reviewed for appliance condition, exact city or ZIP code, access, current demand and available route or partner capacity before a pickup time is confirmed.

Appliances We Review

Washers

Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins. Mention leaks, unusual noise, balance problems or error codes.

Dryers

Identify gas or electric and tell us whether the dryer tumbles and heats. Include any control, timer or vent-related issue you know about.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the appliance is currently plugged in and cooling or freezing correctly. Include an interior photo and exterior condition.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Gas and electric cooking appliances may qualify. Tell us whether burners, oven functions and controls work normally.

Desert Heat Makes Cooling Details Important

For refrigerators and freezers, accurate cooling information matters in Las Vegas because appliances can be exposed to very high garage or outdoor temperatures. Tell us where the appliance is stored, whether it is currently plugged in, and whether it maintains proper temperature. A unit that is unplugged cannot be treated as confirmed working based only on appearance.

If possible, include a model-label photo and a clear interior photo. Large side-by-side or French-door refrigerators also need access information because doorways, islands and tight turns can affect removal.

Gates, Apartments, High-Rises and Loading Rules

Las Vegas Valley requests can come from gated communities, apartment complexes, condos, high-rise buildings, rental homes, storage areas and businesses. Tell us the floor level, number of stair flights, elevator availability, gate or call-box requirements, parking restrictions and whether a loading area must be reserved.

If building management requires proof of insurance, a scheduled loading window or elevator reservation, include that information before service is confirmed. For narrow halls or large refrigerators, send a photo of the removal path if door removal or extra maneuvering may be needed.

Best first request: ZIP + appliance list + working condition + clear photos + floor/stairs + gate/elevator/loading details + total number of appliances.

Rental Turnovers and Property Managers

For rental turnovers or multiple units, list every appliance and its condition in one request. Multiple qualifying appliances at one property or nearby properties can make a route more practical than a single low-value or nonworking appliance.

Property managers should include unit numbers, access instructions, site contact information, gate codes, preferred windows and whether appliances are already disconnected and ready for removal. Accurate information helps prevent missed routes and unnecessary return trips.

Photos Help Us Review Before Anyone Drives

Send one clear exterior photo of each appliance, an interior photo for refrigerators and freezers, and a model-label photo when available. Photos help us review condition, size and reusability before a truck or local partner is assigned.

Submitting a form starts the review. Pickup is confirmed only after qualification, access and available route or partner coverage are checked.

Las Vegas Valley Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is pickup automatically free in Las Vegas or Henderson?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based. Location alone does not guarantee service; condition, access, demand and available route or partner coverage are reviewed first.

Can North Las Vegas and surrounding communities submit requests?

Yes. Submit the exact ZIP code, appliance details, photos and access information so the request can be matched to the appropriate regional route.

What if the appliance is upstairs or behind a gate?

Include stair flights, elevator availability, gate instructions and any loading restrictions. Difficult access can affect whether a pickup can be accepted and which crew is suitable.

What if my appliance does not work?

A nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repair needs may be considered, and eligible mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.
