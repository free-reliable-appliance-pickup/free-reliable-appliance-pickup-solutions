San Joaquin County Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

This is a regional request hub, not a storefront or municipal facility. We review qualifying appliance pickup requests based on the real pickup address, appliance type, tested condition, safe access, local demand and current route or independent-partner capacity.

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs can be considered when reuse value remains. A single completely nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify under the standard free-pickup rules.

Free to submit · No account required. Send the exact address, appliance type, working condition, clear photos, floor level, stairs, gates, parking/loading restrictions and carrying distance. A request starts the review; it is not a confirmed appointment until the route accepts it.

Major Appliances We Review

Refrigerators & Freezers

Send interior and exterior photos, model information when visible and whether the unit currently holds the correct temperature. Keep sealed refrigerant systems intact.

Washers & Dryers

Describe washer fill, wash, drain and spin functions and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. Stockton laundry-only requests should use the dedicated Stockton washer-and-dryer page.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Include fuel type, tested burners and oven functions, width and whether the appliance is already safely disconnected.

Multiple Appliances

Landlords, property managers, remodelers and businesses can list each appliance separately with its condition. Mixed loads may qualify when most major appliances are working and reusable.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

San Joaquin County Pickup Access & Route Details

Access can change whether a route is practical. Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first-floor room, upstairs unit, apartment, storage area or commercial location. Include stair flights, elevator availability, gates, narrow doors, long carries, loading-zone restrictions and parking limitations.

County routing can involve longer distances between Stockton, Manteca, Tracy, Lodi and surrounding communities, so the exact address matters more than a broad ZIP or city name. Send the real pickup location before moving the appliance outside.

Official San Joaquin County Appliance Recycling Alternatives

If a request does not qualify for our reuse-focused route, San Joaquin County publishes a separate appliance-recycling path for residents. The County lists major white goods such as refrigerators, freezers, clothes washers and dryers, ovens, stoves, dishwashers and water heaters among appliances accepted through its solid-waste facilities, subject to current limits and facility rules.

The County currently directs residents to facilities including Foothill Sanitary Landfill near Linden, the Lovelace Materials Recovery Facility and Transfer Station in Manteca, and the North County Recycling Center and Sanitary Landfill near Lodi. Refrigeration units and other appliances may have special handling requirements, and customers should confirm current limits before transporting them.

San Joaquin County Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free everywhere in San Joaquin County?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based. Working and reusable appliances receive priority, and the exact address, access, demand and current route capacity must be reviewed before service is confirmed.

Which page should I use for Stockton?

Use the dedicated Stockton appliance pickup page for an address inside Stockton. It is the primary Stockton city page.

What if I live in Manteca, Tracy, Lodi, Lathrop, Ripon or Escalon?

Use this county hub and submit the exact address. We review the real location before confirming whether a current route or partner can accept the request.

Do broken appliances qualify?

A single completely nonworking appliance generally does not qualify under the standard free route. Minor repairs or eligible mixed loads may be considered depending on local demand.

Where can I take an appliance if the pickup does not qualify?

San Joaquin County publishes appliance-recycling options at county solid-waste facilities. Check the County's current appliance page for limits and handling requirements before transporting the item.

Can apartments or property managers submit several appliances?

Yes. List each appliance and condition, plus building access, stairs/elevators, loading restrictions and the number of units or addresses involved.
