When Is Portland Appliance Pickup Free?

Qualifying fully working appliances can receive free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Tell us the condition of every appliance in the load; a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered after review.

Dishwashers are accepted only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

We confirm the complete load, address, access and schedule before approving pickup.

Portland Metro Appliance Disposal Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our pickup, Metro Central Transfer Station in Portland accepts large metal appliances. Metro lists metal dishwashers, microwaves, water heaters, stoves, ovens, washers and dryers in its appliance category; coolant-containing appliances such as refrigerators and freezers have separate handling and fees.
