Our Portland-area team reviews pickup requests from homeowners, renters, landlords and property managers. Tell us what you are replacing, whether it still works and where it is located so we can confirm whether the pickup qualifies before scheduling.

We commonly pick up qualifying refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves, ranges and other household appliances.

When Is Portland Appliance Pickup Free?

Qualifying fully working appliances can receive free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Tell us the condition of every appliance in the load; a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered after review.

Dishwashers are accepted only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

We confirm the complete load, address, access and schedule before approving pickup.

Garage, Driveway and Inside Pickup in Portland

You can request pickup from a garage, driveway or inside a property. Include the floor level, number of stair flights and any narrow doors, gates or elevator restrictions. For apartments, tell us about parking or loading access and any building arrangements needed before arrival.

Send a full appliance photo and a photo of the model label when available. For a refrigerator, include an interior photo and tell us whether both compartments cool properly. If access is tight, include the appliance dimensions and a photo of the exit route.

Replacing appliances at a rental? List each unit separately with its condition and location so we can review the complete pickup together.

Portland Metro Appliance Disposal Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our pickup, Metro Central Transfer Station in Portland accepts large metal appliances. Metro lists metal dishwashers, microwaves, water heaters, stoves, ovens, washers and dryers in its appliance category; coolant-containing appliances such as refrigerators and freezers have separate handling and fees.
