Lakewood Pickup Is Based on Reuse Value and Real Access

Lakewood requests can come from a driveway garage, a townhouse, an apartment building or a home where the appliance must move through a basement stairway. We review both the appliance and the path out of the property. That lets us separate an easy reusable-appliance pickup from a job that requires a different removal option.

Our strongest candidates are working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges. A single appliance that is completely nonworking does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repair situations and mixed loads can still be reviewed when most of the major appliances are working.

Important: submitting the request is free, but pickup is confirmed only after condition, access and route availability qualify.

What to Tell Us About Your Lakewood Appliance

Washer & Dryer

Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. A working pair is especially useful to submit together.

Refrigerator

Say whether it is currently plugged in and holding temperature. Include exterior and interior photos when possible.

Freezer

Chest and upright freezers are useful Colorado reuse items. State whether the unit freezes correctly, is empty and can be reached safely.

Range or Oven

Identify gas or electric, whether it is disconnected, and which burners, oven controls and heating functions are working.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only when included with two or more major working appliances.

Lakewood Access Details Matter Before the Truck Is Routed

Lakewood has a wide mix of property layouts, so include practical handling information rather than only the street address. Tell us if the appliance is in a garage, main floor, basement or upper floor; list stair flights; and note narrow doors, tight turns, long carries, elevators or loading restrictions.

For stacked laundry, say whether the machines are still stacked and whether they are inside a closet. For refrigerators, mention doorways or kitchen islands that could affect the exit path. If a building or HOA controls loading areas, elevator reservations or move-out times, include that information in the request.

A Useful Lakewood Alternative When Free Pickup Does Not Qualify

The City of Lakewood explains that bulky items such as appliances that are not suitable for reuse may be handled by a resident's waste hauler or a junk-removal provider for a fee. The city also points residents toward reuse options for working appliances. That makes accurate condition information important: a working appliance may have a reuse path that a dead or heavily damaged appliance does not.

Photos Help Us Decide Faster

Lakewood Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can you pick up from a basement?

Basement pickups can be reviewed. Include stair count, width, turns and whether the appliance is already disconnected.

Do broken refrigerators qualify?

A nonworking refrigerator by itself does not qualify for our free service. Submit accurate condition information so we can determine whether another option is more appropriate.

Do you serve nearby Denver-area communities?

Yes, our Colorado network also reviews Denver, Aurora and surrounding communities. Route availability varies by city and appliance type.
