How Everett appliance pickup requests work

We collect appliance details and check whether a local buyer, repair business, reseller or pickup professional can accept the request. This page does not claim a staffed Everett location or a daily truck route. Do not set an appliance outside or treat a submitted request as a scheduled appointment until a pickup partner confirms the details.

Working, reusable major appliances are the best candidates. Mixed multi-appliance loads may be considered when about 80% are working. A single completely nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify for free removal. Whether a request can be accepted depends on the appliance, access and current coverage at the exact address.

Everett locations and access information

For Everett neighborhoods such as downtown, Riverside, Bayside, Silver Lake and the area around Paine Field, please provide the exact ZIP code and pickup address in your request. We do not assume the same partner covers every part of Everett or nearby Snohomish County.

Describe whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, apartment, condo, basement, upper floor or commercial space. Note stairs, elevators, loading docks, restricted parking, narrow doorways and long carrying distances. Property managers should include any loading reservations or building access requirements.

Major appliances we review

Washers and dryers: Tell us whether a washer fills, drains and spins and whether a dryer tumbles and heats. Mention stacked installations and stair access.

Refrigerators and freezers: Explain whether the unit cools and freezes correctly. Include dimensions, floor level and whether doors or gates restrict access.

Stoves, ranges and ovens: Describe which burners and oven functions work, and whether the unit is electric or gas. Do not disconnect a gas appliance solely for the request.

Dishwashers: A dishwasher request is considered only when accompanied by at least two other major working appliances. Microwaves are not accepted as a standalone pickup.

Everett homeowners, rentals and commercial requests

Homeowners, renters, landlords, apartment managers and appliance sellers can submit details for review. If multiple units are available, list each appliance separately with its condition. Photos and accurate access details help prevent unsuitable matches. Reuse-focused pickup is different from municipal bulky-item collection or scrap disposal; if a unit cannot qualify, check the City of Everett or your local waste provider for available disposal options and current rules.

Everett pickup questions

Is appliance pickup automatically free?

No. Submitting a request is free; pickup itself is free only when the appliance and a confirmed local partner route qualify.

Can you remove an appliance from upstairs?

Sometimes, depending on the available partner, stairs, elevator access, appliance size and safety. Include those details before pickup is accepted.

How soon can pickup happen?

There is no guaranteed Everett pickup time. Timing depends on a partner accepting the request and agreeing to the access and appliance details.
