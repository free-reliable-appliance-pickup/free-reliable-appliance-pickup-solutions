# Platform V2 Competitor Benchmark

Last reviewed: 2026-09-30

This file is a standing release gate for local SEO and marketplace work. V2 should not expand a city/market batch without checking the current strongest competing pages first.

## Current benchmark set

### TakeMyAppliance
Current strengths observed on Rancho Cucamonga and Fontana pages:
- Clear city + county hierarchy.
- Short three-step request workflow.
- Free-to-submit / no-account conversion language.
- Local detail sections, neighborhoods and ZIP codes.
- Local disposal / alternative information.
- Appliance-type internal links.
- Nearby-area internal links.
- Partner recruitment CTA.
- Commercial / recurring pickup paths covering retailers, warehouses, reverse logistics, multifamily, hospitality, installers, commercial refrigeration/laundry and recurring programs.

V2 response:
- Keep city + market + county hierarchy.
- Keep dedicated washer/dryer pages instead of forcing all intent into one generic page.
- Use real appliance photos and condition/access detail.
- Keep qualification honest; do not promise every appliance or every address is free.
- Route eligible requests through approved/verified partner logic.
- Keep commercial and recurring program links on local pages.
- Preserve nearby intent-matched internal links.

### Pick Up My Appliance
Current strengths observed:
- Very simple ZIP-first request entry.
- Concise three-step flow.
- Strong local-network message.
- Dedicated appliance-type pages.
- Partner recruitment integrated with customer funnel.

V2 response:
- Keep the request form short enough to complete quickly while still collecting condition/access data needed to prevent bad pickups.
- Use exact city/ZIP as the first routing key.
- Keep customer contact information private until a valid assignment/claim flow.
- Keep appliance-specific intent and local-market intent connected.

Do not copy:
- Blanket claims that every condition/appliance/location is guaranteed free unless the real operating model can support that promise.

### Fast Free Appliance Removal
Current strengths observed:
- Strong reuse/refurbish/resale explanation.
- Clear washer/dryer demand story.
- Local independent-operator model.
- Bulk apartment/business appliance pickup messaging.
- Local phone-driven conversion.

V2 response:
- Explain reuse/resale/repair value where useful.
- Keep washer/dryer as a priority category.
- Expand recurring property, senior living, retail, installer and multifamily funnels.
- Keep local phone/text options alongside the online form.

## V2 advantages to protect

- Dedicated washer/dryer pages and routing.
- Real appliance photos rather than generic stock-only presentation.
- Qualification based on condition, access, location and route capacity.
- SGV -> Inland Empire corridor architecture.
- Exact source-page and attribution tracking.
- Owner dashboard connecting Search Console performance to leads.
- Approved/verified partner matching with territory + appliance + condition checks.
- Customer contact details remain locked until assignment/claim logic permits release.
- Commercial and recurring pickup programs.
- Staging remains noindex until SEO and operational parity are verified.

## Required competitor check before a city batch is considered ready

For the main queries in that market, review the strongest current results and compare:

1. Search intent and title/H1 alignment.
2. City/county/region hierarchy.
3. Local detail that actually helps pickup decisions.
4. Neighborhood/ZIP coverage.
5. Appliance-type internal links.
6. Nearby-city internal links.
7. Request-form friction and conversion path.
8. Phone/text/contact visibility.
9. Washer/dryer-specific intent.
10. Commercial/recurring program paths.
11. Partner-network/recruitment path.
12. Schema and canonical behavior.
13. Image quality and uniqueness.
14. Qualification accuracy and trust language.
15. Search Console impressions, clicks and query/page overlap after release.

## Guardrails

- Do not manufacture local facts simply to make a page longer.
- Do not create thin near-duplicate city pages.
- Do not promise active local pickup when the territory has no confirmed coverage.
- Do not copy competitor wording.
- Do not expose customer contact details to unapproved partners.
- Do not change or remove a working live URL without redirect/canonical/measurement review.
- Use competitor patterns as a benchmark, then improve the customer and partner system rather than merely adding more text.
