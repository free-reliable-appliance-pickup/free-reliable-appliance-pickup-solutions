Aurora Washer & Dryer Pickup Review

Aurora laundry pickup requests are reviewed as part of the Denver–Aurora route. Working washers and dryers are useful reuse categories, especially complete sets with clear test results. Single working machines can also be reviewed.

Tell us whether the washer fills, washes, drains and spins and whether the dryer is gas or electric, tumbles and heats. For apartment, condo or rental turnover requests, include building access before a pickup time is promised.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets receive priority. A single completely nonworking machine does not automatically qualify; repairable units are reviewed only when condition, local demand and the current route support pickup.

What to Send for a Faster Laundry Pickup Decision

Washer Test

Tell us whether the washer powers on, fills, washes or agitates, drains and completes the final spin. Mention leaks, unusual noise, error codes or a door-lock problem.

Dryer Test

Identify gas or electric service and tell us whether the dryer powers on, tumbles and heats. If it is a ventless or combination unit, include that in the request.

Photos

Send a clear full photo of each machine and a model-label photo when practical. For stacked units, show the full installation so height and removal access are clear.

Removal Path

Include floor level, stairs or elevator, narrow doors, hallway turns, gates, parking or loading limits and the carrying distance to the pickup vehicle.

Aurora Laundry Access & Local Route Details

Aurora covers a large geographic area with apartments, established neighborhoods, newer subdivisions and multifamily communities. The City publishes neighborhood and ZIP-code maps, so we use the exact street address rather than treating every Aurora mailing address as the same route.

For upper-floor laundry, provide stairs or elevator information and any building move restrictions. For a garage or first-floor pickup, tell us whether a truck can load close to the machines. For stacked units, include the overall installation and any closet clearance that affects separation or removal.

Washer & Dryer Requests Only

This page is specifically for washers, dryers, laundry centers and washer/dryer sets in Aurora. For refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges, ovens or mixed major-appliance loads, use the general Aurora appliance pickup page.

If you have several laundry machines from a rental turnover or property refresh, list each machine separately and identify which units are fully working, need repair, do not work or are unknown. Grouped working equipment at one address is easier to evaluate than several incomplete requests.

Aurora Laundry Pickup Qualification

A working washer, working dryer or complete working set can be submitted for free-pickup review. Minor repair needs may be considered when you describe the issue accurately. If you are submitting a mixed load, list every major appliance so we can review the working share of the complete load.

Do not describe a machine as working unless the key functions have actually been tested. Honest condition details help prevent wasted trips and make it easier to match the request to the correct local route.

Aurora Local Reuse or Disposal Backup

If working laundry equipment does not qualify for our route, the City of Aurora lists Habitat for Humanity ReStore as a reuse option for clean, fully working appliances less than 15 years old; the City's appliance page specifically includes washers and dryers among the categories ReStore may accept.

The City lists the Aurora ReStore at 1640 S Abilene St, Aurora, CO 80012. For disposal instead of reuse, Aurora also lists the Denver Arapahoe Disposal Site at 3500 S. Gun Club Road and advises customers to confirm fees and accepted materials.
