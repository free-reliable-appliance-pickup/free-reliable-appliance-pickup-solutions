Ontario Refrigerator Pickup Review

A working refrigerator is a strong candidate for review. A single refrigerator that no longer cools does not automatically qualify for free pickup through our reuse-focused route.

Ontario requests can come from houses, apartments, condos, garages and commercial properties. Include floor level, gated access, stairs or elevator, loading/parking limits and carrying distance so the request can be reviewed against the correct Inland Empire route.

Cooling test

Tell us whether the refrigerator and freezer sections cool normally and whether the unit is plugged in and testable.

Photos

Send the exterior, interior, model label and visible damage. Include approximate width for large units when known.

Access

Mention stairs, gates, narrow doors, interior removal and the distance to the loading area.

Fast review: Text clear photos to 909-375-6685 and include your Ontario ZIP code plus the condition details above.

How the Ontario Review Works

1. Send the basics

Prepare the Refrigerator Safely

Keep the sealed cooling system intact. Do not cut refrigerant lines or remove the compressor. Empty food and loose shelves after an accepted pickup is confirmed.

Ontario Local Disposal Alternative

The City of Ontario provides appointment-based bulky-item collection for residents and lists appliances among accepted items at its community cleanup events. That public program is separate from our reuse-focused pickup review.

Can a working refrigerator qualify for free pickup in Ontario?

What should I send for refrigerator pickup?

Send clear photos, current working condition, ZIP code and the appliance-specific testing and access details listed on this page.
