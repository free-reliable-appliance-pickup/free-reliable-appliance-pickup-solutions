Anthem Washer Pickup, Dryer Pickup & Removal

Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets can be submitted for free pickup review. Complete working sets receive strong priority because both machines can be evaluated together for reuse. A single working washer or dryer may also qualify when condition, route distance, access and current demand fit.

Best laundry request: washer fill/agitate/drain/spin + dryer tumble/heat + gas or electric + clear photos + exact ZIP/address + floor/stairs + gate/elevator/parking details.

How to Describe the Machines

Washer

Tell us whether it fills, agitates, drains and spins. Mention leaks, loud bearing noise, error codes, rust or missing parts.

Dryer

Tell us whether it tumbles and heats, whether it is gas or electric, and whether controls and door operate normally.

Complete Set

Matching or compatible working sets are especially useful. Send front, side and control-panel photos when possible.

Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working machines receive priority. Minor repair needs may be considered when reuse or repair demand supports the route. A single nonworking washer or dryer generally does not qualify under the standard free-pickup rules.

For multiple appliances, list every machine separately and describe which ones are working.
