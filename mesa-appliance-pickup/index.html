Mesa Appliance Recycling Is Separate From Bulk Trash

Mesa has a city appliance-recycling program that is different from ordinary bulk-item collection. That distinction matters when an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused free pickup. The City of Mesa says scheduled appliance collection is available for items including refrigerators, dishwashers, stoves, washers, dryers, water heaters, microwaves and evaporative coolers. Refrigerators and other refrigerant-bearing equipment require proper refrigerant handling before recycling.

For Mesa trash and recycling customers, the city currently lists a per-appliance collection fee, with an additional refrigerant-removal charge for cooling appliances. Mesa also lists a no-charge resident drop-off option at its Household Hazardous Materials Facility, subject to the city program's limits and rules. HOA or multi-unit residents may need to coordinate through the property manager because solid-waste arrangements can differ by property.

Why this matters for our service: if your refrigerator, washer, dryer, freezer or range is still working or reusable, send it to us before paying for disposal. If it does not qualify for our route, Mesa's official program is a practical fallback rather than treating the appliance like ordinary bulky trash.
