How Free Appliance Pickup Works

Clear information about our qualification-based appliance pickup service.

Request PickupService Areas

1. Submit the appliance details

Start by telling us what major appliance or appliances you want picked up. Include the appliance type, working condition, city and ZIP code, floor level, stairs and access notes. Photos are strongly recommended because they help us see the overall condition and identify obvious damage before a route is planned.

For refrigerators and freezers, tell us whether the unit is currently plugged in and cooling or freezing. For washers and dryers, mention whether the machines complete their normal cycles. For stoves and ranges, tell us whether the burners or elements and oven operate.

2. We review condition and local demand

Free pickup is qualification-based. Fully working and reusable appliances receive priority because they have the best chance of being reused rather than simply discarded. Minor-repair appliances may be considered depending on age, model, overall condition, local demand and route availability.

A single nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the load is working. This lets us evaluate the whole pickup rather than treating every item the same.

3. Access is part of the review

Moving a refrigerator from a garage is very different from moving one down a narrow upstairs hallway. Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, second or third floor, basement, outside area or another location. Include stairs, narrow doorways, tight corners, gates and anything else that affects the path to the pickup vehicle.

Accurate access information helps us determine what equipment and staffing are needed and prevents failed pickup attempts.

4. Special appliance rules

Microwaves are accepted only when they are included with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only when they come with two or more major working appliances. These rules help keep routes focused on major household appliances that can be handled efficiently.

5. Scheduling depends on active coverage

Even when an appliance is a good candidate, free pickup still depends on an active route, current demand and scheduling capacity. Coverage is organized through state and regional service hubs, with a small number of core-market pages where we have established operations. Submit your exact city and ZIP code so the request can be matched to the appropriate route before pickup is confirmed.

6. Wait for an explicit pickup confirmation

Submitting the form, calling or texting begins the review; it does not reserve a truck or create a confirmed appointment. The team may request additional photos, measurements, model information or access details before deciding whether the appliance and location can be accepted.

If the request qualifies and coverage is available, the pickup details must be confirmed directly. Keep your contact information accurate and report any change in appliance condition, address, stairs or removal access before the scheduled arrival.

Three possible outcomes: the request may be accepted for scheduling, held while local coverage is checked, or declined when condition, access, distance or capacity does not meet the current pickup requirements.

Multiple Appliances, Rentals and Business Requests

Homeowners, renters, landlords, property managers and businesses can submit more than one appliance in the same request. List every unit separately, including its condition and location within the property. For apartment turnovers or repeated property-management work, include the number of units and whether appliances are spread across multiple addresses.

A complete load description is important because mixed-load qualification is based on the major appliances being reviewed together. Do not add an undisclosed nonworking appliance after the route has been approved.

Before the pickup

Remove food and personal items, clear the path, and make sure pets and children are away from the moving area. Water, electrical and gas connections should be handled safely. Do not disconnect gas fittings unless you are qualified to do so. If you were asked to keep a working refrigerator or freezer plugged in so its condition can be confirmed, keep it operating until the appropriate time.
