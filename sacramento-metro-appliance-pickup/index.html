Appliances We Review

Washers & Dryers

Working laundry equipment is a priority category. Complete sets are especially useful, and single working machines can also be reviewed.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Include cooling condition, interior/exterior photos, missing shelves or drawers and whether the unit is currently plugged in.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Identify gas or electric service and describe burner, oven, control-panel, glass-top or ignition problems.

Qualification: fully working major appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. A nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify.

Why Sacramento Is a Strong Reuse & Resale Market

Working appliances do not always need to enter the disposal stream. Sacramento metro requests can be reviewed for local reuse, repair, refurbishment or resale when a suitable buyer, reseller, repair business or pickup partner is available.

That makes condition details important. A working washer, dryer, refrigerator or range with clear photos and tested functions can be more useful to a local operator than an appliance described only as “old.”

Sacramento Public Appliance Options & Multifamily Rules

Our service is a qualification-based reuse and pickup network, not the municipal collection system. City of Sacramento residential customers may also have access to the City's appliance and e-waste appointment program. Sacramento County customers in eligible areas have separate bulky-waste and appliance drop-off options.

City of Sacramento

The City currently states that eligible residential collection customers can schedule two appliance/e-waste appointments per calendar year, with up to two appliances collected per appointment.

Sacramento County

County residential customers may have bulky-waste pickup and free appliance drop-off options, subject to current service-area rules and limits.

Multifamily Properties

Large multifamily properties have separate landlord/property-manager and commercial-hauler requirements, which is why recurring apartment turnover requests are a useful fit for our property-manager and partner network.
