Qualification Rules

Fully working appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of major appliances work. Minor repair needs may be considered. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Anthem Gated Community & North Valley Pickup Planning

Anthem appliance requests often come from planned neighborhoods where gate access, community rules and garage placement can affect the pickup. Include the exact address, community or gate instructions, driveway access and whether the appliance is in the garage or still inside the home. Photos of the appliance and removal path help with a faster decision.

For refrigerators, confirm cooling and whether shelves and doors are complete. For laundry sets, report washer fill/drain/spin and dryer tumble/heat. Property managers, senior households and homeowners replacing appliances during remodels can submit multiple items together, which may fit a north-Valley route more efficiently than separate single-item requests.
