Arizona Pickup Requests

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens. Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Free pickup is confirmed only after condition, location, access, demand and available route or partner coverage are reviewed.

Send useful details: city/ZIP, appliance type, working condition, photos, floor level, stairs and access restrictions.

Primary Arizona Region

Phoenix Metro

Phoenix, Mesa, Chandler, Scottsdale, Glendale, Tempe, Gilbert, Peoria and surrounding Valley communities through one regional hub.

Other Arizona Requests

Requests outside Phoenix metro may still be reviewed when service or partner coverage is available. Listing a location does not guarantee free pickup.

Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance alone generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Condition and Access

For washers include fill, drain and spin status. For dryers include heat/tumble and gas or electric. For refrigerators and freezers include cooling status and clear interior/exterior photos.

Tell us whether appliances are in a garage, driveway, apartment, upper floor or gated property and report stairs, elevators, narrow doors and other removal restrictions.

Phoenix Valley Pickup Planning

Appliance requests can involve very different access conditions across Phoenix, Mesa, Chandler, Scottsdale, Glendale, Tempe, Gilbert and Peoria. Include the exact city and ZIP code so the request can be matched to the correct part of the Valley instead of relying only on “Phoenix metro.”

Homes and garages

Report whether the appliance is inside the home, in an attached garage or already outside. Mention gravel, steep driveways, side gates, long carries and any required gate code.

Apartments and communities

Include the floor, elevator availability, stair count, building access, loading rules and property-manager requirements before scheduling.

Arizona heat

Do not place a working refrigerator, freezer or electronic appliance in direct sun before pickup is confirmed. Explain whether it is currently plugged in and operating in an indoor or cooled location.

Photos and Details That Improve Review

Send clear front, side and interior photos along with a model-label photo when available. For large refrigerators, freezers and ranges, include approximate width, depth and height. Tell us whether refrigerator shelves and drawers are complete and whether a range is gas or electric.

When submitting multiple appliances, list the condition of each one separately. A working washer does not automatically qualify a nonworking refrigerator, but the complete load may be reviewed under the approximately 80% working guideline.

Before disconnecting: confirm that washers drain and spin, dryers tumble and heat, and refrigerators or freezers hold temperature. Accurate operating details help avoid delays and unnecessary trips.

Requests Beyond Phoenix Metro

Tucson, Prescott, Flagstaff, Yuma and other Arizona communities are not represented as established local routes on this page. Customers may submit those locations for partner-availability review, but pickup must be confirmed for the exact address.

Distance, appliance value, reusable condition, property access and current local capacity can affect whether free pickup is available. If a request cannot be accepted, review the appliance disposal alternatives before paying for or arranging another removal option.
