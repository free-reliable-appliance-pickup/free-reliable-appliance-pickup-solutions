Arizona Appliance Types and Qualification

Major appliances reviewed include refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves, ranges and ovens. Working and reusable appliances are the strongest candidates for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when most major appliances are working. Microwaves require another qualifying major appliance, and dishwashers require at least two other major working appliances.

Arizona Appliance Pickup Questions

Is appliance pickup automatically free everywhere in Arizona?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based and depends on appliance condition, access and current route availability.

Which page should I use if I live in Phoenix?

Use the dedicated Phoenix appliance pickup page. Use the Metro hub for an address in another Valley city.

Can I submit several appliances?

Yes. List every appliance and its condition so the full load can be reviewed together.
