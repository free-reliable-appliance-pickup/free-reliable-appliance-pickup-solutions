What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Send the appliance condition, city or ZIP code, photos and access details. Pickup must be confirmed based on the load, location, access and available coverage before scheduling.

Rancho Mirage Appliance Disposal Alternative

Rancho Mirage uses Burrtec for municipal solid-waste and recycling services. City planning documents identify bulky-item pickup, electronic-waste service, household-hazardous-waste service and used-motor-oil programs among the residential services available through the City's waste contractor.

That municipal option is different from our reuse-focused appliance pickup. If your refrigerator, washer, dryer, stove or freezer is still working or otherwise qualifies, send us the condition and access details first. If it does not qualify, Rancho Mirage residents can check Burrtec's current bulky-item requirements and appointment availability instead of leaving the appliance unscheduled at the curb.

Rancho Mirage's General Plan also identifies the Edom Hill Transfer Station in nearby Cathedral City as the transfer facility used for much of the area's solid waste. Residents considering self-haul should verify current public access, fees and accepted materials directly with the facility before transporting an appliance.
