Free Appliance Pickup in San Clemente: What Qualifies

Use this page for major-appliance pickup and removal requests at a San Clemente address. Qualifying working or reusable refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves, ranges and ovens may receive free pickup after condition, exact location, removal access, local demand and current route availability are reviewed.

Fully working appliances are the strongest candidates. A mixed load may qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances work. A single completely nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for our reuse-focused free route.

For a washer, dryer or complete laundry set only, use the San Clemente washer & dryer pickup page. For a refrigerator, freezer, stove, range, oven or mixed major-appliance load, continue here.

San Clemente Bulky-Item & White-Goods Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused route, the City of San Clemente's solid-waste guide describes a separate CR&R bulky-item service. The current guide states that residential customers receive four bulky-item pickups free of charge per calendar year.

The same guide treats household appliances containing refrigerant as white goods and says CR&R collects bulky items and white goods from the curbside. Refrigerators require special handling because of refrigerant and other chemicals. Confirm current scheduling and preparation rules directly before placing an appliance outside.

San Clemente Appliance Removal FAQ

Is appliance pickup automatically free in San Clemente?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based. We review condition, exact address, safe access, local demand and route availability before confirming service.

Can one working appliance qualify?

Yes. One working refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer, stove, range or oven can be submitted for review.

Can you remove an appliance from a condo or upstairs unit?

Those requests can be reviewed. Include floor level, stairs or elevator, hallway/door clearance, parking/loading rules and any HOA or building-access requirements.

What if the appliance is broken?

A single completely nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for our free reuse-focused route. The City/CR&R bulky-item and white-goods option above may be a better fit for end-of-life units.

Do you handle several appliances from a property turnover?

Yes. List every appliance and condition together so the complete load can be reviewed efficiently.
