San Clemente Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

San Clemente removal requests can involve coastal hills, condos, garages, narrow residential streets and properties where parking or carrying distance affects the route. We review exact access before confirming pickup.

Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Pickup must be confirmed. A submitted request starts review; it does not guarantee acceptance or a particular pickup time.

Major Appliances We Review

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the unit is plugged in and holding temperature. Send exterior, interior and model-label photos when possible.

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether the drum turns and heats.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and describe burner, oven and control operation.

Multiple Appliances

List every appliance and condition together when several items are at the same property.

San Clemente ZIP Areas

City records and facilities use ZIP codes 92672 and 92673. Those two ZIP areas cover different parts of San Clemente, so the exact pickup address and access path are important for routing.

For inside refrigerators or stacked laundry, describe stairs, hallway turns and whether doors or machines need to be disconnected before removal.

San Clemente Appliance Condition Review

Access Details That Matter

Include steep or sloped access, condo stairs or elevators, garage location, narrow streets, parking restrictions and carrying distance.

Do not move a heavy appliance to the curb or into a hallway just because a request was submitted. Keep it safely located until pickup is reviewed and confirmed.

San Clemente Appliance Removal FAQ

Do you offer appliance removal near me?

Yes. Use the exact San Clemente address or ZIP so the route can be reviewed accurately.

Can one broken appliance qualify?

A single completely nonworking appliance generally does not qualify. Minor-repair units and qualifying mixed loads may still be reviewed.

Do photos help?

Yes. Clear appliance and access photos can speed review and help prevent failed trips.
