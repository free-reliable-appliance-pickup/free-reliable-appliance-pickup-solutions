Questions

Common questions from buyers

What will I see when an opportunity is offered?

Before committing, qualified buyers can review relevant appliance details, general location, reported condition, access needs and proposed business terms. We share specific private pickup details only when a lawful arrangement and necessary permissions are confirmed. We do not sell homeowners' contact information.

Do I need an appliance store to join?

No storefront is required to submit your interest. We welcome independent buyers, Marketplace/OfferUp resellers and established stores. You remain responsible for any legal requirements applicable to your transactions and location.

Do you have inventory available right now?

This page collects buyer interest and does not display live inventory. Specific opportunities will only be shared once they are verified and authorized; supply, condition and pickup arrangements are not guaranteed.

Can I pick up an appliance directly from a homeowner?

Only after the necessary homeowner and lead-source permissions are confirmed and an authorized pickup assignment is made. We do not sell or publicly disclose customer contact information.

How do payments work?

Registration is free. For any approved appliance sale or service arrangement, we agree privately on the price, what is included, responsibilities, and any applicable payment and refund terms before proceeding.

Will you cover Sacramento, Eugene or Seattle?

These are priority recruiting areas. We are building local buyer and pickup capacity; submitting an interest form does not guarantee available inventory or assignments.
