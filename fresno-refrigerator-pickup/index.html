Fresno Refrigerator Pickup Is a Separate Search Need

Refrigerators are heavier and more difficult to dispose of than many other household items, and Fresno's public bulky-item guidance treats refrigerators and freezers differently from common furniture and appliances. This page is specifically for Fresno refrigerator and fridge pickup requests that may fit our reuse-focused route.

For washers and dryers, use the Fresno washer & dryer pickup page. For mixed loads, stoves, ranges, ovens or several appliance types, use the Fresno appliance pickup page.

What Helps a Fresno Refrigerator Qualify?

Cooling Condition

Tell us whether the refrigerator section stays cold and whether the freezer section actually freezes. Working and reusable units receive the strongest priority.

Clear Photos

Send front, side and interior photos plus the model label when available. Show shelves, drawers, door bins and any visible damage.

Complete Appliance

Disclose missing shelves, broken handles, damaged seals, dispenser problems, heavy rust, strong odor or other defects before pickup is considered.

Practical Access

Include floor level, stairs, doorway width, tight turns, gates, elevators, parking or loading limits and carrying distance.

Important: a single nonworking refrigerator generally does not qualify for free pickup. Do not describe a nonworking unit as working; accurate condition saves unnecessary trips.

Refrigerator Types We Review in Fresno

Top-Freezer Refrigerators

Standard top-freezer models can be useful when cooling correctly, complete and reasonably clean.

Side-by-Side Refrigerators

Send width and doorway information because larger side-by-side models can require extra clearance during removal.

French-Door Refrigerators

Include approximate dimensions, cooling condition and whether the doors or drawers may need to be removed to clear the exit path.

Bottom-Freezer Models

Tell us whether both sections hold temperature and whether freezer drawers, baskets and refrigerator shelves are complete.

Fresno Refrigerator Removal Access

Tell us where the refrigerator is located before pickup is confirmed: kitchen, garage, apartment, upstairs unit, storage area or business. Photos of narrow doors, hallway turns, stairs or gates are useful when clearance is tight.

If the refrigerator has a connected water line or ice maker, disclose that in the request. Do not cut refrigerant lines or attempt sealed-system work. Removal and disposal of refrigerant must be handled through appropriate procedures.

Why Refrigerators Need a Different Fresno Disposal Plan

The City of Fresno's current Operation Clean Up guidance says furniture and appliances may be collected but specifically excludes refrigerators and freezers. The City's One-Time Bulky Item Pickup page also lists refrigerators and freezers among items not accepted. Those programs are separate from our service, but the distinction is useful when deciding how to handle an unwanted cooling appliance.

Fresno Refrigerator Pickup FAQ

Is refrigerator pickup free in Fresno?

Qualifying working or reusable refrigerators may receive free pickup after condition, location, access, local demand and route availability are reviewed.

Do you pick up a broken refrigerator for free?

A single nonworking refrigerator generally does not qualify for free pickup. Send the actual condition so we can give you a clear answer before scheduling.

Should I keep the refrigerator plugged in?

When practical and safe, keeping a working refrigerator plugged in can help confirm current cooling condition. Remove food and personal items before pickup.

What photos should I send?

Send the front, interior, sides when useful, model label if visible, and any damage. If the exit is tight, include a photo of the doorway or route out.
