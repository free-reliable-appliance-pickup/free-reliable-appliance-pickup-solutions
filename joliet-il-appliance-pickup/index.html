What makes a Joliet pickup request different?

Joliet has older residential blocks, apartments and newer subdivisions with different appliance access. An appliance from a basement utility room can require more lifting than one already in a ground-level garage. Requesters should record the true Joliet address rather than assuming nearby Plainfield, Crest Hill or Lockport shares one route. Those are separate availability checks.

Condition details that help decide whether we can assist

A dryer that tumbles but does not heat, a refrigerator with poor freezer cooling, and a range missing knobs should be described accurately—not simply marked 'working.' Give the quantity, approximate model and visible problems for each item. Working major appliances are preferred, while broken items may be considered as part of mixed loads.

We focus on reusable, working major appliances. A nonworking appliance is considered individually and is not automatically eligible for free removal. Dishwasher requests require two other qualifying working major appliances, and microwaves are not normally standalone pickups.

Stairs, disconnection and loading

Mention which floor, the number and shape of stair flights, porch steps, garage-door clearance, driveway access and any gate or parking restrictions. If a landlord is clearing multiple apartments, specify whether items are at one location and whether management has authorized release.

Do not set out a refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer or range for us before an appointment is expressly confirmed. Cooling equipment must be handled without venting refrigerants, and connected gas appliances require qualified disconnection.

Joliet's separate city waste-service contact

The City of Joliet garbage and recycling guidance identifies Waste Management as its contracted residential collector and lists 815-280-7854 for collection questions and special-service arrangements. Ordinary cart collection does not establish eligibility for large appliances. If our reuse-oriented pickup request cannot be approved, ask Waste Management whether your specific refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer or stove is eligible, what preparation is required and whether an additional fee applies. Do not place the machine curbside based only on this page. City collection is independent from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.

For a multi-appliance Joliet request, list how many units work, whether an apartment building has elevator or loading-dock restrictions, and whether a stove still needs professional gas disconnection. Those details help evaluate whether a reusable load can be accepted; no Joliet partner or route has been confirmed.
