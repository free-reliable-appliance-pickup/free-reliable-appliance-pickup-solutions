Vernon Business & Loading Access Details

Vernon pickup requests often need more access detail than a typical curbside residential request because an appliance may be inside a commercial building, warehouse, break room, rental unit or secured property. When submitting a request, identify whether a loading dock, roll-up door, freight elevator, alley, gated lot or appointment window is involved. Also note whether the appliance can be tested where it sits and whether facility staff will be available to provide access.

For refrigerators and freezers, include interior and exterior photos plus cooling status. For washers and dryers, include the tested cycle functions and whether the dryer is gas or electric. For ranges, include fuel type and operating condition. These details help separate a reusable appliance pickup from a disposal-only request and reduce delays caused by arriving without the right access information.
