What Helps a Washer or Dryer Request Qualify?

Working condition

Working machines receive the strongest consideration, especially complete washer and dryer sets.

Clear photos

Photos of the full machine, controls, drum or tub and model label help speed up review.

Accurate testing

For washers, report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, report gas or electric type, tumble and heat.

Easy access

Floor level, stairs, elevators, gates, narrow doors, parking and carrying distance can affect whether a route can accept the stop.

What Washer Condition Should I Report?

Tell us whether the washer powers on, fills, washes or agitates, drains and reaches the final spin. Mention leaking, loud bearings, error codes, a damaged lid or door lock, water remaining in the tub, a torn front-load gasket or a machine that stops during the cycle.

Clear photos of the front, controls, drum and model label help appliance buyers and repair-minded providers make faster decisions.

What Dryer Condition Should I Report?

Tell us whether the dryer powers on, tumbles and heats. Identify gas or electric when known. Mention no-heat problems, unusual noise, damaged controls, a door problem, long drying time or a dryer that shuts off early.

Do not disconnect a gas dryer unless you are qualified to do so. Tell the pickup provider if the unit is still connected, stacked or installed inside a tight closet.

Working, Repairable & Nonworking Machines

Working washers and dryers receive the strongest priority across California. Complete working sets can be especially attractive because both machines can be handled at one stop.

Repairable or nonworking machines are market-specific. Some repair technicians and appliance buyers may want a machine with a common fix, but do not assume every broken appliance qualifies. The Fresno-area route is especially repair-focused; other California markets are reviewed according to current buyer and provider demand.

Free pickup is never automatic. Appliance condition, completeness, exact location, access, demand and current route capacity are reviewed before scheduling.

California Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Is washer and dryer pickup free in California?

It can be free for qualifying requests where local coverage and demand are available. The exact machine, condition, city or ZIP code and access details determine whether a pickup can be confirmed.

Can I submit only one washer?

Yes. A single working washer can be reviewed. Some repair-focused buyers may also consider a washer needing repair.

Can I submit only one dryer?

Yes. Gas or electric dryers can be reviewed. Tell us whether the drum turns and whether the machine heats.

What is the main San Gabriel Valley to Inland Empire corridor?

It starts around La Crescenta and La Cañada Flintridge, runs through Pasadena, South Pasadena, San Marino, Altadena, Sierra Madre, Arcadia, Monrovia, Duarte, Azusa, Glendora, Covina, West Covina, San Dimas, La Verne, Claremont, Pomona and Montclair, then continues through Upland, Rancho Cucamonga and Fontana to San Bernardino.

Why are washers and dryers a major focus?

They can attract demand from appliance buyers, repair technicians, resellers, small appliance businesses and pickup professionals, not just one type of partner.
