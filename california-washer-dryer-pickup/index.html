What Washer Condition Should I Report?

Tell us whether the washer powers on, fills, washes or agitates, drains and reaches the final spin. Mention leaking, loud bearings, error codes, a damaged lid or door lock, water remaining in the tub or a machine that stops during the cycle.

For a nonworking Fresno-area washer, describe the failure as accurately as possible and include clear front, control-panel and model-label photos. A repair-minded partner can often make a faster decision with complete information.

What Dryer Condition Should I Report?

Tell us whether the dryer powers on, tumbles and heats. Identify gas or electric when known. Mention no-heat problems, unusual noise, damaged controls, a door problem or a dryer that runs but does not dry correctly.

Do not disconnect a gas dryer unless you are qualified to do so. Tell the pickup partner if the appliance is still connected, stacked or installed in a tight closet.

Working, Repairable & Nonworking Machines

Working washers and dryers receive the strongest priority across the network. Complete working sets can be especially attractive because both machines can be handled at one stop.

Repairable or nonworking machines are market-specific. Do not assume a broken appliance automatically qualifies. The Fresno-area route is unusual because the local partner repairs laundry appliances and can review some units that other routes would decline.

Free pickup is never automatic. Appliance condition, completeness, exact location, access, demand and current route capacity are reviewed before scheduling.

California Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Is washer and dryer pickup free in California?

It can be free for qualifying requests where local partner coverage is available. The exact appliance, condition, city or ZIP code and access details determine whether a pickup can be confirmed.

Can I submit only one washer?

Yes. A single working washer can be reviewed. Some repair-focused routes may also consider a washer needing repair.

Can I submit only one dryer?

Yes. Gas or electric dryers can be reviewed. Tell us whether the drum turns and whether the machine heats.

Which California cities have the strongest dedicated repair route?

Fresno, Clovis, Fowler, Sanger and Selma currently share a dedicated washer-and-dryer partner who can review some repairable or nonworking machines.
