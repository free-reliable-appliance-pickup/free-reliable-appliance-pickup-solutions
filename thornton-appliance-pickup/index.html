Thornton Appliance Pickup for Qualifying Loads

Thornton requests are reviewed within our northern Denver-metro coverage. We focus on appliances that are working or useful enough to support a free pickup route. The fastest reviews include accurate condition details, a complete list of appliances and clear access notes.

Free pickup is not automatic. Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single appliance that does not work does not qualify for free pickup.

Describe the Appliance Before We Route It

Washers

Tell us whether the washer fills, agitates or tumbles, drains and spins. Include leaks or error messages rather than simply saying it is “good.”

Dryers

Tell us whether the drum turns and the machine heats. If it is part of a laundry pair, submit both machines together.

Refrigerators & freezers

Cooling performance matters. Include exterior and interior photos and say whether the unit maintains temperature.

Stoves & ranges

These may qualify when the working condition and access are clear. Include the appliance type and any known functional issue.

Garage, Basement & Rental Cleanout Details

A garage pickup with clear driveway access is different from a basement or apartment removal. Tell us exactly where the appliance sits now. If stairs, narrow doors, elevators, gates or parking restrictions are involved, put them in the request before a pickup time is offered.

Property managers can submit multiple appliances in one request. List every item and condition individually. That helps us evaluate whether the overall load meets the working-appliance threshold and whether a larger route makes sense.

Category Limits

Microwaves are accepted only when included with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Unknown-condition items may need additional photos or testing information before approval.

Do not rely on the form alone as confirmation. The Colorado team reviews the request and confirms the pickup window only after the route and load are accepted.

Thornton's City Bulky-Item Option

If an appliance does not qualify for our service, Thornton residents can review the City's Click & Haul program. The City lists appliances among eligible bulky items and publishes current appointment, pricing and placement rules. Thornton also publishes separate appliance drop-off information for certain items. Thornton Pickup FAQs

Can a working chest freezer qualify?

Yes. Working freezers are an important Colorado category. Tell us its size, cooling condition and location.

Can I request pickup from a rental property?

Yes. Property owners and managers can submit appliances left after a move-out or replacement project, subject to the same qualification rules.
