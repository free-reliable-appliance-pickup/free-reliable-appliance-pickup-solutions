Riverside Stove, Range & Oven Pickup Review

Riverside stove and range requests span a broad city route, so send the exact Riverside ZIP plus the removal path instead of only the appliance type. Homes, apartments, rentals and larger properties can have very different stairs, gates, driveway, alley and parking conditions.

Removal path

Include floor level, stairs, gates, alley or driveway access, parking distance and whether the appliance is safely disconnected.

Riverside Local Disposal Alternative

For City of Riverside residents, the current Public Works bulky-item program is a separate backup when a stove does not qualify for our reuse-focused route. The City currently allows two bulky-item appointments per year and up to five items per appointment.

Riverside specifically lists stoves, washers and dryers as accepted curbside appliances. Refrigerators and freezers containing refrigerant are handled differently and are directed to separate programs.

Riverside Stove, Range & Oven Pickup FAQ

Can a working stove or range qualify for free pickup in Riverside?

Yes. We review working and reusable units by condition, exact Riverside location, access and current route availability.

What should I send?

Send gas/electric type, burner and oven condition, photos, Riverside ZIP, floor level, stairs, gate or driveway access and disconnection status.

Does Riverside accept stoves through city bulky pickup?

The City's current Public Works page lists stoves among accepted curbside appliances and sets annual appointment and per-appointment item limits.

Are refrigerators and freezers handled the same way?

No. Riverside directs refrigerant-containing refrigerators and freezers to separate programs.

Is our free pickup guaranteed?

No. Condition, safe access, demand and route capacity must be confirmed first.
