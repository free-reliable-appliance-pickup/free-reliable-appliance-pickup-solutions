How we assess appliance in Battle Creek

Working refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers and stoves have the highest potential for reuse. Sets and multiple mostly working machines can be easier to evaluate; nonworking or heavily damaged appliances are reviewed individually.

Preparation: List each unit separately, describe what actually functions, report the floor and access, and share inside/outside photos of the machines and carry-out path.

How the conditional pickup request works

Independent recycling and municipal disposal options

The Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE) advises checking recyclers' refrigerant-handling procedures before recycling appliances containing refrigerants. Removal for scrap or disposal must follow applicable refrigerant regulations. That state resource is separate from our unconfirmed private reuse pickup.

Check county solid-waste programs or retailer haul-away when reuse pickup is not available. Different providers have different eligibility, fee and refrigerant-handling rules; none of these alternatives is a confirmed pickup partner of this website.
