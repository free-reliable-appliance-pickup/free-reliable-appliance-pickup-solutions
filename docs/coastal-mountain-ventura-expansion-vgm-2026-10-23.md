## Conversion and customer policy

- Free pickup remains **conditional** on working/reusable appliances, multi-item working share (typically around 80%), exact location, route and access. Do not promise a day/time or list a partner as active until verified.
- Dishwasher only with at least two other major working appliances; no microwave pickup alone.
- On the original first-party Formspree request forms, optional `paid_removal_preference` is blank by default; `free_only` forbids paid offers and `quote_ok` authorizes only a separate quote for review if free pickup is unavailable. No automatic payment, appointment or external lead-sharing.
- Preserve customer consent, privacy, original phone numbers and verified hero photos. Do not claim stock images or collection photos depict pickups in the named localities.
- Recruiting independently operating pickup partners requires verified service cities, lifting equipment, license/insurance as applicable, capacity, stairs/liftgate access, permissible disposal/refrigerant procedures, speed of response and consentful handoff.

## Release and review gates for each new city owner

1. Inspect the live regional/city pages, repository source and open PRs. Check cannibalization and choose one primary page per intent/location; record existing owner and query baseline.
2. Confirm at least one genuine local operational capability or qualify as **request-only** without guaranteed availability. Obtain facts specific to local access/market, not spun city boilerplate.
3. Compare current organic competitors and actual GSC finalized search queries/impressions/CTR. Use separate washer/dryer or other specialty pages only when real query intent and unique help justify them.
4. Use verified real one-photo-per-page appliance category imagery, accurate alt text and mobile above-fold contact. Ensure required phone and approved Formspree, consent, schema, canonical, internal links, sitemap/indexability as appropriate.
5. Make one bounded PR, run targeted negative/positive regression tests and exact SHA CI, merge only green, verify deployment and public HTML, then separately monitor Google indexing, rankings and qualified leads.
6. Keep a change freeze for ranking experiments and review after enough finalized data, typically at least two weeks. No pages solely to manipulate location SERPs.
