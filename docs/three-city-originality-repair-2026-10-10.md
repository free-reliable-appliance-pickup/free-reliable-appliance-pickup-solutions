# Three-city originality and factual accuracy repair — October 10, 2026

## Evidence and scope

A normalized text five-word overlap check (remove head, scripts, nav, footer, forms, and city names; compare unique 5-grams using Sørensen–Dice) measured the following original city-page overlap:

| City pair | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Commerce / South Gate | 59.1% | 32.7% |
| Commerce / Huntington Park | 58.8% | 34.1% |
| South Gate / Huntington Park | 59.1% | 32.7% |

These are **internal QA similarity measures, not Google's scores** and not evidence of a Google penalty. This targeted edit replaces one highly duplicated appliance-qualification grid and the short city-disposal section on each page with genuinely city-specific, researched useful customer guidance. No new location URLs or doorway pages created.

## Official sources verified
- City of Commerce Waste Collection: https://www.commerceca.gov/city-hall/public-works/environmental-services/residential-trash-recycling-collection — names Athens Services for municipal residential hauling, separate residential eligibility caveat for post-2002 development, advance notice, curb collection and appliances eligible.
- City of South Gate Bulky Item Pickup: https://www.cityofsouthgate.org/Government/Departments/Public-Works/Maintenance-Services/Bulky-Item-Pickup — Universal Waste Systems, up to 10 free monthly residential/multifamily bulky items, separate business pricing and collection-day instructions.
- City of Huntington Park Residential Trash/Recycling Collection: https://www.hpca.gov/145/Residential-Trash-Recycling-Collection — CR&R, limited resident categories, up to four items per weekly collection and weight/size limits. **Corrects obsolete/inaccurate assertion on site that Valley Vista was the city provider.**

## Safeguards
- All three pages have no running protected SEO experiment as of Oct 10.
- Preserve each original title, canonical, schema, form destination, city 909 phone/text routing, hero imagery, appliance acceptance rules and internal service links.
- Preserve explicit 80% mostly-working mixed-load limit; dishwashers only with two or more other working major appliances; no microwaves alone. Never imply that our private pickup service has the city's collection rules, or vice versa.
- Do not assume this edit immediately improves organic ranking. Monitor Search Console index / actual query-to-page impressions, clicks, city-specific rank and qualified leads. Google must recrawl/reassess.
- Focus future revisions on *documented original city facts and on-site operational experience*, not adding hundreds of thin pages or swapping city names.

## Root cause hypothesis in progress
- The business has an extensive network of closely similar city and appliance pages and relatively new Google exposure, while tracked exact-city organic results are often weak.
- Some query-to-page overlap is observed in Search Console: eleven different URLs surfaced for general Upland keyword and six for general Fontana keyword. The only valid diagnosis is that ranking signals/page intent are not yet concentrated. A sitewide algorithmic penalty is **not** established by these data.
- Four high-value pages (Pomona, Covina, San Dimas, La Verne) showed 44–45% overlap in the same QA test, but their active Oct 14 experiments must be evaluated before speculative rewriting. Preserve the observation baseline.
