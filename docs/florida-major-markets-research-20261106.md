## Market signals and route-study boundaries

Florida 2025 census estimates and new housing construction point to population density in Miami/Fort Lauderdale/West Palm Beach, Tampa Bay, Orlando and Jacksonville, with separate growth corridors in St. Johns, Osceola, Polk, Lee, Sarasota, St. Lucie and Brevard counties. Used and refurbished appliance retail is present across the major metros, a potential partner-search signal but **not direct proof of pickup demand or available operators**.

### Miami, Fort Lauderdale & West Palm Beach

Use separate Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach dispatch clusters, roughly 20–30 miles from each confirmed operator; do not imply one Miami pickup vehicle covers the entire coast.

Pilot cities: Miami, Hialeah, Doral, Miami Gardens, Homestead, Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Pompano Beach, Coral Springs, West Palm Beach, Boca Raton, Boynton Beach, Delray Beach.

### Tampa Bay and surrounding cities

Review Tampa/Hillsborough at approximately 25–30 miles and Pinellas/St. Petersburg/Clearwater separately at approximately 15–25 miles, accounting for bay bridges and drive time. Wesley Chapel and Plant City require route-specific review.

Pilot cities: Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel, Plant City.

### Orlando, Kissimmee and growth suburbs

Use 25–35 miles around each confirmed Orlando/Orange-Seminole-Osceola operator; evaluate St. Cloud and Clermont as outer growth clusters, not assumed free routes.

Pilot cities: Orlando, Kissimmee, Sanford, Apopka, Winter Garden, St. Cloud, Clermont, Altamonte Springs.

### Jacksonville and St. Johns growth corridor

Start Jacksonville with an approximately 25–35-mile operator review zone. Treat St. Augustine and Palm Coast as separate growth markets when local route travel is impractical.

Pilot cities: Jacksonville, Orange Park, Jacksonville Beach, St. Augustine, Palm Coast.

### Tallahassee and nearby counties

Start with an approximately 25–30-mile research area around Tallahassee, with rural, Gadsden and Wakulla requests reviewed separately by actual drive/access.

Pilot cities: Tallahassee, Quincy, Crawfordville.

### Lakeland and Polk County housing corridor

Use separate 20–30-mile Lakeland and Winter Haven/Haines City route studies; avoid assuming all of Polk County has an active free pickup partner.

Pilot cities: Lakeland, Winter Haven, Haines City.

### Port St. Lucie growth market

Evaluate a 20–30-mile local route around Port St. Lucie; Fort Pierce and Stuart need individual route checks.

Pilot cities: Port St. Lucie.

### Cape Coral, Fort Myers, Sarasota and North Port

Use local 20–30-mile clusters for Cape Coral–Fort Myers and Sarasota–Bradenton, with North Port as its own growth-zone review. Bridges, water and county boundaries make a single radius misleading.

Pilot cities: Cape Coral, Fort Myers, Sarasota, Bradenton, North Port.

### Palm Bay housing growth market

Review a local 20–30-mile Palm Bay–Melbourne area; do not imply coverage to Orlando or every Brevard coastal location.

Pilot cities: Palm Bay.
