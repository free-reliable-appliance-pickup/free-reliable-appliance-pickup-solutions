# Appliance Opportunity Marketplace Blueprint

## Core promise
We do not sell raw phone numbers. We organize, qualify and distribute local appliance opportunities.

## Customer flow
1. Customer submits appliance details and multiple photos.
2. Each appliance is recorded separately when multiple machines are present.
3. System classifies residential/commercial, owner-reported condition, visual quality, completeness, access and demand.
4. Customer receives an immediate confirmation email.
5. Coverage-aware messaging sets expectations: strong coverage can use a short response window; uncovered areas must not promise contact.
6. When a provider accepts/purchases the opportunity, the customer receives an assigned-provider notification.
7. Pickup completion is confirmed and recorded.

## Photo qualification
Photos should be uploaded from the customer's phone. The form should request detailed views depending on appliance type.

Refrigerator examples: exterior, refrigerator interior, freezer interior, shelves/drawers/bins, controls/display, model label, damage/missing parts.
Washer/dryer examples: exterior, drum/tub, controls, model label, hookups/back when practical, damage.
Commercial equipment examples: multiple exterior angles, interior, controls, equipment label, power/gas information when known, damage.

If an appliance is naturally still connected or powered on, customers may include that photo. Do not require reconnection or mechanical testing.

## Condition language
- Owner reports working / working when last used
- Owner reports an issue
- Not working
- Unknown

Visual condition and mechanical condition must remain separate. AI/photo analysis may assist classification but must not claim mechanical verification.

## Buyer rules
- Customer name, phone, email and exact address remain locked before payment.
- Buyers see enough non-private details to evaluate the opportunity.
- First successful payment secures an opportunity when multiple qualified buyers receive it.
- A claimed opportunity locks immediately to prevent double sale.
- Prompt customer contact is required after purchase.
- Response/contact time becomes part of buyer reliability history.

## Territory states
- Protected: existing trusted operator/partner receives the defined priority/protection.
- Open: qualified buyers can purchase individual opportunities.
- Territory Available: market is open for recruitment of an ongoing buyer/partner.

Protection is geographic and performance-based, not automatically statewide.

## Buyer progression
Individual opportunity -> repeat buyer -> wallet/credits -> priority access -> monthly territory -> premium/exclusive territory -> trusted protected provider.

## Opportunity categories
Residential working, residential repair, non-working/parts, unknown, commercial working, commercial repairable, commercial parts, premium/high-value, multi-appliance, hot opportunity.

## Urgency
Urgency must be truthful. If several qualified buyers receive the listing, tell them clearly. Do not fabricate competition or fake countdowns.

## Customer timing
When reliable coverage exists, the confirmation can ask the customer to allow a defined period (for example, up to two hours) for local provider contact. If no provider is active in the area, do not make that promise.

## Reliability data
Track buyer claims, completed pickups, contact time, cancellations, no-shows, disputes, homeowner complaints and confirmation results.

## Owner Control Center
Track at minimum:
- requests received
- qualified opportunities
- sold/claimed opportunities
- unsold/expired opportunities
- revenue
- completion rate
- buyer response time
- buyer shortages by market
- high-demand appliance categories
- commercial opportunity performance
- territory upgrade candidates
- markets with strong supply but weak buyer coverage

## Phase order
### Phase 1: Revenue foundation
Qualified opportunity records, buyer profiles, territory states, customer privacy, pay-per-opportunity preview flow, photo qualification, customer/buyer communications.

### Phase 2: Automation
Matching, timed/simultaneous alerts, first-payment lock, wallet, reliability scoring, customer confirmation, reminders, bundles, route suggestions and upgrade offers.

### Phase 3: National scale
Dynamic territory pricing, commercial-specialist networks, auto-buy rules, premium auctions for exceptional opportunities, advanced demand data, multi-user business accounts and inventory exchange.

## Security rule
Never expose Supabase secret/service-role credentials in browser code or public GitHub files. Private customer data must be accessed through server-side controls and payment/assignment authorization.
