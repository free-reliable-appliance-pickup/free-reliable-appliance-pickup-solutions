Working electric stoves and ranges

Springfield customers can submit an electric stove by itself or as part of a household appliance replacement. For Thurston and outlying McKenzie-area properties, note rural or gravel driveways, steep approaches and the distance from the appliance to a safe parking spot.

We review electric ranges with coil burners and smooth glass-top electric ranges. The oven, burner controls and cooking elements should work for the strongest chance of qualifying.

Free does not mean automatic: a single nonworking stove usually does not qualify. Send photos and condition details before planning a pickup.

What to check before requesting pickup

For coil-top ranges, report any missing or damaged heating coils and whether each burner heats normally. For smooth glass-top ranges, mention cracks, chips and known electrical faults. Let us know if the oven heats, the door shuts properly and any controls or knobs are missing.

If you have an electric range together with a working washer, dryer or refrigerator, mention every item in your request. Mixed loads are reviewed based on condition and access.

Do not attempt to move a heavy appliance alone. Do not touch damaged wiring or disconnect the stove if unsafe.
