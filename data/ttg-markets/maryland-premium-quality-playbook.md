## High-value lead intake
- Working washer/dryer **set**, front-load or recent matching pair, tested complete cycles; collect model numbers, age, rust/odor, and pictures of both fronts and drums.
- Working stainless side-by-side/French-door refrigerator with documented cooling, dimensions, ice/water status and door/access widths.
- Working gas/electric range, especially intact glass-top or stainless, with fuel type and professional disconnection condition.
- Working freestanding freezer or single laundry unit only when buyer interest and travel justify the route.
- Collect name/phone, pickup street/ZIP, condition, photo availability, floor/stairs/elevator, parking/access, measured dimensions, item count, and move-out deadline. Ask for clear outside and inside photos before allocating a truck.
- Do not promise free pickup of nonworking, built-in, contaminated, extremely heavy or upstairs equipment; separate paid quotes are optional only after explicit customer approval.
