The 38 tracked landing pages were reported to have correct sitemap/self-canonical/noindex checks, but those checks alone do **not** establish that Google indexed every URL. The true per-page indexing/Google-selected canonical status requires first-party URL Inspection or Page Indexing reports.

## Fresno ownership diagnosis
**Existing target**: `/fresno-appliance-pickup/` for general Fresno appliance pickup; `/fresno-washer-dryer-pickup/`, `/fresno-stove-oven-pickup/`, `/fresno-freezer-pickup/`, `/fresno-refrigerator-pickup/` for specific appliance type. The general Fresno page already has a self-canonical, H1, form, real photos, local municipal sources and inbound links from Central Valley and California hubs; creating another Fresno city page would be counterproductive.

**Non-owner URL appearances:** Clovis general owns tracked #1 `stove pickup fresno` and #3 `washer dryer pickup fresno` results; Sanger laundry and Fresno County also own some tracked appliance-specific variants. These can be legitimate sibling pages benefiting from broader searches. **Preserve them** until finalized, sufficiently large query-by-page data justifies a one-variable experiment. A new redirect, canonical change or mass internal-link rewrite risks sacrificing existing first-page visibility.
