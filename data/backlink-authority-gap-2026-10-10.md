**No backlinks were acquired or submitted during this research.** Do not use fake testimonials, payment-for-links, fake partner relationships, mass-automated profiles or copied competitor articles.

## Free-first legitimate opportunities (verify before submitting)
1. Accurate business/site profiles only where supported for a service-area business: Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Google Business Profile (where policy allows), relevant Facebook business presence, Yelp/Nextdoor, and local chamber listings that are genuinely free/eligible. Do not create multiple fake physical locations or claim an unverified branch in every service city.
2. Ask **real** appliance dealers, refurbishers and independent route partners to link to the relevant city pickup resource only when an actual partnership and customer benefit exist.
3. Publish genuinely useful original resources and real customer/partner field photos (with permission), clearly explaining working vs. broken appliance qualifications. Promote them to local property managers, landlords, appliance shops, recycling organizations, and neighborhood news sites if directly relevant.
4. Audit official city or county pages only for legitimate resource-list eligibility. Provide truthful eligibility and no guarantee of municipal endorsement; request inclusion only when a public resource is genuinely appropriate.
5. Track newly acquired unique relevant referring domains *and* Google Search Console clicks, city-query page ownership, valid lead submissions, and answered phone inquiries. A backlink count without resulting qualified business has low value.
