# Indexing Quality Policy

This repository prioritizes useful, people-first service pages over unlimited location-page growth.

## Indexable location rules

1. The approved indexable location list is capped at the current audited baseline of 503 entries. A new indexable location should normally replace or consolidate an existing entry rather than increase the footprint.
2. Indexable location pages must contain at least 300 core words of visible editorial/service content after shared navigation, forms, scripts, headers and footers are excluded.
3. Indexable location pages must not be near-clones. The active-content audit rejects normalized 5-word-shingle similarity of 0.65 or higher.
4. Pages must use a self-referencing canonical, index/follow robots directive, crawlable internal links, and accurate service-area wording.
5. City pages should add real user value beyond a city-name swap: useful access guidance, appliance-condition details, route context, official disposal/utility alternatives where relevant, or clearly different local property/logistics information.
6. Regional/state hubs are preferred when a separate city page would not add meaningful value.
7. Request-based or partner-supported markets must not be described as guaranteed direct service. Established local markets and request-based markets should remain clearly distinguished.
8. Do not add pages primarily to capture keyword variants. Appliance-specific pages should exist only when they provide meaningful category-specific guidance.
9. Sitemap lastmod dates should change only after significant content, structured-data or internal-link changes.
10. External links and partner mentions must be factual. Do not require backlinks, participate in link exchanges, buy ranking links, or make partnership approval conditional on a link.
