## Competitive observations
- TakeMyAppliance consistently offers a clear request → local review/matching → confirmation sequence, a short reasons-to-choose section, appliance coverage, FAQs, and nearby links. This architecture is easier to scan; **do not copy its wording**.
- TakeMyAppliance's Redlands and Rialto city pages add concrete local considerations. Several other competitor city pages rely on a much thinner recurring template. Our Colton and Monterey Park pages already provide useful alternatives or access instructions beyond those thinner competitor templates.
- Our differentiator is an honest **qualification-based** pickup policy with condition specifics and direct city phone/text routing. We should not copy the competitor's broad all-conditions-free claims, fake same-day guarantees, claims of local licensing, or unverified partner counts.
- Our title, self-canonical, H1, meta description, general service intake and local 909 number were source-checked in the current 19-page Inland Empire/SGV technical batch, with no failures on those checks. This is a source audit, not Google Search Console or index-status evidence.

## Rank-impact hypotheses, not verified causal findings
1. **Priority gap: Rialto, Redlands, Highland.** On existing pages, many similarly phrased H2 headings may create repetitive content that competes with the local access, machine qualification and simple request flow. After the experiment review gates, assess whether consolidating duplicate sections and foregrounding genuine local operating details improves finalized GSC impressions, query/page ownership, CTR and average position.
2. **Control examples: Colton and Monterey Park.** These pages are already materially tighter. Comparing them with the longer cities offers a potential within-site contrast; do not change them without performance evidence.
3. **Authority gap:** page structure alone does not measure backlinks, citations, genuine review presence, exact Google rankings, or actual qualified calls. Gather those separately before attributing the competitor's rankings to content length.
4. **Relevance gap:** keep the right city/page owner for general pickup, washer/dryer, refrigerator, and wider county intent. Avoid redirects or mass links merely to inflate counts.
