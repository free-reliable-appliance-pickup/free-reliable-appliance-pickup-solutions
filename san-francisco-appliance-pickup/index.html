San Francisco Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens for qualifying pickup in San Francisco. Working and reusable appliances receive the strongest priority. Free pickup is confirmed only after condition, exact location, access, local demand and available route coverage are reviewed.

Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify under the general free-pickup rules.

Send useful details first: appliance type, working condition, exact city or ZIP, floor level, stairs, access restrictions and clear photos.

San Francisco Appliance Pickup vs. Recology Bulky-Item Collection

San Francisco residents can compare our qualification-based appliance pickup with the City's Recology bulky-item program. Our route prioritizes useful appliances for reuse or repair, while Recology provides a separate curbside collection service for eligible residential accounts.

Our reuse-focused pickup

Working refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves and ranges can be reviewed with photos, floor level, elevator details, stairs, narrow access and loading restrictions.

Recology bulky-item service

Recology states that residential customers in buildings with five units or fewer receive two curbside bulky-item collections per year at no additional charge, while units in larger multi-family buildings receive one no-charge collection.

Large appliances accepted

Recology's accepted-item list includes residential refrigerators, washers, dryers, stoves and other kitchen appliances. The San Francisco Environment Department also recommends reuse first when an item is still usable.
