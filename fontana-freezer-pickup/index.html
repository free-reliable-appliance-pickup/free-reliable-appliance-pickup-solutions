Fontana Freezer Pickup Review

Working and reusable freezers receive priority. A single nonworking freezer does not automatically qualify for free pickup through this route.

Fontana requests commonly come from single-family homes, garages, side-yard access and newer residential areas. Tell us if the appliance is inside, in a garage, behind a gate, or requires a long carry from the home to the loading area.

Chest or upright

Tell us the freezer style, approximate size and whether the lid or door seals and closes normally.

Freezing test

Report whether the unit reaches and holds freezing temperature and whether it is currently plugged in and testable.

Weight and access

Large chest freezers can be heavy and awkward. Include stairs, gates, narrow exits and carrying distance.

Prepare the Freezer Safely

Empty the freezer and keep the sealed cooling system intact. Do not cut refrigerant lines or remove the compressor. If defrosting is needed after pickup is accepted, confirm timing first so the floor stays dry and safe.

Do not move a heavy appliance alone if doing so is unsafe. Photos of the appliance in its current location can help us evaluate the removal path first.
