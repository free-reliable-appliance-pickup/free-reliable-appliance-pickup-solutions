What qualifies for a free pickup review?

Working washers and dryers, refrigerators, freezers and stoves have priority. We also consider reuse-ready multi-appliance loads, including property-manager replacements. A single broken item may not qualify. Dishwashers and microwaves have additional restrictions.

Specify working status, ZIP, floor, stairs, long carry and any gate or loading access restrictions. Any collection is confirmed only after reviewing available local pickup or buyer partners.

Pickup questions

Does every city here have free pickup today?

No. This is an expansion-market request network; route and partner availability must be confirmed for the specific address.

Can an apartment manager submit several appliances?

Yes. List all major appliances and working status, the building floor, stairs and loading arrangements.
