Delaware Appliance Recycling and Safety Options

Delaware DNREC directs residents to check their municipal public works department or existing hauler for large-appliance collection options. The Delaware Solid Waste Authority accepts household white goods at transfer stations and landfills, potentially for a fee. Confirm the current facility rules and charges before traveling.

Refrigerators and freezers require appropriate refrigerant handling; do not dismantle sealed cooling systems. For Delaware County information, consult Delaware DNREC household waste guidance, the Delaware Solid Waste Authority and US EPA appliance disposal guidance. These agencies and programs are independent of Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.
