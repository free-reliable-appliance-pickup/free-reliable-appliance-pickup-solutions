Whittier Bulky-Item Pickup & Appliance Disposal Alternative

Whittier residents have a separate municipal option when an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused route. The City says residential and multi-family customers receive four on-call bulky-waste or e-waste pickups per year, with up to three items per pickup through the City's solid-waste service provider.

City materials list large household items such as dryers, refrigerators and washing machines as bulky-item examples. Large appliances are not permitted in the regular black trash container, and the City also states that Savage Canyon Landfill does not accept appliances.

Whittier Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is free appliance pickup guaranteed in Whittier?

No. Free pickup depends on appliance condition, exact address, safe removal access, local demand and current route availability.

Can one working appliance qualify?

Yes. A single fully working or reusable refrigerator, washer, dryer, freezer, stove or range can be submitted for review.

Can nonworking appliances be submitted?

Yes, but a single nonworking appliance normally does not qualify for free pickup. Minor-repair units and qualifying mixed loads may still be reviewed.

Do you review inside-home pickup?

Yes. Include floor level, stairs, doorway or gate limitations and other access details before scheduling.
