California Appliance Recycling & Disposal Resources

Use this guide when an appliance does not fit a reuse-focused free-pickup route, or when you want to compare official recycling, utility and local waste options.

Start With Reuse When the Appliance Still Works

A working or reusable refrigerator, washer, dryer, freezer or stove may still have useful life. Our first option is to review it for qualification-based pickup rather than sending a usable appliance directly to disposal.

Free pickup is not automatic. Condition, exact address, removal access, local demand and current route availability are reviewed first.

Official California Recycling Resources

CalRecycle — Where to Recycle

CalRecycle provides statewide recycling information and a map for locating recycling resources for items that are not handled through normal curbside service.

Open CalRecycle recycling resources

CalRecycle Facility Search

The Solid Waste Information System includes information about regulated solid-waste facilities, transfer stations and disposal sites throughout California.

California Waste Restrictions

Some products require special handling and should not be placed in ordinary trash. CalRecycle publishes statewide guidance for materials that require alternative disposal routes.

Review CalRecycle disposal guidance

Los Angeles Refrigerator & Freezer Programs

For eligible Los Angeles Department of Water and Power customers, LADWP publishes refrigerator/freezer programs with specific appliance and customer qualifications. Because utility programs can change, confirm current eligibility directly with LADWP before relying on a rebate or pickup.

Refrigerator Turn-In & Recycling

LADWP has published a refrigerator/freezer turn-in and recycling program for qualifying residential electric customers and qualifying working units.

LADWP refrigerator recycling information

Refrigerator Exchange

LADWP also publishes a refrigerator exchange program with specific age, size, working-condition and customer requirements.

LADWP refrigerator exchange information

Orange County Appliance Disposal

Orange County Waste & Recycling states that appliances are accepted at its landfills and charged normal disposal fees. Its public guidance also distinguishes appliances from electronics, which may have different collection rules.

Orange County Waste & Recycling FAQ

Check before you go. Facility hours, accepted materials, preparation requirements and fees can change. Verify current requirements with the facility or local waste provider.

Choose the Right Path by Appliance Type

Refrigerators & Freezers

Use a reuse-focused pickup when the appliance qualifies. Otherwise use a utility, municipal or qualified recycling route that can handle refrigerated appliances appropriately.

Refrigerator pickup guide · Freezer pickup guide

Washers & Dryers

Working laundry equipment and complete sets may qualify for pickup. Nonqualifying units can be compared against local bulky-item, retailer take-back or recycling programs.

Washer & dryer pickup guide

Stoves & Ranges

Working or reusable stoves/ranges may qualify for pickup. For end-of-life units, check local bulky-item, scrap-metal or disposal rules before moving the appliance.

Stove, range & oven pickup guide
