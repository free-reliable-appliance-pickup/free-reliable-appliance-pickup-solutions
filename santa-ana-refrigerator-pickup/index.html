Santa Ana Refrigerator Pickup Review

Working and reusable refrigerators receive priority. A single refrigerator that no longer cools does not automatically qualify for the free reuse-focused route.

Santa Ana refrigerator requests are reviewed by exact address, cooling condition and removal access. Apartments, duplexes, older multifamily properties, garages and single-family homes can have very different hallway, doorway, stair, elevator, parking and loading conditions, so include those details with the request.

Cooling test

Tell us whether the refrigerator and freezer sections cool normally and whether the unit is plugged in and testable.

Photos and size

Send exterior, interior and model-label photos. Include approximate width for larger French-door, side-by-side or built-in units.

Removal path

Mention stairs, gates, narrow doors, kitchen islands, interior removal and carrying distance to the loading area.
