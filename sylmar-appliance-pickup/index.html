Sylmar Appliance Pickup & Reuse Review

Working and reusable refrigerators, washers, dryers, washer/dryer sets, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority review. Free pickup is not automatic; condition, exact location, access, current demand and route availability are checked first.

For faster review: send the exact address, appliance type, tested condition and clear photos. Include stairs, gates, parking, elevator or carrying-distance details.

Major Appliances We Review

Washers & Dryers

Report washer fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether the drum turns and heats.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Include cooling status plus exterior, interior and model-label photos when possible.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and describe burner, oven and control operation.

Multiple Appliances

Mixed loads may qualify when most major appliances are working or otherwise reusable.

A single broken appliance may not qualify for free pickup. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Sylmar Access & Route Planning

Sylmar properties can include longer driveways, gates, garages and hillside-edge access. Include gate instructions, driveway access, stairs and carrying distance.

For apartments, condos, senior communities, rentals and managed properties, include unit or floor, stairs/elevator, gate or loading instructions and where the appliance is located now.

Property Managers, Landlords & Recurring Replacements

Property managers, landlords, maintenance teams and senior-community staff can submit one-time or recurring appliance replacement loads. Include item count, condition, access, staging details and expected replacement cadence.
