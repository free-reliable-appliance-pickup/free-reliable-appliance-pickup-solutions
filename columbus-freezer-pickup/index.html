About freezer pickup in Columbus

What qualifies for freezer pickup?

Working chest and upright freezers that freeze properly receive priority. Tell us the type, approximate size, and whether the freezer operates normally. If it needs repair, describe the problem and include photos so a local partner can review the request.

Include your ZIP code, floor level, stairs, and access details. Free pickup is subject to qualification and partner availability. Never cut refrigerant lines or dismantle the sealed cooling system.

How to request freezer pickup

1. Send appliance details and photos. 2. Share your Columbus-area ZIP code and access conditions. 3. We review qualification and partner coverage. 4. If accepted, a pickup time is coordinated before any visit.

Ask the confirmed pickup partner when to unplug or defrost it. Remove food before pickup.

What we review for pickup

Tell us whether the appliance powers on and performs its normal functions. For refrigerators and freezers, confirm whether they cool or freeze properly. For washers, tell us whether they fill, drain and spin; for dryers, whether the drum turns and heats. If unsure, choose Unknown in the form.

Working single appliances and multi-appliance loads may qualify. Nonworking appliances or loads containing mostly nonworking items may not qualify for free service. Dishwashers are only considered alongside at least two other major working appliances; microwaves are not accepted alone.
