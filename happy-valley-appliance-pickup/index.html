Happy Valley Appliance Removal Requests

We review major-appliance pickups from Happy Valley homes, rentals and property managers as part of the greater Portland-area network. The most useful requests give us a clear picture of what the appliance does today and how difficult it will be to remove safely.

Qualification matters: fully working appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A broken appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

What Makes a Strong Happy Valley Request?

Show the appliance

A clear exterior photo is the starting point. For refrigerators and freezers, an interior photo helps. A model-label photo can clarify size and configuration.

Describe the exit route

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, first floor, upper floor, basement or outside. Include stair flights, gates and narrow doorways.

Be precise about condition

Say whether it is fully working, needs repair or does not work. For multiple appliances, describe each item separately instead of one general condition.

Washers, Dryers, Refrigerators, Freezers & Stoves

Working washers and dryers are priority categories. For a washer, tell us whether it fills, drains and spins. For a dryer, tell us whether it heats and tumbles. For a refrigerator, tell us whether both compartments cool. For a freezer, tell us whether it holds temperature. For a stove or range, include whether it is gas or electric and which functions work.

Smaller categories have limits. We do not pick up a microwave by itself. A microwave may be included with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Planning Pickup Access in Happy Valley

Truck access and the path from the appliance to the loading area can affect whether a route is practical. Tell us about steep or long driveways, limited curb space, gated communities, apartment loading rules or any distance between the appliance and the nearest safe truck position. Do not move a heavy refrigerator or laundry machine down stairs just to make the request easier; describe the current location first.

Nearby Portland-metro requests may connect with Gresham, Portland, Clackamas-area routes and other approved coverage depending on the day. Exact scheduling is confirmed only after the address, load and access are reviewed.

Local Disposal Alternative

The City of Happy Valley identifies Clackamas County as the local garbage and recycling authority and lists franchised haulers serving the city. If an appliance does not qualify for our free service, use the City or County resources to confirm the correct hauler, accepted items and any fee before moving the appliance.

Official Happy Valley garbage & recycling information

The City also publishes information for community Dumpster Day events, where acceptance rules can differ by item and refrigerant type. Confirm the current event rules before transport.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can landlords submit several appliances?

Yes. List every appliance, unit location and working condition so the complete load can be reviewed together.

Do you remove appliances from inside?

Inside pickup may be reviewed. Stairs, doorway width and parking access should be disclosed before scheduling.

Can I submit a freezer?

Yes. Tell us whether it is an upright or chest freezer, whether it maintains temperature and where it is located.
