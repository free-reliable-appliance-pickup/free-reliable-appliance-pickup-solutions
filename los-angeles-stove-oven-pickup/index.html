Los Angeles Stove, Range & Oven Pickup Review

Working gas and electric ranges, freestanding stoves and qualifying ovens can be submitted for review. Tell us the fuel type, whether the burners or elements work, whether the oven heats normally and whether the appliance is already disconnected and accessible.

Free pickup is qualification-based. A request is reviewed for condition, completeness, exact address, access, local demand and current route availability before service is confirmed.

Gas vs. Electric Cooking Appliances

Gas Ranges

Identify the appliance as gas and report whether burners ignite and the oven heats. Tell us whether the gas connection has already been safely disconnected.

Electric Ranges

Report whether the cooktop elements and oven work and whether the unit is plug-in or hardwired. Do not perform electrical work simply to prepare a pickup request.

Dual-Fuel & Premium Ranges

For larger or premium ranges, send the brand, model label, approximate width and clear photos. Weight and doorway clearance can affect the removal plan.

Freestanding Ranges vs. Built-In Ovens

Freestanding and slide-in ranges are usually straightforward once they are safely disconnected. Built-in wall ovens, cooktops and custom installations require more information because cabinetry, hardwiring, gas connections and mounting can be involved.

We do not promise gas, electrical or cabinetry work as part of free pickup. Tell us the current installation status before scheduling. When professional disconnection or uninstallation is required, that work should be completed safely by an appropriately qualified person.

What to Test and Photograph

For the fastest review, send a full front photo, cooktop photo, oven-interior photo and model label when available. Report which burners or elements work, whether the oven reaches temperature, whether ignition and controls operate normally and any cracked glass, missing knobs, error codes or other damage.

For premium or oversized ranges, measurements and the removal path matter. Include the approximate width and describe stairs, elevator access, narrow hallways, gates, loading restrictions and parking.

Los Angeles Apartment & Property Access

Los Angeles stove and range pickups can involve apartment kitchens, narrow doorways, service elevators, gated buildings and limited curb access. Include the unit floor, stair flights, elevator requirements, service entrance, loading area and the path from the kitchen to the pickup vehicle.

For rental turnovers, list multiple appliances separately so a working range can be evaluated accurately even when other items in the unit have different conditions.

If Your Stove or Oven Does Not Qualify

If a cooking appliance does not qualify for this reuse-focused service, compare municipal, retailer, metal-recycling or other responsible disposal options for the actual Los Angeles address. LA Sanitation & Environment also provides metal-appliance service information through its official Metal Appliance Pickup program; customers should check current eligibility and accepted-item requirements directly with the City.

Los Angeles Stove & Oven Pickup Questions

Can a working range qualify for free pickup?

Yes. Working and reusable ranges may qualify after condition, access, exact address and route availability are reviewed.

Do you take gas and electric ranges?

Both can be submitted. Identify the fuel type and whether the appliance is already safely disconnected.

Can a built-in wall oven qualify?

It can be reviewed, but built-in appliances require clear installation and access details. Uninstallation, hardwiring or gas work should not be assumed to be included.

Does a dead stove qualify by itself?

Not automatically. A single nonworking appliance should use an appropriate disposal or recycling option when it does not qualify for the reuse-focused service.
