Appliance Types We Review

Appliance

Working major appliances are prioritized for reuse, especially multiple working washers, dryers, refrigerators, stoves and freezers. Non-working appliances are reviewed individually. Dishwashers are considered only with at least two other working major items.

Washer & Dryer

Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer heats and tumbles. If it is a pair, report the condition and any existing damage of each machine separately.

Refrigerator

State whether the refrigerator cools, whether the freezer compartment works, and if the water dispenser or ice maker has faults. Describe the width, height and access. Do not cut refrigerant lines.

Stove & Oven

Identify gas, electric or dual-fuel, whether the burners and oven work, and who has handled disconnection. Gas supply disconnection must be completed by qualified personnel.

Freezer

Specify upright or chest style, capacity if known, freezing condition and how far the freezer must be carried. Never puncture refrigerant tubing or sealed components.

Maine Appliance Recycling Alternatives

For independent disposal options in southern Maine, ecomaine's special-waste guidance explains that refrigerant-containing appliances may be accepted for an additional fee, subject to current terms. Your town transfer station or public works department may have a different program.

For refrigerators and freezers, follow the US EPA guidance on responsible refrigerant handling. Do not vent refrigerant, puncture cooling lines or attempt unsafe disconnections. These authorities and recyclers are independent of Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.

Before You Submit

For the most useful review, give current inside and outside appliance photos, tested working condition, quantity, accurate street address and ZIP code, and the floor or stairs. Do not assume a truck is already assigned.
