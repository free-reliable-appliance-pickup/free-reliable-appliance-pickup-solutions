What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Every request is reviewed for condition, exact city, building access, local demand and route availability before a pickup time is confirmed.

Pickup must be confirmed. Submitting the form starts a review. It does not guarantee acceptance or a particular pickup time.

Orange County Coverage by Area

Orange County is compact compared with many California counties, but pickup conditions can vary sharply between inland neighborhoods, dense apartment areas, coastal communities and South County planned communities. We use the city, ZIP code and access details to determine whether a current route can accept the request.

North Orange County

Anaheim, Fullerton, Brea, Buena Park, La Habra, Placentia and nearby communities often involve a mix of single-family homes, apartments and older multi-unit properties.

Central Orange County

Santa Ana, Orange, Tustin, Garden Grove and nearby areas can include apartments, condos, commercial properties and rental turnovers where parking or loading access matters.

Coastal Orange County

Huntington Beach, Costa Mesa, Newport Beach, Laguna Beach, Dana Point and nearby communities may require more detail about garages, narrow access, gated properties and limited curb or loading space.

South Orange County

Irvine, Lake Forest, Mission Viejo, Rancho Santa Margarita, Laguna Niguel, San Clemente and San Juan Capistrano requests are reviewed with exact community and access details.

Appliances We Review

Washers

Tell us whether the washer fills, agitates, drains and spins. Mention leaks, error codes or bearing noise.

Dryers

Identify gas or electric and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. Complete washer/dryer sets receive strong priority.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the appliance is currently plugged in and maintaining temperature. Include an interior photo when possible.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Identify gas or electric and describe whether the burners, oven and controls operate normally.

Accurate working-condition information matters. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Apartment, Condo and Gated-Community Access

Orange County requests frequently come from apartments, condos, townhomes and gated communities. Include the floor level, elevator availability, number of stair flights, gate instructions, hallway width concerns and whether there is a loading zone or easy parking near the appliance.

For a large refrigerator inside a kitchen, send a photo of the exit path if the doorway looks tight. Tell us if refrigerator doors may need to be removed for clearance. For laundry rooms, mention stacked installations, closets or narrow side clearances.

For garage or driveway pickup, tell us whether a pickup vehicle can reach the appliance directly or whether there is a long carry, slope, alley or gate.

Rental Turnovers and Property Managers

Landlords and property managers can submit multiple appliances in one request. List each item separately with its working condition and unit location. Tell us whether all appliances will be collected from one garage or whether access is required to several units.

For multi-property work, use the message field to identify which appliances are at each address. A mixed load may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work, but every load is reviewed before scheduling.

Photos and Information That Speed Up Review

Send a clear full photo of each appliance, the exact city and ZIP code, working condition and access details. For refrigerators and freezers, include an interior photo and cooling status. For washers and dryers, tell us the basic test results. A model-label photo is helpful when available.

Call or text 909-375-6685, Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM. Photos can be sent by text when that is easier than describing the appliance.

Orange County Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is pickup free everywhere in Orange County?

Free pickup is available only for qualifying requests. Condition, city, access, local demand and route availability are reviewed before service is confirmed.

Can I request pickup from an upstairs apartment?

Yes, but include the floor, stair flights, elevator information and any narrow hallways or building rules so access can be reviewed.

Does a broken appliance qualify?

A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

What should I send for a refrigerator?

Send a full exterior photo, an interior photo when possible, the model label if available, and tell us whether the refrigerator is currently plugged in and cooling.
