What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Pickup must be confirmed. Condition, access, local demand, route coverage and scheduling are reviewed before a pickup time is accepted.

Appliances We Review

Washers & Dryers

Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets receive priority. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the appliance currently maintains temperature. Interior and model-label photos are helpful when available.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Identify whether the appliance is gas or electric and describe any burners or oven functions that do not work.

Photos and accurate condition details help us review requests faster.

Access and Pickup Details

For apartments and multi-unit properties, include the floor, elevator availability, stair flights and narrow hallways. For garage or driveway pickups, tell us whether the appliance can be reached directly from the street or parking area. For gated communities, include entry and loading information.

Landlords and property managers can list multiple appliances in one request and describe the condition of each item.
