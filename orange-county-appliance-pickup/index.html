What Qualifies for Free Appliance Pickup

Qualifying working or reusable refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens may receive free pickup after review. A single fully working major appliance may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. A nonworking appliance by itself does not automatically qualify for free pickup.

For washer- or dryer-only requests, use the Orange County washer and dryer pickup page.

Orange County Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is pickup free everywhere in Orange County?

Free pickup is available only after the appliance or load qualifies and the local route is confirmed. Working and reusable major appliances receive priority, and availability can vary by city and current route.

Do you offer appliance removal near me?

Use the city directory above to open the page matching the actual Orange County pickup address, or submit the county form if you are unsure which local page applies.

Can I request pickup from an upstairs apartment?

Yes. Include the floor, stair count, elevator availability, hallway or doorway restrictions and parking or loading details so access can be reviewed before pickup is confirmed.

Does a broken appliance qualify?

A nonworking appliance by itself does not automatically qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work, and minor repair needs can be reviewed case by case.
