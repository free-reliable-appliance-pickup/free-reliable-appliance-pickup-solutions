What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Send the appliance condition, city or ZIP code, photos and access details. Pickup must be confirmed based on the load, location, access and available coverage before scheduling.

South, Central and North Orange County

South County requests commonly include laundry sets, refrigerators and cooking appliances from homes, garages and rental properties. Central and North County requests may include single qualifying appliances or multiple-appliance loads. In every area, working condition, access and current demand are reviewed before pickup is confirmed.

Apartment, garage and property-manager pickups

For apartments or multi-unit properties, include the floor level, elevator availability and any stairs or narrow hallways. For garage or driveway pickups, say whether the appliance can be reached directly from the street or parking area. Landlords and property managers can list multiple appliances in one request and describe the condition of each item.
