What Makes a Strong Mission Viejo Request?

Working Washer & Dryer

Tell us if the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. Submit both together when they are available as a set.

Cooling Refrigerator

State whether it is currently plugged in and maintaining temperature. Include exterior, interior and model-label photos when practical.

Usable Freezer

Chest and upright freezers can be good reuse items. Confirm freezing performance and whether the unit is empty and easy to access.

Range or Oven

Identify gas or electric and report burner, oven and control-panel operation. Built-in units require different handling than freestanding ranges.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Garages, Hillside Driveways and Community Rules

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, first floor, upper floor or another location. If the driveway is steep, access is gated or parking is controlled, include that information. For condos and townhomes, list stairs, elevator access, hallway turns and any association time restrictions.

For refrigerators, note narrow kitchen doors or islands that may affect removal. For stacked laundry, tell us if the machines remain stacked and whether there is enough room to separate them safely. These details help determine whether the route fits a free pickup before anyone drives to the property.
