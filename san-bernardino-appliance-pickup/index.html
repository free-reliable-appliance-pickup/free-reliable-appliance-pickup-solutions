San Bernardino Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free in San Bernardino?

It can be free for qualifying fully working appliances or eligible mixed loads after condition, location, access and route availability are reviewed.

Do broken appliances qualify?

A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify. Minor repairs or qualifying mixed loads may be considered depending on local demand.

Do you claim a storefront in San Bernardino?

No. This page describes pickup coverage and request routing, not a local storefront or municipal facility.

San Bernardino Appliance Pickup vs. City Bulky-Item Collection

San Bernardino residents have a public bulky-item option in addition to our qualification-based appliance pickup review. Our service focuses on appliances that may still have reuse, repair or resale value; the City program is a separate disposal service for residents.

Our appliance pickup review

Send working condition, photos, ZIP code, floor level and access details for washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges. Working and reusable appliances receive the strongest priority.

San Bernardino resident program

The City of San Bernardino states that residents receive two free bulky-item collections per year, up to five items each time, through Burrtec. The City's information includes old appliances, refrigerators and freezers among bulky items.

Use the option that matches the item

If an appliance is useful and accessible, submit it for reuse-focused pickup review first. If it does not qualify, the City program may provide another removal path for eligible San Bernardino residents.
