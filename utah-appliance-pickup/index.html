What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Fully working appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

What to Include

Send appliance type, condition, city or ZIP code, photos when available, floor level, stairs and any tight-access details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are washers and dryers a priority?

Yes. List each appliance and its condition in one request.
