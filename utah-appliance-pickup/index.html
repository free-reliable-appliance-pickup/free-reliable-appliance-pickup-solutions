What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

What to Include in Your Request

Send the appliance type and condition, city or ZIP code, photos when available, floor level, stairs and any tight-access details. This helps us review the request before scheduling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is appliance pickup free everywhere in Utah?

No. Free pickup is offered only for qualifying requests where coverage is available.

Are washers and dryers a priority?

Yes. Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are important pickup categories.

Can I submit several appliances together?

Yes. List each appliance and condition in one request.
