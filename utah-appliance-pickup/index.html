Wasatch Front Guide Service Areas

Find your part of the Wasatch Front

Utah request information is organized around the Wasatch Front. Use the regional guide for these areas:

  • Salt Lake area: Salt Lake City, West Valley City, Sandy and Draper.
  • Utah County: Lehi, American Fork, Orem and Provo.
  • Layton and Ogden area: include the exact city and ZIP so your request can be distinguished from a Salt Lake-area stop.

Read the Wasatch Front pickup guide for condition, photos and access information. Availability for one part of the region does not confirm a pickup in another.

Outside the listed region

The Wasatch Front guide does not establish statewide coverage. Check your location with us before relying on a pickup elsewhere in Utah. Provide your city, ZIP, appliance types and the date you need them removed.

A request remains subject to condition, access and available route or partner capacity. Wait for an explicit scheduling confirmation before arranging building access or planning around a pickup date.

Before arranging removal

Use the qualification guide to check working-appliance and mixed-load requirements, including the microwave and dishwasher exceptions. Once a pickup is confirmed, the preparation checklist explains what to have ready.

If coverage or reuse is unsuitable, consult our other disposal options before committing to a deadline.
