What Makes a Strong Pickup Request?

Working sets

Complete working washer and dryer sets receive especially strong priority because both machines can be reviewed and routed together.

Single working machines

A single working washer or dryer can also be reviewed when current local demand and route capacity are available.

Clear condition details

For washers, report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, report gas or electric, power, tumble and heat.

Accurate access information

Include garage or interior location, floor level, stairs, gates, parking or loading restrictions and carrying distance.

Access Details Matter

South and coastal Orange County requests can involve garages, interior laundry rooms, condos, apartments, gated neighborhoods and planned communities. Exact access information helps prevent delays and lets the pickup route be reviewed before confirmation.

Best submission: clear front and control-panel photos, exact community and ZIP, tested functions, gas/electric dryer type, floor level, stairs, gate instructions and the path from the appliance to the loading point.

South Orange County Washer & Dryer FAQ

Is free pickup guaranteed?

No. Pickup is qualification-based and depends on condition, completeness, exact location, access and current route availability.

Can repairable machines be reviewed?

Yes, selected repairable machines may be reviewed when current local repair or resale demand exists. Working machines receive stronger priority.
