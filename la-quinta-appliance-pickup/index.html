What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Send the appliance condition, city or ZIP code, photos and access details. Pickup must be confirmed based on the load, location, access and available coverage before scheduling.

La Quinta Bulky Appliance Alternative

For a major appliance that does not qualify for our pickup, La Quinta publishes a separate resident bulky-item option through Burrtec. The City says bulky-item service covers items too large for the regular trash barrel, including appliances, and currently allows eligible residents up to four pieces per week at no charge when the pickup is scheduled.

La Quinta's code-compliance information also says discarded furniture and appliances should not be stored where they are visible from the public right-of-way. Residents should schedule the city's bulky-item service rather than placing an appliance out without an appointment. The City also encourages reuse or donation when an item remains in good condition.

This gives La Quinta households two different paths: contact us first for a qualifying reusable refrigerator, washer, dryer, range or freezer, or use the City's current bulky-item program when the appliance is not a fit for reuse. Confirm the City's current eligibility and scheduling rules before set-out.
