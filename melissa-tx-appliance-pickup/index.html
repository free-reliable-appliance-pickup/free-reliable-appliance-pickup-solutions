Common Melissa questions

Can I request free major appliance pickup in Melissa?

You can submit a request for free. The actual pickup can be offered at no charge only if the equipment, access and an available route qualify.

Which appliances are considered?

Working refrigerators, washers, dryers, washer/dryer sets, freezers and stoves receive priority. Nonworking items require individual review. Dishwashers usually need at least two other working major appliances, and a microwave alone does not qualify.

Will someone pick it up today?

No same-day or next-day pickup is promised. We confirm whether a local route is available and agree on any scheduling only after reviewing your details.
