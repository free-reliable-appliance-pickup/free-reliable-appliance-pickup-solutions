San Fernando Bulky-Item Alternative for Appliances That Do Not Qualify

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup, the City of San Fernando says residents can schedule bulky-item pickup through Republic Services. The City lists appliances, including refrigerators, among accepted bulky items and currently allows up to six bulky-item pickup requests per year for residents.

This public waste-service option is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup. Confirm the current rules and scheduled collection details before placing an appliance at the curb.
