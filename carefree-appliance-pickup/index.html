All Major Appliance Pickup in Carefree

This is the main Carefree all-appliance page. We review qualifying washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges, ovens and multi-appliance loads. Working and reusable appliances receive priority.

Best request: exact address/ZIP + appliance type + working condition + clear photos + floor/stairs + gates/elevators/parking details.

What We Need for Each Appliance

Washers & Dryers

Washer: fill, agitate, drain and spin. Dryer: tumble and heat. Tell us gas or electric and whether it is a complete set.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the unit is plugged in and holding temperature. Send inside/outside photos and note missing shelves, drawers or handles.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Tell us gas or electric, whether burners and oven heat work, and whether the appliance is already safely disconnected.

Carefree Premium-Access Planning

Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repair needs may be considered. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Nearby Phoenix Metro Routes

Nearby routes may include Cave Creek, north Scottsdale, Anthem and north Phoenix.
