Monrovia Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Can one working washer or dryer qualify?

It may. Complete working sets receive especially strong priority, but a single working machine can also be reviewed.

Can pickup be reviewed from an apartment?

Yes. Include floor level, stairs or elevator, hallway access and loading restrictions.

Should I send photos of the removal path?

Yes when access is tight or unusual. Photos can help identify steps, turns or clearance issues before scheduling.
