What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.
