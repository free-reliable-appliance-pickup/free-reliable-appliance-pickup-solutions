Los Angeles Refrigerator Pickup Review

If you are replacing a refrigerator that still cools, send the exact Los Angeles address, clear exterior and interior photos, and the current cooling condition. Working and reusable refrigerators receive priority because this service is focused on reuse and practical local routing.

Free pickup is not automatic. A single nonworking refrigerator does not qualify for this free service. Condition, local demand, access and current route availability are reviewed before a pickup is confirmed.

What to Send for a Faster Refrigerator Decision

Cooling Condition

Tell us whether both the refrigerator and freezer sections are holding temperature and whether the unit is currently plugged in.

Photos & Size

Send a full exterior photo, interior photo and model label when available. Approximate width, height and depth are useful for oversized or built-in units.

Removal Access

Include floor level, stairs, elevator access, gates, narrow doors, parking or loading restrictions and carrying distance to the pickup vehicle.

Los Angeles Apartments, Hillsides & Tight Access

Refrigerator removal in Los Angeles can vary significantly by building and neighborhood. Apartment pickups may require elevator reservations or loading instructions. Hillside and narrow-street properties may have limited vehicle access or longer carries. Tell us about those conditions before scheduling so the refrigerator and removal path can be reviewed together.

Large French-door, side-by-side and built-in refrigerators may require extra clearance planning. Do not remove doors, disconnect water lines or move a heavy refrigerator unsafely just to make the request easier; describe the current setup first.

Working Refrigerator vs. Disposal-Only Refrigerator

A refrigerator that is still operating may have reuse value and can be reviewed here for qualification-based free pickup. A nonworking refrigerator by itself should use an appropriate disposal or recycling route. Los Angeles residents served by LA Sanitation can also review the City's appliance pickup options through MyLA311.

Los Angeles Refrigerator Pickup Questions

Is refrigerator pickup free in Los Angeles?

Qualifying working and reusable refrigerators may receive free pickup after cooling condition, exact address, access and current route availability are reviewed.

Do you take a refrigerator from inside an apartment?

Inside pickup can be reviewed. Send the floor level, stairs or elevator, hallway and doorway details, loading access and parking restrictions.

Can a refrigerator with a minor problem qualify?

Minor-repair units may be considered when local demand supports pickup. Describe the exact problem instead of marking the refrigerator as fully working.

Does a dead refrigerator qualify by itself?

No. A single nonworking refrigerator does not qualify for this free reuse-focused service.
