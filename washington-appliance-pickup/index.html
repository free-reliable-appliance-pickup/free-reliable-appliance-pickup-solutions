Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance alone generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

What to Send

Include city or ZIP, appliance type, working condition, photos, floor level, stairs and access restrictions. For refrigerators and freezers include cooling status; for washers include fill/drain/spin; for dryers include heat/tumble and gas or electric.

Inside pickup: tell us about elevators, gates, narrow hallways, door removal, loading zones and parking restrictions before scheduling.

Seattle and dense neighborhoods

Include apartment floor, elevator, loading zone, parking restrictions, narrow halls and any scheduled building access. Photos of the removal path can prevent delays.

Vancouver and Clark County

Provide the exact ZIP code so the request can be reviewed against the Oregon–Washington route. Mention garages, side gates, stairs and longer driveway carries.

Longer Washington routes

Olympia, Bellingham, Yakima, Tri-Cities and Spokane requests depend heavily on reusable condition, load size and available partner capacity.

Photos, Measurements and Preparation

Send exterior and interior photos plus a model label when available. Measure large refrigerators, freezers and ranges. For multiple appliances, list the operating condition of each machine rather than describing the entire load as simply working or not working.

Keep refrigerators and freezers plugged in when practical so cooling can be confirmed. Before scheduling, disclose stairs, elevators, gates, ferry-dependent access, islands, long rural drives and property rules. Do not leave an appliance outside until pickup is explicitly confirmed.

Faster review: send city/ZIP, appliance list, condition, photos, measurements and access information together. Complete requests are easier to evaluate for the correct Washington route.
