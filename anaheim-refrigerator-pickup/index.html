Anaheim Refrigerator Recycling & Bulky-Item Alternatives

This specialty page is for qualification-based free pickup review. If a refrigerator does not qualify, Anaheim residents have separate city programs to check.

Anaheim Public Utilities refrigerator/freezer recycling: the city currently lists a $50 incentive for eligible residential electric customers who recycle an operating refrigerator or freezer between 10 and 32 cubic feet. The program says customers arrange an independent pickup service, participation is generally limited to one operating unit every five years, and funding/eligibility rules apply.

Anaheim bulky-item collection: the city currently provides up to three no-cost bulky-item pickups per household per calendar year, with up to 20 items per pickup, through Republic Services. Collection is curbside; Republic does not enter the home.
