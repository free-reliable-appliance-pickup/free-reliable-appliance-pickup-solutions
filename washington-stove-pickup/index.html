What we need to review your request

We review working electric stoves, gas ranges and ovens. Tell us which burners and oven functions work, whether the unit is freestanding, and whether stairs or tight doorways affect access. Gas and hardwired appliances must be safely disconnected by a qualified person before pickup; we do not promise disconnection service.

Send your city or ZIP code, appliance photos, operating condition, floor level, stairs, and whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway or inside the property. We review Seattle–Tacoma and Puget Sound, Vancouver and Clark County, Olympia, Spokane, Bellingham, Yakima and the Tri-Cities where routes or partners are available.

When is pickup free?

Fully working and reusable major appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Nonworking items alone generally do not qualify. Free pickup is confirmed only after reviewing condition, access, local demand and available routes. Submission does not guarantee pickup.
