Do stairs and gates matter?

Yes. Include stairs, gates, narrow doors, hallway turns and carrying distance so access can be reviewed before pickup is confirmed.

Sometimes, depending on condition and current local demand, but free pickup is not guaranteed for nonworking equipment.
