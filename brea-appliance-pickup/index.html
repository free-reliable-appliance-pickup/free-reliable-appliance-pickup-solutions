Brea Appliance Pickup Review

Brea requests are reviewed through our Orange County network. We prioritize working appliances and complete loads where condition is clearly described. Exact address, appliance type, photos and access details help us determine whether the request fits an active route.

Current qualification: fully working qualifying appliances may receive free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when around 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify.

Hillside, Garage & Multi-Level Access

Some Brea properties have sloped driveways, garages below living space, interior steps or longer carries from the appliance to the truck. Tell us where the appliance is located and whether stairs, gates or tight turns are involved. For upper floors, include the number of flights and whether an elevator is available.

For rental or property-management requests, group appliances by unit and condition. This makes it easier to review several appliances without confusing working and nonworking items.

Appliances With Strongest Pickup Potential

Working laundry machines

Washers and dryers are a priority. Note whether the washer spins and drains and whether the dryer heats normally.

Cooling appliances

Refrigerators and freezers should be cooling correctly. Photos of seals, shelves and exterior condition are useful.

Stoves and ranges

Gas or electric units can be submitted when the working condition and removal access are clear.

Items That Need Additional Qualifying Appliances

Microwaves are not accepted by themselves. Dishwashers are reviewed only when included with two or more major working appliances. If an appliance has a minor defect, describe it accurately so we can review the request without making assumptions.

Free pickup is never automatic from form submission. We confirm only after the request is accepted.

Brea Pickup FAQs

Can you review a pickup with a sloped driveway?

Yes. Describe the slope, truck access and how far the appliance must be moved.

Can a freezer qualify?

Yes. Working freezers can be strong pickup candidates. Include cooling condition and photos.

Do you accept a dishwasher alone?

No. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
