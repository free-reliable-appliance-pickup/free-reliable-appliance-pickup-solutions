What makes an Aurora pickup request different?

Aurora extends along the Fox River and across several county boundaries, so a ZIP code and real pickup city are more useful than saying 'western Chicago suburbs.' A request near downtown or a multistory apartment may require parking permission and stairway dimensions; a west-side single-family home may have a garage, side-yard gate or basement stairs. None of these are announced service zones.

Condition details that help decide whether we can assist

For a washer/dryer replacement, test the two appliances separately and specify whether the dryer is gas or electric. For a refrigerator, document whether both sections cool and whether interior shelves and door seals are intact. For a cooking range, list working burners, oven heat, and whether a safe gas or electrical disconnection is still needed.

We focus on reusable, working major appliances. A nonworking appliance is considered individually and is not automatically eligible for free removal. Dishwasher requests require two other qualifying working major appliances, and microwaves are not normally standalone pickups.

Stairs, disconnection and loading

Mention driveway slope, gate width, stair turns, garage storage and loading distance. If equipment is still installed, do not disconnect a gas line, water pipe or electrical hardwire yourself unless qualified. If several machines are being replaced at an Aurora property, list how many work and how many have faults.

Do not set out a refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer or range for us before an appointment is expressly confirmed. Cooling equipment must be handled without venting refrigerants, and connected gas appliances require qualified disconnection.

City of Aurora appliance collection is a separate option

If a reusable-appliance pickup cannot be approved, Aurora residents can consult the City of Aurora Special Garbage Items guidance. The city states that collection of appliances such as refrigerators and stoves must be arranged in advance with its waste contractor, Groot, at 630-892-9294, and that an appliance needs one garbage sticker. Follow the city's current requirements and confirm eligibility with Groot; this municipal collection is independent of Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.

For a potentially reusable washer/dryer set, include whether both units run, the dryer's gas or electric connection, and any basement stairs. For a refrigerator, tell us whether both compartments cool and if the item is safely disconnected. These facts help evaluate reuse before considering separate disposal alternatives.
