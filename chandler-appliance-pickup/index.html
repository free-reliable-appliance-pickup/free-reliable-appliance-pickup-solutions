Chandler Bulk Collection Can Include Appliances

Chandler handles appliances differently from neighboring Gilbert. For residents who pay for city solid-waste service, Chandler's scheduled curbside bulk program can include major appliances such as washers, dryers, dishwashers and stoves. Cooling appliances such as refrigerators and freezers are subject to a separate coolant-removal fee under the city's current rules.

Chandler also ties curbside bulk collections and visits to its Recycling-Solid Waste Collection Center together under an annual allowance. The city currently says the first four combined qualifying bulk collections or center visits in a calendar year are free, with a fee after that threshold. Online bulk requests generally require advance scheduling, and the city specifies placement near the front-property curb or sidewalk rather than alley collection. The material list supplied with the request also matters because different trucks may handle different materials.

Our Chandler pickup review serves a different purpose. If the appliance is working or reusable, submit photos and operating condition before choosing a city disposal route. We can evaluate a qualifying item or mixed load for free reuse-focused pickup. If the appliance is completely nonworking or otherwise does not qualify, Chandler's municipal bulk/recycling program is a clear local fallback.

Check Chandler curbside bulk and appliance rules · Chandler scheduling information

Chandler preparation tip: do not disconnect or move a heavy appliance unsafely just to submit it. Send its current location, access path and condition first; municipal schedules and fees can change, so verify the city page before using that option.

Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repair needs may be considered. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
