Chandler Appliance Pickup & Removal

We review major appliance pickup requests from Chandler homeowners, renters, landlords and property managers. Working and reusable appliances receive priority.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Condition, access, local demand and available coverage are reviewed before scheduling.

Priority Appliances

Washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are priority categories. Refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens may also qualify depending on condition and demand.

What Qualifies?

Fully working appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances, and microwaves only with another qualifying major appliance.

Pickup Access

Include floor level, stairs, gates, narrow doors, tight hallways and whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upstairs or outside. Photos help us review the request faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you pick up washers and dryers?

Yes. Working washers, dryers and laundry sets are priority requests.

Can I submit several appliances?

Yes. List every appliance and its condition in one request.

Chandler pickup details

Chandler requests are reviewed for appliance condition, access and current East Valley route availability. Working washers and dryers are a priority, and working refrigerators, freezers and cooking appliances are also reviewed. Include your ZIP code, clear photos and whether the appliance is in a garage, first floor, upper floor or outside.

For refrigerators and freezers, tell us whether the appliance is plugged in and cooling. For laundry equipment, describe whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. Complete information helps us evaluate a Chandler pickup request faster.
