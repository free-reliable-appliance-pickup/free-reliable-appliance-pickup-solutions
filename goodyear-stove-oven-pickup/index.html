For Goodyear requests, tell us whether the stove is being removed as part of a kitchen remodel, an appliance delivery or a rental turnover. Goodyear Municipal Bulk Trash vs. Reuse Pickup

The City of Goodyear directs residents replacing appliances toward retailer take-back or its scheduled residential bulk collection. Its bulk-trash program uses address-based collection days and preparation rules, so verify the current instructions before placing a range at the curb.

Read Goodyear's official bulk-trash collection guidance. Our service reviews reusable stoves separately and does not operate on behalf of the city.
