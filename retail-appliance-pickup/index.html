Who This Pickup Review Is For

Appliance dealers, retailers, home-improvement stores, showrooms, outlet locations and regional retail operators can submit one-time or recurring appliance replacement work for review. The goal is to collect enough operating detail to decide whether the load fits a current pickup route before anyone schedules service.

Retail loads should separate reusable working equipment from damaged, incomplete or nonworking units so the pickup route can evaluate the useful inventory before service is confirmed.

What to Include Before We Schedule

Include store or warehouse address, appliance categories, average weekly or monthly volume, dock or rear-door access, appointment rules, staging area, condition mix and whether units are palletized or loose.

Also list each appliance category, how many units are ready, whether each unit works, whether photos are available and whether the request is one-time, recurring or part of a larger replacement project.

Appliance Categories We Review

Washers & Dryers

Working laundry equipment and complete sets receive strong priority. Include basic test results when available.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Include cooling status, size, interior/exterior photos and safe removal access.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Identify gas or electric service, working functions and whether the unit is safely disconnected.

Mixed Loads

For replacement projects, list the full appliance mix so the working/reusable share can be reviewed accurately.

Not every appliance automatically qualifies for free pickup. Nonworking, incomplete, unsafe or difficult-access equipment may need a different removal or recycling option.

Retail Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can appliance dealers submit display or return pickups?

Yes. Display changes, customer returns, trade-ins and qualifying replacement inventory can be reviewed by store or route.

What should a retail pickup request include?

Include store location, appliance type and condition, quantity, packaging or staging status, receiving or dock hours and authorized release contact.

Can recurring retail appliance flow be reviewed?

Yes. Weekly, monthly, seasonal or project-based retail appliance flow can be submitted as a recurring program for review.

Request Commercial Appliance Pickup Review
