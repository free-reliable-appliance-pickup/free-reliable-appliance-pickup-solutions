Montclair Appliance Pickup Review

Montclair connects the eastern San Gabriel Valley with Ontario, Upland, Claremont and Pomona. We review qualifying appliance requests for homes, apartments, rentals and businesses through the 909 regional network.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup.

Appliances We Review in Montclair

Washers & dryers

Working laundry machines are a priority. Describe fill, drain, spin, tumbling and heating performance for each appliance.

Refrigerators & freezers

Tell us whether both compartments maintain temperature and include clear exterior, interior and model-label photos.

Stoves, ranges & ovens

Gas and electric cooking appliances may be reviewed when operation, fuel type and removal access are clearly described.

Apartments, Commercial Properties & Tight Loading Areas

For Montclair apartments or commercial locations, include the floor, elevator availability, loading-zone rules, gates, parking limits and the distance from the appliance to the truck. If a refrigerator must pass through a narrow doorway, provide the appliance width and the tightest exit measurement.

Multiple Appliances and Mixed Loads

List every appliance in one request and state whether each is fully working, needs repair or does not work. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Clear condition details are especially important for property turnovers and business replacements.

Important Pickup Limits

Minor-repair appliances may be considered after accurate disclosure. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Submitting the request starts a review and does not guarantee free pickup. We confirm condition, exact address, access and route availability before scheduling.

Montclair Appliance Pickup FAQs

Do you review Montclair washer and dryer sets?

Yes. Working laundry sets are strong requests. State whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer tumbles and heats.

Can a freezer qualify?

A working chest or upright freezer may qualify. Tell us whether it holds freezing temperature and where it is located.

Do you accept a dishwasher by itself?

No. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.
