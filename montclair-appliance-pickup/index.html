Apartments, Commercial Properties & Tight Loading Areas

For Montclair apartments or commercial locations, include the floor, elevator availability, loading-zone rules, gates, parking limits and the distance from the appliance to the truck. If a refrigerator must pass through a narrow doorway, provide the appliance width and the tightest exit measurement.

Multiple Appliances and Mixed Loads

List every appliance in one request and state whether each is fully working, needs repair or does not work. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. For example, a load of five major appliances with four fully working and one needing repair can be reviewed together, but acceptance is not automatic. List the condition and known problems of every item so a pickup partner knows what to expect before travelling. Clear condition details are especially important for property turnovers and business replacements.

Important Pickup Limits

Minor-repair appliances may be considered after accurate disclosure. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.
