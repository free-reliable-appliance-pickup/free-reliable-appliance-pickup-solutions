Arcadia Access Details That Matter

Arcadia includes established residential streets, apartments, condos, commercial properties and City Center or mixed-use locations. For an interior refrigerator, show the tightest doorway and any stairs, gates or turns between the appliance and the loading area. For apartments or commercial sites, include elevator, building-access and parking/loading information.

For oversized or built-in refrigerators, include the model label and approximate width. Do not assume doors, water lines or cabinetry can be removed during pickup; describe the current installation first.

Prepare the Refrigerator Safely

Keep the sealed cooling system intact. Do not cut refrigerant lines or remove the compressor. Keep the refrigerator testable until its condition has been reviewed when practical. After an accepted pickup is confirmed, remove food and loose items and follow the preparation instructions provided for the route.

Arcadia Bulky-Item Alternative for Refrigerators

If a refrigerator does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup route, Arcadia residents have a separate municipal option. The City of Arcadia says Waste Management offers four free bulky-item pickups per calendar year for each single-family residential account and two per calendar year for each multi-family residential unit. The City lists appliances among qualifying bulky items.
