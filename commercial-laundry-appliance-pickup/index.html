Commercial Washer & Dryer Pickup for Businesses

Laundries, multifamily properties, hotels, senior communities and other businesses can submit washer and dryer pickup requests for replacements, laundry-room upgrades, returns, cleanouts or recurring equipment programs. Include machine type, count, condition, access and expected cadence.

Common Commercial Laundry Pickup Work

Laundry-room replacements

Coin, card, stack, commercial and standard washers or dryers being replaced in shared laundry areas.

Property and hospitality refreshes

Equipment changes across apartments, senior communities, hotels and other managed properties.

Recurring multi-site programs

Repeated washer and dryer replacements across multiple properties or service areas.

Machine Details to Include

Separate washers from dryers and list type, quantity, stack status, approximate size, condition and whether units are coin, card, commercial or standard residential-style machines. Photos help clarify the equipment mix.

Utilities, Access and Pickup Cadence

Include water, drain, gas or electric status where relevant, plus stairs, elevators, doorways, parking, loading, staging and appointment rules. For recurring work, describe whether the schedule is weekly, monthly, turnover-based, seasonal or tied to a project.

Safety: utility disconnection and site preparation should be handled by the appropriate qualified team when required.

One Site or Multiple Locations

A commercial inquiry can cover one laundry room, a property portfolio or a multi-site program. List each location or service area, approximate equipment volume and access requirements.

Commercial Laundry Pickup Questions

Can one request include washers and dryers from several properties?

Yes. List each property, machine count, condition, access and expected replacement schedule.

Do all commercial laundry machines qualify for free pickup?

No. Qualification depends on equipment condition, size, access, local demand and route availability.

What details are most useful?

Machine type, quantity, stack status, condition, photos, utility/disconnection status, loading path and pickup frequency.
