What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Every request is reviewed for appliance condition, exact location, access, local demand and available route coverage before a pickup time is confirmed.

Pickup is not automatic. Sending a request starts the review process; it does not guarantee acceptance or a specific pickup time.

Riverside County Is a Large Service Region

Riverside County stretches from the western communities near the Inland Empire through southwest Riverside County and the San Jacinto Valley to the Coachella Valley and eastern desert communities. Because those areas are far apart, one route does not cover the whole county at the same time. The exact city and ZIP code matter when we review availability.

Western Riverside County

Riverside, Jurupa Valley, Corona, Eastvale, Norco and nearby communities are often reviewed with Inland Empire routing in mind.

Southwest Riverside County

Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee, Lake Elsinore and Wildomar requests are reviewed as a separate route cluster because of travel distance and scheduling.

San Jacinto Valley & Pass

Hemet, San Jacinto, Beaumont, Banning and Calimesa requests depend on current route direction and appliance demand.

Coachella Valley

Palm Springs, Cathedral City, Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Indian Wells, La Quinta, Indio, Coachella and nearby desert communities require exact city and ZIP details so the request can be matched to current coverage.

Appliance Condition Details That Help

Washers

Tell us whether the washer fills, agitates, drains and spins. Mention leaks, loud bearings or error codes.

Dryers

Identify gas or electric and tell us whether the dryer tumbles and heats. Complete washer/dryer sets receive strong priority.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the unit is currently plugged in and cooling or freezing. Include an interior photo when possible.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Identify gas or electric and describe whether the burners, oven and controls work normally.

Access Matters in Riverside County

Pickup conditions vary widely between single-family homes, gated communities, apartments, mobile-home communities, rentals and desert properties. Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first-floor room, upstairs unit, storage area or outside.

Include stair flights, elevator availability, gates, narrow doorways, steep driveways, long carries, parking restrictions and whether doors must be removed from a refrigerator. For gated communities, tell us how entry will be arranged. For apartment or condo pickups, include loading instructions if the building limits access or parking.

For rural or edge-of-route addresses, the exact street address and ZIP code are especially important because travel distance can determine whether a route is practical.

Landlords, Property Managers and Multiple Appliances

If several appliances are being replaced at one property, list each appliance separately with its working condition. Tell us whether the items are together in one garage or spread across multiple units. A mixed load may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

For turnover work, photos and unit-access details help us review the load faster. If appliances will be moved outside before pickup, say so in the request; do not move heavy appliances unsafely just to qualify.

Riverside County Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free everywhere in Riverside County?

Free pickup is available only for qualifying requests. The appliance condition, access, city, local demand and current route coverage are reviewed before service is confirmed.

Can I request pickup in the Coachella Valley?

Yes. Submit the exact city, ZIP code, appliance condition and access information. Desert-route availability can vary by day and distance.

Do broken appliances qualify?

A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

What should I send for a refrigerator or freezer?

Send a full appliance photo, an interior photo when possible, the model label if available, and tell us whether the appliance is plugged in and maintaining temperature.
