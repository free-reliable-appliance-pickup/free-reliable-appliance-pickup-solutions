What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Send the appliance condition, city or ZIP code, photos and access details. Pickup must be confirmed based on the load, location, access and available coverage before scheduling.

Riverside County requests are reviewed from Riverside, Jurupa Valley, Corona, Moreno Valley, Temecula, Murrieta, Menifee, Beaumont, Banning and surrounding communities when coverage is available. Because the county covers a large area, city, ZIP code and appliance details are especially important for route review.

Metro Riverside, Temecula Valley and Pass-area requests

Metro Riverside requests often involve washers, dryers, refrigerators and stoves from homes, apartments and rental properties. Temecula Valley and Menifee-area requests are reviewed with the same qualification standards. Beaumont and Banning requests depend on current route availability and the value and condition of the appliance load.

Multiple-appliance loads

If you have several appliances, list every item and its condition. A mixed load may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Riverside County and Coachella Valley Pickup Requests

Coachella Valley requests include Palm Springs, Cathedral City, Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Indian Wells, La Quinta and Indio when a route is available. Send your city and ZIP code; a route available in Riverside or Corona does not automatically cover a Coachella Valley address.

Working washers and dryers receive priority. Describe each machine separately: whether the washer fills, drains and spins, and whether the dryer heats and tumbles. For a refrigerator or freezer, tell us whether it maintains temperature and include interior photos when available.

For a gated community or rental property, tell us how entry will be arranged, who can provide access and whether the appliances are inside, upstairs, in a garage or outside. Include stair flights and any narrow doorway or loading restrictions.
