What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Send the appliance condition, city or ZIP code, photos and access details. Pickup must be confirmed based on the load, location, access and available route coverage before scheduling.

Riverside County Appliance Pickup & Removal

We review qualifying washers, dryers, refrigerators, stoves, ranges, ovens and freezers across Riverside County. Because the county covers a large area, route availability varies.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Condition, local demand, access and scheduling determine whether a request can be accepted.

What to Include With Your Request

Condition

Tell us whether each appliance is fully working, needs repair, is not working or is unknown.

Location

Provide the city, ZIP code and pickup address.

Access

Include floor level, stairs, elevators, gates, tight halls or door-removal issues.

Photos

Photos of the appliance, model label and access area help us review the request.

Appliance and Access Details

Working washers and dryers receive priority. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer heats and tumbles. For refrigerators and freezers, tell us whether they maintain temperature. For a stove, range or oven, identify gas or electric and describe any functions that do not work.

For gated communities, apartments and rental properties, tell us how entry will be arranged and whether the appliances are inside, upstairs, in a garage or outside. Include stair flights, elevator availability, narrow doorways and loading restrictions.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
