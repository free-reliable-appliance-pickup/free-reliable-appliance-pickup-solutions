Los Angeles Freezer Pickup Review

If your chest freezer or upright freezer still reaches and holds freezing temperature, send the exact Los Angeles address, clear exterior and interior photos and the current condition. Working and reusable freezers receive priority because this service is focused on reuse and practical local routing.

Free pickup is qualification-based. A single nonworking freezer does not automatically qualify. Condition, size, access, local demand and current route availability are reviewed before a pickup is confirmed.

Chest Freezer vs. Upright Freezer Removal

Chest Freezers

Chest freezers are wide and often located in garages, side yards or utility spaces. Send the width, doorway or gate clearance, carrying distance and whether steps are involved.

Upright Freezers

Upright freezers move more like refrigerators. Send height and width when known and describe tight hallways, doorways, stairs or elevator access.

Freezing Condition

Tell us whether the unit is plugged in and whether it maintains freezing temperature. Mention frost problems, weak cooling, unusual noise or other known issues.

Prepare a Freezer for Pickup

Remove all food and loose items before a confirmed pickup. If the freezer will be unplugged for defrosting, protect the floor from meltwater and keep the area around the appliance clear. Do not cut refrigerant lines, remove the compressor or alter the sealed cooling system.

If the freezer is still being tested for qualification, tell us whether it is currently plugged in before unplugging it. A photo of the inside and outside, plus the model label when available, helps us review the request.

Los Angeles Apartment, Garage & Hillside Access

Freezers may be located in apartment laundry areas, garages, converted spaces, side yards or hillside properties. Include floor level, stairs, elevator availability, gates, parking or loading restrictions and the full carry path to the vehicle. For wide chest freezers, doorway and gate measurements can prevent a failed trip.

Los Angeles routes vary by exact address. Neighborhood names alone are not enough to confirm pickup; the appliance condition and complete access details are reviewed together with the current route.

If Your Freezer Does Not Qualify

For eligible City of Los Angeles single-family customers with LASAN curbside collection, LA Sanitation & Environment currently lists refrigerators and freezers among the large metal household appliances it will collect at no extra cost after a service request. Review the current City requirements directly on the LASAN Metal Appliance Pickup page or through MyLA311.

This municipal service is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup. Customers outside that eligibility should use the appropriate utility, municipal or certified recycling option for the actual address.

Los Angeles Freezer Pickup Questions

Can a working freezer qualify for free pickup?

Yes. Working and reusable chest and upright freezers may qualify after the exact address, condition, access and current route availability are reviewed.

Can you review a freezer from a garage or inside a property?

Yes. Send the carrying route, gates, steps, floor level, elevator details and parking or loading restrictions.

Should I unplug and defrost the freezer first?

If the unit is still being evaluated as working, tell us whether it is currently plugged in before disconnecting it. Once a pickup is confirmed, emptying and safely defrosting the freezer can make removal cleaner and safer.

Does a dead freezer qualify by itself?

Not automatically. A single nonworking freezer should use an appropriate municipal or recycling option when it does not qualify for the reuse-focused service.
