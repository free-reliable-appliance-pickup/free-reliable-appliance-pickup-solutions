Who This Program Is For

Distribution centers, warehouses, third-party logistics operations, return centers and regional consolidation sites can submit one-time or recurring loads for review.

Working and reusable equipment receives priority. Free pickup is qualification-based and is confirmed only after equipment condition, access, location, load mix and current route availability are reviewed.

Common Repeat Work

Transit-damaged inventory

Separate damaged and incomplete units from working or reusable equipment and include photos when possible.

Consolidated customer returns

List the appliance mix, pallet status, packaging and normal/peak load ranges at the transfer location.

Closeout or vendor-return loads

Describe what is approved for release and whether units are loose, wrapped or palletized.

Scheduled warehouse sweeps

Recurring pickup can be reviewed around dock appointments, staging zones and inventory-release procedures.

Build a Clear Commercial Pickup Scope

Equipment

List appliance type, count, condition, dimensions when relevant, whether it is disconnected and whether photos are available.

Cadence

State whether the work is one-time, weekly, monthly, seasonal, turnover-based or tied to a renovation phase.

Access

Include stairs, elevators, docks, gates, parking, service windows, staging areas and any vehicle or appointment restrictions.

Closeout

Tell us whether your team needs photos, a pickup confirmation, work-order reference, signature or other completion record.

Site & Access Details to Include

Include facility address, dock door or loading zone, appointment/check-in process, loose versus palletized counts, dimensions or weight range when known, site safety requirements, release authority and required completion records.

California Priority Markets

Current California priority coverage includes Los Angeles, San Gabriel Valley, Inland Empire, Orange County, Riverside/San Bernardino corridors and Coachella Valley. Other U.S. locations can also submit for review.
