Lake Forest Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Lake Forest requests are evaluated through our Orange County route network. Working appliances receive priority because reuse value helps support a free pickup. We review appliance condition, the complete load, the pickup address and access before a time is confirmed.

For searches such as appliance pickup, appliance removal, appliance recycling, washer pickup, refrigerator pickup or appliance removal near me in Lake Forest, send the exact address or ZIP, appliance condition and access details so the current 909 route can be reviewed.

Free pickup rule: fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking item does not qualify.

What to Include With Your Request

Clear condition

Describe what the appliance does now. For refrigerators and freezers, state whether it maintains temperature. For laundry equipment, describe cycle performance.

Good photos

Send a full exterior photo and model-label photo when available. An interior photo is useful for refrigerators and freezers.

Access details

Include stairs, narrow doors, gated access, parking restrictions, elevators or a long carry from the appliance to the truck area.

Lake Forest Homes, HOAs & Rental Properties

For gated communities or HOA-managed properties, tell us about access procedures and any limits on truck entry or loading. For apartments or rentals, include floor level, elevator access and whether property management needs advance notice. For garage pickups, say whether the appliance is already disconnected and whether the loading path is clear.

If you have more than one appliance, list all items and conditions together. Complete working loads can be more practical to route than separate requests.

Appliance Limits

Microwaves are accepted only when included with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Repair needs must be described accurately so we can decide whether the appliance is still suitable for review.

Submitting the form does not guarantee pickup. We confirm the route after the request is accepted.

Lake Forest Bulky-Goods Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our service, Lake Forest's solid-waste program includes bulky-goods collection for items such as stoves, washers and dryers. The City's franchise agreement provides separate handling for appliances containing refrigerant, including refrigerators. Confirm current pickup allowances, fees and scheduling with the City's contracted provider.
