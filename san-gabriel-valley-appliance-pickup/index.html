Appliance Types We Review

Washers & Dryers

Working single machines and complete laundry sets receive strong priority. Include whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the unit cools or freezes correctly and include interior and exterior photos when possible.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

State whether the burners and oven operate and whether the unit is gas or electric. Appliances must be safely disconnected before pickup.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Inside Pickup, Apartments & Gated Properties

Include stair flights, elevator availability, gate instructions, parking or loading restrictions, narrow hallways and doorway concerns. For large refrigerators, a photo of the exit path can help prevent a failed trip.

San Gabriel Valley Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free in San Gabriel Valley?

It can be free for qualifying fully working appliances or eligible mixed loads after condition, location, access and route availability are reviewed.

Do broken appliances qualify?

A single nonworking appliance does not qualify under the general free-pickup rules. Minor repair needs may be considered, especially as part of a qualifying mixed load.
