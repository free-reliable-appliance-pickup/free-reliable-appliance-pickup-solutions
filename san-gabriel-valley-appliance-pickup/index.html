Free Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

We review qualifying major-appliance pickup requests across San Gabriel Valley. Working and reusable washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens receive the strongest priority. Free pickup is not automatic; each request is reviewed for condition, exact location, access, local demand and current route availability.

Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs can be considered in some cases, while a single nonworking appliance does not qualify under the general free-pickup rules.

Best request: send the appliance type, working condition, city and ZIP, floor level, stairs, gates or loading restrictions, and clear photos.

Appliance Types We Review

Washers & Dryers

Working single machines and complete laundry sets receive strong priority. Include whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the unit cools or freezes correctly and include interior and exterior photos when possible.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

State whether the burners and oven operate and whether the unit is gas or electric. Appliances must be safely disconnected before pickup.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Inside Pickup, Apartments & Gated Properties

For large refrigerators, a photo of the exit path can help prevent a failed trip.
