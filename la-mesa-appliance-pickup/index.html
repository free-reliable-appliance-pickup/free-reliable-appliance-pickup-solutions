La Mesa General Appliance Pickup Review

This page handles La Mesa requests for refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges, ovens and mixed major-appliance loads. Working and reusable appliances receive priority; final free-pickup eligibility depends on condition, exact address, access, local demand and route availability.

Washer or dryer only? Use the La Mesa washer & dryer pickup page. Keep mixed loads with cooling or cooking appliances on this general page.

Describe the Appliance and the Removal Path

For refrigerators and freezers, send interior and exterior photos and say whether the unit is cooling. For stoves and ranges, identify gas or electric and whether the appliance is disconnected. For mixed loads, list each item, its condition and where it is located before scheduling.

La Mesa Access Review for Cooling, Cooking & Mixed Loads

Kitchen and interior turns

For a refrigerator or range inside the home, note narrow doors, hallway turns, stairs and any exterior steps between the appliance and loading area.

Garage or staged items

If appliances are already in a garage or other staging area, say whether a vehicle can get close and whether gates or sloped driveways affect the carry.

Multiple-item planning

For several appliances, give the location and condition of each item so the route can be reviewed as one load rather than separate requests.

La Mesa General-Appliance FAQ

Can a single working refrigerator, freezer or range qualify?

Yes, but free pickup is not guaranteed. Describe the issue and include photos so the request can be reviewed accurately.
