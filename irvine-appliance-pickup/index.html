What Makes an Irvine Appliance a Good Candidate?

Working Laundry Equipment

Washers that fill, drain and spin and dryers that tumble and heat are strong candidates. Submit a washer/dryer set together when both are available.

Cooling Refrigerators

State whether the refrigerator is currently plugged in and holding temperature. Photos should show the exterior and, when possible, the interior.

Usable Freezers

Chest and upright freezers can be valuable reuse items. Tell us whether the unit freezes correctly and whether it is empty and accessible.

Working Cooking Appliances

For gas or electric ranges and ovens, describe burner operation, oven heat and control-panel condition.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only when included with another qualifying major appliance.

Condos, Apartments, HOAs & Gated Access

For Irvine properties with controlled access, tell us what is required before scheduling. Include whether a gate must be opened, whether an elevator reservation is needed, whether the building has a loading zone, and how close a pickup vehicle can get to the appliance. Do not put private gate codes in public messages; provide necessary access information through the request process.

For upper-floor units, list the floor and stair flights even when an elevator is present. For refrigerators, mention narrow doors, kitchen islands or turns that could affect removal. For stacked laundry or appliances inside closets, explain the installation so we know whether extra handling may be required.

If the appliance is already in a garage, state whether the garage opens directly to a driveway or whether there is a long carry through a common area.
