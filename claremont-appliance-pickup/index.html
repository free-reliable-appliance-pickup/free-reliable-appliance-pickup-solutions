Claremont Appliance Pickup vs. City Bulk-Item Service

Claremont’s City-run bulk-item program is a separate disposal option when reuse-focused pickup is not a fit. The City currently says single-family households may receive three no-charge bulk-item pickups per calendar year, and large appliances are accepted. Appointments are first-come, first-served; only listed items are collected, and scheduled items must be at the front curb by 6:00 AM.

Reuse-focused pickup

Send working condition, photos and access details to 909-375-6685. Working and reusable major appliances receive priority.

City single-family option

Call Claremont Community Services at (909) 399-5431 to schedule the separate City bulk-item collection.

Multi-family pilot

The City’s current multi-family bulky-item pilot provides up to three free bulky-item loads per unit per calendar year, including large appliances. Residents should contact Community Services for current eligibility and scheduling.

Recycling center / HHW

The City Yard recycling center at 1616 Monte Vista Avenue accepts ordinary recyclables, but the City says not to leave large household items such as furniture there. HHW/e-waste disposal uses separate City/LA County programs.
