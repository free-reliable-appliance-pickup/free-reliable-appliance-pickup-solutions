How Selma Requests Are Prioritized

The strongest Selma requests are appliances that can still be reused or repaired without major rebuilding. A working refrigerator, freezer, stove, washer or dryer can be reviewed on its own. For a mixed load, send the complete list at one time so the route can evaluate the stop as a whole.

Cooling Appliances

For a refrigerator or freezer, tell us whether it cools, whether shelves and doors are complete, and whether the unit can be tested before removal.

Cooking Appliances

For a stove or range, note whether it is gas or electric, whether burners and oven controls work, and whether it will already be safely disconnected.

Laundry Appliances

Washers and dryers are routed through the local laundry page when they are the only items. A complete working set usually provides clearer reuse value than one unknown-condition machine.

Property & Multi-Unit Loads

For apartment turns, remodels, senior housing, or property-management stops, include quantity, floor level, access window, and which units are still working.

Mixed loads may qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. A single nonworking general appliance normally does not qualify by itself.

Selma request rules: microwaves are not accepted alone. A dishwasher is considered only when it comes with two or more major working appliances.

Selma Address, ZIP & Carry-Out Details

For Selma, the ZIP context is 93662, but the ZIP alone is not enough to approve a stop. Send the street address and describe where the appliance is sitting now: inside the home, apartment, garage, driveway, side yard, business, or another pickup point.

Access notes are especially important when the route must plan around stairs, a locked gate, an alley entrance, a long carry, a narrow doorway, limited parking, or a unit that is not on the ground floor. For a refrigerator or freezer, measure tight doorways if the exit path is close.

Giving these details at the beginning helps avoid a route being accepted and then rejected later because the actual carry-out is different from what was expected.

Selma Appliance Pickup FAQ

Do I have to move the appliance outside first?

No. Describe its current location before moving it. If it is inside, send the floor level, stairs, doorway width, gate information and parking situation so the carry-out can be reviewed correctly.

Can I request pickup for one working refrigerator or stove?

Yes. One working major appliance can be reviewed for Selma. Approval still depends on condition, exact address, access and route capacity.

Should I send photos?

Yes when possible. Exterior, interior and model-label photos help verify condition and make it easier to decide whether the appliance fits a reuse-focused route.

What if the appliance does not qualify?

Use the local bulky-item option described above or another appropriate recycling or paid haul-away path. A submitted request is never a guarantee that our route will accept the appliance.

Do you have a Selma storefront?

No storefront is claimed. This page is for pickup-request coverage through the Central Valley network and available local route capacity.
