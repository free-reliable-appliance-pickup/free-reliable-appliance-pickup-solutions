San Gabriel Appliance Pickup & Removal Review

Residents, landlords, property managers and businesses can submit working and reusable major appliances for pickup review. Free pickup depends on condition, exact address, access, local demand and route availability.

Best request: send clear photos, tested condition, exact address, floor level, stairs, gates, parking and carrying distance.

Appliances We Review

Washers & Dryers

Report washer fill, drain and spin and dryer tumble, heat and gas/electric type.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Include interior/exterior photos and cooling status.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and whether burners, controls and oven functions work.

Multi-Item Loads

List each appliance and condition separately. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances; microwaves only with another qualifying major appliance.

San Gabriel Bulky-Item Alternative

Mission District, Valley Boulevard & Local Pickup Review

San Gabriel's Planning Division maintains both the Mission District Specific Plan and the Valley Boulevard Neighborhoods Sustainability Plan. Those areas differ from many of the city's established residential streets, which makes the exact address important when reviewing appliance removal access.

Include appliance condition, floor level, stairs, gates, parking or loading details and carrying distance. Apartment, mixed-use and commercial properties should include elevator, loading-zone or property-management requirements before scheduling.
