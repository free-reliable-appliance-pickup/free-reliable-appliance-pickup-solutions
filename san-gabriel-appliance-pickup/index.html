San Gabriel Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup route, the City of San Gabriel directs residents to Athens Services for bulky-item collection. The City says bulky items can include refrigerators and that both single-family and multi-family properties can request service.

This public waste-service option is separate from our qualification-based appliance pickup. Confirm current item limits and scheduling rules with the City or Athens before setting anything outside.

San Gabriel Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is free appliance pickup guaranteed in San Gabriel?

No. Free pickup depends on appliance condition, exact address, safe removal access, local demand and current route availability.

Can one working appliance qualify?

Yes. A single fully working or reusable refrigerator, washer, dryer, freezer, stove or range can be submitted for review.

Can nonworking appliances be submitted?

Yes, but a single nonworking appliance normally does not qualify for free pickup. Minor-repair units and qualifying mixed loads may still be reviewed.

Do you review apartment or inside-home pickup?

Yes. Include floor level, stairs, doorway limits, parking and carrying distance before scheduling.
