What Happens After a Qualifying Pickup?

Our first preference is to keep useful appliances in circulation when practical. A working or reusable appliance may be cleaned, tested, resold, reused or used for recoverable parts. Appliances that are no longer practical to reuse should go through an appropriate recycling or disposal path. Refrigerators and freezers require refrigerant-aware handling at end of life.

We do not promise the same final destination for every appliance because condition and local options vary. For end-of-life guidance, see our Free Appliance Recycling Near Me guide and refrigerator pickup guide.

What We Review

Washers & Dryers

Working laundry machines are a priority. For washers, report whether the machine fills, drains and spins. For dryers, state gas or electric and whether it tumbles and heats.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Include exterior and interior photos, approximate size and current cooling status. If the unit is still plugged in and cold, say so.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric units may be reviewed when burners, oven functions and controls work and the removal path is practical.

Multiple Appliances

Submit a complete property cleanout or replacement load together so the working percentage and route needs can be evaluated accurately.

Access Details Matter

A garage pickup, upstairs apartment, gated community and inside kitchen removal require different planning. Tell us the floor level, stair flights, elevator availability, gates, long carries, narrow doors, parking restrictions and whether appliance doors may need removal.

For property managers or landlords with several units, list every appliance and its condition. If the items are at multiple addresses, explain that in the request instead of combining them as though they are at one property.

Important Qualification Limits

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances. Minor repairs can be considered when the issue is described accurately.

A submitted request begins review. It does not guarantee acceptance, a specific pickup date or a particular arrival time.

Inland Empire Appliance Pickup FAQs

Is appliance pickup free in the Inland Empire?

It can be free for qualifying working appliances or eligible mixed loads. Condition, demand, access and route availability all matter.

Do you offer appliance removal near me?

Use the dedicated city page when one matches your address, or submit through this Inland Empire hub with your city and ZIP code so the request can be routed correctly.

Which appliances receive priority?

Working washers, dryers and laundry sets receive strong priority. Reusable refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges are also reviewed.

Can you haul away an old refrigerator in the Inland Empire?

Qualifying refrigerators can be reviewed for free pickup and removal. Send cooling condition, clear interior and exterior photos, the exact pickup address and access details. A nonworking refrigerator is not automatically eligible for free pickup.

Do you remove washers and dryers?

Yes. Working washers, dryers and complete sets receive strong priority. Send basic test results, photos and access details for route review.

Do you remove stoves, ranges and freezers?

Qualifying stoves, ranges, ovens and freezers can be reviewed. Tell us whether the major functions work and include photos, size and removal access.

Will you pick up a broken appliance for free?

Not automatically. Free pickup is qualification-based. Fully working or reusable appliances receive priority, while broken or nonworking appliances are considered only when current local demand and route capacity support them.
