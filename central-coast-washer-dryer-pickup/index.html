Qualification & Route Review

Working washers, dryers and complete sets receive the strongest consideration. Free pickup is confirmed only after working condition, exact location, stairs or elevator access, parking, carrying distance, local demand and current route availability are reviewed.

For faster review, send clear photos plus washer fill/drain/spin results and dryer tumble/heat results. Include whether the dryer is gas or electric when known.

For refrigerators, freezers, stoves and other major appliances, use the Central Coast appliance pickup hub. For statewide routing, see California appliance pickup.

Central Coast laundry requests: explain loading and access

For a washer or dryer in Ventura, Oxnard, Santa Barbara, Santa Maria or San Luis Obispo, submit the city where the appliances are actually located. A coastal area listing does not confirm that a particular address is on an active route. Please identify an upstairs unit, limited driveway access, a shared building entrance or any long carrying path before a pickup is discussed. If the appliances are in a rental, confirm that you are authorized to release them for reuse or removal.

Photos are especially helpful for laundry sets: show both machines separately, their control panels, and any visible damage. State what has been tested, including whether a washer drains and spins and whether a dryer tumbles and heats. Do not represent an untested machine as fully working. Gas-dryer disconnection should be handled safely by a qualified person. After reviewing condition, exact ZIP, safe access and local availability, we can determine whether a no-cost pickup is possible.
