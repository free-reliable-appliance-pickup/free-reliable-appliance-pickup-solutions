Sacramento County Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Working and reusable major appliances receive the strongest priority. A fully working refrigerator, freezer, stove, range or oven may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify under the standard free route.

Send useful details first: exact address or ZIP, appliance type, tested condition, clear photos, floor level, stairs, gates, parking/loading restrictions and carrying distance.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

How Sacramento County Routing Differs by Address

Sacramento County includes the City of Sacramento, incorporated suburban cities and unincorporated communities. Public disposal programs, collection rules and pickup providers can differ by service address, so we do not treat the entire county as one municipal system.

Inside Sacramento City

The City of Sacramento says residential collection customers can schedule two free appliance and e-waste appointments per calendar year, with up to two appliances per appointment. That public program is separate from our reuse-focused pickup review.

City appliance and e-waste pickup information

Unincorporated Sacramento County

Sacramento County says eligible curbside customers in unincorporated areas receive one bulky-waste pickup every 12 months as part of standard service, and appliances must be identified when the appointment is scheduled.

Self-Haul Alternatives

Sacramento County also states residents may drop off up to two large appliances per day at no fee at the North Area Recovery Station or Kiefer Landfill, subject to current facility rules.

Sacramento County Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free everywhere in Sacramento County?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based. Condition, exact address, access, local demand and current route or partner capacity are reviewed before service is confirmed.

Should Sacramento city residents use this page?

You can start here, but the dedicated Sacramento city page is the primary URL for a pickup address inside the city.

What if the appliance is broken?

A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for the standard free route. Public municipal or county programs may be a better fit when reuse is not practical.

Can I submit several appliances from a rental or apartment turnover?

Yes. List every appliance separately, describe the working condition, and include stairs, elevators, gates, parking and carrying distance.
