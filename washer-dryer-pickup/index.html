Looking for Washer or Dryer Pickup Near Me?

People search in different ways: free washer pickup, dryer removal, washer and dryer haul-away, appliance recycling near me, or simply appliance pickup. You do not need to know which regional page to use before requesting service. Send the exact pickup address or ZIP code and we review the request against the active local route.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Fully working washers and dryers receive strong priority. A single nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify, and scheduling depends on current local coverage.

What Laundry Appliances Receive Priority?

Working washer and dryer sets

Complete working sets receive particularly strong priority because both machines can often stay together for reuse.

Working washers

Tell us whether the washer fills, washes or agitates, drains and reaches the final spin.

Working dryers

Tell us whether the dryer powers on, tumbles and heats, and identify gas or electric when known.

Minor-repair machines

Minor issues can be described for review, but free pickup is not guaranteed for a machine that needs repair.

How to Describe Washer Condition

Report whether the machine powers on, fills with water, agitates or washes, drains completely and spins. Mention leaks, loud bearing noise, error codes, a damaged lid or door lock, or water left in the drum. For front-load washers, note visible gasket damage or strong odor.

Do not leave water inside a washer. If it cannot drain, tell us before scheduling because retained water adds weight and can spill during removal.

How to Describe Dryer Condition

Tell us whether the dryer powers on, tumbles and produces heat. Identify gas or electric if known and mention unusual noise, damaged controls, door problems or a unit that tumbles but does not heat. A photo of the model label or rear connection can help when the fuel type is uncertain.

Inside Pickup, Stairs and Laundry Closets

Inside pickup may be reviewed from garages, laundry rooms, basements, apartments and upper floors. Include the floor level, number of stair flights, elevator access, narrow doors, tight hallways, gates, parking or loading restrictions, and whether the machines are stacked or installed inside a closet.

Clarify whether stacked machines are two separate units with a stacking kit or a one-piece laundry center. Do not disconnect a gas appliance unless you are qualified to do so.

Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Do I have to type my city when searching for pickup?

No. You can request service from the main form and provide the actual pickup address. City-specific pages are available for many priority markets, but the request is routed using your submitted location.

Can I submit only one washer or dryer?

Yes. A fully working single washer or dryer can be reviewed. Complete working sets receive strong priority.

Can a broken washer or dryer be picked up free?

A single nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify. Describe the issue accurately so it can be reviewed.

What about multiple appliances?

Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. List the condition of every item.

Free Pickup Rules

Fully working appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Submitting a request does not guarantee free pickup. Appliance condition, access, location, local demand and route availability are reviewed before scheduling.
