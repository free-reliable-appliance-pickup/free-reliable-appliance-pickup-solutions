Free Washing Machine Pickup

Looking for free washing machine pickup near you? Tell us whether the washer is top-load, front-load, compact, stackable or part of a matching set. For a working washer, report whether it powers on, fills with water, washes or agitates, drains and reaches the final spin.

If the washer has a problem, describe it accurately. Useful details include no spin, no drain, leaking, loud bearings, an error code, damaged controls, a lid-lock problem, a torn front-load gasket or water left inside the drum.

Do not hide a known problem. Repair-focused local buyers are more likely to accept a machine when they know exactly what they are evaluating before they drive to the pickup.

Free Dryer Pickup

For a dryer pickup request, identify gas or electric when known and tell us whether the dryer powers on, tumbles and produces heat. Mention no-heat problems, unusual noise, damaged controls, a broken door switch or a dryer that runs but takes too long to dry.

Gas and electric dryers may both be accepted. Do not disconnect a gas appliance unless you are qualified to do so. If the dryer is stacked, installed in a closet or on an upper floor, include those details before scheduling.

Why Local Appliance Buyers Want Washers & Dryers

Laundry machines have several possible second-life paths. A fully working washer or dryer may be reused immediately. A repairable machine may be restored by a technician. A matching set may be more attractive because both machines can be placed together. Certain machines can also provide useful components to legitimate repair businesses.

This creates stronger pickup demand than simply treating every machine as scrap. Our goal is to route useful laundry appliances toward local people who can reuse, repair or responsibly handle them.

Important: high demand does not mean every washer or dryer automatically qualifies for free pickup. Severely damaged, incomplete, contaminated, inaccessible or out-of-route machines may still be declined.

Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Can I submit only one washer?

Yes. Single working washers receive strong priority, and selected repair-focused markets may consider a washer that needs repair.

Can I submit only one dryer?

Yes. Tell us whether it is gas or electric, whether the drum turns and whether the dryer heats.

Do broken washers and dryers qualify?

Sometimes, depending on the market. Repair-focused local partners may accept some nonworking machines. Working appliances remain the easiest to match.

Are matching washer and dryer sets preferred?

Complete working sets receive especially strong priority because both machines can often remain together for reuse or resale.

Can landlords submit multiple machines?

Yes. List every washer and dryer, condition, location and access details in the same request when possible.

Is pickup available nationwide?

Requests are accepted nationwide, but actual free pickup depends on local coverage, demand, condition and route availability.
