What Condition Details Help Us Review a Washer or Dryer?

Washer details

Tell us whether it powers on, fills with water, agitates or tumbles, drains and reaches the final spin. Mention leaks, loud bearing noise, error codes, lid-lock problems or water left in the drum.

Dryer details

Tell us whether the dryer is gas or electric, whether it powers on, whether the drum turns and whether it heats. Mention unusual noise, no-heat problems or damaged controls.

Matching set details

If the washer and dryer match, submit both together. Include brand/model information when available and mention pedestals or stacking kits that are part of the set.

Access Matters Before Pickup Is Confirmed

Pickup review is not based on the machine alone. Tell us where the appliances are located and what the removal path looks like so the pickup route can be reviewed safely and efficiently.

Floor and stairs

Include the floor level, number of stair flights and whether an elevator is available.

Doors, gates and hallways

Mention narrow doors, tight turns, side-yard gates, long hallways or other access restrictions.

Garage and driveway

For garages or outdoor locations, include driveway grade, gate access and carrying distance to the loading point.

Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Can I submit one washer or one dryer?

Yes. Single working machines can be submitted for review.

Can a broken washer or dryer qualify?

Sometimes. It depends on the problem, local repair-focused demand, access and route availability.

Are matching sets preferred?

Complete working sets receive especially strong priority because both machines can often be routed together.

Can you review stacked machines?

Yes. Tell us that the units are stacked and include floor level, stairs and any tight access details.

Is free pickup guaranteed?

No. Every request is reviewed for appliance condition, demand, access and local availability before pickup is confirmed.
