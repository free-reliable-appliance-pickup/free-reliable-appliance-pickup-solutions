Irwindale Washer & Dryer Pickup Review

Working washers, dryers and complete matching sets receive the strongest consideration. A single working machine can also be reviewed. Repairable equipment may sometimes be considered when condition, parts demand and current route capacity make pickup practical.

Tell us what each machine actually does. For washers, report fill, wash or agitation, drain and spin. For dryers, report whether the drum turns and heats and whether the machine is gas or electric.

For faster review: include clear photos, brand/model if known, tested functions, floor level, stairs, gates, driveway or parking access and carrying distance.

Access Details That Matter in Irwindale

Garage or interior

Tell us whether the machines are in a garage, laundry room, apartment, utility room or outside area and whether they are already disconnected.

Stairs and tight turns

Include stair flights, narrow doors, hallway turns, elevators and any stacked installation that changes the removal plan.

Vehicle access

Describe gates, driveway length, street parking, loading restrictions and the carrying distance from the machines to the truck.

Complete sets

Submit both machines together when they are a pair. A complete working set is easier to evaluate accurately as one request.

Irwindale Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Can one working washer or dryer qualify?

Yes. Single working machines can be reviewed. Complete working sets generally receive stronger priority.

Should I send photos before scheduling?

Yes. Clear appliance and access photos make qualification more accurate and help prevent problems on pickup day.

Can nonworking equipment qualify?

Sometimes, depending on condition and current local demand, but free pickup is not guaranteed for nonworking equipment.
