San Bernardino Stove, Range & Oven Pickup Review

Working and reusable cooking appliances receive priority. A single nonworking stove, range or oven does not automatically qualify for free pickup.

San Bernardino requests can involve older homes, apartments, garages, side gates and multi-unit properties. Include stairs, narrow doorways, alley or parking access, floor level and carrying distance before a route is confirmed.

Gas or electric

Identify the fuel type and whether the appliance is freestanding, slide-in or built-in. Do not guess about utility disconnection.

What to test

Report burner or element operation, oven heat, ignition and control-panel condition when safely testable.

Removal access

Mention stairs, narrow kitchen exits, floor level, gates, parking distance and whether the appliance is already safely disconnected.
