What Usually Qualifies for Free Laundry Pickup?

Working washer

Tell us whether it powers on, fills, agitates or washes, drains and spins. A short description of the test helps the request get reviewed faster.

Working dryer

Report whether it powers on, tumbles and heats. State whether the dryer is gas or electric.

Washer & dryer set

Submit both machines together when they are a pair. Include photos and condition details for each machine.

Needs repair

Repairable machines can be reviewed when the problem is described clearly, but a single completely nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Mixed appliance loads: a multi-item load may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are not accepted alone.

San Gabriel Valley Laundry Pickup FAQs

Can I request only a washer?

Yes. A working washer can be reviewed by itself. Report whether it fills, drains and spins and send clear photos.

Can I request only a dryer?

Yes. Tell us whether it tumbles and heats and whether it is gas or electric.

Do stairs automatically disqualify a pickup?

No, but stairs and carrying distance affect the review. Include the floor level and number of flights before scheduling.

Do you guarantee same-day pickup?

No. Requests are reviewed by condition, access, demand and route availability. A submitted form is a request, not a guaranteed appointment.

Is this a city trash service?

No. This is a qualification-based reuse pickup network. Public waste or bulky-item programs are separate services with their own rules.
