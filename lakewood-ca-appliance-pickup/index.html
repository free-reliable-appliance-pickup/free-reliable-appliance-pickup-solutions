What Major Appliances Usually Qualify in Lakewood?

Refrigerators & Freezers

Working cooling appliances receive strong consideration. Keep them plugged in when practical and send interior, exterior and model-label photos.

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, report gas or electric type, power, tumble and heat.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Identify gas or electric and report burner, control and oven operation. Clear front and cooktop photos help with review.

Mixed Appliance Loads

Multiple-item loads may qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. A single nonworking appliance usually does not qualify.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are not accepted by themselves.

Condition proof
