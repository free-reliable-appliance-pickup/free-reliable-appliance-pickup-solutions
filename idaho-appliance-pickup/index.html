What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

What to Include in Your Request

Tell us the appliance type, whether it works, your city or ZIP code, floor level, stairs and access details. Include photos when available and mention narrow doors, gates, basements or other removal concerns.

For washers, tell us whether they fill, drain and spin. For dryers, tell us whether they heat and tumble. For refrigerators and freezers, tell us whether they maintain temperature.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is every Idaho appliance pickup free?

No. Each request is reviewed based on appliance condition, location, access, local demand and available coverage.

Do you pick up washers and dryers?

Yes. Washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are priority categories.

Can I submit several appliances?

Yes. List every appliance and its condition in one request.
