Idaho Pickup Requests

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens. Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Free pickup is confirmed only after condition, location, access, demand and available route or partner coverage are reviewed.

Send useful details: city/ZIP, appliance type, working condition, photos, floor level, stairs and access restrictions.

Primary Idaho Region

Boise & Treasure Valley

Boise, Meridian, Nampa, Caldwell, Eagle, Kuna, Star and surrounding communities through one regional hub.

Other Idaho Requests

Eastern, south-central and north Idaho requests may be reviewed when route or partner coverage is available. A submitted location does not itself guarantee free pickup.

We organize geographic information around meaningful service regions rather than creating separate search landing pages for every city.

Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance alone generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Condition and Access

For washers include fill, drain and spin. For dryers include heat/tumble and gas or electric. For refrigerators/freezers include cooling status and clear photos. For stoves include fuel type and known problems.

Report garages, basements, upper floors, stairs, elevators, gates, narrow doors and other removal restrictions before scheduling.
