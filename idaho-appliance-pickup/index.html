How Appliance Pickup Works

  1. Tell us which appliance you need picked up.
  2. Tell us whether the appliance is working or needs repair.
  3. Send clear pictures of the appliance.
  4. Include stairs, floor level and access information.
  5. We review the request before confirming pickup.

Qualifying appliances may receive free pickup. Approval depends on appliance type, condition, location and pickup accessibility.
