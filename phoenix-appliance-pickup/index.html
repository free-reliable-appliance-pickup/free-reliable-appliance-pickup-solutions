Appliances We Review

Washers & Dryers

Working laundry appliances and complete sets are priority categories. Tell us whether washers fill, drain and spin and whether dryers tumble and heat.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the appliance is currently plugged in and maintaining temperature. Interior and exterior photos are helpful.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Gas and electric cooking appliances may qualify. Include whether burners and the oven operate normally.

Multiple Appliances

Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. List the condition of every appliance separately.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
