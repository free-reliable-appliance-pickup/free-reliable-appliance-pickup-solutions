Free Reuse Pickup vs. City of Phoenix Appliance Recycling

Our service is qualification-based and focuses first on working or reusable appliances. For Phoenix residential solid-waste customers, the City of Phoenix separately offers transfer-station drop-off and scheduled appliance pickup.

Qualifying Reuse Pickup

If your washer, dryer, refrigerator, freezer, stove or range is working or reusable, submit it here first. When the request qualifies and local coverage is available, pickup can be free.

City Non-Refrigerant Pickup

The City of Phoenix lists a $30 scheduled curbside charge for up to five non-refrigerant appliances such as washers, dryers and stoves.

City Refrigerant Pickup

The City lists a $25 curbside charge per refrigerant appliance such as a refrigerator or freezer. Residential customers can also drop off one refrigerant appliance per year at a city transfer station at no cost under the current program.

If your appliance does not qualify for our route, review the official City of Phoenix appliance recycling program.
