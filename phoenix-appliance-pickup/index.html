Phoenix Appliance Pickup & Removal

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and qualifying multiple-appliance loads in Phoenix. Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Free pickup depends on condition, access, local demand and available coverage.

Appliances We Review

Working washers and dryers are priority categories. For refrigerators and freezers, tell us whether the appliance is currently plugged in and maintaining temperature. Gas and electric cooking appliances may also qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Phoenix Pickup Details

Include your exact ZIP code, appliance condition, clear photos and access information. Tell us whether the appliance is inside, in a garage or outside, and mention stairs, gates, narrow doors or other removal issues. For refrigerators and freezers stored in hot garages, current cooling condition is especially important.

Prepare Your Request

Submitting complete information helps us determine eligibility and whether an available route can handle the pickup. Service is confirmed only after review.
