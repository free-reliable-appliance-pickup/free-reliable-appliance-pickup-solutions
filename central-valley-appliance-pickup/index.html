Central Valley Coverage

We use one useful regional page rather than separate near-duplicate pages for every city. Requests may be reviewed from Stockton, Modesto, Tracy, Sacramento-area communities, Fresno, Visalia, Bakersfield, Yuba City and nearby Central Valley locations.

Because distances are substantial, exact ZIP code, appliance condition, number of appliances and route availability matter. A community being listed does not guarantee free pickup.

What We Review

Appliance condition

Working and reusable washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. Tell us exactly what functions correctly.

Load size

Multiple qualifying appliances can make longer Central Valley routes more practical. List every appliance and its condition in the same request.

Pickup access

Tell us whether appliances are in a garage, driveway, house, apartment, upstairs unit or property with gates, stairs or narrow access.

Longer Routes Need Better Information

Central Valley requests can involve longer driving distances than compact metro routes. Clear photos, model information and accurate working-condition details help us decide whether a route is practical before anyone travels. Landlords and property managers should list all available appliances together when clearing multiple units or properties.

Helpful request: city/ZIP + appliance type + condition + photos + floor/stairs + access + total number of appliances.

Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

California Contact

Central Valley requests use 310-774-4304. Service hours are 8:00 AM–8:00 PM daily. Contact starts a review; pickup is confirmed only after location, condition and route availability are evaluated.
