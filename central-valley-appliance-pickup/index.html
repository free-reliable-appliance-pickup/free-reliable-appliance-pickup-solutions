A Regional Hub for California's Central Valley

The Central Valley covers a long north-to-south corridor, so pickup planning is different from a compact metro area. This page groups requests into one useful regional resource rather than creating near-duplicate pages for every city. Requests may be reviewed from Stockton, Modesto, Tracy, Sacramento-area communities, Merced, Fresno, Visalia, Bakersfield, Yuba City and nearby locations.

A city being listed does not guarantee free pickup. Longer distances make appliance condition, total load size, city or ZIP code, access and current route availability especially important.

Best request format: city/ZIP + every appliance + working condition + clear photos + floor/stairs + access + total number of items.

What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Working washers and dryers are high-priority categories. Refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens are also reviewed. For refrigerators and freezers, tell us whether the unit is plugged in and cooling. For washers, describe fill, drain and spin. For dryers, tell us whether the drum turns and whether the unit heats.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Why Load Size Matters on Longer Routes

Central Valley driving distances can be substantial. A single appliance that is far from an active route may not be practical, while several working appliances at one stop can be easier to match with a route. Landlords, property managers and businesses should list all available appliances together and identify whether they are at one property or multiple addresses.

For rental turnovers, include the unit count, appliance condition for each item, whether appliances are already disconnected, and whether they can be staged in a garage or common loading area. This helps us evaluate the request before anyone drives to the property.

Access Details Can Change the Pickup Plan

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, house, apartment, upstairs unit, basement, storage room or commercial property. Include stairs, elevators, gates, narrow hallways, tight kitchen or laundry-room doors, parking restrictions and any long carry distance.

For large refrigerators, send a photo of the exit path when doorway clearance is tight. For stacked laundry equipment, say whether the units can be separated. For gas dryers and ranges, identify the fuel type when possible.

Central Valley Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is free pickup guaranteed if my city is listed?

No. Every request is reviewed for appliance condition, distance, local demand, access and available coverage before pickup is confirmed.

Can a broken appliance be picked up for free by itself?

Generally no. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered, and mixed loads may qualify when most of the major appliances work.

Do multiple appliances help?

They can. For longer routes, several working appliances at one stop may be easier to match with available capacity than one distant appliance.

What phone number should I use?

Central Valley requests use 310-774-4304. Service hours are 8:00 AM–8:00 PM daily.
