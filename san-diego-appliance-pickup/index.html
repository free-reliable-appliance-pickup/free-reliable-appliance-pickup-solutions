San Diego Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens for qualifying pickup in San Diego. Working and reusable appliances receive the strongest priority. Free pickup is confirmed only after condition, exact location, access, local demand and available route coverage are reviewed.

Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify under the general free-pickup rules.

Send useful details first: appliance type, working condition, exact city or ZIP, floor level, stairs, access restrictions and clear photos.

San Diego Appliance Pickup vs. Local Recycling Options

San Diego is different from several other California cities because the City does not currently provide a general curbside bulky-item pickup service. That makes it especially useful to compare reuse-focused pickup with regional recycling and drop-off options.

Our pickup review

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges can be reviewed with photos, condition, access and ZIP code. Reuse and repair value are the strongest factors.

City guidance

The City of San Diego directs residents to WasteFreeSD for local appliance reuse, recycling and disposal options. City guidance also notes that major appliances should not go into regular curbside bins.

Miramar recycling option

The City's Miramar Recycling Center publishes fees for large household appliances and separate pricing for refrigerators, freezers and other CFC-containing equipment. Customers should verify current acceptance and fees before self-hauling.
