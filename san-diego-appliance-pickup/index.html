What Qualifies for Free Pickup in San Diego?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Working and reusable washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. Pickup must be confirmed after the appliance condition, location, access, current demand and available coverage are reviewed.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Refrigerator Pickup

Top-freezer, bottom-freezer, side-by-side and French-door refrigerators. Send an interior photo and tell us whether both compartments maintain temperature.

Chest and upright household freezers in reusable condition. Include an interior photo and whether the freezer maintains freezing temperature.

Stove & Range Pickup

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may qualify. Describe whether the burners and oven operate normally.

Apartment, Rental and Property Access

San Diego pickup requests can involve upstairs apartments, condo buildings, garages, narrow halls and managed loading areas. Tell us the floor level, stair flights, elevator availability, gates, doorway width concerns and parking or loading restrictions.

For a rental turnover or multiple-unit property, list each appliance and its condition separately. If building management requires a loading reservation or specific access time, include that information before pickup is confirmed.

San Diego Appliance Pickup Questions

Is appliance pickup free in San Diego?

Qualifying fully working appliances and eligible mixed loads may receive free pickup after review. Free pickup is not automatic for every appliance.

Do you pick up a broken appliance by itself for free?

No. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work.

Do you pick up washers and dryers?

Yes. Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are priority categories.

Do you serve areas outside the City of San Diego?

Selected surrounding San Diego County requests can be submitted for review. Availability depends on the specific city, ZIP code, appliance load, access and current route coverage.

How do I request pickup?

Use the online request form or call or text 310-774-4304 with appliance condition, photos, city or ZIP code and access details.
