Corona Appliance Pickup & Removal Review

Corona sits between the western Riverside County and Inland Empire route clusters. We review requests from homeowners, renters, landlords, property managers and businesses for working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and qualifying multi-appliance loads.

Send the appliance type, exact address, honest operating condition and access details before scheduling. Working major appliances receive priority because the free service depends on reuse or resale value rather than ordinary junk hauling.

Free pickup is qualification-based. A fully working major appliance may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup.

Appliances We Review in Corona

Washers & Dryers

Tell us whether a washer fills, agitates, drains and spins. For dryers, state whether the unit is gas or electric and whether it tumbles and heats.

Refrigerators

Send exterior and interior photos, approximate size and current cooling status. If it is still plugged in and cold, say so in the request.

Freezers

Chest and upright freezers can be reviewed. Confirm that the appliance maintains freezing temperature and is empty enough to move safely.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric cooking appliances may qualify when burners, oven functions and controls work and removal access is practical.

Corona Access Details Can Change the Pickup Plan

Corona properties range from street-level garages to hillside driveways, apartments and gated communities. Include stairs, gate codes or gate procedures, elevator access, narrow turns, long carries, parking restrictions and whether appliance doors may need removal.

For an inside refrigerator pickup, measure the appliance and the narrowest doorway when clearance looks close. For upstairs laundry equipment, state the number of flights and whether the staircase has landings or tight turns. Accurate access information helps prevent a route from arriving with the wrong equipment or crew plan.

Appliance Recycling & Bulky-Item Alternatives in Corona

If your item does not qualify for our service, the City of Corona publishes a separate residential bulky-item program administered through WM. The city currently lists refrigerators, washers and dryers among accepted bulk items and explains that residential customers may schedule qualifying special collections under its own limits and preparation rules.

That city program is independent from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup. Confirm current eligibility and scheduling requirements directly with the city or WM before placing an appliance at the curb.

Mixed Loads, Microwaves & Dishwashers

When replacing several appliances at one property, submit the complete load in one request. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work, but every item must be described accurately.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances. These smaller or lower-demand items are not accepted as standalone free pickups.

Submitting a form starts a review. It does not guarantee acceptance, a particular day or a specific arrival time.

Do you pick up every appliance for free in Corona?

No. Free pickup depends on appliance condition, load composition, access, demand and route availability.

Yes. Include clear photos and tell us whether the refrigerator and freezer sections are cooling normally.

Can landlords submit several units at once?

Yes. List each appliance and its condition, and explain whether the items are at one address or several nearby units.
