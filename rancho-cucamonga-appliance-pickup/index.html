Appliance Removal, Recycling & Disposal Options in Rancho Cucamonga

If a major appliance is still working or reusable, start with our free-pickup review. If it is end-of-life or you need guaranteed paid hauling, Rancho Cucamonga also has municipal and private removal options.

Our reuse-focused route

Send photos, condition and the exact address. Qualifying major appliances can be picked up at no charge after acceptance.

City / Burrtec bulky-item service

The City says eligible single-family residential customers can use Burrtec bulky-item collection. The current residential guide describes up to four free collections per year, with up to five bulky items per collection. Verify current eligibility with Burrtec at (909) 987-3717.

Paid removal or self-haul

For broken appliances or guaranteed timing, a paid hauler may be the better fit. San Bernardino County also publishes current landfill fees and accepted-waste rules for self-haul customers.

Microwaves and household hazardous waste: the City lists the Household Hazardous Waste facility at 8794 Lion Street for eligible household hazardous/electronic items, including microwave ovens. Check the official rules before visiting.

Cooling appliances: do not cut refrigerant lines or remove a compressor yourself. Use an appropriate refrigerant-recovery or disposal process for end-of-life refrigerators and freezers.

What Happens to the Appliance?

Reusable appliances may stay in service through reuse, repair or resale. End-of-life appliances should follow an appropriate recycling or disposal path. Cooling appliances require refrigerant-aware handling; see the EPA appliance-disposal guidance.

Rancho Cucamonga Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is pickup really free?

It can be. Free pickup is confirmed after we review the appliance condition, address and removal access.

Can you take a broken appliance?

A single completely nonworking appliance normally does not qualify for the free reuse-focused route. Minor repair needs or qualifying mixed loads can still be reviewed.

Do I need to put it at the curb?

No. Keep a reusable appliance protected until pickup is confirmed. Garage, driveway and some inside pickups can be reviewed.

Do I need to disconnect it first?

Tell us how it is installed. Do not cut refrigerant lines or disconnect a gas line unless it can be done safely by an appropriate person.

What if it does not qualify?

Compare the City/Burrtec bulky-item program, a qualified paid hauler or an appropriate self-haul option.

Do you handle business or property-manager pickups?

Yes. Apartments, senior communities, property managers, installers and other businesses can submit one-time or recurring appliance loads for review.
