Rancho Cucamonga Appliance Pickup FAQ

Do you pick up water heaters for free in Rancho Cucamonga?

No. Water heaters are not part of our free major-appliance pickup program. Use the appropriate City/Burrtec option or a qualified paid removal service.

Do I need to put the appliance at the curb before pickup?

No. Keep a working or reusable appliance protected until pickup is confirmed. Garage, driveway and some inside pickups can be reviewed when access is safe; send photos and access details first.

Do I need to disconnect the appliance before pickup?

Tell us how it is installed. Do not cut refrigerator/freezer refrigerant lines or disconnect a gas line unless it can be done safely by an appropriate person.

What happens to a refrigerator or freezer that cannot be reused?

End-of-life cooling appliances should follow an appropriate refrigerant-aware recycling or disposal path. Refrigerant must be recovered before final disposal; do not cut refrigerant lines or remove the compressor yourself.

Where can I take a microwave or household hazardous waste in Rancho Cucamonga?

The City HHW facility at 8794 Lion Street accepts microwave ovens and listed household hazardous/electronic items from eligible county residents. The HHW facility is not the destination for a full-size refrigerator, freezer, washer or dryer; check the official HHW rules.

How much does appliance removal cost in Rancho Cucamonga?

How soon can appliance pickup be arranged in Rancho Cucamonga?

Do you deliver or move new appliances in Rancho Cucamonga?
