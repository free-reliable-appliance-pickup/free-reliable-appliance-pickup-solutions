Chino Hills Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Chino Hills requests are evaluated through our Inland Empire route network. Working and reusable appliances receive priority, and the address plus removal path are reviewed before scheduling. This is especially important for hillside homes, gated communities and locations where the loading area may be farther from the appliance.

For searches such as appliance pickup, appliance removal, appliance recycling, washer pickup, refrigerator pickup or appliance removal near me in Chino Hills, send the exact address or ZIP, appliance condition and access details so the current 909 route can be reviewed.

Qualification rule: fully working major appliances may receive free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single broken appliance does not qualify for our free service.

Condition Details That Help

Laundry

For washers, describe filling, draining and spinning. For dryers, describe tumbling and heat. If you have a set, submit both units together.

Cold appliances

For refrigerators and freezers, tell us whether the unit maintains temperature and whether shelves, drawers and doors are complete.

Cooking appliances

For stoves or ranges, include gas or electric type and any known burner or oven issue. Clear photos help with size and condition.

Driveway, Gate & Interior Access

Describe the exact pickup location: garage, driveway, first floor, upper floor, basement or outside. Include stairs, tight turns, narrow doors, gate codes, loading restrictions and any long carry to a safe truck position. If a refrigerator is in a tight kitchen opening, include a photo of the doorway and route out.

Rental owners and property managers can submit several appliances together. List every item separately so we can review the complete load and working percentage.

Microwave & Dishwasher Limits

Microwaves are accepted only when included with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Minor repair issues may be reviewed, but a completely nonworking appliance by itself is not eligible for free pickup.

A request is not a confirmed appointment until the route, load and access have been approved.

Chino Hills Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify, the City of Chino Hills publishes bulky-item service information through its waste provider. The City currently lists stoves, washers, dryers and refrigerators among bulky items and explains the number of included pickups and current scheduling rules.
