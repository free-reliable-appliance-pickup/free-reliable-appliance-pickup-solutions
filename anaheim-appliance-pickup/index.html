Appliance Types Anaheim Customers Can Submit

Washers

Working top-load and front-load washers are useful requests. Tell us whether the machine fills, drains and spins and note any leaks, noise or error codes.

Dryers

State whether the dryer is gas or electric and whether it tumbles and heats. Complete laundry sets receive strong priority.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Cooling status is essential. Send exterior and interior photos and tell us whether the unit is currently plugged in and maintaining temperature.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Gas and electric cooking appliances can be submitted. Describe the burners, oven, controls and any known problems.

A nonworking appliance alone does not qualify for free pickup. Microwaves require another qualifying major appliance; dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Anaheim Apartments, Rentals & Multi-Unit Properties

Anaheim has many pickup situations where access matters as much as the appliance. If an item is in an apartment or upstairs unit, tell us the floor level, number of stair flights and whether a usable elevator is available. Include gate codes or access instructions only through the request process, and tell us about parking or loading limitations before scheduling.

Property managers and landlords replacing several appliances can submit them as one group. List each item, its working condition and the unit or location. If everything will be staged in one garage or outside area, say so. If access is required to several units, explain that instead.

For large refrigerators, measure or photograph a tight exit route if necessary. An appliance that fits the kitchen opening may still be too wide for a hallway or exterior door without removing refrigerator doors.
