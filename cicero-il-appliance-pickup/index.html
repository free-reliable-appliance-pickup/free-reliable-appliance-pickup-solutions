Cicero appliance location and moving access

Cicero has many two- and three-flat buildings and attached rear entries, with appliance access that may involve shared porches, lower-level laundry spaces or narrow walkways. A tenant or property manager should specify the unit and floor, whether the appliance passes through a narrow rear doorway and where a vehicle could safely load. Do not submit a Chicago or Berwyn address as Cicero to try to obtain a wider service area.

Report the condition of each machine

For older but working top-load washers, tell us about filling, spinning, draining and leaks. For dryers, whether heat works and whether the unit is electric or gas are essential. Used refrigerators should have confirmed cooling, intact door seals and photos of the inside. On cooking ranges, note missing knobs, burner ignition, oven temperature and whether a qualified person is needed to disconnect gas.

Refrigerators, stairs, and multiple-appliance requests

When several appliances are being replaced in one property, list each washer, dryer, stove or refrigerator individually and identify any units that do not work. Add images and precise dimensions for large models, and do not leave an appliance unattended on a porch or sidewalk expecting pickup. If there are second-floor stairs or basement turns, describe them before a potential operator evaluates the job.

Cicero, Illinois: blue-bag recycling is not appliance disposal

The Town of Cicero Public Works recycling program describes blue bags for household recyclables such as paper, plastics and aluminum. That program does not by itself authorize placing a refrigerator, washer, dryer or range in a recycling bag or leaving it at the curb. For the current appliance collection process, contact Cicero Public Works at 708-656-3600, ext. 217, or check with the waste service contracted for your specific building.

Illinois EPA explains that appliance white goods require proper treatment of refrigerants and other regulated components before disposal. Do not cut a refrigerator's cooling lines, remove gas connections yourself or leave a machine outside simply because you submitted a reuse request here. Our pickup request review and town services are unrelated, and neither is a guaranteed appointment.
