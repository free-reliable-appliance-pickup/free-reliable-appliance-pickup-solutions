What Receives Priority?

Freezers that reach and hold freezing temperature, have working lids or doors, intact baskets or shelves and no major cabinet damage receive priority. Clean working chest and upright freezers can be strong pickup candidates.

What Should I Include?

Tell us whether the freezer is currently plugged in and freezing, whether it is chest-style or upright, approximate dimensions, condition of the lid or door and any known electrical or temperature problems.

Useful Photos and Access Details

A clear exterior photo, interior photo and model-label photo are ideal. If the freezer is in a garage, basement, shed or utility room, explain stairs, narrow doors, tight turns, gates and the path to the pickup vehicle.

Do Broken Freezers Qualify?

Not automatically. A single nonworking freezer generally does not qualify for free pickup. Working appliances are prioritized; mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% are working.

How to Describe Freezer Condition

Temperature and operation

Tell us whether the freezer is holding a freezing temperature. Mention heavy frost, warm spots, compressor noise, electrical issues or a unit that runs without freezing correctly. If it has already been unplugged, say whether it was working before disconnection.

Chest vs. upright

For chest freezers, note whether the lid seals correctly and whether baskets are present. For upright freezers, mention door seal condition, shelves, drawers and whether the door closes securely.

Pickup Preparation

Remove all food and personal items, keep the path clear and do not move a working freezer into a harder-to-access location. If the freezer is working and safely accessible, keep it plugged in when practical until its condition has been confirmed.
