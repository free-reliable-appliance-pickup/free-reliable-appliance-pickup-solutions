What receives priority?

Freezers that reach and hold freezing temperature, have working lids or doors, intact baskets or shelves and no major cabinet damage receive priority. Clean working chest and upright freezers can be strong pickup candidates.

What should I include?

Tell us whether the freezer is currently plugged in and freezing, whether it is chest-style or upright, approximate dimensions, condition of the lid or door and any known electrical or temperature problems.

What photos are useful?

A clear exterior photo, interior photo and model-label photo are ideal. If the freezer is in a garage, basement or shed, explain the path to the pickup vehicle.

Do broken freezers qualify?

Not automatically. A single nonworking freezer generally does not qualify for free pickup. Working appliances are prioritized; mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% are working.

What happens after I submit?

We review whether the freezer is working, its style and approximate size, access conditions, photos and local demand. Working chest and upright freezers can be useful inventory, but pickup availability still depends on the current route and service coverage. Before pickup, remove all food and personal items and make sure the path from the freezer to the exit is clear.

Free Pickup Is Qualification-Based

Fully working appliances receive priority. A single nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the load is working. Microwaves are accepted only with another major qualifying appliance, and dishwashers only with two or more major working appliances.
