Looking for Freezer Pickup or Freezer Recycling Near Me?

Have a working chest or upright freezer you no longer need? Request pickup and tell us whether it freezes normally, its dimensions and where it is located. Include your address and any stairs or narrow doorways. For a freezer that does not qualify, check local disposal alternatives before arranging transport.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Working freezers receive priority. A single nonworking freezer does not qualify for free pickup, and scheduling depends on current local coverage.

Freezers That Receive Priority

Minor issues can be described for review, but a freezer needing repair is not automatically approved for free pickup.

Chest Freezer Pickup: What to Send

For a chest freezer pickup request, include whether the cabinet is currently freezing correctly, the approximate width, depth and height, and clear exterior and interior photos. Mention the lid seal, hinges, baskets or dividers, visible rust or damage, and whether the freezer is empty.

Access matters because chest freezers can be wide and heavy. Tell us whether the unit is in a garage, basement, shed or utility room and include stairs, tight turns, narrow doors, slopes or long carrying distance. Review free-pickup qualification before submitting.

How to Confirm Freezing Condition

Tell us whether the freezer is currently plugged in and holding food frozen. When safe and practical, a freezer thermometer can help confirm operating temperature. Hearing a compressor run does not by itself prove the cabinet is freezing correctly.

Mention heavy frost, warm spots, unusual compressor noise, electrical problems, damaged controls or a unit that runs without reaching freezing temperature. Do not energize an appliance with a damaged cord, wet connection or breaker-tripping problem.

Photos, Measurements and Access

Send an exterior photo, interior photo and model-label photo when available. Include approximate width, depth and height. For a chest freezer, measure the widest cabinet points and tell us whether the lid can open fully. For an upright freezer, include the door swing and narrowest doorway.

Freezers are often located in garages, basements, sheds or utility rooms. Include stairs, tight turns, narrow doors, gates, slopes, long carries and whether a pickup vehicle can reach the appliance.

Pickup Preparation

Remove all food and personal items and keep the exit path clear. Do not move a working freezer into a harder-to-access location before pickup. If it is working and safely accessible, keep it plugged in when practical until its condition has been confirmed.

Freezer Pickup FAQ

Can one working freezer qualify?

Yes. A fully working freezer can be reviewed as a single appliance. Condition, access, local demand and route availability still determine acceptance.

Can a broken freezer be picked up free?

A single nonworking freezer does not qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Should the freezer stay plugged in?

When practical and safe, keeping a working freezer plugged in helps confirm that it is freezing correctly. Remove all food before the scheduled pickup.

Free Pickup Rules

Fully working appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Submitting a request does not guarantee free pickup. Appliance condition, access, location, local demand and route availability are reviewed before scheduling.
