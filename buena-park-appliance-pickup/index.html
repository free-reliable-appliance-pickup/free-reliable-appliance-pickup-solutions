Buena Park Bulky-Item Alternative for Appliances That Do Not Qualify

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup, Buena Park residential refuse customers have a separate bulky-item option through the local EDCO / Park Waste service. The Buena Park service page states residents may schedule up to three free bulky-item collections per calendar year, with up to six large items per collection.

The accepted-item list includes refrigerators, stoves, microwave ovens, air conditioners, water heaters, scrap metal and other large household items. The service is curbside and requires advance scheduling. Commercial and multi-family customers with drop-off bins should contact the local waste provider about their available options.

This service is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup. Confirm current preparation and acceptance rules directly with the local waste provider before moving an appliance outside.
