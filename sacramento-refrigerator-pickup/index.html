Sacramento Refrigerator & Fridge Pickup Only

This page owns refrigerator and fridge pickup intent for addresses inside Sacramento. If the request includes a stove, freezer or several appliance types, use the Sacramento appliance pickup city page. For broader regional routing outside the city, use the Sacramento County appliance pickup hub. Washer- or dryer-only requests use the dedicated Sacramento washer & dryer page.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Working and reusable refrigerators receive priority. A request is not an appointment until condition, address, access and current route availability are confirmed.

What We Need Before a Sacramento Refrigerator Pickup

Cooling condition

Tell us whether both the refrigerator and freezer sections hold temperature. If the unit has a known cooling problem, describe it accurately.

Photos & model

Send a front photo, interior photo and model-label photo when available. Mention dents, missing shelves, broken handles or dispenser problems.

Size & configuration

Tell us whether it is top-freezer, bottom-freezer, side-by-side, French-door, counter-depth or built-in. Approximate width helps with doorway and route planning.

Removal access

Include stairs, elevators, gates, narrow doors, kitchen islands, long carries and parking restrictions. Do not cut refrigerant lines or remove the compressor.

Sacramento Neighborhood & Access Review

Refrigerator requests can come from Downtown and Midtown, East Sacramento, Land Park and Curtis Park, Pocket-Greenhaven, North and South Natomas, Tahoe Park, Oak Park, North Sacramento, Del Paso Heights, Meadowview, Parkway, Valley Hi and other city neighborhoods.

The exact street address matters more than the neighborhood name alone. Garage and driveway pickups are often simpler; apartments, senior communities, condos and managed properties should include floor level, elevator, loading, gate and parking information.

If the Refrigerator Does Not Qualify for Our Free Route

The City of Sacramento operates a separate appliance and e-waste collection program for eligible residential solid-waste customers. The City currently states that customers can schedule two free appliance/e-waste appointments per calendar year and that up to two appliances can be collected per appointment.

Current City instructions say appliances should be placed in the street, visible and accessible, no earlier than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment. Refrigerators must be free of food or food residue. Tenants in multifamily properties with five or more units that are not serviced by City solid waste are directed to their landlord, property manager or commercial hauler.

Check the current rules before setting anything out: City of Sacramento Appliance and E-Waste Pickup. The City accepts appointment requests through 311, the Sac 311 app or its published service-request channels.

Sacramento Refrigerator Pickup FAQ

Can a single working refrigerator qualify for free pickup?

Yes. A single working or strongly reusable refrigerator can be submitted for review. Acceptance still depends on the exact address, condition, safe access, local demand and route availability.

Can you pick up a refrigerator that is not cooling?

You can submit it, but a single nonworking refrigerator generally does not qualify for the standard free-pickup route. Give the true cooling condition so the correct option can be reviewed.

Should I put the refrigerator at the curb first?

No. Keep a working or reusable refrigerator protected until our pickup is confirmed. If you use the City program instead, follow the City's current placement instructions for the scheduled appointment.

Can I text photos before I submit the form?

Yes. Text clear exterior and interior photos plus the Sacramento ZIP code and cooling condition to 310-774-4304.
