Which Appliances Receive Priority in Walnut?

Refrigerators & freezers

Working cooling appliances receive priority. Include clear exterior and interior photos and tell us whether the appliance is plugged in and cooling normally.

Walnut Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup automatically free in Walnut?

No. Free pickup is available for qualifying appliances after condition, access, exact location and current route availability are reviewed.

Can a single appliance qualify?

Yes. A single working refrigerator, washer, dryer, freezer, stove or range may qualify depending on condition and route demand.

Can nonworking appliances be submitted?

Yes, but free pickup is not guaranteed. Repair or nonworking appliances depend on current demand, while mixed loads may qualify when most major appliances are working and reusable.

Do you review inside-home pickup?

Yes. Include floor level, stairs, doorway or gate limitations and any other access details before scheduling.
