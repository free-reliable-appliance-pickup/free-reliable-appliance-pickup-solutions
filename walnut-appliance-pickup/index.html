Major Appliance Pickup Requests in Walnut

Washers & Dryers

Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. Working laundry sets and testable single machines receive strong priority.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Send exterior and interior photos, cooling status and whether the appliance is currently plugged in. Working, clean and complete units are the strongest reuse candidates.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Identify gas or electric and whether burners, controls and oven functions work. The appliance must be safely disconnected before pickup.

Multi-Appliance Loads

For rental turnovers or several replacements, list each appliance and condition. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Qualification rules: a single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances, and microwaves only with another qualifying major appliance.
