Working-test details for washers and dryers

Tell us whether you have one machine or a matched or mismatched set. For the washer, say if it fills, agitates or tumbles, drains and spins without a known leak. For the dryer, say if the drum turns and whether it heats on a normal cycle.

For an apartment pickup, note if the washer and dryer sit in a stacked closet, upstairs laundry alcove or shared utility room. Confirm whether building access or parking must be reserved.

Important: Free pickup is conditional. A single nonworking machine normally does not qualify. For mixed major-appliance loads, we review how many items work, local reuse demand, safe access and the actual route. No appointment is guaranteed until confirmed.

Prepare your washer & dryer pickup request

A complete working pair is useful to describe clearly: mention whether the dryer is electric or gas, if both machines were recently operated and whether hoses or venting are disconnected. We do not ask customers to perform risky disconnections. If a machine needs repairs, state the fault rather than labeling it working.

  • Specify washer, dryer or both and the model numbers if known
  • Describe fill, drain, spin, tumble and heat tests
  • State gas or electric dryer; disconnect only if safe
  • Share laundry room access, steps and door width

If you are moving, updating a rental, or replacing a still-working laundry or kitchen appliance, photos and a recent working test help us identify possible reuse options.

How qualification and pickup review work

  1. Send your city/ZIP, contact number, appliance photos and tested condition.
  2. Include property type, floor, stairs, door access and a safe pickup/loading location.
  3. We check free-pickup qualification and whether a local direct or partner route is available.
  4. If the request qualifies, the pickup details are confirmed before a visit. Otherwise, you can review other removal options.

Microwaves are not collected alone. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. For nonqualifying items, check the applicable local waste or retailer take-back options; we do not guarantee free disposal.

Eugene washer & dryer pickup questions

Will you take only a washer or only a dryer?

A single fully working washer or dryer may qualify, subject to demand, access and an available pickup route. A working set can help with reuse opportunities.

Is pickup automatically free?

No. We first review appliance working condition, actual location, safe access and current route or partner availability. A request is not a confirmed appointment.

Can I request pickup outside Eugene?

Nearby Lane County and the southern Willamette Valley communities can submit requests. We evaluate the exact address and available route; the approximate 45-mile request area is not a guarantee of service.
