Multi-Item Loads

List each appliance and condition separately. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances; microwaves only with another qualifying major appliance.

La Habra Heights Driveway & Property Access Review

For La Habra Heights properties, access details can determine whether a pickup can be safely matched to a route. If the appliance is behind a gate, up or down a sloped driveway, inside a garage, on another floor or far from vehicle parking, include that information with the first request. Photos of the appliance and the path from the appliance to the loading area are helpful when access is unusual.

Also identify whether a refrigerator or freezer is currently cooling, whether a washer completes fill-drain-spin, whether a dryer tumbles and heats, and whether a range is gas or electric. A clear condition report helps us distinguish a reusable appliance from a disposal-only item. If the appliance does not qualify, the City’s solid-waste and cleanup programs listed above provide a separate local disposal path.
