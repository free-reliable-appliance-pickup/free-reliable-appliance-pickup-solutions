Provo Appliance Pickup & Removal

Submit washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges for pickup review. Working and reusable appliances receive priority.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Condition, access, demand, location and available coverage are reviewed first.

Priority Appliances

Washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are priority categories. Refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens may also qualify.

What Qualifies?

Fully working appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify. Dishwashers require two or more major working appliances, and microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Pickup Access

Include floor level, stairs, narrow doors, tight halls, gates and whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upstairs or outside.
