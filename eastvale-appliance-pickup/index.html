Eastvale Appliance Pickup & Removal Review

Eastvale is part of our western Riverside County and Inland Empire route system. We review qualifying major appliances from homeowners, renters, landlords, property managers and businesses, including washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges.

Working major appliances receive priority because the free service depends on reuse or resale value. Send the appliance type, exact address, truthful operating condition and access information before scheduling.

Free pickup is qualification-based. A fully working major appliance may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup.

What We Review in Eastvale

Washers & Dryers

Working laundry sets are a priority. State whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer tumbles and heats.

Refrigerators

Include exterior and interior photos, approximate dimensions and current cooling status. Say whether the refrigerator is still plugged in.

Freezers

Chest and upright freezers can be reviewed when freezing performance, size and moving access are clearly described.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric units may qualify when burners, oven functions and controls work and the path out is safe.

Eastvale Garages, Gated Access & Interior Pickups

Many Eastvale requests come from garages, newer residential developments and gated communities, but interior removals may involve kitchen islands, narrow side passages, stairs or long carries to the truck. Tell us where the appliance is located, the number of stair flights, gate or parking requirements and whether door removal may be needed.

For large refrigerators, include the approximate width and depth when available. For upstairs laundry rooms, mention landings and tight turns. Clear access notes help us evaluate the request before dispatch.

Eastvale Bulky-Item & Recycling Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our service, Eastvale residents have a separate WM bulky-item collection program through the city. Current city information states that curbside-cart customers can request free bulky-item pickups during the year, with appliances such as washers, dryers and refrigerators included among eligible bulky items.

The WM program is independent from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup. Confirm the current number of allowed pickups, item limits and scheduling requirements directly with Eastvale or WM before setting anything curbside.

Mixed Loads, Microwaves & Dishwashers

If you are replacing several appliances at once, submit the complete group in one request. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work, but every appliance must be described honestly.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Submitting the form starts a review. It does not guarantee acceptance, a specific pickup date or a particular arrival time.

Eastvale Appliance Pickup FAQs

Can a working washer and dryer set qualify?

Yes. Working laundry equipment is a priority. Describe what each machine does correctly and include photos.

Can you review an appliance inside the home?

Yes. Tell us the floor, stairs, narrow doors, turns, gate access and distance to the loading area.

What if the appliance is not working?

A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup. Use the city or waste-hauler option that matches the item.
