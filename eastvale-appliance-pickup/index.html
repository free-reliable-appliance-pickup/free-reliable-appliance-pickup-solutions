Eastvale Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Eastvale has a location issue that makes exact-address routing especially important: the city shares ZIP geography with neighboring communities, and addresses historically have been associated with Corona or Mira Loma. Our routing uses the actual pickup address instead of assuming the mailing label tells the whole story.

Working refrigerators, laundry sets, freezers, stoves and multiple-appliance loads receive strong consideration when condition and access are clearly described.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single completely nonworking appliance generally does not qualify.

Major Appliances We Review in Eastvale

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the appliance is plugged in and holding temperature. Exterior, interior and model-label photos help us understand style, size and condition.

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report power, fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether the drum turns and produces heat.

Multiple Appliances

For property turnovers or several replacements, list every major appliance and its individual condition so the complete load can be reviewed together.

Eastvale Shared-ZIP Geography Matters

The City of Eastvale explains that it currently uses ZIP codes 91752 and 92880. ZIP 91752 is shared with Jurupa Valley, while 92880 has historically been shared across nearby communities. That is why a lead labeled only “Corona” or “Mira Loma” can be misleading without the actual street location.

For your request, submit the full Eastvale pickup address so the route can be assigned from the property location rather than the city name alone.

Eastvale Pickup Access

Many Eastvale requests involve newer homes, garages, HOA communities, side-yard gates and multi-appliance replacement projects. Tell us whether the appliances are already disconnected, whether a truck can reach the driveway, and whether gates, narrow paths or stairs affect removal.

Eastvale Bulky-Item Alternative

If a request does not qualify for reuse-focused pickup, the City of Eastvale's current WM program states that curbside-cart residents are eligible for three free bulky-item pickups per calendar year. Current city guidance allows up to six bulky items per pickup and lists items such as washers, dryers and refrigerators among examples.

Access Details That Affect Pickup

Include gated entry, HOA procedures, garage access, side-yard width, stairs, parking limitations and the carrying distance to the loading point.

Do not move a heavy appliance to a curb, hallway or stair landing just because a request was submitted. Keep it safely located until pickup is reviewed and confirmed.

Qualification Limits

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Minor-repair items can be described for review, but approval is not automatic.

Submitting the form starts a review. It does not guarantee free pickup, a specific day or a particular arrival time.

Eastvale Appliance Removal FAQ

Do working appliances receive priority?

Yes. Working and reusable appliances receive the strongest priority because reuse is preferred over disposal whenever practical.

Should I send photos?

Yes. Clear front, interior and model-label photos can help us evaluate the appliance faster and reduce failed trips.

Can landlords or property managers submit several appliances?

Yes. List every appliance, condition, unit location and access detail in one request when they are at the same property.
