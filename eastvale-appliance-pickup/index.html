Access Details That Affect Pickup

Include gated entry, HOA procedures, garage access, side-yard width, stairs, parking limitations and the carrying distance to the loading point.

Qualification Limits

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Minor-repair items can be described for review, but approval is not automatic.

Submitting the form starts a review. It does not guarantee free pickup, a specific day or a particular arrival time.
