Glendora Appliance Pickup Review

Glendora is a priority stop in the 909 corridor between Azusa, Covina, San Dimas and the Inland Empire. Homeowners, renters and property managers can submit qualifying appliances for review before arranging disposal or moving them outside.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify.

Appliances Reviewed in Glendora

Washers and dryers

Working laundry machines are a priority. Explain the operating condition of each machine in a set.

Refrigerators and freezers

Report cooling performance, size, shelves, seals and access. Include interior and exterior photos.

Stoves and ranges

Gas and electric units may be reviewed when function, fuel type and removal access are clear.

Long Driveways, Hillside Access & Interior Removal

Some Glendora properties require a longer carry, a sloped approach or movement through an interior hallway. Tell us where the truck can safely load, whether stairs or gates are present and whether appliance doors must be removed. Apartment requests should include elevator and parking arrangements.

Cooling and Laundry Equipment Details

For a refrigerator or freezer, report whether it maintains temperature and photograph the interior, exterior and model label. For laundry equipment, state whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. These details help confirm a route without unnecessary follow-up.

Important Qualification Limits

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances. Minor repairs may be considered after accurate disclosure.

A submitted form begins review; pickup is confirmed only after condition, address, access and route availability are accepted.

Glendora Pickup FAQs

Are working freezers considered in Glendora?

Yes. Working chest and upright freezers can be strong candidates. Include temperature performance, size and access.

Can a property manager submit several appliances?

Yes. List each appliance, unit location and condition in one request so the working share is clear.

Do you pick up microwaves alone?

No. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.
