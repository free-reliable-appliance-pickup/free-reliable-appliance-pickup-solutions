Independent recycling and removal options

Philadelphia public disposal: The City's residential bulky-item appointment program has separate eligibility and preparation rules for large appliances. It is a municipal service, not free private reuse pickup through us. Do not remove refrigerator doors for a reuse inquiry; city collection preparation is separate.

Residents who cannot qualify for reuse should check with their municipality or county recycling program, a local licensed recycler, or a retailer's separate haul-away service. Appliance handling and fees vary by program. Never cut refrigerant lines or handle connected gas fittings yourself.

Public or paid recycling services are independent from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup and should be verified with the actual provider. Never damage a refrigerant system or disconnect gas without qualified help.
