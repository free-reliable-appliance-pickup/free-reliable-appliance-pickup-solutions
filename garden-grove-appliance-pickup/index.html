Free Appliance Pickup in Garden Grove

This is the primary Garden Grove page for general appliance pickup and removal. Qualifying working or reusable refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens may receive free pickup after condition, exact address, access and current route availability are reviewed.

Free pickup is qualification-based. A single working major appliance may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A completely nonworking single appliance does not automatically qualify. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

For washer- or dryer-only requests, use the dedicated Garden Grove washer & dryer pickup page.

Garden Grove Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused free pickup, Garden Grove residents have a separate bulky-item collection program through Republic Services. The City currently states that single- and multi-family households receive up to three bulky-item pickups per calendar year with up to 10 items per pickup, by appointment.

Garden Grove community cleanup events use different acceptance rules and currently exclude refrigerators, freezers and air-conditioning units. For refrigerant-containing appliances or other special items, confirm the current scheduled-collection requirements directly with Republic Services before setting anything out.
