What qualifies for a possible free pickup review?

Fully working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers and stoves are prioritized, particularly matched laundry pairs and groups with mostly working units. Nonworking items, stairs and difficult carrying require separate review. An optional paid quote requires separate consent.

Indiana recycling and disposal alternatives

Tippecanoe County refrigerant alternative: its official refrigerator disposal resource advises certified refrigerant removal prior to metal recycling and lists independent local options. This is not a partner or confirmed private pickup service.

Official recycling alternative: Indiana's IDEM white goods guidance says old appliances may contain refrigerants, PCB-related components and compressor oil requiring proper handling. The IDEM directory of recycling programs directs residents to county solid waste districts for actual locations and pickup details. These public programs are independent of our private reuse pickup request.

County drop-off rules and private hauling are separate from appliance-reuse requests. Do not puncture refrigerator tubing or modify gas connections.
