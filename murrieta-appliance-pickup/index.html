Murrieta Bulky-Item Alternative for Appliances That Do Not Qualify

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup, Murrieta residents have a separate city-contracted bulky-item option through Waste Management. The City of Murrieta states that residential customers are entitled to two free bulky-item pickups during the year and should contact Waste Management at least 24 hours before the regular collection day. Size and amount limits apply.

The City also lists annual community cleanup events that accept appliances under event-specific rules. These municipal and waste-hauler programs are completely separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup, so confirm current eligibility, preparation and scheduling directly with the City or Waste Management before setting an appliance out.
