San Marino Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Can one working washer or dryer qualify?

It may. A single working washer or dryer can be reviewed, while a complete working set receives stronger priority.

Should I include access photos?

Yes when access is tight. A photo of steps, narrow doors or the route from the laundry area can help avoid surprises.

Can a machine needing repair qualify?

Sometimes. Repairable equipment depends on model, completeness, condition and current demand. Describe the problem accurately.
