How Diamond Bar Free Pickup Qualification Works

Free pickup is not automatic for every unwanted appliance. We review the request first so working and reusable machines can be matched with an active local route.

1. Condition

Choose fully working, needs repair, not working or unknown. Accurate condition information helps avoid failed pickups.

2. Photos

Send clear photos showing the appliance, interior and access area. A model or serial-label photo is helpful when available.

3. Access

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, kitchen, laundry room, upstairs location or other space with stairs, gates or narrow doorways.

Diamond Bar Appliance Pickup FAQ

What appliances can qualify for free pickup in Diamond Bar?

Qualifying working refrigerators, washers, dryers, washer-and-dryer sets, freezers, stoves and ranges can be reviewed for free pickup.

Can nonworking appliances be submitted?

Yes, but free pickup is not guaranteed. Mixed loads may qualify when most of the major appliances are working and reusable, while repair or nonworking items depend on demand and route availability.

Do you pick up from inside the home?

Inside-home requests can be reviewed. Include floor level, stairs, doorway or gate limits and any other access details before scheduling.
