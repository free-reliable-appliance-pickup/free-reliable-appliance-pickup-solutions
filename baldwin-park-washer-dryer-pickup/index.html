What to Test Before Pickup

Washer

Tell us whether it powers on, fills, washes or agitates, drains and spins.

Dryer

Tell us whether the drum turns, whether it heats and whether it is gas or electric.

Complete Set

Submit both machines together when they are a pair. Complete working sets receive strong priority.

Access

Include floor level, stairs, tight doors, stacked installation, gate access and carrying distance.
