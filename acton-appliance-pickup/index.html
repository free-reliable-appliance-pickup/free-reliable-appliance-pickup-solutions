Acton Rural & Foothill Access

Acton pickups can involve long driveways, gates, hillside properties, unpaved access and more distance between the appliance and a safe truck-loading point than a typical city stop. Those details can affect whether a route is practical.

Send the exact location, gate instructions, whether a pickup truck can enter and turn around safely, and whether the appliance is in a kitchen, garage, barn, detached structure or another area away from the driveway.

Free Pickup Rules

A single working refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer, stove or range can be reviewed. Minor repair needs may sometimes be considered. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for the standard free route.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
