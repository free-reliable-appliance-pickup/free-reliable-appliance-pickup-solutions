What Qualifies for Free Pickup in Los Angeles County?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. Every request is also reviewed for city, access, current demand and available pickup coverage before scheduling.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Los Angeles County Appliance Pickup & Removal

Los Angeles County includes very different pickup environments—from apartments and dense city streets to suburban garages and longer Antelope Valley routes. Send the exact pickup address, appliance condition and access details so the request can be matched to the right coverage area.

Free pickup is qualification-based. A request is not scheduled until the load, location, access and current coverage are reviewed.

Major Appliances We Review

Washers

Top-load and front-load washers. Tell us whether the machine fills, drains and spins and mention leaks or error codes.

Dryers

Gas and electric dryers, including complete laundry sets. Identify the fuel type and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Refrigerators

Top-freezer, side-by-side, bottom-freezer and French-door refrigerators. Include an interior photo and cooling condition.

Freezers

Chest and upright household freezers. Include an interior photo and tell us whether the unit maintains freezing temperature.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may qualify. Tell us whether the burners and oven operate normally.

Multiple Appliances

For rental turnovers or several appliances, list every item separately with its working condition.

Apartment, Condo & Property Access

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upstairs unit, condo building, storage area or business. Include stair flights, elevator availability, narrow hallways, gates, doorway concerns and parking or loading restrictions.

For dense areas where curb space or loading access is limited, include parking instructions. For large refrigerators, send a photo of the exit route if the doorway or hallway is tight.

Photos and Information to Send

Send one clear full photo of each appliance, the working condition, city, ZIP code and access details. A model-label photo is useful when available. Refrigerator and freezer requests should include an interior photo and cooling status.

For most Los Angeles County requests, call or text 310-774-4304, Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM. San Gabriel Valley requests use 909-375-6685.

Los Angeles County Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free throughout Los Angeles County?

Free pickup is available only for qualifying requests. Condition, location, access, demand and current coverage are reviewed first.

Do you take a broken appliance by itself for free?

No. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work.

Can I request pickup from an apartment or condo?

Yes. Include the floor level, stairs, elevator information, parking and building-access restrictions.

Do Palmdale and Lancaster have separate pages?

Yes. Both Antelope Valley cities have dedicated pages with city-specific request and access information.

How do I request pickup?

Use the online request form or call or text the correct regional number with appliance photos, working condition, city or ZIP code and access details.
