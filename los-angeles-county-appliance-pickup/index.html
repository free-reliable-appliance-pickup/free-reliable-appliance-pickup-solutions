Los Angeles County pickup requests are reviewed by the actual city or unincorporated community, appliance condition and removal access. Use the local page that matches the pickup address when one is available, or submit through this county hub.

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. Every request is also reviewed for city, access, current demand and available pickup coverage before scheduling.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Major Appliances We Review

Washers

Top-load and front-load washers. Tell us whether the machine fills, drains and spins and mention leaks or error codes.

Dryers

Gas and electric dryers, including complete laundry sets. Identify the fuel type and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Refrigerators

Top-freezer, side-by-side, bottom-freezer and French-door refrigerators. Include an interior photo and cooling condition.

Freezers

Chest and upright household freezers. Include an interior photo and tell us whether the unit maintains freezing temperature.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may qualify. Tell us whether the burners and oven operate normally.

Multiple Appliances
