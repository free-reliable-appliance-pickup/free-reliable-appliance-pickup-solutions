Free Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Los Angeles County customers may search for free appliance pickup, appliance removal, appliance recycling, washer pickup, refrigerator pickup or appliance pickup near me. This county hub connects those searches to the correct city or regional route while keeping qualification rules clear.

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. Every request is also reviewed for city, access, current demand and available pickup coverage before scheduling.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

How Free Appliance Pickup Works in Los Angeles County
