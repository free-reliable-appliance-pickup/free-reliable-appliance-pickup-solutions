Arvada Appliance Pickup With Reuse Value Checked Up Front

Arvada is an important Denver-metro market for our Colorado route. We review working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges, with photos and access details considered before a pickup time is confirmed.

A completely nonworking single appliance does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repair situations may be considered, and mixed loads can be reviewed when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. Colorado demand is especially useful for good laundry equipment and working freezers, so tell us exactly what functions have been tested.

Priority details: working condition, gas or electric connections, stairs, garage access and whether a refrigerator or freezer is currently cold.

Appliance Types We Review in Arvada

Washers

Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins. Mention leaks, bearing noise, door or lid problems and error codes.

Dryers

State whether it tumbles and heats and whether it is gas or electric. Working sets are useful to submit together.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether cooling is normal now. Include interior photos and note missing shelves, bins or major cosmetic damage.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric, describe burner and oven operation and tell us whether the unit is already disconnected.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Stairs, Garages and Gas Connections

For Arvada pickups, describe the appliance path from its current location to the truck. List basement or upper-floor stairs, narrow doors, landings, garage access and how close a vehicle can park. For refrigerators, mention tight turns or whether doors may need removal for clearance.

For gas dryers and gas ranges, tell us the fuel type and whether the appliance is already safely disconnected. Do not attempt unsafe gas work for a pickup request; the appliance should be ready for transport according to appropriate utility and safety requirements.

Arvada's Bulk Program Is Only a Partial Backup

If an appliance does not qualify for our free reuse-based pickup, the City of Arvada offers on-demand bulk pickup for residents participating in the City's waste-hauling service, with prepayment and an additional item charge. The City lists washers and dryers as acceptable, but specifically lists refrigerators, stoves and appliances with Freon among unacceptable bulk items.

See current City of Arvada bulk-pickup rules and accepted items.

Arvada Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can you review a freezer pickup?

Yes. Working freezers are a strong Colorado category. Tell us whether the unit freezes correctly and whether it is empty and accessible.

Does Arvada city bulk pickup take refrigerators?

The City's current bulk-pickup page lists refrigerators and Freon appliances as unacceptable, so residents should check the current official rules for alternatives.

Can you review a basement washer or dryer?

Yes. Include stair count, turns, width and whether the machines are disconnected.
