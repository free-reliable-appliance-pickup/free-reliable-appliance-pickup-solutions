Which appliances are most likely to qualify?

Working washer/dryer sets: say whether the washer drains and spins and the dryer heats. Refrigerators and freezers: describe current cooling, door seals and interior condition. Stoves and ranges: provide gas/electric type and whether burners and oven heat. We prioritize appliances suitable for reuse. Nonworking machines are considered case by case, especially in mixed loads; a broken unit alone does not automatically qualify for free pickup.

Microwaves are not normally standalone pickup requests. Dishwashers are reviewed only with at least two other major working appliances. Property managers and apartment maintenance teams should list quantities, models and whether everything is accessible from one location.

Why Illinois requests need access details

Elevator reservations and loading-dock windows are important for Chicago high-rises, while older houses and flats may have narrow staircases. In the western and northwest suburbs a washing machine may be in a basement, attached garage or utility closet. Ask the property manager for moving rules, and identify steep stairs, tight turns and safe parking before anyone proposes collection. Do not disconnect gas, plumbing or refrigeration lines unless an appropriately qualified person has arranged a safe disconnection.

Independent donation and recycling alternatives

If an appliance is not suitable for reuse or no pickup is available, consult the Illinois EPA household appliance disposal guidance. Cooling equipment can require specialist refrigerant handling; do not vent refrigerants. Habitat for Humanity ReStore Chicago may consider eligible gently used appliance donations. Contact ReStore directly to verify acceptance criteria, fees (if any), and whether pickup is available for the specific address; we do not arrange or guarantee their services. The listed organizations are independent of Free Reliable Appliance Pickup and are not confirmed local pickup operators for our requests.

Illinois pickup request questions

Is pickup free?

Submitting this eligibility form is free. A working appliance may qualify for free removal only if an actual local operator accepts the job. No Illinois region is guaranteed free pickup.

Do you have an Illinois partner or route today?

Not confirmed on this page. We review the city, condition and access and do not activate a territory based solely on a web page or inquiry.

Can a landlord submit several units?

Yes. Include the number of refrigerators, laundry sets or ranges, the percentage fully working and whether there is a safe first-floor loading area. Bulk requests also require individual route approval.
