How free appliance pickup requests work

  1. Specify the used appliance and tested working condition.
  2. Supply inside and outside photos, exact ZIP, stairs, floor and safe loading access.
  3. We check whether an accepting local operator can handle the appliance and that location.
  4. Only after operator acceptance can terms and pickup scheduling be discussed. A separate paid quote is optional and needs customer approval.

No Vermont operator or ZIP route has been verified. Free pickup is not booked or guaranteed by submitting a request.

Vermont appliance reuse and recycling guidance

Large appliances including refrigerators, freezers, stoves, washers and dryers are prohibited from ordinary Vermont landfill disposal. The Vermont Department of Environmental Conservation recommends reuse for working units and special white-goods recycling for nonworking appliances. Check your town's transfer station and proper refrigerant handling procedures. Public facilities are not affiliated with our business.
