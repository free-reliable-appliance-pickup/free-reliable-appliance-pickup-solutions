What qualifies?

Working washers, dryers, complete sets, refrigerators that cool, freezers that freeze and usable stoves are priority candidates for reuse. A single nonworking unit, long carry or upper-floor removal may require a separate review or optional paid quote. Dishwashers are generally considered only with at least two other working major appliances. We do not pick up microwaves alone.

Before confirming a route, send clear inside and outside photos, size if known, condition, floor, stairs and the practical loading path.
