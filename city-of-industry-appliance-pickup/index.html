What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Fully working and reusable major appliances receive the strongest consideration. Minor-repair appliances may be reviewed when local demand and route capacity support the pickup. For larger commercial or mixed loads, list the condition of every appliance.

Free pickup is not automatic. A submitted request begins a review; it does not create a guaranteed appointment. See the full free pickup qualification rules.

City of Industry Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is free appliance pickup guaranteed in City of Industry?

No. Free pickup depends on appliance condition, exact address, loading access, local demand and current route availability.

Can one working appliance qualify?

Yes. A single fully working or reusable refrigerator, washer, dryer, freezer, stove or range can be submitted for review.

Do you handle warehouse or commercial pickups?

Yes. Include appliance count, loading dock or bay access, business hours, vehicle access and whether the appliances are already staged.

Can nonworking appliances be submitted?

Yes, but a single nonworking appliance normally does not qualify for free pickup. Minor-repair units and qualifying mixed loads may still be reviewed.
