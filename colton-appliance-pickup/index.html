Colton Homes, Apartments & Property Access

Requests may come from houses, apartments, garages, rentals, senior communities and managed properties. Clear access details help prevent failed trips, especially when there are stairs, tight doorways, gated areas, long carries or limited curb access.

Property managers and businesses can also submit multi-appliance or recurring requests. Include the appliance count, condition of each item, access and preferred scheduling window.

Free Pickup Rules

A single working refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer, stove or range can be reviewed. Minor repair needs may sometimes be considered. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for the standard free route.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Colton Bulky-Item Alternative

The City of Colton lists residential bulky-item curbside collection by appointment through CR&R. The city says single-family households may receive up to eight bulky-item pickups per year, with up to four items per pickup, and lists large appliances among accepted bulky items. That municipal program is separate from our qualification-based reuse pickup.
