What Helps a La Puente Appliance Pickup Qualify?

Free pickup is based on appliance condition, reuse potential, access and current route capacity. The fastest reviews include enough detail to identify what is being picked up and how it can be removed safely.

Working condition

Tell us what works and what does not. Refrigerators should cool, washers should fill/drain/spin, dryers should tumble/heat, and stoves should have major cooking functions working.

Clear photos

Send exterior and interior photos when useful. A model or serial-label photo can help identify age, configuration and reuse potential.

Access details

Include floor level, stairs, gates, alley or driveway access, narrow doorways and carrying distance. For apartments or multi-unit properties, include building access information.

Multiple appliances: list every major appliance and its condition. Mixed loads may qualify when most of the major appliances are working and reusable.
