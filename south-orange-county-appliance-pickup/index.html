South Orange County Appliance Pickup Review

Working refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, laundry sets, stoves and ranges can be submitted for review. Free pickup is qualification-based and depends on appliance condition, exact location, access, current demand and route availability.

Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working and reusable. A completely nonworking single appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Use the city page that matches the actual pickup address. South Orange County properties can vary widely in gates, stairs, hills, garages, parking and carrying distance.

What Makes a Strong Request?

Working condition

Describe what works. Refrigerators should cool, washers should fill/drain/spin, dryers should tumble/heat and ranges should have major cooking functions working.

Clear photos

Send appliance, interior and model-label photos when useful.

Access details

Include garage or interior location, floor, stairs, gates, parking, driveway conditions and carrying distance.

Multiple appliances

List each appliance separately with its condition so the complete load can be reviewed.

South Orange County Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup automatically free across South Orange County?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based and depends on appliance condition, exact pickup location, property access, local demand and current route availability.

Which appliances receive priority review?

Working refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, washer-and-dryer sets, stoves and ranges receive priority review.

What access details matter most?

Include floor level, stairs or elevator, gates, HOA or security access, garage or interior location, parking or loading limits and carrying distance.

Which city page should I use?

Use the city page matching the actual pickup address. If the address falls between listed communities, submit the regional form with the exact city and ZIP.
