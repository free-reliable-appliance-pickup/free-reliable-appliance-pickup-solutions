How the Partner Program Works

  1. Apply to join the Free Reliable Appliance Pickup partner network.
  2. Tell us the cities, counties or metro areas you can reliably service.
  3. We review your service area, equipment and pickup capabilities.
  4. Approved partners may receive available customer pickup opportunities.
  5. Additional lead or territory options may become available as the network expands.

Partner Territories Across the United States

We are developing partner coverage in markets across the country. Opportunities can include major metropolitan areas, surrounding suburbs and smaller cities where qualified appliance pickup coverage is needed.

Territory availability varies. Some markets may already be assigned, reserved or operated directly by Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.

Who We Are Looking For

  • Reliable local appliance pickup operators
  • Independent appliance resellers and haulers
  • Operators with pickup trucks, trailers, cargo vans or other suitable vehicles
  • People who communicate professionally with customers
  • Partners who can consistently service their approved territory

Appliances Commonly Requested

Washers

Top-load, front-load and stackable washing machines.

Dryers

Electric and gas dryers, including washer and dryer sets.

Refrigerators

Top-freezer, side-by-side, French-door and other full-size refrigerators.

Stoves & Ranges

Major electric and gas cooking ranges and stoves.

Freezers

Upright and chest freezers that qualify for pickup.
