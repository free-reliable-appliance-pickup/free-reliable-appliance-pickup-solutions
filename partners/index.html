Priority Markets

Our current priority states include California, Oregon, Washington, Arizona, Colorado, Utah, Nevada and Idaho.

Certain cities in these states may be protected for our own operations or selected partners. Other cities throughout the United States may be considered for lead or territory opportunities.

Information Available With Qualifying Requests

Customer pickup requests may include information such as:

  • Appliance type
  • Working or nonworking condition
  • Appliance photos
  • City and ZIP code
  • Floor level
  • Stairs or access information
  • Other pickup details provided by the customer
