How Appliance Leads Work

Lead Preview

Available lead previews may include:

  • Appliance type
  • Brand when available
  • Working condition
  • City and ZIP code
  • Garage, first floor, second floor or basement location
  • Stairs and access information
  • Customer appliance photos when available
Customer information remains private until the lead is unlocked.

The customer's full name, exact street address, phone number and email are not included in the shared preview.

For shared leads, the first eligible partner who successfully purchases the available lead receives the customer information.

Premium & Exclusive Territories

As our network grows, selected high-performing cities may become available as premium monthly territories.

We will evaluate real lead volume, lead quality, appliance demand and partner demand before establishing territory prices.

Better markets can have higher territory values. This allows territory pricing to reflect the actual performance of individual cities rather than charging the same price everywhere.
