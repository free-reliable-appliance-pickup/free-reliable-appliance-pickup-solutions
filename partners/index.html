How the Partner Program Works

  1. Apply to join the Free Reliable Appliance Pickup partner network.
  2. Tell us which cities, counties or metro areas you can service.
  3. We review your service area and pickup capabilities.
  4. Approved partners may receive available customer pickup opportunities.
  5. Additional lead and territory opportunities may become available as the network grows.

Partner Territories Across the United States

Partner opportunities may be available in major metro areas, suburbs and smaller cities across the United States.

Territory availability depends on customer demand, existing partners and markets operated directly by Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.

Important: Some territories may already be assigned, reserved or unavailable.

Who We Are Looking For

  • Reliable local appliance pickup operators
  • Independent appliance resellers and haulers
  • Operators with pickup trucks, trailers, vans or suitable vehicles
  • People who communicate professionally with customers
  • Partners who can consistently service their approved territory

Appliances Commonly Requested

Washers

Top-load, front-load and stackable washing machines.

Dryers

Electric and gas dryers, including washer and dryer sets.

Refrigerators

Top-freezer, side-by-side, French-door and full-size refrigerators.

Stoves & Ranges

Electric and gas stoves and ranges.

Freezers

Upright and chest freezers that qualify for pickup.
