Build Your Local Appliance Pickup Territory

Our central network receives appliance pickup requests and reviews them for qualification and local coverage. Approved partners can support defined local markets based on capacity, equipment and territory assignment.

Application first, approval second. Selecting a city is a territory preference. It does not guarantee exclusivity, lead volume, pricing, coverage or approval.

How Customer Requests Reach Local Partners

Request ReviewWe review appliance condition, photos, location, stairs and access before routing.
Market MatchThe city is matched to a direct operation, assigned partner territory or open recruiting market.
Capability MatchAppliance type, service radius, vehicle, inside-pickup ability and capacity are checked.
Pickup ConfirmationA request is offered only when coverage and qualification are confirmed. Applications and leads are never guaranteed.

Partner Lead Access & Territory Preferences

Approved partners can tell us how they would prefer to receive opportunities as the network grows. These selections help us design routing and territory assignments; they are not a purchase, subscription, exclusivity promise or guarantee of lead volume.

Shared Lead AlertsReceive notice when a qualifying request matches one of your selected markets. Initial alerts can be limited to useful request details until assignment is confirmed.
Exclusive Territory InterestTell us which markets you would consider operating as a primary partner if exclusive or priority territory access becomes available.
Review Before ContactAppliance type, condition, photos when available, city and access details can be reviewed before a request is assigned.

Important: lead-access and territory options are currently application preferences used for partner review and future network configuration. Any paid lead or subscription feature will be disclosed separately before a partner is charged.

When provided by the customer, routing information can include the city or ZIP code, appliance type, condition, photos, floor level, stairs, access notes and other pickup details. Contact details remain controlled until the request is assigned through the network.
