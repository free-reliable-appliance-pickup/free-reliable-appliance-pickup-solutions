Glendale Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

We review working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and eligible multi-appliance loads in Glendale. Fully working appliances receive the strongest consideration. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work, while a single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Glendale requests often involve apartments, condos, older homes, hillside streets, garages and interior removals. Include the actual pickup address, appliance condition, floor level, elevator or stairs, doorway or hallway restrictions, parking and the carrying distance to the loading point.

For faster review: send clear exterior photos, interior refrigerator/freezer photos when relevant, tested working details, exact address and access notes.

Appliances We Review

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, report tumble and heat and identify gas or electric when known.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the unit is currently plugged in and maintaining temperature. Interior and exterior photos are useful.

Stoves & Ranges

Report burner and oven function and whether the unit is gas or electric. Appliances must be safely disconnected before pickup.

Multiple Appliances

List every appliance and condition together so the complete load can be reviewed rather than judging one item in isolation.

Glendale Route Connections

Nearby coverage connects with Burbank, Pasadena, Los Angeles and the Los Angeles County appliance pickup hub. For laundry-specific requests, use Glendale washer & dryer pickup.

Glendale Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused route, Glendale Integrated Waste lists appliances such as refrigerators and washers/dryers among accepted bulky items. Residential homes and smaller 1–4 unit properties can request bulky-item pickup directly through the City, while larger apartment and condo properties use their property manager or designated hauler.
