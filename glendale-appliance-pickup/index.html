Glendale Appliance Pickup & Removal

We review pickup requests for washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges from homes, apartments, rentals and property turnovers.

Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Free pickup depends on condition, access, local demand and available coverage.

Priority Appliance Categories

Washers, dryers and laundry sets are high-priority requests. Refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens may also qualify.

What Qualifies?

Fully working appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when about 80% of the appliances work. A nonworking appliance alone does not qualify. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances, and microwaves only with another qualifying major appliance.

Glendale Pickup Access

Include stairs, gates, narrow doors, hallways and whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upstairs or outside. Photos help with review.
