Start Your San Bernardino County Washer & Dryer Pickup Review

Fastest review: send clear photos of both machines, the exact pickup address, current working condition and the removal path. Complete working washer and dryer sets receive especially strong consideration.

1. Send Washer Test Results

Report power, fill, wash or agitation, drain and final spin. Mention leaks, error codes or unusual noise.

2. Send Dryer Test Results

Identify gas or electric service and report power, drum tumble and heat. Include the model label when available.

3. Show the Removal Path

Include floor level, stairs or elevator, gates, narrow doors, stacked installation, parking or loading limits and carrying distance.

What Helps a Pickup Qualify?

Working condition

For washers report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers report gas or electric, power, tumble and heat.

Complete sets

Matching working washer and dryer sets receive especially strong priority because both machines can be routed together.

Clear photos

Send front and control-panel photos plus the model label when available.

Access details

Include garage or interior location, floor level, stairs, gates, driveway access and carrying distance.
