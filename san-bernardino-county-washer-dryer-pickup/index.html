Why Washers & Dryers Are a Priority Here

San Bernardino County is one of the largest appliance markets in Southern California, especially across the populated western side of the county. Washers and dryers are a strong category because working laundry equipment can often be reused or resold, while selected repairable machines may interest local repair technicians, independent buyers, resellers and small appliance businesses.

This page is focused specifically on laundry appliances. It complements the broader Inland Empire and Southern California service pages while giving washer-and-dryer searches a useful county-level destination.

Best chance of free pickup: working washer and dryer sets, single working washers, single working dryers, and complete repairable machines when a local repair-minded buyer has current demand.

For a washing machine, tell us whether it powers on, fills, washes or agitates, drains and reaches the final spin. Mention leaks, loud bearings, error codes, a lid-lock problem, torn front-load gasket or water left in the drum.

Free Dryer Pickup

For dryers, identify gas or electric when known and tell us whether the machine powers on, tumbles and produces heat. Mention no-heat problems, unusual noise, damaged controls, a door-switch issue or unusually long drying time.

Do not disconnect a gas dryer unless you are qualified to do so. Tell us if the dryer is stacked, installed in a closet, upstairs or behind a narrow doorway.

Homes, Apartments, Rentals & Property Managers

Requests can come from garages, laundry rooms, apartments, condos, rental turnovers and multi-unit properties. Include the floor level, stairs, elevator access, gates, parking restrictions and whether the machines are stacked or already disconnected.

Landlords and property managers with several laundry machines should list every item and its condition. Multiple working machines from one location can be especially efficient for a pickup route.

San Bernardino County Washer & Dryer FAQ

Is washer and dryer pickup free?

It can be free for qualifying requests when appliance condition, location, access and current local demand fit an available route.

Can a broken washer or dryer qualify?

Sometimes, but only when a repair-minded buyer or technician is interested in that specific machine. Working appliances receive the strongest priority.

What should I send first?

Send the city or ZIP code, washer or dryer type, working condition, photos, floor level, stairs and any access restrictions.
