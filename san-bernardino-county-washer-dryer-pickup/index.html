What Helps a Pickup Qualify?

Working condition

For washers report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers report gas or electric, power, tumble and heat.

Complete sets

Matching working washer and dryer sets receive especially strong priority because both machines can be routed together.

Clear photos

Send front and control-panel photos plus the model label when available.

Access details

Include garage or interior location, floor level, stairs, gates, driveway access and carrying distance.

San Bernardino County FAQ

Is every washer or dryer automatically picked up free?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based and depends on appliance condition, completeness, location, access and current route availability.

Can one working machine qualify?

Yes. A single working washer or dryer can be reviewed.

Do you cover Grand Terrace and Bloomington?

Yes. They are regular county coverage areas and can submit requests through this page.

What about the east route beyond Yucaipa?

Oak Glen has a dedicated premium page, while Calimesa, Cherry Valley, Beaumont and Banning continue through the connected Riverside County side.
