San Diego County Pickup Requests

This county page is the main request page for San Diego County rather than creating a separate near-duplicate search page for every city. Requests are reviewed from the City of San Diego, South Bay, East County, inland communities and North County. We use the exact city, ZIP code, appliance condition and access details to decide whether a current route can accept the pickup.

Submitting a request does not automatically guarantee free pickup. We confirm service only after reviewing whether the appliance is working or reusable, whether the access can be handled safely, whether the pickup fits current local demand and whether a route or qualified partner is available.

Pickup must be confirmed. The form starts a review. A pickup date is not reserved until the request is accepted.

What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Microwaves are accepted only when included with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Working washer and dryer sets, usable refrigerators, freezers and ranges are among the most useful items for route planning.

Appliances We Review

Washers

Tell us whether the washer fills, agitates, drains and spins. Mention leaks, error codes, loud bearings or whether a matching dryer is included.

Dryers

Identify gas or electric and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. Complete washer and dryer sets usually provide better route value than a single nonworking unit.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the appliance is currently plugged in and maintaining temperature. Include an interior photo and mention ice-maker or cooling problems if known.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Identify gas or electric and describe whether burners, oven and controls operate normally. Tell us if the appliance has already been disconnected.

Condition information must be accurate. A nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Apartment, Condo, Hillside and Gated Access

San Diego County pickups can involve downtown apartments, beach communities, hillside properties, multi-level condos, gated developments and suburban garages. Include the floor level, elevator availability, number of stair flights, gate instructions, hallway width concerns and whether a pickup vehicle can park near the appliance.

For large refrigerators, send a photo of the doorway and removal path when clearance looks tight. Tell us if refrigerator doors may need to be removed. For laundry closets or stacked units, include a photo showing side and front clearance. For properties with long carries, slopes or detached parking, describe the path before scheduling.

Rental Turnovers and Property Managers

Landlords and property managers can submit multiple appliances in one request. List each appliance separately, identify which unit or property it is in, and describe working condition. If several units are involved, explain whether the appliances will be staged in one garage or whether access is needed to multiple occupied or vacant units.

For larger turnover jobs, photos and a simple item list help us determine whether the working-to-nonworking ratio qualifies and whether one route can handle the load.

Photos and Details That Speed Up Review

Send a clear full photo of each appliance, the exact city and ZIP code, working condition and access details. For refrigerators and freezers, include an interior photo and cooling status. For washers and dryers, tell us the basic test results. A model-label photo is helpful when available.

Call or text 310-774-4304, Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM. Photos can be sent by text when that is easier than describing the appliance.

San Diego County Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is pickup free everywhere in San Diego County?

Free pickup is available only for qualifying requests. Condition, exact location, access, local demand and route availability are reviewed before service is confirmed.

Can I request pickup from an upstairs apartment?

Yes, but include the floor, stair flights, elevator information and narrow hallway or building restrictions so access can be reviewed.

Does a broken appliance qualify?

A nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

What should I send for a refrigerator?

Send exterior and interior photos when possible, the model label if available, and tell us whether the refrigerator is currently plugged in and cooling.
