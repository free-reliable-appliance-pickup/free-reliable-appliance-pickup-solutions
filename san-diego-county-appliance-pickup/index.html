Listing a community does not guarantee free pickup. We confirm service only after reviewing appliance condition, exact location, access, local demand and available route or partner coverage.

What We Need Before Scheduling

Condition

Tell us whether each appliance is fully working, needs a minor repair, is not working or has unknown condition. Working and reusable appliances receive priority.

Photos

Send clear exterior photos and model-label photos. For refrigerators and freezers, interior photos and cooling status are especially useful.

Access

Tell us about stairs, floor level, elevators, gates, narrow hallways, parking restrictions, garages and possible door removal before scheduling.

Appliance Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

San Diego County Access Considerations

Pickup conditions can differ between dense San Diego neighborhoods, apartment and condominium properties, inland homes and North County communities. Accurate parking, gate, elevator, stair and loading information helps us determine whether a crew can safely handle the request before a trip is arranged.

For stacked laundry equipment, tell us whether the units can be separated and whether there is enough clearance for removal. For gas dryers and ranges, identify the fuel type when possible.

Contact and Request Review

San Diego County requests use our California contact line: 310-774-4304. Service hours are 8:00 AM–8:00 PM daily. Calling or submitting the form starts a review; it does not automatically confirm free pickup.

For a faster review: include city or ZIP code, appliance type, working condition, photos, floor level, stairs and access details.
