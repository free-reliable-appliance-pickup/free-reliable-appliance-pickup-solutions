What Qualifies for Free Appliance Pickup Review?

Working & Reusable Appliances

Working refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves, ranges and ovens receive the strongest priority because the route is reuse-focused.

Repairable or Mixed Loads

Some repairable machines and multi-item loads can be reviewed. Describe each appliance honestly and identify which units are fully working.

Access Matters

Send stairs, floor level, gates, elevator, garage or driveway access, parking/loading restrictions and carrying distance.

Acceptance boundaries: microwaves are not accepted by themselves. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. A submitted form is a review request, not a guaranteed appointment.

Fresno County Recycling & Disposal Resources

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup is a private reuse-focused pickup service, not the Fresno County government. When an appliance does not qualify, compare the current public options before arranging disposal.

The County of Fresno Public Works and Planning site maintains recycling and solid-waste links and resources, including its incorporated-city directory and state/environmental resources. The County also publishes landfill operations information. Check current facility rules, eligibility, hours, fees and accepted materials directly with the responsible public agency before making a trip.

Fresno County Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is pickup really free?

It can be for a qualifying request. Free pickup is confirmed only after the appliance, address, access and current route are reviewed.

Should a Fresno city address use this county page?

Use the dedicated Fresno appliance pickup page for general Fresno city requests, or the Fresno washer & dryer pickup page for laundry-only requests.

Can I request pickup outside Fresno, Clovis, Sanger, Fowler or Selma?

Yes, you can submit an exact Fresno County address here for review. Coverage and free qualification are not guaranteed.

What if my appliance is completely nonworking?

A single completely nonworking appliance normally does not fit the free reuse-focused route. Mixed loads and some repairable equipment may still be reviewed; otherwise use an appropriate recycling, disposal or paid-removal option.
