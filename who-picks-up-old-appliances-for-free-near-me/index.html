Who Picks Up Old Appliances for Free Near Me?

Free appliance pickup can be available when the appliance is working, reusable, repairable, or part of a qualifying mixed load. The right option depends on condition, access, location and local route availability.

The Short Answer

Local appliance pickup providers, recyclers, reuse operators, charities, retailers and municipal bulky-item programs may all be options. For Free Reliable Appliance Pickup, free service is reviewed case by case. Working and reusable appliances receive priority. A mixed load may also qualify when most major appliances are working.

Best first step: send clear photos, tell us whether each appliance works, and include your city or ZIP plus stairs or access details. There is no charge to submit a request.

Which Appliances May Qualify?

Refrigerators

Cooling condition, cleanliness, size and access matter. Working refrigerators receive priority.

Washers & Dryers

Working machines and complete laundry sets are strong candidates for reuse pickup.

Freezers

Chest and upright freezers should be complete and able to reach freezing temperature for the strongest chance of free pickup.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Condition, safe disconnection and removal access are reviewed before pickup.

Important: microwaves are not accepted alone. Dishwashers are accepted only when included with two or more major working appliances.

Can Broken Appliances Be Picked Up for Free?

Sometimes, but not every broken appliance qualifies by itself. If you have several major appliances and most of the load is working, send the full list and photos. Mixed loads are reviewed as a whole. When a free route is not available, use the disposal and recycling guide for alternatives.

How to Get the Fastest Answer

  1. Take clear photos of the front, inside and model label when available.
  2. Say whether the appliance fully works, needs repair, does not work, or is unknown.
  3. Include floor level, stairs, narrow doors, gates, elevators or other access details.
  4. Provide the city or ZIP so the request can be routed to the correct service area.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who will pick up old appliances for free near me?

Qualifying local pickup providers may remove working, reusable or mixed-load appliances for free. Availability depends on the unit, access and local route capacity.

Do appliances have to work?

Working appliances receive priority. Broken appliances can sometimes be included when they are part of a qualifying mixed major-appliance load.

Is free pickup guaranteed?

No. Submit the appliance details first. Qualification and route availability must be confirmed before pickup.

What if my appliance does not qualify?

Compare municipal collection, retailer haul-away, reuse, scrap and recycling options in the old appliance disposal guide.
