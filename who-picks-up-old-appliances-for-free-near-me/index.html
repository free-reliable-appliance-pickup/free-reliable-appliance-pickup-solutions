Which Appliances May Qualify?

Refrigerators

Cooling condition, cleanliness, size and access matter. Working refrigerators receive priority.

Washers & Dryers

Working machines and complete laundry sets are strong candidates for reuse pickup.

Freezers

Chest and upright freezers should be complete and able to reach freezing temperature for the strongest chance of free pickup.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Condition, safe disconnection and removal access are reviewed before pickup.

Important: microwaves are not accepted alone. Dishwashers are accepted only when included with two or more major working appliances.

Can Broken Appliances Be Picked Up for Free?

Sometimes, but not every broken appliance qualifies by itself. If you have several major appliances and most of the load is working, send the full list and photos. Mixed loads are reviewed as a whole. When a free route is not available, compare the appliance recycling guide and appliance disposal options for alternatives.

How to Get the Fastest Answer

  1. Take clear photos of the front, inside and model label when available.

  3. Include floor level, stairs, narrow doors, gates, elevators or other access details.
  4. Provide the city or ZIP so the request can be routed to the correct service area.

Where Free Old-Appliance Pickup Can Come From

There is no single nationwide program that accepts every appliance in every condition. The best option depends on whether the appliance still works, what type it is, the pickup address and whether special handling is required.

Reuse-Focused Appliance Pickup

Working and reusable refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves and ranges may have enough reuse value to support free pickup in an active service area. Our service reviews condition, access, location, local demand and route availability before confirming pickup.

Municipal or Contracted Hauler

Some cities include bulky-item or appliance collection for eligible residents. Appointment limits, accepted appliances, curbside preparation and fees can vary by address, so check the program that actually serves the property.

Retailer or Utility Program

A retailer may offer old-appliance haul-away with delivery of a replacement. Some utilities also operate refrigerator or freezer recycling programs. Eligibility can depend on the appliance, utility account and service territory.

Qualified Recycler

For end-of-life appliances, a recycler or scrap facility may be appropriate. Refrigerators and freezers need refrigerant-aware handling; do not cut the sealed-system lines as a disposal shortcut.

Who Picks Up Refrigerators, Washers, Dryers, Freezers and Stoves for Free?

Refrigerator Pickup

A working refrigerator is one of the strongest reuse candidates. Include current cooling condition, inside and outside photos, model-label photos when possible, and access details.

Washer & Dryer Pickup

Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets receive strong priority. Report whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer tumbles and heats.

Freezer Pickup

Chest and upright freezers can be reviewed when complete and reusable. Tell us whether the unit still freezes and where it is located.

Stove, Range & Oven Pickup

Gas and electric cooking appliances can be reviewed when complete and reusable. State what works and whether the appliance is safely disconnected.

Why Working Condition Changes the Answer

Free pickup is easier to support when an appliance still has practical reuse value. That is why we ask whether it works instead of treating every old appliance as junk. A functioning washer, dryer, refrigerator, freezer or stove may be reusable; a heavily damaged or incomplete appliance may be better suited to a municipal or recycling route.

For mixed loads, send the entire list. A load with mostly working major appliances can be reviewed differently from a single nonworking item. Microwaves are not accepted alone through our service, and a dishwasher is considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who will pick up old appliances for free near me?

Qualifying local pickup providers may remove working, reusable or mixed-load appliances for free. Availability depends on the unit, access and local route capacity.

Do appliances have to work?

Working appliances receive priority. Broken appliances can sometimes be included when they are part of a qualifying mixed major-appliance load.

Is free pickup guaranteed?

No. Submit the appliance details first. Qualification and route availability must be confirmed before pickup.
