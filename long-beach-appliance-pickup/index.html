Apartments, Alleys, Garages and Loading Access

For Long Beach apartment or condo pickups, list the floor, stair flights, elevator availability and any management rules. For houses and duplexes, say whether the appliance is in the kitchen, laundry room, garage, side yard or another location. Mention narrow hallways, exterior steps or gates. Accurate access notes help prevent a pickup from being accepted when the physical removal is not practical.
