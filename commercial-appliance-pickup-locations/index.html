Commercial Appliance Pickup Locations

Businesses, properties, installers and multi-site operators can submit one commercial inquiry for a single site, a service area or a recurring program. California is the current priority market, but the form accepts other United States locations for review.

A location page or inquiry does not guarantee service. Actual availability is confirmed only after the appliance mix, site access, condition, route and local coverage are reviewed.

What Makes a Location Inquiry Useful

Operating Footprint

List the primary city/state, ZIP codes, properties, stores or facilities. Multi-market requests can include more than one city.

Appliance Flow

Give categories, normal and peak counts, condition mix and whether units are loose, staged, installed or palletized.

Pickup Pattern

Tell us whether work is one-time, weekly, monthly, turnover-triggered, seasonal or tied to project phases.

Site Rules

Include docks, gates, elevators, stairs, loading windows, check-in requirements, parking and onsite contact details.

Commercial Appliance Pickup Locations FAQ

Can one inquiry cover multiple commercial locations?

Yes. One inquiry can describe a single site, multiple properties or a broader service area. List the primary location plus additional cities, ZIP codes or addresses.

Do you guarantee service in every U.S. city?

No. Requests are reviewed by location, appliance condition, access, volume and current independent-provider coverage before service is confirmed.

What information helps a multi-site review?

Include location count, appliance categories, normal and peak volume, expected frequency, access or dock rules and the desired start window.
