Which Items Might Qualify for Free Pickup?

Appliance

Working major household appliances including washers, dryers, refrigerators, stoves and freezers are considered first for reuse. Broken units are reviewed individually, and dishwashers are considered only with two or more other working major appliances.

Washer & Dryer

Please say whether the washer fills, drains and spins, and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. For a set, report both conditions, visible damage and whether power and water are safely disconnected.

Refrigerator

Please report refrigerator and freezer cooling, the model configuration, dimensions, any water or ice maker issues, and whether the doorways are wide enough. Do not cut sealed refrigerant lines.

Stove & Oven

Please specify gas, electric or dual-fuel range, whether the burners and oven work, and whether gas disconnection has been completed by a qualified professional. Do not disconnect gas yourself.

Freezer

Describe whether it is a chest or upright freezer, whether it is currently freezing, any broken seals or damage, and basement or doorway access. Keep cooling lines intact.

North Dakota Appliance Recycling Options

The North Dakota Department of Environmental Quality explains that appliance recycling is encouraged at scrap-metal recyclers and community collection sites. Its Guideline 15 explains that major appliances are prohibited from landfill disposal and refrigerant recovery must be handled properly. Verify local collection requirements and any fees independently.

Do not cut refrigerant lines, vent cooling gases, or disconnect gas plumbing yourself. These independent agencies are not affiliated with Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.
