What Qualifies for Free Pickup in San Jose?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. We also review the San Jose pickup address, building or property access, current demand and available route coverage before scheduling.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

San Jose Appliance Pickup & Removal

Submit a request for appliance replacement, move-out, rental turnover, garage cleanup or removal of reusable household appliances from a home or business. Give the condition of every appliance rather than describing the load as a single item.

Photo-first review helps. Send a full appliance photo, model-label photo when available, and pictures of difficult stairs, hallways or doorways so the request can be reviewed before a pickup time is confirmed.

Appliances We Review

Washers

Top-load and front-load washers. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and mention leaks, noise or error codes.

Dryers

Gas and electric dryers, including laundry sets. Identify the fuel type and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Refrigerators

Top-freezer, side-by-side, bottom-freezer and French-door refrigerators. Include an interior photo and whether both sections maintain temperature.

Freezers

Chest and upright household freezers. Include an interior photo and confirm whether the unit freezes properly.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may qualify. Tell us whether the burners and oven operate normally.

Multiple Appliances

For property turnovers or several appliances, list each item separately with its working condition.

San Jose Areas We Review

We review qualifying requests across San Jose, including Downtown San Jose, Willow Glen, Almaden Valley, Rose Garden, Berryessa, Evergreen, Blossom Valley, Cambrian and surrounding neighborhoods.

Selected nearby Santa Clara County requests may also be submitted for coverage review. Send the exact city and ZIP code because route availability varies by location and appliance load.

Townhome, Apartment & Garage Access

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, kitchen, laundry room, upstairs townhome, apartment, condo or business. Include the floor level, stair flights, elevator availability, shared-garage restrictions, gates, narrow hallways and parking or loading limitations.

For rentals and managed properties, include any move-in/move-out hours, loading-zone rules or building access instructions. For large refrigerators, send a photo of tight doorways or turns along the exit path.

Photos and Information to Send

Send one full photo of each appliance, the working condition, San Jose ZIP code and access details. A model-label photo is useful when available. Refrigerator and freezer requests should include an interior photo and cooling status.

For washers, tell us whether the machine fills, drains and spins. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether it heats and tumbles. Call or text 310-774-4304, Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM.

San Jose Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free in San Jose?

Qualifying working appliances and eligible mixed loads may receive free pickup after condition, access, location, demand and coverage are reviewed.

Do you pick up a broken appliance by itself for free?

No. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work.

Do you review washer and dryer sets?

Yes. Working laundry sets are a priority category. Tell us the condition of both machines and whether the dryer is gas or electric.

Can I submit a townhome or apartment pickup?

Yes. Include floor level, stairs, elevator, garage and building-access details before scheduling.

How do I request pickup?

Use the online request form or call or text 310-774-4304 with appliance photos, condition, San Jose ZIP code and access details.
