What Usually Qualifies in Redlands?

For Redlands washer and dryer requests, show the actual machines and explain whether both are functional. A complete, testable pair may be easier to review for reuse than an untested load, but condition, removal access and current route capacity determine eligibility.

Washer test results

Say whether the washer fills, agitates, drains and reaches final spin without a leak. Add a close-up photo if an error code, corrosion or damaged hose is visible.

Dryer test results

Tell us if the dryer is gas or electric and whether it tumbles and heats. If it is still connected or stacked, explain that so the removal plan does not assume it is ready to carry.

Hillside or sloped driveways

For a sloping driveway or raised entrance, provide a photo of the loading path and note steps between the laundry room and the vehicle. Include a gate or parking restriction where relevant.

Older homes and rentals

Measure a narrow laundry doorway or turning point if space is limited. For an apartment or rental changeover, confirm who can grant entry and whether a building elevator must be reserved.

Single working machines may be submitted. For damaged or untested units, describe the issue and wait for an availability review before assuming removal will be free.

When Redlands Free Reuse Pickup Is Not Available

The City of Redlands bulky-item pickup program is a separate option to investigate. The City states that eligible single-family residential customers receive two free bulky-item pickups each calendar year, with up to three items per pickup. Call City Customer Service at 909-798-7529 to confirm whether your specific washer or dryer is accepted, available appointment dates and any preparation requirements.

The City's collection crews do not enter a residence to retrieve bulky items; approved items must be ready for curbside collection on the scheduled day, according to City guidance. If your laundry units are upstairs or still connected, confirm safe removal arrangements separately. This municipal collection option is not a booking through Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.
