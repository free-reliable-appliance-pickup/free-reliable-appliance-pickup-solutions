Local Rules and Independent Alternatives

Independent Rhode Island recycling options: The Rhode Island Resource Recovery Residential Recycling Area accepts certain white goods as self-delivered materials. Check its current fees, requirements and accepted-items list before traveling. For qualifying working refrigerators or freezers, Rhode Island Energy has a separate appliance recycling program with its own requirements. These organizations are not affiliated with Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.

Refrigerants must be managed by qualified personnel. Never cut a sealed cooling line or disconnect a gas connection yourself. Local town collection rules and fees can change.

For town information, see the official Rhode Island cities and towns directory. Availability, fees and municipal residency rules vary.
