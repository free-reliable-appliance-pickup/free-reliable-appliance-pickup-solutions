How free appliance pickup requests work

  1. Choose an appliance and accurately report whether it works.
  2. Provide a current photo, exact ZIP, floor, stairs, and loading access.
  3. We check whether an accepting independent local operator can serve that ZIP and appliance.
  4. If an operator accepts, pickup terms are confirmed before scheduling. A separately approved paid quote is optional.

Rhode Island pickup operators and ZIP service coverage have not been verified. A submission does not book or guarantee free pickup.

Rhode Island appliance recycling alternatives

RI Resource Recovery handles selected white goods through its special-items program, with posted rules and possible charges. Providence publishes a separate municipal collection option for qualified residents. Rhode Island Energy publishes a working-refrigerator/freezer recycling program with conditional rebates. These are independent options, not services performed by Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.
