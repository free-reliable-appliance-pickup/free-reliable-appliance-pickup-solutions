Fully working and reusable major appliances receive the strongest consideration. Minor-repair appliances may be reviewed when current local demand and route capacity support the pickup. For mixed loads, tell us the condition of every machine rather than describing the whole load as working.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Submitting a request begins a review; it does not create a guaranteed appointment. Read the full qualification rules before submitting a heavily damaged or nonworking load.
