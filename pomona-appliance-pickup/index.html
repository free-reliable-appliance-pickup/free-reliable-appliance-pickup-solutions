Pomona City Bulky-Item Pickup for Appliances We Cannot Accept

The City of Pomona operates a separate residential bulky-item collection program through Athens Services. The City's current guidance includes refrigerators, stoves, and washer/dryer pairs among eligible bulky items. For qualifying customers it lists six no-charge appointments per calendar year, with a limit of four large items per collection, subject to the program's rules.

If your item is broken or cannot be accepted on our reuse-focused route, contact Athens Services at (888) 314-0061 to ask about curbside collection.
