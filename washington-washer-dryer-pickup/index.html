When is pickup free?

Fully working and reusable major appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Nonworking items alone generally do not qualify. Free pickup is confirmed only after reviewing condition, access, local demand and available routes. Submission does not guarantee pickup.
