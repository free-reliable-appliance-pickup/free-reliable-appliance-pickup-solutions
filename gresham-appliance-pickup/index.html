Gresham Appliance Pickup for Reusable Major Appliances

Gresham is a priority part of our Portland-metro service area. We review requests for working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges, with the appliance condition and pickup path considered together. A garage pickup with driveway access can be very different from an appliance in a basement, upstairs apartment or long interior carry.

A single appliance that is completely nonworking does not qualify for our free service. Minor repair situations may be considered, and mixed loads can be reviewed when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. That qualification protects the free-pickup model while giving reusable equipment a chance to stay in use.

Best request: send photos, tested working details and the exact access situation before a route is scheduled.

Appliances We Most Often Want to Review

Washers

Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins. Mention leaks, bearing noise, lid or door-lock problems and any error codes you know about.

Dryers

Report whether the dryer tumbles and heats and whether it is gas or electric. Working washer-dryer pairs are especially useful to submit together.

Refrigerators & Freezers

State whether the unit is currently plugged in and holding temperature. Include interior photos and note missing shelves, bins or major damage.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and describe burner, oven and control operation. Tell us whether the appliance is already disconnected.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Garages, Basements, Apartments and East-Side Routes

For Gresham homes, tell us whether the appliance is in the garage, kitchen, laundry room, basement or outside. List exterior and interior stairs, narrow doors, tight turns and long carries. For apartments, include the floor, elevator availability, hallway access and where the pickup vehicle can load.

For rental turnovers or property-manager requests, list each appliance separately and identify which ones work. If several units are already staged in a garage or maintenance area, say so. Grouped working appliances may be much easier to route than separate unknown-condition items across several occupied units.

Gresham Hauler Pickup Is a Paid Backup When Reuse Does Not Fit

If an appliance does not qualify for our free reuse-based pickup, the City of Gresham publishes bulky-item and appliance pickup rates through local garbage haulers. The current city rate page lists a trip charge plus an item-specific fee, with higher charges for refrigerators and freezers than for washers, dryers and stoves. Residents should check the current City rate table and contact their hauler before setting an appliance out.

For a washer or dryer, describe the actual cycle functions. For a refrigerator or freezer, tell us whether it is cold now and how long it has been plugged in. Send a clear full photo, plus an interior image for cooling appliances and a model-label photo when practical.

Gresham Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can you review a basement appliance?

Yes. Tell us the stair count, width, turns and whether the appliance is already disconnected so the removal path can be evaluated.

Are working washer and dryer sets a priority?

Yes. Working laundry equipment is in strong demand. Submit both machines together and describe what you tested.

What if my appliance does not qualify?

Gresham residents can check the City's current hauler pickup rates or other authorized disposal and recycling options.
