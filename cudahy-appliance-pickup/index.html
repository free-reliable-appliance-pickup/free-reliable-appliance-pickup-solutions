Free Pickup Rules

A single working refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer, stove or range can be reviewed. Minor repair needs may sometimes be considered. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for the standard free route.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are not accepted by themselves.

Cudahy Compact-Lot Access & Bulky-Item Timing

Cudahy requests often involve apartments, rear units, narrow side paths and shared parking on compact lots. Send the entire removal path, not only the appliance photo: front or rear unit, gate width, stairs, narrow doorways, shared-yard obstacles and the distance to a legal curb or alley loading point.

The City of Cudahy identifies Republic Services as its trash provider and publishes bulky-item timing guidance. That municipal collection is separate from our qualification-based reuse pickup. Customers with a nonqualifying appliance should confirm the latest city rules before curb placement.
