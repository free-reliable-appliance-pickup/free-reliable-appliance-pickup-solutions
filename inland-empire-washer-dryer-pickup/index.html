Washer Condition We Need

Power & fill

Tell us whether the washer powers on and fills normally without persistent error codes.

Wash, drain & spin

Report whether the washer agitates or tumbles, drains completely and reaches final spin.

Leaks, noise & locks

Mention leaks, loud bearings, broken lid or door locks, damaged controls or water left inside the machine.

Dryer Condition We Need

Gas or electric

Identify the dryer type so the request can be reviewed correctly.

Tumble & heat

Tell us whether the dryer powers on, the drum turns and the machine produces heat.

Complete laundry sets

Submit both machines together when they are a pair. Complete working sets receive especially strong priority.

Apartment, Garage & Interior Access

Inland Empire requests come from garages, houses, apartments, condos, gated communities, rental turnovers and business properties. Include the appliance location and removal path before scheduling.

Stairs & elevators

Give the floor level, number of stair flights and whether an elevator can be used.

Gates & parking

Include gate instructions, loading rules, parking restrictions, alleys or long carries.

Stacked laundry

Tell us whether the machines are separate stacked units or a one-piece laundry center and whether they can be accessed safely.

Inland Empire Washer & Dryer FAQ

Can one working washer qualify?

Yes. A single working washer can be reviewed. Report whether it fills, washes, drains and spins.

Can one working dryer qualify?

Yes. Tell us whether it is gas or electric, tumbles and heats.

Do upstairs pickups automatically disqualify a request?

No. Stairs, elevators and carrying distance are reviewed as part of access. Include those details before scheduling.

Is free pickup guaranteed?

No. Condition, exact location, access, local demand and current route availability must be confirmed first.
