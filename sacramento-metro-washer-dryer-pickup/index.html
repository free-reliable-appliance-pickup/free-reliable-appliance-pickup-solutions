Why Washer & Dryer Pickup Is a Priority Sacramento Market

Working washers and dryers can have strong reuse value because buyers, repair professionals and resellers may be able to test, repair, refurbish and place them back into service. Complete working sets are especially useful, but single working machines can also be reviewed.

For a washer, report power, fill, wash/agitation, drain and final spin. For a dryer, identify gas or electric service and report power, tumble and heat.

Photos + test results = stronger request. They help determine whether the machine is reusable before a pickup route is assigned.

Sacramento Metro Washer & Dryer Pickup vs. Public Programs

Public appliance programs vary by municipality across the Sacramento Metro area, so the pickup address matters.

City of Sacramento

The City currently lists washing machines and dryers among appliances accepted through its residential appliance/e-waste appointment program. Eligible residential collection customers receive two free appliance/e-waste appointments per year, with up to two appliances per appointment.

Sacramento County

County bulky-waste service is appointment-based in eligible service areas, and appliances are special set-out items that should be added through Customer Service. The County also lists large appliances among items accepted at designated free residential drop-off facilities, subject to current rules.
