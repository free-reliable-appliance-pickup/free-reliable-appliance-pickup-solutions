Santa Barbara Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens for qualifying pickup. Fully working major appliances may qualify, and eligible mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A nonworking appliance by itself generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Santa Barbara requests can involve older homes, apartments, hillside access, narrow driveways, scheduled building access and parking limits. Send the actual ZIP code, floor level, stairs or elevator details, and whether the appliance is already disconnected and ready to move.
