Santa Clarita Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Santa Clarita is not one small neighborhood; it includes multiple communities and ZIP areas across a large valley. A form entry that only says “Santa Clarita” is not enough for reliable routing, so we use the actual pickup address and ZIP.

Working and reusable refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. A fully working major appliance may qualify for free pickup; mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Qualification first: submitting the form starts a review. Pickup is confirmed only after condition, exact location, access and route availability are checked.

Major Appliances We Review

Refrigerators & Freezers

Report cooling status and send exterior, interior and model-label photos when possible.

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether the drum turns and heats.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and describe burner, oven and control operation.

Package Loads

List every appliance and individual condition when several machines are available at one property.

Santa Clarita Communities & ZIP Routing

City materials identify Santa Clarita Valley ZIP areas including 91321, 91350, 91351, 91354, 91355, 91381, 91382, 91384, 91387 and 91390. Core city communities include Newhall, Saugus, Valencia and Canyon Country.

Because the valley covers a broad area, route distance can change significantly between addresses. Submit the full street address, not just the community name.

Santa Clarita Home & Property Access

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, interior laundry room, kitchen, apartment, gated community or hillside property. Include stairs, gates, long driveways, parking restrictions and carrying distance.

Santa Clarita Bulky-Item Alternative

Santa Clarita's current waste services are handled by Burrtec, and the City's Green Santa Clarita resources list furniture, appliances and other large household items as acceptable bulky items. Use the City's current bulky-item guidance when an appliance does not qualify for reuse-focused pickup.

Santa Clarita Appliance Removal FAQ

Do working appliances receive priority?

Yes. Reusable appliances receive the strongest priority because the goal is to keep useful machines in service when practical.

Can landlords or property managers submit several appliances?

Yes. List all appliances, condition and access details together when they are at the same property.

Should I send photos?

Yes. Photos help us identify appliance style, visible condition and access issues before a route is committed.
