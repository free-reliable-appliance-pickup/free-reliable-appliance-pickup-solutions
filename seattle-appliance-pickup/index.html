Seattle Appliance Pickup Through the Washington Partner Network

This Seattle page is a request and qualification resource, not a claim that a permanent Free Reliable Appliance Pickup truck is stationed in Seattle. Washington is currently handled through a partner-coverage model. A request can be reviewed for a Seattle-area appliance buyer, reseller, repair business or pickup professional when one is available for the actual address and appliance.

Working condition matters because the network is designed first around reuse. Refrigerators and freezers should still cool correctly; washers should fill, drain and spin; dryers should tumble and heat; stoves and ranges should have working burners and oven functions. A fully working major appliance may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may be considered when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single completely nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify.

Do not move a reusable appliance to the curb just because a form was submitted. A request is confirmed only after qualification and partner availability are accepted directly.

Seattle Apartments, Condos, Hills and Loading Access

Seattle pickup details often depend on the building more than the ZIP code. Tell us whether the appliance is in a single-family home, apartment, condo, townhome, business or managed property. For an upper-floor unit, include the floor, elevator availability, elevator reservation rules and the distance from the unit to the loading area. If a building requires a certificate, loading appointment or property-manager approval, include that before a partner is matched.

Stairs and narrow interiors

Describe interior or exterior stairs, tight hallway turns, basement steps and narrow doors. Photos of the path can prevent a partner from accepting a request that needs equipment or labor not planned for.

Parking and loading

Seattle streets and multifamily buildings can have limited loading access. Tell us whether there is a driveway, alley, loading zone, garage clearance restriction or long carry from the appliance to the vehicle.

Weather and outdoor staging

Keep a reusable appliance protected while the request is being reviewed. Rain exposure can damage controls, insulation and metal surfaces, reducing reuse value. Wait for direct pickup confirmation before placing an appliance outside.

Major Appliances We Review in Seattle

Refrigerators and Freezers

Send exterior and interior photos, cooling status and model information when available. Keep the unit plugged in when practical so working condition can be verified.

Washers and Dryers

Tell us what was tested on each machine. Complete working laundry sets are useful, but each washer and dryer should have its own condition description.

Stoves, Ranges and Ovens

Identify gas or electric, working burner and oven functions, and whether the appliance is safely disconnected. Do not disconnect gas equipment solely to submit a request.

Multiple Appliances

List every major appliance separately. Mixed loads can be more practical than a single repair item when most of the load is working and the pickup is at one accessible location.

Special categories: microwaves are not accepted alone. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Seattle Public Utilities Special Item Pickup Is a Separate Option

If an appliance does not fit our reuse-focused partner network, Seattle Public Utilities has an official Special Item Pickup program for SPU customers. SPU lists large furniture, large electronics and appliances among the items it can collect. Current appliance charges depend on the item, and the utility account holder must be able to authorize charges. Renters and multifamily residents may need the property manager or owner to arrange the service.

SPU also advertises an annual free pickup for batteries and a Special Item Box beginning April 1, 2026. That free annual offer should not be confused with the standard large-appliance category, which has its own item-specific pricing and rules.

Official resource: Seattle Public Utilities Special Item Pickup · SPU customer service: 206-684-3000. Verify current charges, eligibility and setout instructions with SPU before scheduling.

Commercial and Recurring Seattle Appliance Sources

Property managers, apartments, senior communities, installers, appliance businesses and other commercial sources can submit recurring inventory. Include how often appliances become available, the typical appliance mix, whether equipment is tested, how many items are stored at one address and whether loading access is controlled by building management.

Recurring Seattle supply is especially useful for partner development because it can support a dependable local route instead of isolated one-time travel. A commercial submission still does not guarantee acceptance; the equipment condition and available partner capacity must be reviewed.

Seattle Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is free appliance pickup guaranteed in Seattle?

No. Submission is free, but pickup is confirmed only after appliance condition, access, exact location and available Seattle-area partner coverage are reviewed.

What Seattle appliance details should I send?

Send the appliance type, tested working condition, exact pickup address or ZIP code, photos, floor level, stairs, elevator or loading details and any building access restrictions.

What if my Seattle appliance does not qualify for the partner pickup network?

Seattle Public Utilities offers Special Item Pickup for eligible utility customers, including large appliances. The utility account holder or property manager should verify current charges, scheduling and setout rules directly with SPU.
