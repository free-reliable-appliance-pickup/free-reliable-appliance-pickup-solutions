Freezer Pickup in Denver

Working upright and chest freezers can be submitted for Denver pickup review. Freezing performance, cleanliness, completeness, size and property access help determine whether a free route is practical.

Send first: exact Denver ZIP/address, clear photos, working condition, brand/model if known, floor/stairs and access details.

What We Need to Know

Freezing Condition

Tell us whether the freezer is plugged in and maintaining proper frozen temperature.

Type & Size

State whether it is an upright or chest freezer and include approximate dimensions when possible.

Location

Hot-garage, patio or outdoor storage can affect condition, so tell us where the freezer is currently operating or stored.

Denver Address & Access Review

Denver requests may come from apartments, condos, basements, garages and single-family homes across Downtown, Capitol Hill, Five Points, Central Park, Washington Park, Highlands and nearby neighborhoods. Submit the exact street address and ZIP so the route can be reviewed correctly.

Include stairs, basement turns, elevator rules, doorway clearance, parking/loading access and carrying distance before pickup is confirmed.

Free Pickup Is Qualification-Based

Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Minor repair needs may be considered when local reuse demand supports the route. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify under the standard free-pickup rules.

Need Pickup for a Different Appliance?
