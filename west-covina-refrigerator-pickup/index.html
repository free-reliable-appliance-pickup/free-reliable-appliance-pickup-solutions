Prepare the Refrigerator Safely

Keep the sealed cooling system intact. Do not cut refrigerant lines or remove the compressor. Keep the refrigerator testable until its condition has been reviewed when practical. After an accepted pickup is confirmed, remove food and loose items and follow the preparation instructions provided for the route.
