Appliances We Review for Free Pickup

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges are the primary categories. Fully working appliances are the strongest candidates because the service is based on reuse potential. Minor repair may sometimes be considered, while a single completely nonworking appliance does not qualify by itself.

Mixed loads may qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only when included with two or more major working appliances.
