Vernon: business equipment and permission to remove

Washer and dryer requests from Vernon can come from industrial properties, maintenance departments, small businesses or the city's limited residential properties. Identify whether a machine is standard household laundry equipment or a commercial unit; capacity and dimensions affect whether a normal appliance pickup is practical. If it sits inside a fenced workplace, provide the business contact, permitted pickup window and whether the appliance can be reached from ground level. Note security check-in rules, freight elevator requirements, loading dock height or whether the driver would need to park on the street. Do not assume a dock or forklift will be available.

Before submitting a business request, confirm you have authority to release the equipment and that it is not part of a leased or serviced installation. Explain if electrical, water or gas connections are still attached; gas lines and hardwired equipment should be disconnected only by qualified personnel. Provide clear photos of the whole machine and model plate when available, plus working-test results. A washer that leaks or fails to spin and a dryer that tumbles without heating should be described accurately. Free pickup is conditional and is not guaranteed for commercial or unusually heavy appliances. When reuse pickup is unsuitable, the City of Vernon's listed solid-waste hauler options above provide a separate disposal path.
