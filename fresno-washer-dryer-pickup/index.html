Free Washer Pickup in Fresno

If you are replacing a washing machine, moving, clearing a rental, or simply need an unused washer removed, submit the model condition and pickup location. For a working washer, tell us whether it powers on, fills with water, washes or agitates, drains and reaches the final spin.

Prepare a Washer or Dryer for Fresno Pickup

Washer preparation

If it is safe to do so, unplug the washer, shut off the hot and cold water valves and let hoses drain before pickup. If a valve is stuck, the washer still contains water or you are unsure what to disconnect, leave it in place and describe the setup in the request.

Electric dryer preparation

If the dryer is electric and safely accessible, unplugging it can simplify removal. Tell us if the plug or outlet is behind a stacked unit, inside a tight closet or difficult to reach.

Gas dryer preparation

Do not disconnect a gas dryer unless you are qualified to do so. Tell us that the dryer is gas and whether it is still connected so the pickup can be reviewed correctly.

Stacked units & laundry centers

Send a full photo showing the installation. Say whether it is a one-piece laundry center or two separate machines joined by a stacking kit, and include ceiling, doorway and stair clearance.

Inside Pickup, Garages, Stairs & Laundry Closets

Washers and dryers are heavy and can be difficult to remove from tight spaces. Tell us where each machine is located: garage, driveway, laundry room, apartment, upstairs unit, basement, storage area or another location.

Include the number of stair flights, elevator access, tight doors, long hallways, gates, parking restrictions and any unusually long carry distance. If the machines are stacked, say whether they are two separate units with a stacking kit or a one-piece laundry center.

Do not leave water inside a washing machine. If the washer will not drain, mention that before pickup because retained water adds weight and can spill during moving.
