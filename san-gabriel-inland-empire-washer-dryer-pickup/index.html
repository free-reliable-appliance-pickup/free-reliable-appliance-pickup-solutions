One Connected Premium Washer & Dryer Corridor

This corridor connects the foothill communities and Pasadena area with the central San Gabriel Valley, Pomona Valley and the Inland Empire. The goal is to give customers one useful regional hub while still providing dedicated pages for important cities where local access, routing and appliance condition can differ.

Working washers, dryers and complete matching sets receive the strongest consideration. A single working machine can also be reviewed. Repairable or nonworking equipment is considered only when current demand and route capacity make pickup practical.

For faster review: include clear photos, brand/model if known, tested washer and dryer functions, gas or electric dryer type, floor level, stairs, gates, driveway or parking access and the exact city or ZIP code.

What Makes a Strong Pickup Request?

Working condition

For washers, report fill, wash or tumble, drain and spin. For dryers, report gas or electric, power, drum movement and heat.

Clear photos

Send front and control-panel photos, inside the washer or dryer if useful, and a model/serial label when available.

Removal access

Include garage or interior location, stairs, narrow doors, gates, parking, driveway access and carrying distance.

Complete sets

Matching working washer and dryer sets receive especially strong priority because both machines can often be routed together.

Corridor FAQ

Is every washer or dryer pickup automatically free?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based and depends on condition, access, location and current route availability.

Can I submit only one washer or one dryer?

Yes. A single working machine can be reviewed. Complete working sets generally receive stronger priority.

Do you review Ontario and Rancho Cucamonga requests too?

Yes. Ontario, Upland and Rancho Cucamonga are part of the eastbound corridor toward Fontana and San Bernardino.
