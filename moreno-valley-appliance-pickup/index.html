Moreno Valley Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Moreno Valley is a large Riverside County city with different access conditions across established neighborhoods, apartment communities, newer developments and properties closer to the eastern edge of the valley. Exact address and access information matter more than a city-wide promise.

We prioritize appliances that can remain in use, including fully working refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves and complete laundry sets. Minor-repair appliances and mixed loads can also be reviewed when the overall request supports the route.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single completely nonworking appliance generally does not qualify.

Major Appliances We Review in Moreno Valley

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the appliance is plugged in and holding temperature. Exterior, interior and model-label photos help us understand style, size and condition.

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report power, fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether the drum turns and produces heat.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and describe whether burners, oven functions and controls operate normally. Safe disconnection is required before pickup.

Multiple Appliances

For property turnovers or several replacements, list every major appliance and its individual condition so the complete load can be reviewed together.

Moreno Valley Route & Property Access

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, apartment, home interior, driveway or outside. Include stairs, gates, parking, narrow doors and carrying distance. For rental turnovers, list all appliances at the property together rather than submitting each machine separately.

For refrigerators, report current cooling status and send interior and exterior photos when possible. For laundry equipment, report tested functions instead of simply writing “works.”

Access Details That Affect Pickup

Include apartment floor, stairs, gated entry, garage or driveway location, parking distance and any tight hallways or kitchen turns.

Do not move a heavy appliance to a curb, hallway or stair landing just because a request was submitted. Keep it safely located until pickup is reviewed and confirmed.

Qualification Limits

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Minor-repair items can be described for review, but approval is not automatic.

Submitting the form starts a review. It does not guarantee free pickup, a specific day or a particular arrival time.

Moreno Valley Appliance Removal FAQ

Do working appliances receive priority?

Yes. Working and reusable appliances receive the strongest priority because reuse is preferred over disposal whenever practical.

Should I send photos?

Yes. Clear front, interior and model-label photos can help us evaluate the appliance faster and reduce failed trips.

Can landlords or property managers submit several appliances?

Yes. List every appliance, condition, unit location and access detail in one request when they are at the same property.
