Spokane Appliance Pickup Request Review

Have a working washer, dryer, refrigerator, freezer, stove or range to remove in Spokane? Submit the appliance details for review. Free pickup is possible only if the appliance qualifies and a local pickup partner confirms availability for your address.

This is not a staffed Spokane storefront or a confirmed daily pickup route. Do not set an appliance outside or treat a submitted request as an appointment until the pickup is approved.

Request appliance pickup in Spokane

Submitting is free. Tell us what works, how the appliance can be accessed, and the pickup ZIP code. We will review the request before confirming whether free pickup is possible.

Working major appliances receive priority; a single nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when most major appliances work. Dishwashers require two or more major working appliances; microwaves are not accepted alone.
