Signal Hill: apartment move-outs and loading arrangements

In Signal Hill, a washer and dryer may be in a condominium laundry closet, an upper-floor apartment, a garage or a business storage area. Apartment requests should identify the unit floor, elevator availability and any move-out or loading-hour restrictions. For hillside homes, explain driveway slope and whether a truck or trailer can park close to the door without blocking traffic. For a commercial location, include a contact who can authorize release of the appliance and describe any loading gate or dock. These details help distinguish a straightforward garage pickup from a stop needing extra labor or approval.

If the machines form a set, describe the washer and dryer separately: the washer's filling, draining and spinning; the dryer's drum rotation and heating; and whether the dryer is gas or electric. Visible rust, leaks, missing knobs, unusual noises or error codes should be mentioned instead of selecting fully working. Current photos of both appliances are needed for qualification, but this request form does not upload images directly. Please arrange to text photographs during follow-up. No pickup time is reserved by sending a form; we first verify condition, access and local capacity. For a disposal route rather than a reuse review, consult the city's separate bulky-item collection resource linked above.
