Eugene Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

This is the Eugene-specific page for general major-appliance pickup requests. We review qualifying refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves, ranges, ovens and mixed household appliance loads using the actual pickup address, appliance condition and removal access.

Free pickup is not automatic. Fully working major appliances may qualify, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Best request: send clear appliance photos, tested working condition, exact Eugene address or ZIP code, floor level, stairs, gates, parking or loading limits and carrying distance.

Photos & Working Tests Help Eugene Route Review

Working-condition details are more useful than a generic statement that an appliance is "good." For a washer, report fill, drain and spin. For a dryer, report tumble and heat. For a refrigerator or freezer, say whether it is currently plugged in and holding temperature.

Send one clear full photo plus interior photos for cooling appliances when practical. If there is rust, a cracked shelf, cosmetic damage or a known repair issue, show or describe it accurately so the request can be reviewed before a route is scheduled.

Qualification Limits

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances. Minor-repair appliances may be submitted honestly for review, but acceptance depends on current demand and route capacity.

Submitting a request starts the review process. It does not guarantee free pickup or a specific pickup time until condition, location, access and route availability are confirmed.

Lane County Appliance Disposal Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup, Lane County Waste Management publishes current appliance recycling and disposal options. County guidance says appliances are accepted at most Lane County transfer sites, with a separate appliance fee for refrigerant-containing units such as refrigerators and freezers. Lane County also recommends reuse for newer appliances when practical.

That public disposal path is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup. Check Lane County's current fees, preparation rules and site availability before transporting an appliance.
