What Qualifies for Free Pickup in Palmdale?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. We also review the Palmdale address, access, local demand and available Antelope Valley route coverage before scheduling.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Palmdale Appliance Pickup & Removal

Submit a request when replacing an appliance, clearing a rental, handling a move-out, cleaning a garage or removing reusable household appliances. Tell us the condition of each item so the entire load can be reviewed accurately.

Photos help us review faster. Send a full photo of each appliance, a model-label photo when available, and pictures of difficult access such as stairs, gates or tight doorways.

Major Appliances We Review

Washers

Top-load and front-load washers. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and mention leaks or error codes.

Dryers

Gas and electric dryers, including matching laundry sets. Identify the fuel type and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Refrigerators

Top-freezer, side-by-side, bottom-freezer and French-door refrigerators. Include an interior photo and cooling condition.

Freezers

Chest and upright household freezers. Include an interior photo and confirm whether the unit freezes properly.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may qualify. Tell us whether the burners and oven operate normally.

Multiple Appliances

For garage cleanouts or property turnovers, list every appliance and its working condition separately.

Palmdale & Antelope Valley Areas

We review qualifying requests throughout Palmdale, including central Palmdale, Rancho Vista, west Palmdale, east Palmdale and surrounding neighborhoods. Selected nearby Antelope Valley requests may also be reviewed depending on route capacity.

Garage, Driveway & Inside Access

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, kitchen, laundry room, first floor, upstairs apartment, storage area or business. Include stairs, gates, long carries, narrow halls, doorways and parking or driveway restrictions.

For large refrigerators, send a photo of the exit route if a doorway or turn is tight. For rentals or managed properties, include access instructions before pickup is confirmed.

Photos and Information to Send

Send one full photo of each appliance, the working condition, Palmdale ZIP code and access details. Refrigerator and freezer requests should include an interior photo and whether the unit maintains temperature.

For washers, tell us whether the machine fills, drains and spins. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether it heats and tumbles. Call or text 310-774-4304, Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM.

Palmdale Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free in Palmdale?

Qualifying working appliances and eligible mixed loads may receive free pickup after condition, access, location, demand and route coverage are reviewed.

Do you pick up a broken appliance by itself for free?

No. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Do you review washer and dryer sets?

Yes. Working laundry sets are a priority category. Tell us the condition of both machines and whether the dryer is gas or electric.

Can I submit a nearby Antelope Valley address?

Yes. Send the city and ZIP code for review. Coverage depends on the exact location, appliance load and current route capacity.

How do I request pickup?

Use the online request form or call or text 310-774-4304 with appliance photos, condition, Palmdale ZIP code and access details.
