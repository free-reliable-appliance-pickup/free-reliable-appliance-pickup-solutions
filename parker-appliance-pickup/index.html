Parker Appliance Pickup Through the Denver-Aurora Network

Parker requests are reviewed through our Colorado network serving the Denver and Aurora area and surrounding routes. We do not automatically promise every appliance or every address. Instead, we review condition, route distance, access and current appliance demand before confirming a free pickup.

Free pickup is qualification-based. A fully working major appliance may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances work. A standalone nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup.

Colorado Categories We Value Most

Washers & dryers

Working laundry machines and complete sets are priority requests. Describe whether the washer completes a cycle and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Freezers

Working chest and upright freezers are useful Colorado inventory. Include approximate size, current temperature performance and pickup location.

Refrigerators

Tell us whether both compartments cool correctly. Include exterior and interior photos when possible and mention missing shelves or cosmetic damage.

Stoves & ranges

Major cooking appliances may be reviewed when the working condition and access are clearly described.

Parker Access Information That Matters

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, main floor, upper floor or basement. A simple garage pickup is different from moving a large refrigerator through a narrow interior path, so we need the current location before assigning a route.

For homes with limited curb space, gated access or a long carry from the appliance to the truck area, include those details in the request. If multiple appliances are available, list them together because a larger working load can change route efficiency.

Free Pickup Limits

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Appliances with significant damage or unknown condition may require additional photos or information before a decision is made.

Submitting a form is a request for review, not a guaranteed appointment. We confirm the address, condition, access and an available Colorado route before pickup is scheduled.

Parker Appliance Pickup FAQs

How far is Parker from your main Colorado route?

Parker is reviewed as part of the broader Denver-Aurora coverage area. Exact approval depends on the address and current route.

Can I request a freezer from a garage?

Yes. Include whether it is chest or upright, whether it holds temperature and whether the garage has clear loading access.

Can I include a nonworking appliance with working ones?

Possibly. Mixed loads are reviewed as a whole, with the working share being an important factor.
