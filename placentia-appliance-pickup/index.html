Placentia Appliance Pickup Review

Qualifying working and reusable major appliances can receive free pickup in Placentia after review. We review the appliance condition, exact pickup address, property access, local demand and current north Orange County route availability before confirming a free pickup.

For the fastest review, send clear photos and tested condition. For refrigerators and freezers, include cooling status and interior photos. For washers, report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, report tumble and heat. For stoves or ranges, tell us whether the burners and oven work.

Free pickup is qualification-based. A single working refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer, stove or range can be reviewed. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

When an Appliance Does Not Qualify for Our Free Pickup

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup, the City of Placentia lists a separate bulky-item service through Republic Services. The City's current residential information states that single-family customers receive four free curbside bulky-item collections per year, with up to eight items per pickup, and appliances are included among eligible bulky items.

Municipal bulky-item service is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup and can have different rules for multi-family or commercial properties. Confirm current requirements directly with the City or Republic Services.
