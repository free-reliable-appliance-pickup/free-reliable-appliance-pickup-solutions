Irwindale Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Residents, landlords, property managers and businesses can submit washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and other qualifying major appliances for review. Working and reusable equipment receives priority because reuse is the first goal.

Free pickup is not automatic. We review the appliance condition, exact pickup location, route direction, local demand and removal access before confirming service. A single nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify.

Best request: include clear photos, whether the appliance is currently testable, exact address, floor level, stairs, gates, narrow doorways and carrying distance.

High-Priority Appliance Requests in Irwindale

Washers & dryers

Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. Submit both machines together when they are a set.

Refrigerators & freezers

Report whether the unit is plugged in and holding temperature. Interior, exterior and model-label photos help with review.

Stoves & ranges

Identify gas or electric and describe whether burners, oven functions and controls work normally.

Rental turnovers

List every appliance separately with its condition and access so a multi-item request can be reviewed accurately.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Local Route and Access in Irwindale

Irwindale requests may come from residential properties, mixed commercial areas and locations near industrial corridors. Tell us whether the appliance is inside a home, garage, business or secured property and include gate, loading and vehicle-access information.

Garage and driveway pickups are usually easier to evaluate when the loading vehicle can get close. Indoor pickups need more detail about doorways, flooring, stairs, elevators, gates and tight turns. For apartments or shared properties, include parking and loading restrictions before scheduling.

Irwindale Bulky-Item & Appliance Disposal Alternative

The City of Irwindale states that Athens Services is the City's exclusive waste hauler and that the City and Athens hold two residential bulky-item cleanup campaigns each year, one in spring and one in fall. The City's accepted-item list includes refrigerators, washers and dryers.

That municipal program is separate from our qualification-based reuse pickup. Working or reusable appliances can be submitted here first; items that do not qualify can be checked against the City's current bulky-item rules and campaign dates.

Irwindale Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can one working washer or dryer qualify?

Yes. A single working machine can be reviewed, while complete working sets generally receive stronger priority.

Can you review inside pickups?

Yes. Include floor level, stairs, doorway limits, gates and carrying distance so the removal path can be evaluated.

What if the appliance needs repair?

Minor-repair appliances may sometimes be considered depending on age, condition, parts demand and the current route, but free pickup is not guaranteed.

Irwindale Address, Industrial & Residential Pickup Review

Irwindale's planning framework includes residential, commercial, industrial and special-plan areas, and the City completed major General Plan and Housing Element updates in 2025. Appliance pickup can therefore involve very different access conditions depending on whether the property is a home, apartment, business or mixed-use location.

Use the exact street address and include garage or interior location, floor level, stairs, gates, loading-area access, parking restrictions and carrying distance. For commercial properties, include any dock, security or appointment requirements before scheduling.
