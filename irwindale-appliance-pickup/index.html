Irwindale Bulky-Item & Appliance Disposal Alternative

The City of Irwindale states that Athens Services is the City's exclusive waste hauler and that the City and Athens hold two residential bulky-item cleanup campaigns each year, one in spring and one in fall. The City's accepted-item list includes refrigerators, washers and dryers.

That municipal program is separate from our qualification-based reuse pickup. Working or reusable appliances can be submitted here first; items that do not qualify can be checked against the City's current bulky-item rules and campaign dates.

Irwindale Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is free appliance pickup guaranteed in Irwindale?

No. Free pickup depends on appliance condition, exact address, safe loading or removal access, local demand and current route availability.

Can one working appliance qualify?

Yes. A single fully working or reusable refrigerator, washer, dryer, freezer, stove or range can be submitted for review.

Can nonworking appliances be submitted?

Yes, but a single nonworking appliance normally does not qualify for free pickup. Minor-repair units and qualifying mixed loads may still be reviewed.

Do you review business or warehouse pickup?

Yes. Include appliance count, loading access, gates, parking restrictions and whether equipment is already staged.
