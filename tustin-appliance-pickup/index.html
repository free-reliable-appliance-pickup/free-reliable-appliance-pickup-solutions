Tustin Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused route, the City of Tustin publishes a separate bulky-item program through CR&R. The City states that single-family residential customers can receive up to four bulky-item collections per calendar year at no additional cost, with up to four items per pickup.

The City's accepted bulky-item list includes refrigerators, washers and dryers, stoves, dishwashers, microwaves, air conditioners and water heaters. Multifamily residents are directed to work through their property manager because collection arrangements differ from single-family service.

Tustin also instructs residents disposing of a refrigerator through the bulky-item program to remove the refrigerator doors before set-out. Always confirm current CR&R rules before placing an appliance outside.

Tustin Appliance Pickup FAQs

Can you pick up a refrigerator in Tustin for free?

A working refrigerator may qualify after cooling condition, photos, access and route availability are reviewed. Free pickup is not guaranteed for every refrigerator.

Can landlords submit several appliances?

Yes. List every appliance, unit or property location and working condition so the full load can be reviewed together.

Do you remove appliances from upstairs units?

Some upstairs requests can be reviewed, but stairs, elevator access, carrying distance and safety all matter. Send those details before scheduling.

What if my appliance is broken?

Minor-repair items may be considered depending on the appliance and current demand, but a single fully nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify.
