Free Pickup Qualification in Altadena

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges are the main categories we review. Fully working appliances are the strongest candidates. Minor repair may sometimes be considered, but one completely nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup by itself.

Mixed loads may qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Stairs, Driveways and Detached Garages

Include the number of exterior and interior steps, gate width, driveway access and whether a truck can get close to the appliance. If the item is in a detached garage or rear area, estimate the carry distance. For large refrigerators, doorway measurements can help prevent surprises.

Landlords and property managers may submit several appliances at once. List each unit and condition so the load can be reviewed under the mixed-load qualification rule.
