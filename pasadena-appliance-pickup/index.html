Appliance Condition Details That Matter

Washers

Report whether the machine fills, agitates or tumbles, drains and spins without major leaking or error conditions.

Dryers

Tell us whether it is gas or electric, whether the drum turns and whether it produces heat. Submit the matching washer too when available.

Refrigerators & Freezers

State whether the unit is currently cold or freezing and whether it has been unplugged. Interior photos help us judge cleanliness and completeness.

Ranges

Identify fuel type and report burner, oven and control operation. Built-in wall ovens require different handling than freestanding ranges.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. A dishwasher is considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Older Homes, Apartments and Property-Manager Loads

For a house with steps, give the number of exterior and interior stairs and mention narrow landings or turns. If the appliance is in a detached garage, state whether there is driveway access or a long carry. For apartments, include the floor, elevator, hallway and building access rules.

Landlords and property managers can list several appliances in one request. Tell us which items are working, which need repair and which are dead. If appliances are already grouped in a garage or vacant unit, say so. A clear inventory makes it easier to review the whole load under the mixed-load qualification rule.

Pasadena Has a Municipal Bulky-Item Option for Eligible Customers

The City requires items to be placed at the parkway and notes that staff do not enter private property to move them.

Photos Make Qualification More Accurate

A useful Pasadena request includes one full exterior photo and, for a refrigerator or freezer, an interior photo. If a washer or dryer is working, describe the tested cycle rather than only writing “good.” A model-label photo is useful for size, age and configuration when available.

Do not hide dents, rust or missing parts. We would rather know the true condition before routing. Also include whether the appliance is disconnected, whether doors must come off to clear an opening, and where a pickup vehicle can park safely.

Pasadena Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can you review a refrigerator in an older home with tight doors?

Yes. Measure or describe the narrow points and tell us whether refrigerator doors may need removal before scheduling.

Can a property manager submit several appliances?

Yes. List each appliance and condition. Mixed loads are reviewed together, especially when most major appliances are working.

Does a dead washer qualify for free pickup by itself?

No. A completely nonworking single appliance does not qualify for our free service.

Pasadena Appliance Pickup vs. City Bulky-Item Pickup

Pasadena residents can compare our qualification-based appliance pickup with the City's bulky-item program. Our route prioritizes appliances with reuse or repair value, while Pasadena's public service is a separate curbside option for eligible refuse customers.
