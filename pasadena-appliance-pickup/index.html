How Pasadena Appliance Pickup Works

1. Send appliance details

For a Pasadena property, describe the appliance and any recent tests, then provide photos and the actual unit location. Some requests involve upper floors, older doorways, parking or gated access; these details matter when deciding whether a reuse pickup is practical.

2. Send clear photos and access

If the appliance is in a multi-unit building or on a hillside lot, describe stairs, hallway turns, elevator size or booking rules, and where a vehicle can legally load. For a heavy refrigerator or stacked laundry unit, the exit width and any steps are especially useful.

3. Confirm qualification and route

We first determine whether the Pasadena item can be reused and whether pickup coverage fits the address and load. If accepted, the pickup provider confirms access, disconnection requirements and timing; the submitted form is not itself a booking.
