One request for different kinds of major appliances

For a home cleanout, rental turnover or appliance upgrade, you can submit multiple major appliances in one request. List each item separately and mark which ones have been tested working. We review the complete load rather than promising that every single appliance is accepted.

A replacement project may leave a working electric range in the kitchen and laundry machines in the garage. If several items are being removed, send one request listing every item so the load can be evaluated together.

Important: Free pickup is conditional. A single nonworking machine normally does not qualify. For mixed major-appliance loads, we review how many items work, local reuse demand, safe access and the actual route. No appointment is guaranteed until confirmed.

Prepare your appliance pickup request

Our priority is reusable equipment. A working refrigerator, a tested electric range or a complete laundry set can be more suitable for free pickup than an isolated nonworking item. Mixed multi-appliance loads may qualify when roughly 80% of the major appliances work; final acceptance depends on access and route availability.

  • Identify every major appliance and its working status
  • Photograph each appliance and any access challenges
  • Note stairs, elevators, gates and parking
  • Provide your city, ZIP code and best contact number

How qualification and pickup review work

  1. Send your city/ZIP, contact number, appliance photos and tested condition.
  2. Include property type, floor, stairs, door access and a safe pickup/loading location.
  3. We check free-pickup qualification and whether a local direct or partner route is available.
  4. If the request qualifies, the pickup details are confirmed before a visit. Otherwise, you can review other removal options.

Microwaves are not collected alone. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. For nonqualifying items, check the applicable local waste or retailer take-back options; we do not guarantee free disposal.
