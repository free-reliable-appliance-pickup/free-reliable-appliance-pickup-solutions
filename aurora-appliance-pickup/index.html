What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Send the appliance condition, city or ZIP code, photos and access details. Pickup must be confirmed based on the load, location, access and available coverage before scheduling.

Appliances We May Pick Up in Aurora

Refrigerators

Qualifying household refrigerators may be considered, including side-by-side, French-door, top-freezer and bottom-freezer models.

Washers

Working top-load and front-load washers receive priority. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins. Describe leaks, unusual noises or error messages, and give the condition of each machine if you are submitting a laundry set.

Dryers

Working electric and gas dryers receive priority. Tell us the fuel type and whether the dryer heats and tumbles. Include a full photo and the model label when available.

Stoves & Ranges

Qualifying electric and gas stoves, ranges and ovens may be considered.

Freezers

Working upright and chest freezers are a priority in Colorado. Tell us whether the freezer holds its temperature, its approximate size and whether it is in a basement, garage or inside the home.

Microwaves

We do not normally take microwaves by themselves. A microwave may be considered when it comes together with another qualifying major appliance.

What We Need Before Confirming an Aurora Pickup

Please provide the following information with your request:

  • Clear photos of the appliance
  • Appliance type and brand when known
  • Whether the appliance is fully functioning
  • Whether it needs repair or is not working
  • What is wrong with the appliance if repair is needed
  • Aurora address and ZIP code
  • Exact location of the appliance
  • First floor, second floor, third floor, basement, garage or outside
  • Whether stairs are involved
  • Whether there are narrow hallways or doorways
  • Any difficult access or removal conditions
