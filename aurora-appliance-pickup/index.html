Aurora Appliance Pickup & Removal

We review appliance pickup requests from Aurora homeowners, renters, landlords, property managers and businesses. Working and reusable appliances receive priority, and every request is evaluated before pickup is confirmed.

Free pickup is qualification-based. We review appliance condition, exact location, access, local demand and current route or partner coverage before scheduling.

Appliances We Review in Aurora

Washers

Working top-load and front-load washers are a priority. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins, and describe leaks or error messages.

Dryers

Working electric and gas dryers are a priority. Include the fuel type and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Refrigerators

Standard, side-by-side, bottom-freezer and French-door refrigerators may qualify. Tell us whether both compartments maintain temperature.

Freezers

Working upright and chest freezers are an important Colorado category. Include approximate size and whether the freezer holds temperature.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may be submitted for review.

What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Qualifying fully working major appliances may receive free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Aurora Pickup Access & Route Details

Our Colorado route centers on Aurora and Denver, with requests reviewed approximately 35–45 miles around the metro area. This is a route guideline, not a guarantee of service. Send the exact address or ZIP code so coverage can be checked before scheduling.

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upper floor, basement or outside. Include stairs, elevators, narrow doors, tight hallways, gates, parking or loading restrictions and any building requirements.

For apartments, rental properties and multi-appliance pickups, list each appliance separately and explain where each item is located. This helps us review the complete load rather than treating every neighborhood or nearby city as a separate service page.

What to Send the Colorado Pickup Team

Photos

Send one clear full-appliance photo and a model-label photo when available. For refrigerators and freezers, an interior photo is helpful.

Condition

Tell us whether each appliance is fully working, needs repair, does not work or is unknown. For a laundry set, describe both machines separately.

Access

Include floor level, stair flights, elevators, doorway clearance, gates and vehicle loading access.
