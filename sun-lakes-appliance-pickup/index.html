Qualification Rules

Fully working appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of major appliances work. Minor repair needs may be considered. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Sun Lakes 55+ Community Appliance Replacement Details

Sun Lakes is a strong fit for planned appliance replacement requests because many owners replace refrigerators, laundry sets or ranges while the old unit still works. When possible, send photos and tested condition before delivery crews move the appliance outside. Include the community name, gate procedure and whether pickup is from a garage, driveway or interior room.

For recurring work, property managers, maintenance contacts and senior-community teams can group several upcoming replacements and provide approximate dates. Clearly identifying working units helps prioritize reuse. If an appliance is not working, describe the exact issue instead of simply marking it broken so the request can be evaluated accurately.
