What We Check Before Confirmation

Tell us whether the washer fills, washes or tumbles, drains and completes the final spin. Mention leaking or error codes.

Dryer operation

Tell us whether it powers on, tumbles and heats, and whether it is gas or electric.

Removal path

Include interior or exterior steps, tight laundry closets, side-yard access, long carries and parking limitations.

Complete pair

Submit the washer and dryer together when they belong to the same set. Complete pairs receive strong priority.
