What Usually Qualifies in Rialto?

A working pair: Tell us whether the washer completes a full fill, drain and spin cycle and whether the dryer tumbles and produces heat. Include one clear front photo of each machine and note whether both units can still be tested. Matching sets may be reviewed together, but a free appointment is never automatic.

Washer with a fault

Report whether water is trapped in the drum, if there is a leak, and any displayed error code. Do not label a washer fully working if it stops before the final spin.

Gas or electric dryer

Identify the fuel type, whether it runs and heats, and where the dryer sits. If the gas or electrical connection needs professional disconnection, flag that before scheduling.

Single machine or both

A single working washer or dryer can be submitted too. For a pair, give the condition of each unit rather than a single overall rating.

Removal from a rental

Give the exact Rialto pickup address and say whether a tenant, owner or manager will provide access. Apartment and condo moves may require property-management coordination.

If the appliance cannot be tested, select Unknown and describe why. Repair and nonworking requests are reviewed only when a suitable route is available; they are not guaranteed free pickup.

Rialto Bulky-Item Pickup: An Official Alternative

If your washer or dryer does not qualify for our reuse-focused pickup, check the City of Rialto large-item disposal information. The City states that eligible single-family residential solid-waste customers can receive up to two free bulky-item pickups per calendar year. Call Burrtec at 909-877-1596 to ask specifically whether your washer or dryer is accepted and to schedule service; do not place an appliance at the curb before arranging collection.

The City's guidance directs apartment, condominium and mobile-home-park residents to contact property management for disposal arrangements. The City/Burrtec bulky-item program is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup and may have different eligibility and curbside preparation rules.
