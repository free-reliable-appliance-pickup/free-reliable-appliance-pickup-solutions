PGA West Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

PGA West is a specific La Quinta community with gated residential access, golf-course neighborhoods and property-management considerations. A dedicated page lets customers submit accurate access details instead of using a generic La Quinta request.

Working and reusable refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup; mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Pickup is qualification-based. Exact location, appliance condition, access and current route availability are reviewed before a pickup is confirmed.

Major Appliances We Review

Refrigerators & Freezers

Report whether the unit is plugged in and cooling. Send exterior, interior and model-label photos when possible.

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report fill, wash, drain and spin. For dryers, identify gas or electric and whether the drum turns and heats.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and describe burner, oven and control operation.

Package Loads

When several appliances are available at one property, list every item and individual condition together.

Access Details That Matter

Include gate access, HOA requirements, garage or interior location, parking limitations, stairs and carrying distance.

Do not move heavy appliances to a curb, hallway or stair landing just because a request was submitted. Keep them safely located until pickup is reviewed and confirmed.

PGA West Appliance Removal FAQ

Do working appliances receive priority?

Yes. Reusable appliances receive the strongest priority because the goal is to keep useful machines in service when practical.

Can a high-end appliance needing repair still be worth submitting?

Yes. A high-end or desirable appliance with a clear repair issue can still be a useful request when the brand, model, photos and condition are provided.

Should I send several appliances in one request?

Yes. Package loads are easier to evaluate when all appliances and conditions are submitted together.
