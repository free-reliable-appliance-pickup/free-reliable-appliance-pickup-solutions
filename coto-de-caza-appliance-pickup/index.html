Coto de Caza Appliance Pickup & Removal Review

Coto de Caza appliance requests can involve gated-community access, longer private drives, attached garages and interior appliance locations. Working and reusable major appliances receive priority, and gate or loading details are reviewed before a pickup is confirmed.

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working major appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working and reusable. Minor-repair appliances can be described for review, while a completely nonworking single appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Pickup must be confirmed. Submitting a request starts review; it does not guarantee acceptance or a specific pickup time.

Major Appliances We Review

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the unit is plugged in and cooling. Send exterior, interior and model-label photos when possible.

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report power, fill, drain and spin. For dryers, report gas or electric, tumble and heat. Complete working sets receive strong priority.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric ranges can be reviewed when major cooking functions work. Include photos of the cooktop, oven and controls.

Mixed Major-Appliance Loads

List every appliance and its condition. Mixed loads may qualify when most major appliances are working and reusable.

Coto de Caza Gate, Driveway & Garage Access

Include gate or visitor instructions, driveway length or slope, garage placement, floor level, stairs, narrow doors, parking limitations and carrying distance. Tell us whether a pickup vehicle can approach the garage or must load farther away.

Local routing: Coto de Caza connects with Rancho Santa Margarita, Mission Viejo and the Trabuco Canyon side of the South Orange County route. Use the exact pickup address for route review.

Special Qualification Rules

Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. These items do not qualify as stand-alone free pickups.

Coto de Caza Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is free pickup guaranteed?

No. Appliance condition, exact location, access, current demand and route availability are reviewed before confirmation.

Can one working appliance qualify?

Yes. A single working refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer, stove or range may qualify depending on condition and route demand.

Can nonworking appliances be submitted?

Yes, but free pickup is not guaranteed. Repair or nonworking items depend on current demand, while mixed loads may qualify when most major appliances are working.

Do photos help?

Yes. Clear appliance, model-label and access photos can speed review and reduce failed pickup attempts.
