Olympia requests and partner availability

Free Reliable Appliance Pickup reviews requests for major appliances that may be useful to an appliance reseller, repair shop, buyer or pickup professional. We do not represent a staffed Olympia location or guarantee a truck is available on a particular day. A submitted request is not a scheduled appointment.

Fully working major appliances have the best chance of qualifying. Mixed loads may be reviewed when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Single nonworking units do not automatically qualify. Partner availability, appliance condition, exact location and safe access all affect acceptance.

Olympia and Thurston County access details

Requests from downtown Olympia, the Westside, Eastside and neighborhoods near the Capitol campus may have different loading and parking considerations. For any Olympia address, provide the ZIP code, appliance location and carrying distance. Requests from nearby Lacey or Tumwater require their own address and partner-coverage review; proximity does not guarantee acceptance.

Describe stairs, basements, apartment floors, elevators, loading zones, gated access, tight hallways and any building move-out rules. Property managers should note whether a reserved elevator or loading appointment is required. Keep the appliance safely in place until an actual pickup is agreed.

Appliances Olympia customers can submit

Washers and dryers

State whether the washer fills, drains and spins, and whether the dryer heats and tumbles. Include stacked-unit or basement access details.

Refrigerators and freezers

Tell us whether cooling and freezing work normally. Provide approximate dimensions and any narrow-door or stair restrictions.

Stoves, ovens and ranges

Describe working burners, oven operation, gas or electric connection and the current installation. Do not disconnect gas equipment merely to submit a request.

Dishwashers and microwaves

Dishwashers are considered only with at least two other major working appliances. Microwaves are not accepted as a standalone pickup.

Rental turnovers, households and business appliance loads

Homeowners, renters, landlords, property managers and appliance sellers can describe single appliances or multiple-unit loads. List each appliance's condition and access separately. Photos of the appliance and removal path can help a prospective partner assess the job. This reuse-oriented request service is separate from local municipal bulky-item collection or waste disposal; if pickup is not accepted, check with your local waste provider for current options.

Olympia appliance pickup questions

Is pickup guaranteed to be free?

No. Submitting the form is free. Pickup is free only for qualifying appliances when an available local partner confirms the job.

Do you remove appliances from apartments?

Requests can be reviewed, but stair count, elevators, loading rules and partner capability determine whether removal can be accepted.

How quickly will someone arrive?

No Olympia appointment or response time is guaranteed. Scheduling depends on partner acceptance and the details supplied.
