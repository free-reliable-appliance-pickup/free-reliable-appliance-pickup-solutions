Chicago buildings change what pickup is possible

For a downtown apartment or condo in the Loop, River North or South Loop, ask your building manager about freight elevator times, certificate-of-insurance requirements, dock reservations and whether moving is permitted on weekends. In older two- and three-flat buildings in Pilsen, Logan Square and Bridgeport, the main challenge can be narrow rear porches and stairs. None of these neighborhoods are promised pickup territories; they illustrate the access information needed.

If a refrigerator is on an upper floor, provide measurements for the tightest doorway, each stair landing and the exterior exit. For a washer/dryer set, note side-by-side versus stacked installation and the separate condition of both machines. Photos of the machine and its access route help us evaluate whether removal is safe and practical.

How we assess a reusable appliance

Washers and dryers

Tell us whether the washer fills, agitates or tumbles, drains and spins. Test dryer heat and tumble separately; specify natural gas or electric, and whether the machines are attached or stacked.

Refrigerators and freezers

State whether both cooling compartments work, whether the doors seal, and whether there are broken shelves, leaks or damaged handles. Dimensions and photos of the inside and outside matter.

Stoves, ranges and ovens

Identify gas versus electric, whether burners and oven work, and any missing knobs or broken glass. Gas or electrical disconnection must be handled safely before any approved move.

Multiple appliances

Property managers should describe the number of working units, floor/room locations, and whether all appliances are ready at one location. Mixed loads are evaluated individually.

Chicago appliance removal: city collection or private hauler?

The City of Chicago's official residential garbage program serves eligible single-family homes and buildings with four units or fewer. Larger residential buildings generally arrange private waste collection. The city's garbage-fee guidance says bulk collection is included for eligible city-served properties, but that does not establish that any particular refrigerator, washer, dryer or stove qualifies for pickup. Confirm the exact appliance and preparation rules through CHI 311 before arranging curbside collection.

For a high-rise, condo association or apartment building with more than four units, ask the building manager about its authorized hauler, freight elevator reservations, loading-dock access, any certificate-of-insurance requirements and appliance-specific fees. A private-hauler appointment is separate from this website's reuse eligibility request. Neither option is booked by submitting our form, and no active Free Reliable Chicago route is promised.

If this machine cannot be accepted

In the Chicago area, Habitat ReStore Chicago considers qualifying working/gently used appliance donations, with independent acceptance and pickup rules. Illinois EPA explains white-goods disposal and why refrigerators and freezers require proper refrigerant handling. These are independent services, not our pickup partners or confirmed alternatives for every address.

To ask whether municipal bulky-item services apply to your address, contact the City of Chicago through its official CHI311 service portal or call 311. Confirm eligibility, permitted items, any charges, safe preparation and collection instructions directly with the city before setting out an appliance. CHI311 is not affiliated with us, and a city inquiry is not a booking with Free Reliable Appliance Pickup.

Photos and details that make a Chicago pickup request useful

For a washer and dryer set, note whether both machines complete a cycle, whether the dryer uses gas or electricity, and whether hoses and vents can be disconnected safely. For refrigerators, note whether both sides cool, whether the doors seal and whether it is a side-by-side, top-freezer or French-door model. For stoves, specify gas or electric and whether the oven and burners work.

Describe the exact route out: floor level, stair turns, elevator or loading-dock booking, tightest doorway, parking or alley access, and whether building management requires permission.
