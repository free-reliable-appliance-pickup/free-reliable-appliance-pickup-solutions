Chicago buildings change what pickup is possible

For a downtown apartment or condo in the Loop, River North or South Loop, ask your building manager about freight elevator times, certificate-of-insurance requirements, dock reservations and whether moving is permitted on weekends. In older two- and three-flat buildings in Pilsen, Logan Square and Bridgeport, the main challenge can be narrow rear porches and stairs. None of these neighborhoods are promised pickup territories; they illustrate the access information needed.

If a refrigerator is on an upper floor, provide measurements for the tightest doorway, each stair landing and the exterior exit. For a washer/dryer set, note side-by-side versus stacked installation and the separate condition of both machines. Photos of the machine and its access route help us evaluate whether removal is safe and practical.

How we assess a reusable appliance

Washers and dryers

Tell us whether the washer fills, agitates or tumbles, drains and spins. Test dryer heat and tumble separately; specify natural gas or electric, and whether the machines are attached or stacked.

Refrigerators and freezers

State whether both cooling compartments work, whether the doors seal, and whether there are broken shelves, leaks or damaged handles. Dimensions and photos of the inside and outside matter.

Stoves, ranges and ovens

Identify gas versus electric, whether burners and oven work, and any missing knobs or broken glass. Gas or electrical disconnection must be handled safely before any approved move.

Multiple appliances

Property managers should describe the number of working units, floor/room locations, and whether all appliances are ready at one location. Mixed loads are evaluated individually.

Chicago city collection or private hauler?

Chicago’s residential garbage program covers eligible homes and buildings with four or fewer units. The city’s garbage-fee guidance discusses bulk collection, but does not guarantee acceptance of refrigerators, washers, dryers or stoves. Confirm specific appliance rules, fees and preparation with CHI 311.

For high-rises, condos and larger apartment buildings, ask management about its private hauler, loading dock, freight-elevator reservation and insurance rules. These city and hauler services are independent of our unconfirmed pickup route.
