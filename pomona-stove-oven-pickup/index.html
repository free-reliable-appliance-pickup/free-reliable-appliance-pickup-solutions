Pomona Local Disposal Alternative

The City of Pomona's residential bulky-item program is a separate option when a stove or range does not fit our reuse-focused route. The current City page says service is scheduled through Athens Services and explicitly lists a stove as a bulky item.

The City currently limits a scheduled pickup to four large items, described as two metal and two non-metal items, and says residential customers may receive up to six no-charge requests per household per calendar year, limited to one request per bi-monthly billing cycle.
