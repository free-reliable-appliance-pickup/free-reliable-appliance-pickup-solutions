What Qualifies Best?

Working Sets

Complete working washer/dryer sets are the strongest category because both machines can be evaluated together for reuse.

Single Working Machines

A single working washer or dryer can qualify when condition, distance, demand and access fit the route.

Recurring Replacements

Apartment communities, senior living, property managers, installers and appliance sellers can submit recurring working take-outs.

Free Pickup Is Qualification-Based

A nonworking washer or dryer by itself generally does not qualify. Minor repair needs may be considered when local reuse or repair demand supports the pickup. Submit every machine honestly so the route can be evaluated before anyone travels.
