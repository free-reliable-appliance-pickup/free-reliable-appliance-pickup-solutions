Salem Appliance Pickup & Removal

Submit washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges for pickup review in Salem. Working and reusable appliances receive priority.

Free pickup is qualification-based. We review appliance condition, the complete load, exact pickup location, stairs or access issues, local demand and route availability before scheduling.

What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Qualifying fully working appliances may receive free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Appliances We Review

Washers

Working top-load and front-load washers are a priority. Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins, and describe leaks or error messages.

Dryers

Working gas and electric dryers are a priority. Tell us whether the dryer heats and tumbles and whether it is part of a laundry set.

Refrigerators

Tell us whether both compartments cool correctly and include interior and exterior photos when possible.

Freezers

Upright and chest freezers may qualify. Tell us whether the freezer maintains temperature and where it is located.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may be submitted with their working condition and access details.

Salem-Area Route Coverage

Our Salem-area team reviews requests in Salem and West Salem, with nearby requests from communities such as Keizer, Turner, Stayton, Silverton, Dallas, Monmouth, Independence and Woodburn when the route is practical.

These nearby communities are handled as part of the Salem-area route, not as separate search landing pages. Send your exact city and ZIP code so we can confirm current coverage before scheduling.

For broader Oregon information, use the Oregon service page or the complete service-area overview.

Photos and Access Details

Send a full appliance photo and a model-label photo when available. For refrigerators and freezers, include an interior photo and tell us whether the appliance maintains temperature.

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upper floor, basement or outside. Include stairs, elevators, narrow doors, tight hallways, gates and parking or loading restrictions.

For rentals or multi-appliance cleanouts, list every appliance and its condition so the complete load can be reviewed together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is every Salem appliance pickup free?

No. Free pickup depends on appliance condition, the complete load, access, local demand and available route coverage.

Do you pick up washers and dryers?

Yes. Working washers, dryers and complete laundry sets are priority categories.

Do you review requests outside Salem?

Yes. Nearby requests may be reviewed as part of the Salem-area route. Exact coverage is confirmed from the city or ZIP code and current route availability.

Salem-Keizer Disposal Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our pickup, Marion County's Salem-Keizer Recycling & Transfer Station may be an alternative. Confirm current appliance acceptance, refrigerant handling, fees and hours directly with Marion County before transporting an appliance.
