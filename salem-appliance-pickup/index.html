What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Qualifying fully working appliances may receive free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Salem-Keizer Disposal Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our pickup, Marion County's Salem-Keizer Recycling & Transfer Station may be an alternative. The County lists the station at 3250 Deer Park Dr. SE, Salem, OR 97317 and currently shows public hours of 8 a.m.–5 p.m., seven days a week.

Marion County currently lists $5 per appliance without refrigerant and $25 per appliance designed to contain refrigerant. The County explains that refrigerant appliances must be assessed by a professional for proper refrigerant removal and documentation before recycling; removing refrigerant yourself does not eliminate the fee.

Mixed loads can be charged at the regular garbage rate, so confirm the current rules and fees before transporting multiple materials together.
