Official La Habra Bulky-Item Alternative

The City of La Habra states that single-family and multi-family residents receive four free bulky-item pickups per year, and major appliances are included as bulky items. That municipal program is separate from our qualification-based reuse-focused service.

If an appliance is still useful, submit it here first. If it does not qualify, the City's CR&R bulky-item program may be the better disposal option.
