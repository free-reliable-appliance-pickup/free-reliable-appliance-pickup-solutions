What to Tell Us About Your Mesa Stove

Working condition

Do the cooktop burners, oven heat, controls and doors work? Describe any missing parts or known problems.

Gas or electric range

For gas ranges, tell us if the cooktop burners and oven ignite and hold heat. For electric ranges, describe burner, bake and broil performance. Let us know if the stove is already safely disconnected; never disconnect gas or electrical wiring yourself unless qualified.

Stairs, loading and access

For gated retirement properties or multi-unit buildings, share loading access, garage location and whether a property manager must authorize removal.

Send photographs showing the entire stove and cooktop. Include the ZIP code, floor/stairs and a clear path for removal. Do not disconnect a gas line or hardwired range without a qualified professional.
