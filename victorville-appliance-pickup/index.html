RV Appliance & Generator Pickup

Removed RV equipment can be submitted for pickup review, including RV refrigerators, compact washers or dryers, cooktops and ranges, ovens, water heaters, furnaces or heaters, and portable or removed RV generators. Working and reusable units receive the strongest priority.

Propane and fuel-powered equipment: propane appliances, furnaces, water heaters and generators must be safely disconnected before pickup. Fuel tanks, propane cylinders and active gas or electrical connections are not handled as part of appliance pickup. Generators should be drained or otherwise prepared for safe transport according to the manufacturer or a qualified RV technician.

We do not remove built-in RV systems from the vehicle, disconnect propane lines, perform electrical or gas work, or remove the RV itself. Send clear photos, the item type and condition, your ZIP code, and whether the equipment is already removed and accessible.

Free RV-equipment pickup is qualification-based. Condition, reuse potential, access, route distance and current local demand are reviewed before pickup is confirmed.

Victorville Appliance Pickup FAQ

What appliances can qualify for free pickup in Victorville?

Working and reusable refrigerators, washers, dryers, stoves, ranges, ovens and household freezers are the main categories reviewed.

Is every appliance automatically picked up for free?

No. Condition, completeness, local demand, access, distance and current route availability are reviewed first.

Can you pick up from a rental or property turnover?

Yes, requests from homeowners, renters, landlords and property managers can be reviewed. Include access instructions and the condition of every appliance.

What about broken appliances?

A nonworking appliance by itself does not automatically qualify. Minor repairs and mixed loads may be considered under the qualification guidelines.
