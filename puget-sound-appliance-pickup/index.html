Seattle–Tacoma–Puget Sound Requests

This page serves as one regional resource for Seattle, Tacoma, Bellevue, Everett, Shoreline, Redmond, Kirkland, Renton, Kent, Federal Way, Auburn, Lakewood, Burien, Marysville and nearby communities. We do not need a separate near-duplicate landing page for every city.

Pickup depends on appliance condition, exact ZIP code, access, demand and available route or partner coverage. Listing a community does not guarantee free pickup.

Metro Access Details Matter

Seattle & dense neighborhoods

Eastside & suburban routes

For Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland and nearby communities, include gated access, garages, driveway conditions and appliance location.

South & North Sound

What Usually Qualifies?

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance alone generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Appliances We Review

Washers and dryers are priority categories. We also review refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens. For washers, include fill/drain/spin status; for dryers, heat/tumble and gas or electric; for refrigerators and freezers, include cooling status plus interior and exterior photos.

Best first message: city/ZIP, appliance type, working condition, photos, floor level, stairs, elevator or gate details, and parking/loading information.

Request Details for Puget Sound Routes

Send the exact city and ZIP code rather than only “Seattle area.” Route timing can differ across Seattle, the Eastside, South King County, Pierce County and Snohomish County. Include every appliance in the load and describe which units are fully working, need minor repair or do not work.

Cooling appliances

For refrigerators and freezers, confirm whether the unit is currently plugged in and holding temperature. Include interior photos, exterior photos, approximate dimensions and missing shelf or drawer information.

Laundry appliances

For washers, report fill, wash, drain and spin performance. For dryers, report tumble and heat and identify gas or electric. Note stacked or compact installations.

Ranges and ovens

Identify gas or electric service and describe burner, oven, control-panel or glass-top problems. Mention built-in installation or hardwired connections before review.

Seattle, Eastside and South Sound Access

Dense neighborhoods and multi-unit properties may require loading reservations, elevator scheduling, parking permits or property-manager approval. Tell us about stairs, narrow halls, door removal, gated garages and the distance between the appliance and legal truck parking.

For Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Renton, Kent, Federal Way, Auburn, Tacoma and Lakewood, include gated-community rules, steep driveways and garage or basement placement. Everett, Marysville and other North Sound requests should include flexible timing when longer routing is required.

Ferries and islands: disclose any ferry-dependent address or island access at the beginning. Those requests require separate availability review and are not confirmed by inclusion in the broader Puget Sound region.

Before Pickup Is Confirmed

Do not disconnect a working cooling appliance, leave equipment at a curb or schedule building access until the team confirms qualification and availability. Photos and complete testing details help determine whether free pickup may be offered.

If the request includes several appliances, the approximately 80% working guideline is evaluated across the major-appliance load. Standalone microwaves are not accepted, and a dishwasher is considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Washington Contact

Puget Sound requests use 503-868-4455. Service hours are 8:00 AM–8:00 PM daily. Contact begins the review; free pickup is confirmed only after the request is evaluated.
