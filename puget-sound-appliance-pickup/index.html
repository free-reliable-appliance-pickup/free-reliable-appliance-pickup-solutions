Seattle–Tacoma–Puget Sound Requests

This page serves as one regional resource for Seattle, Tacoma, Bellevue, Everett, Shoreline, Redmond, Kirkland, Renton, Kent, Federal Way, Auburn, Lakewood, Burien, Marysville and nearby communities. We do not need a separate near-duplicate landing page for every city.

Pickup depends on appliance condition, exact ZIP code, access, demand and available route or partner coverage. Listing a community does not guarantee free pickup.

South & North Sound

What Usually Qualifies?

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance alone generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Appliances We Review

Washers and dryers are priority categories. We also review refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and ovens. For washers, include fill/drain/spin status; for dryers, heat/tumble and gas or electric; for refrigerators and freezers, include cooling status plus interior and exterior photos.

Best first message: city/ZIP, appliance type, working condition, photos, floor level, stairs, elevator or gate details, and parking/loading information.

Washington Contact

Puget Sound requests use 503-868-4455. Service hours are 8:00 AM–8:00 PM daily. Contact begins the review; free pickup is confirmed only after the request is evaluated.
