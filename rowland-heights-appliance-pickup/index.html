Rowland Heights Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Residents, landlords, property managers and businesses can submit washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and other qualifying major appliances for review. Working and reusable equipment receives priority because reuse is the first goal.

Free pickup is not automatic. We review the appliance condition, exact pickup location, route direction, local demand and removal access before confirming service. A single nonworking appliance does not automatically qualify.

Best request: include clear photos, whether the appliance is currently testable, exact address, floor level, stairs, gates, narrow doorways and carrying distance.

High-Priority Appliance Requests in Rowland Heights

Washers & dryers

Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer tumbles and heats. Submit both machines together when they are a set.

Refrigerators & freezers

Stoves & ranges

Identify gas or electric and describe whether burners, oven functions and controls work normally.

Rental turnovers

List every appliance separately with its condition and access so a multi-item request can be reviewed accurately.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
