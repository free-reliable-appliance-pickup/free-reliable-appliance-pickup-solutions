Stove & Oven Pickup in Aurora

Working gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens can be submitted for Aurora pickup review. Burner operation, oven heat, controls, completeness and safe disconnection are important before pickup is confirmed.

Send first: exact Aurora ZIP/address, clear photos, working condition, brand/model if known, floor/stairs and access details.

What We Need to Know

Gas or Electric

Tell us the fuel type and whether the appliance is already safely disconnected.

Burners & Oven

State whether all burners, oven heat and controls work normally.

Condition & Access

Send clear photos and note stairs, narrow kitchen access, doorway clearance and loading conditions.

Aurora Address & Access Review

Aurora requests may come from apartments, townhomes, garages and single-family homes across North Aurora, Hoffman Heights, Heather Gardens, Heather Ridge, Village East, Meadow Hills, Mission Viejo, Seven Hills, Sterling Hills and southeast Aurora. Submit the exact street address because Aurora spans multiple counties and mailing areas can overlap nearby communities.

Include stairs, elevator rules, doorway clearance, gates, parking/loading access and carrying distance before pickup is confirmed.

Free Pickup Is Qualification-Based

Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Minor repair needs may be considered when local reuse demand supports the route. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify under the standard free-pickup rules.

Aurora Recycling & Disposal Backup

If this stove, range or oven does not qualify for our reuse-focused free route, Aurora residents can check the City of Aurora's appliance recycling resources. Aurora says residential trash and recycling are arranged through permitted private haulers rather than a city-run curbside trash service.
