What Helps Us Review Your Appliance?

Working condition

For washers include fill, drain and spin. For dryers include heat and tumble. For refrigerators and freezers include cooling status.

Photos

Clear exterior, interior and model-label photos help determine size, condition and whether the appliance may be reusable.

Access

Tell us about garages, gates, stairs, apartments, narrow halls, door removal and whether the appliance is inside or already outside.

Phoenix Metro Route Planning

Valley routes can span substantial distances from the West Valley to Mesa, Chandler and the East Valley. Exact ZIP code and complete appliance information help us group requests efficiently. Multiple qualifying appliances should be listed together.

Best request: city/ZIP + appliance type + condition + photos + floor/stairs + access + number of appliances.

Gas and electric cooking appliances may qualify when complete and reusable. Tell us whether burners, oven and controls work.

Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance alone generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
