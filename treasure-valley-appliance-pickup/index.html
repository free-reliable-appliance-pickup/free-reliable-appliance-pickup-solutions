Boise & Treasure Valley Coverage

This regional hub reviews requests from Boise, Meridian, Nampa, Caldwell, Eagle, Kuna, Star and nearby Treasure Valley communities. These locations are grouped into one practical metro resource rather than separate near-duplicate city pages.

Coverage depends on appliance condition, exact ZIP code, access, local demand and available route or partner capacity. Listing a community does not guarantee free pickup.

Information That Helps Us Review a Request

Condition

For washers include fill, drain and spin; dryers heat and tumble; refrigerators/freezers cooling status; and stoves fuel type and known problems.

Photos

Clear exterior, interior and model-label photos help determine size, condition and whether an appliance may be reusable.

Access

Report garages, stairs, basements, gates, apartments, narrow doors, long carries and whether the appliance is already outside.

Treasure Valley Route Planning

Requests may be grouped across Boise, Meridian, Nampa and Caldwell routes. Exact ZIP code and a complete appliance list help us evaluate travel and load size before scheduling. Landlords and property managers should list multiple available appliances together.

Best request: city/ZIP + appliance list + condition + photos + floor/stairs + access.

Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repair needs may be considered. A nonworking appliance alone generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
