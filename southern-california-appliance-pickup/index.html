How Southern California Routing Works

Southern California is too large and varied to treat as a single local market. This hub routes users to county, regional and city pages where condition, address, access and current pickup capacity can be reviewed accurately.

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working major appliances. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working and reusable. A completely nonworking single appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup.

What to Include With a Request

Condition

Describe what works and what does not. Accurate condition prevents failed trips.

Photos

Send clear appliance, interior and model-label photos when useful.

Access

Include floor level, stairs, gates, elevators, parking, driveway conditions and carrying distance.

Exact location

Use the actual city and ZIP instead of a broad county or regional label.

Southern California Qualification Standards

The same core qualification rules apply across the regional network even though local routes differ. Fully working major appliances receive the strongest priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working and reusable. A completely nonworking single appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Refrigerators & freezers

Tell us whether the unit is cooling and whether it is plugged in or testable. Clear exterior, interior and model-label photos help with review.

Laundry appliances

For washers, report fill, drain and spin. For dryers, report gas or electric, tumble and heat. Complete working sets receive strong priority.

Stoves & ranges

Include gas or electric type and whether the major cooktop and oven functions work.

Special items

Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Why Southern California Needs Regional Hubs

Los Angeles County, Orange County, the Inland Empire, Riverside County and the desert markets have different travel patterns, buyer demand, property access and route capacity. A single statewide page cannot describe those differences accurately, so the regional hubs route each request toward more specific local guidance.

Use the city page when one exists. Use a county or regional hub when the pickup address falls between city pages or when you need to compare the surrounding route structure.

Access Patterns Across Southern California

Dense urban areas

Apartments and multi-unit properties may require floor, elevator, parking and loading-zone details.

Suburban routes

Garage pickups are common, but gates, side yards and longer carries can still affect removal.

Hillside & coastal areas

Driveway slope, stairs, narrow roads, limited curb space and gated access should be described before pickup is confirmed.

Desert & lower-density areas

Longer driveways, detached structures, rural-style access and distance between the appliance and loading point may matter more.
