Free Appliance Pickup in Yucaipa: What Qualifies

Use this page for major-appliance pickup requests at a Yucaipa address. Qualifying working or reusable refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, stoves, ranges and ovens may receive free pickup after condition, exact address, removal access, local demand and route availability are reviewed.

Yucaipa Driveways, Garages & Foothill Access

Yucaipa pickups can involve long driveways, grades, gates, gravel or uneven surfaces, detached garages and homes where the appliance is well away from the loading point. Those details can matter as much as the appliance itself when a route is reviewed.

Yucaipa Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused route, the City of Yucaipa publishes a separate bulky-item program through its local waste provider. The City currently says residents can request up to four curbside bulky-item collections during a 12-month period, with a limit of five items per collection, subject to the published collection schedule and advance arrangement.

This municipal program is separate from Free Reliable Appliance Pickup. Confirm the current schedule and preparation rules directly before placing an appliance outside.

Yucaipa Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup automatically free in Yucaipa?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based. We review appliance condition, exact address, safe access, local demand and current route availability before confirming a pickup.

Can one working appliance qualify?

Yes. One working refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer, stove, range or oven can be submitted for review.

Can you pick up from a garage or long driveway?

Those requests can be reviewed. Send the driveway length or grade, gates, surface, carrying distance and whether the appliance is already disconnected and accessible.

What if the appliance is broken?

A single completely nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for our free reuse-focused service. Repairable items may be reviewed as part of a stronger mixed load, or you can compare the City of Yucaipa bulky-item option above.

Do you serve apartments or managed properties?

Yes. Include floor level, stairs or elevator, parking or loading restrictions, appliance count and an onsite contact.
