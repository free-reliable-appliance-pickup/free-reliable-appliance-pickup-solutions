Yucaipa requests are reviewed through our Inland Empire coverage. We prioritize working appliances that can be reused, resold, repaired economically or routed efficiently with other qualifying pickups. Complete photos and honest condition details help us decide faster.

For searches such as appliance pickup, appliance removal, appliance recycling, washer pickup, refrigerator pickup or appliance removal near me in Yucaipa, send the exact address or ZIP, appliance condition and access details so the current 909 route can be reviewed.

Free-pickup qualification: fully working appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when about 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify.

Driveway, Garage & Foothill Access

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, house, rear patio, shed or outside area and whether the truck can approach the loading point. Longer driveways, gates, gravel surfaces and steps can affect the pickup plan, so include those details before scheduling.

For inside appliances, send a photo of narrow doorways, turns or stairs. If a refrigerator is in an alcove or a washer/dryer is stacked, mention that in the request.

What Does Not Automatically Qualify

Microwaves are accepted only with another major qualifying appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. A repair-needed item may be reviewed as part of a stronger load, but one broken appliance alone does not qualify for free pickup.

Pickup is confirmed only after the request is accepted. Please do not move appliances to the curb unless instructed.
