Send first: exact Phoenix ZIP/address, clear photos, working condition, brand/model if known, floor/stairs and access details.

What We Need to Know

Gas or Electric

Tell us the fuel type and whether the appliance is already safely disconnected.

Burners & Oven

State whether all burners, oven heat and controls work normally.

Condition & Access

Send clear photos and note stairs, narrow kitchen access, doorway clearance and loading conditions.

Free Pickup Is Qualification-Based

Working and reusable appliances receive priority. Minor repair needs may be considered when local reuse demand supports the route. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify under the standard free-pickup rules.
