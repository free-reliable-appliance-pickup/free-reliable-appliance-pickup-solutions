Santa Ana Apartments, Rentals & Property Access

Santa Ana requests often come from apartments, duplexes, rental turnovers, garages and single-family homes. For multifamily properties, include floor level, elevator availability, hallway or doorway restrictions, loading rules and any property-manager instructions.

Landlords, property managers, senior communities and businesses can submit multiple appliances. Include the number of units, appliance count, condition of each item and access window.

Free Pickup Rules

A single working refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer, stove or range can be reviewed. Minor repair needs may sometimes be considered. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify for the standard free route.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
