For searches such as appliance pickup, appliance removal, appliance recycling, washer pickup, refrigerator pickup or appliance removal near me in Santa Ana, send the exact address or ZIP, appliance condition and access details so the current 909 route can be reviewed.

Santa Ana requests often come from apartments, duplexes, single-family homes, garages and rental turnovers. We do not treat every address or appliance the same. A working dryer staged in a garage may be simple to collect, while a large refrigerator upstairs with a tight stairwell needs more planning.

Free pickup is intended for qualifying reusable major appliances. Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges are the core categories. A single completely nonworking appliance does not qualify, though minor repair and mixed loads can be reviewed when most of the major appliances are working.

Clear requests are faster to review: give tested functions, photos, floor level, stairs and the location where a truck can load.

What We Need to Know About the Appliance

Washer

Tell us whether it fills, drains and spins and whether there are major leaks, bearing noise or control errors.

Dryer

State gas or electric, whether it tumbles and heats, and whether the vent and power or gas connection are accessible.

Refrigerator or Freezer

Report current cooling or freezing performance. If it is unplugged, tell us how long it has been off and include interior photos.

Stove or Range

Describe burner operation, oven heat and control condition. Tell us whether a gas unit is disconnected before pickup.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only with two or more major working appliances.

Rental Turnovers and Multi-Unit Properties

For landlords and property managers, group the request by property and list each appliance separately. If four appliances are staged in a garage and three are working, that is different from four untested appliances spread across occupied units. Tell us which units are vacant, where each appliance sits and whether keys, gates or management access are needed.

For apartment pickups, include the floor, stairs or elevator, hallway turns and parking/loading situation. If the appliance must cross a courtyard or long walkway, say so. Those details directly affect whether a free route is practical.

Santa Ana Also Uses Public Bulky-Item Programs

For items that do not qualify for reuse pickup, Santa Ana Public Works periodically provides bulky-item programs and coordinates residential collection services with its waste-service partners. Because program dates and acceptance rules can change, residents should verify the current City instructions rather than leaving an appliance at the curb without authorization.
