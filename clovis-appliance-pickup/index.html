What Can Qualify for Free Pickup in Clovis?

Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working. Minor repairs may be considered, but a single nonworking general appliance does not normally qualify by itself.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether the unit is plugged in, cooling correctly and complete. Send exterior, interior and model-label photos when possible.

Washers & Dryers

Working machines and sets receive strong priority. Some repairable laundry machines may also be reviewed through the separate Clovis laundry route.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Gas and electric cooking appliances can be reviewed when complete and reusable. Report burner, oven and control condition.

Multiple-Appliance Loads

Landlords, remodelers and property managers should list every item together. Several qualifying appliances at one stop can be easier to route.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Clovis Public Cleanup Alternative

If a request does not qualify here, the official City of Clovis Community Clean-Up Program is a separate municipal option for eligible city refuse customers. The city describes the program as semi-annual for qualifying single-family residences, with curb placement and sorting rules, no alley pickup, and quantity limits. Check the current city schedule and accepted-material rules before setting anything out.

This municipal program is separate from our qualification-based appliance route; one does not guarantee eligibility for the other.

Clovis Appliance Pickup FAQ

Do you pick up from inside a Clovis home or apartment?

Inside pickup can be reviewed. Send floor level, stairs, elevator, doorway width, gate and parking details before scheduling.

Can I submit one working appliance?

Yes. A single working refrigerator, freezer, washer, dryer, stove or range can be reviewed. Route availability still matters.

Can a broken washer or dryer qualify?

Sometimes. The Clovis/Fresno-area laundry route has repair experience and can review some repairable washers and dryers. Use the dedicated Clovis laundry page and describe the problem honestly.

Do I need an account?

No. The request form is free to submit and does not require a customer account.
