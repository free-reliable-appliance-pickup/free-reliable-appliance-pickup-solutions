Free Pickup Qualification

Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Minor repair needs may be considered. A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify.

Special categories: microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.
