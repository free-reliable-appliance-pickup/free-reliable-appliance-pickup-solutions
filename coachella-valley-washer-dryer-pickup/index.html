Coachella Valley Washer & Dryer Pickup Network

This laundry hub connects Palm Springs, Cathedral City, Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Indian Wells, La Quinta, Indio, Coachella and Desert Hot Springs. Working washers, dryers and matching sets receive the strongest reuse review.

Free pickup is qualification-based: tested condition, exact address, access and current route availability are reviewed before pickup is confirmed.

One Laundry Request, No Account Required

Free to submit · Call or text 909-375-6685 · Text laundry photos · Pickup confirmation required

Send the washer or dryer condition, exact pickup address, city/ZIP, floor, stairs, gates and loading details in one request. The request is reviewed for appliance condition and current Coachella Valley partner capacity before a pickup is accepted.

Washer test

Report power, fill, wash/agitation, drain and final spin. Mention leaks, loud bearings, error codes or balance problems.

Dryer test

Report power, tumble and heat, and identify gas or electric. Note control or timer problems.

Stacked or laundry-center units

Send a full installation photo and say whether the machines are separate stacked units or a one-piece laundry center. Include closet, alcove or doorway clearance.

Property access

For gated, HOA, apartment, senior or seasonal properties, include service-entry hours, elevator rules, gate instructions and carrying distance before partner matching.

Why Matching Working Sets Matter

Washer condition

Report fill, wash, drain and spin. Note leaks, unusual noise or control issues.

Dryer condition

Report tumble, heat and whether the dryer is gas or electric.

Matching sets

Matching working pairs are strong reuse candidates, especially when clean and complete.

Access

Include stairs, gates, elevator, loading rules, carrying distance and whether the machines are disconnected.
