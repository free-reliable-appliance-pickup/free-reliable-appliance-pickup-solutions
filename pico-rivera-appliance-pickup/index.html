Pico Rivera Appliance Pickup & Removal Review

Residents, landlords, property managers and businesses can submit major appliances for reuse-focused pickup review. Working and reusable equipment receives priority. Free pickup depends on condition, exact address, access, local demand and route availability.

Major Appliances We Review

Washers & Dryers

Report washer fill, drain and spin and dryer tumble, heat and gas/electric type. Complete sets receive strong priority.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Include inside and outside photos, cooling condition and whether the unit is currently plugged in.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and whether burners, controls and oven functions work.

Multiple Appliances

List each major appliance separately. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances; microwaves only with another qualifying major appliance.

Pico Rivera Bulky-Item Alternative

The City of Pico Rivera says residents can schedule bulky-item collection through NASA Services. A June 2026 City notice states residents may request up to 10 free bulky-item collections per year, with limits on items per collection. City materials also list appliances such as refrigerators, ranges, washers, dryers and dishwashers among eligible bulky items.

This public disposal option is separate from our reuse-focused service. If an appliance does not qualify here, check the City's current NASA Services instructions before placing it at the curb.
