Residents, landlords, property managers and businesses can submit major appliances for reuse-focused pickup review. Working and reusable equipment receives priority. Free pickup depends on condition, exact address, access, local demand and current route availability.

Major Appliances We Review in El Monte

Washers & Dryers

Report washer fill, drain and spin and dryer tumble, heat and gas/electric type. Complete working sets receive strong priority.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Include interior and exterior photos, whether the unit is plugged in and whether it holds temperature.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric and whether burners, controls and oven functions operate normally.

Rental & Multi-Item Loads

List each appliance separately. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

A single nonworking appliance generally does not qualify. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances; microwaves only with another qualifying major appliance.

El Monte Bulky-Waste Alternative

The City of El Monte says single-family homes are entitled to free bulky-waste pickup for up to 20 bulky items per year through Valley Vista Services, and appliances are listed among accepted items. The City says pickups take place on trash day and should be scheduled in advance.

This municipal disposal option is separate from our qualification-based reuse service. If an appliance does not qualify here, check the City's current bulky-waste instructions.

El Monte Appliance Pickup FAQ

Can one working appliance qualify?

Yes. A single working washer, dryer, refrigerator, freezer, stove or range can be reviewed.

Can apartment pickups be reviewed?

Yes. Include floor level, stairs or elevator access, gates, parking and carrying distance.

What if the appliance needs repair?

Minor-repair appliances may sometimes qualify depending on condition, age, parts demand and route capacity.

Downtown El Monte & Transit-Oriented Pickup Access

El Monte's Downtown Specific Plan covers the historic Main Street area and nearby transit-focused subareas, including areas around the Metrolink and bus stations.
