What Qualifies for Free Pickup?

Free pickup is available for qualifying fully working appliances, or mixed loads where approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs may be considered. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Send the appliance condition, city or ZIP code, photos and access details. Pickup must be confirmed based on the load, location, access and available coverage before scheduling.

Indian Wells Appliance Pickup Preparation

For a faster review, identify every appliance separately and tell us whether it is fully working, needs a minor repair, is not working, or has an unknown condition. For refrigerators and freezers, include an interior photo and confirm whether the unit is currently cooling. For washers and dryers, tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins and whether the dryer heats and tumbles.

Access details matter before a pickup is scheduled. Include the floor level, stairs, gates, narrow doorways, tight hallways, garage or outside location, and whether an appliance door may need to be removed for safe movement. For multiple-appliance requests, list the condition of each item so the qualifying working share of the load can be reviewed in advance.
