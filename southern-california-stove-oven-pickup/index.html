Before Pickup: Prepare a Stove, Range or Oven

Test Functions When Safe

Before disconnection, report whether burners, oven functions, controls and displays work. Do not operate an appliance that smells of gas or appears unsafe.

Confirm Fuel Type

State gas, electric, induction or dual-fuel. Do not assume gas or hardwired electrical disconnection is included; use a qualified person when required.

Built-In vs. Freestanding

Wall ovens, cooktops and built-in units need cabinet and installation details. Freestanding and slide-in ranges should be described with width and removal clearance.

Clear Loose Parts & Path

Secure or remove loose racks and accessories after pickup is confirmed, and describe stairs, kitchen islands, narrow turns, gates and loading access.

After pickup: working cooking appliances may be routed toward reuse or repair where practical; non-reusable units can be directed toward an appropriate recycling or disposal option.

Access Details That Affect Pickup

Cooking appliances may involve gas lines, hardwired electrical connections, cabinet cutouts or anti-tip hardware. Do not assume disconnection is included. State clearly whether the unit is already safely disconnected and accessible.

Garage or driveway

Tell us whether a pickup vehicle can approach the appliance, whether a gate or slope is involved, and the carrying distance.

Interior, apartment or condo

Include floor level, stairs or elevator, doorway clearance, hallway turns, HOA or building rules and parking/loading restrictions.

Photos first

Send a full appliance photo, condition details and the removal path when access is tight. Accurate photos reduce failed trips and speed up review.

Southern California Stove & Oven Pickup Questions

How fast can stove or oven pickup be arranged in Southern California?

Pickup timing depends on working condition, fuel type, disconnection status, exact location, access and current local route availability.

Can I get free stove or range pickup in Southern California?

Qualifying working or reusable stoves, ranges and ovens may receive free pickup after condition, fuel type, exact location, access and route availability are reviewed.

Do you review both gas and electric ranges?

Yes. State the fuel type and whether burners, oven functions, displays and controls are working.

Will the pickup crew disconnect a gas range?

Do not assume gas disconnection is included. Gas appliances should be safely disconnected by a qualified person when required before removal.

Can built-in wall ovens be submitted?

Yes, but built-in units need cabinet, size, electrical or gas connection and removal-access details before acceptance can be confirmed.
