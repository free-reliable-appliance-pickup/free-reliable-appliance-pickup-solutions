Appliance Pickup for Landlords and Property Managers

Landlords, apartment operators and property-management teams can submit appliance pickup requests for unit turns, renovations, tenant move-outs and recurring replacement programs. The strongest requests identify the property, appliance condition, access and expected timing before pickup is scheduled.

Best starting point: property address, appliance count and condition, floor or unit location, stairs/elevators, gate or parking rules, staging details and an onsite contact.

Common Property Appliance Pickup Work

Unit turns

Group refrigerators, ranges, washers, dryers and other major appliances by property or move-out cycle.

Renovations and replacements

Identify appliances being replaced during kitchen, laundry-room or full-unit renovation work.

Recurring portfolios

For multiple buildings or communities, describe the normal weekly or monthly appliance flow instead of submitting every stop as a separate program.

What to Include Before Pickup

Condition

Working, repair-needed, unknown or nonworking status for each appliance.

Access

Stairs, elevators, hallways, gates, parking, loading zones and whether the appliance is already staged.

Volume

Appliance count by property plus expected future replacement frequency.

Documentation

Tell us whether your team needs photos, signatures, unit notes or another completion record.

Qualification and Appliance Types

Working and reusable major appliances are the strongest candidates for free pickup. Mixed multi-appliance loads may qualify when most major appliances are working. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance, and dishwashers require at least two other major working appliances.

Property Manager Appliance Pickup Questions

Can one request cover several properties?

Yes. List each property, expected appliance volume and access details so the request can be reviewed as a portfolio or route.

What should I send for a unit turn?

Send the property address, appliance type and condition, unit or floor location, stairs/elevator information, parking or gate rules and the onsite contact.

Do all broken appliances qualify for free pickup?

No. Free pickup is qualification-based. Working and reusable appliances receive priority, and mixed loads are reviewed from the overall condition and route.

Submit a Property Appliance Pickup Request

For one property or multiple units, list every appliance, working condition, unit or building location and access details. Photos can be requested during review when they are not available in the form.

Submitting a request does not guarantee free pickup. Each load is reviewed for condition, access, current local demand and route availability.
