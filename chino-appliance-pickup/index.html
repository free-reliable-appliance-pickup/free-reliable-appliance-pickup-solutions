Broken Appliance in Chino? Check the City's Bulky-Item Service

If a refrigerator no longer cools or another appliance does not qualify for our reuse-focused route, the City of Chino offers a separate bulky-item collection program through Waste Management for eligible residential customers. The City's current page lists appliances among bulky-item examples. For current limits or to schedule, contact Waste Management at (800) 423-9986.

Do not confuse bulky-item collection with the Chino household hazardous-waste drop-off: the City specifically lists large appliances as not accepted at its HHW facility.
