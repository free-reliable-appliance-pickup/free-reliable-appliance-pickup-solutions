Chino Appliance Pickup for Working & Reusable Equipment

Chino requests are reviewed through our Inland Empire network. We focus on major appliances that are still working or useful enough to support a free pickup route. Homeowners, renters, landlords and property managers can submit a request with appliance condition, address and access details before scheduling.

Free pickup is qualification-based. Fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup.

What We Review in Chino

Washers & dryers

Working laundry equipment is a priority. Tell us whether a washer fills, drains and spins and whether a dryer tumbles and heats. Submit complete sets together when possible.

Refrigerators & freezers

Tell us whether the appliance maintains temperature. Exterior and interior photos help us evaluate size, configuration and condition before assigning a route.

Stoves & ranges

Gas and electric cooking appliances may be reviewed when the working condition and access are clear. Mention burner and oven function when known.

Garage, House & Rental Access in Chino

Garage and driveway pickups are often straightforward, but inside removals require more information. Include the floor level, stair flights, narrow doorways, gates, long carries or parking restrictions. For refrigerators, tell us whether doors may need to be removed to clear the exit path.

For rental turnovers or multi-appliance replacements, list every appliance and its condition in one request. We review the complete load rather than assuming each item qualifies separately.

Pickup Limits

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Minor-repair appliances may be considered after review, but major damage or a completely nonworking single item does not qualify for our free service.

Submitting the form starts a review. Pickup is confirmed only after appliance condition, address, access and route availability are accepted.

Chino Bulky-Item Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our service, the City of Chino directs residents to its contracted waste provider for bulky-item service. The City lists appliances among examples of bulky items and publishes current eligibility and scheduling information. Confirm current limits and requirements before placing an item out.

Chino Appliance Pickup FAQs

Can I request a washer and dryer together?

Yes. Working laundry pairs are strong requests. Describe each machine separately and mention any known issue.

Can you review a refrigerator from inside the house?

Yes. Include the floor, doorway clearance, stairs and whether there is a clear path to the loading area.

Can landlords submit several appliances?

Yes. List all appliances, locations and working conditions so the full load can be reviewed together.
