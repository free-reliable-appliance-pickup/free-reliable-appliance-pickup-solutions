Before Pickup: Prepare a Refrigerator

Verify Cooling First

When practical and safe, keep a working refrigerator plugged in until its cooling condition has been reviewed. Tell us whether both fresh-food and freezer compartments hold temperature.

Empty Food After Confirmation

Once pickup is confirmed, remove food and personal items and wipe loose spills. Do not cut refrigerant lines or remove the compressor.

Water & Ice Connections

Tell us if the refrigerator has a water or ice connection. Do not assume plumbing disconnection is included; arrange safe disconnection when required.

Measure the Exit

Large French-door, side-by-side and built-in units may need door removal. Send the narrowest doorway, tight turns, stairs and elevator details before scheduling.

After pickup: reusable refrigerators may stay in service; non-reusable cooling appliances need an appropriate refrigerant-aware recycling or disposal path.

Access Details That Affect Pickup

Refrigerators can be difficult when they are oversized, built into cabinetry or located behind narrow kitchen doors. Include the narrowest doorway, tight turns, stairs and whether appliance doors may need removal.

Garage or driveway

Tell us whether a pickup vehicle can approach the appliance, whether a gate or slope is involved, and the carrying distance.

Interior, apartment or condo

Include floor level, stairs or elevator, doorway clearance, hallway turns, HOA or building rules and parking/loading restrictions.

Photos first

Send a full appliance photo, condition details and the removal path when access is tight. Accurate photos reduce failed trips and speed up review.

Southern California Refrigerator Pickup Questions

How fast can refrigerator pickup be arranged in Southern California?

Pickup timing depends on cooling condition, exact location, removal access and current local route availability. Clear exterior and interior photos can speed up review.

Can I get free refrigerator pickup in Southern California?

Qualifying working or reusable refrigerators may receive free pickup after cooling condition, exact location, access, local demand and route availability are reviewed.

Should the refrigerator stay plugged in?

When practical and safe, keeping a working refrigerator plugged in helps confirm current cooling condition. Remove food and personal items before pickup.

Do built-in or oversized refrigerators qualify?

They can be submitted for review, but size, weight, cabinetry, doorway clearance and removal access can affect whether a route can accept them.

Which areas receive priority review?

Priority Southern California routes include Los Angeles County, San Gabriel Valley, Inland Empire, Orange County, Riverside County and western San Bernardino County.
