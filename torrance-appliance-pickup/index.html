Torrance Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Torrance is a large South Bay market with single-family neighborhoods, apartments, alley-served properties and commercial areas. The exact address and removal path matter before confirming a pickup.

Working and reusable refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. A fully working major appliance may qualify for free pickup; mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Qualification first: submitting the form starts a review. Pickup is confirmed only after condition, exact location, access and route availability are checked.

Torrance ZIP Areas & Mailing Boundaries

City housing data includes ZIP areas 90501, 90502, 90503, 90504 and 90505. Some Torrance mailing ZIPs can overlap nearby or unincorporated areas, so the actual street address is more reliable than the mailing city alone.

For apartment or multi-family requests, include parking or loading rules and whether the appliance must travel through stairs, hallways or elevators.

Torrance Reuse-Focused Pickup

Working refrigerators, laundry sets, freezers and ranges are reviewed for reuse before disposal. Garage-staged appliances can be easier to route, while inside removals require doorway, stair and carrying-distance details.

Torrance Large-Item Pickup Is the Backup

The City of Torrance explicitly encourages residents to choose reuse first. For City trash customers, Torrance currently offers one free large-item pickup per calendar year per address. The City notes that large-item collections generally go to landfill except for electronics and metal appliances, making reuse a useful first option when an appliance still has value.

Torrance Appliance Removal FAQ

Do working appliances receive priority?

Yes. Reusable appliances receive the strongest priority because the goal is to keep useful machines in service when practical.

Can landlords or property managers submit several appliances?

Yes. List all appliances, condition and access details together when they are at the same property.

Should I send photos?

Yes. Photos help us identify appliance style, visible condition and access issues before a route is committed.
