Upland Refrigerator & Fridge Pickup Review

Working and reusable refrigerators receive priority. Tell us whether the unit is cooling now, whether shelves and drawers are present, whether doors or handles are damaged, and whether the refrigerator is still plugged in and testable.

French-door & side-by-side

Send exterior/interior photos, model label and doorway width. Large units may require door removal before transport.

Top/bottom-freezer

Confirm both refrigerator and freezer sections are holding temperature.

Built-in & premium

For Sub-Zero, Thermador, Bosch, KitchenAid, GE Profile/Café and similar units, include dimensions and safe disconnection status.

Upland Access & Local Route Details

For North Upland and foothill properties, include driveway grade and carrying distance. For Historic Downtown, Euclid Avenue, apartments and condos, include parking, gates, stairs, elevators and narrow-door details. Upland’s primary ZIPs are 91784 and 91786.

City of Upland bulky-item information says residential barrel customers can request free bulky-item pickup up to four times in a rolling calendar year, with up to five items per collection.

Refrigerant & End-of-Life Guidance

Do not cut refrigerant lines or remove the compressor yourself. Upland’s HHW center specifically says it does not accept refrigerators. If the refrigerator cannot be reused, use an appropriate refrigerant-aware recycling/disposal path.
