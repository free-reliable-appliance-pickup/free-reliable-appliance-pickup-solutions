Newport Beach Appliance Pickup, Removal & Recycling Review

Newport Beach requests are reviewed through our Orange County route. We look at working condition, appliance value, the number of items, property access and the current pickup schedule before a request is accepted. Complete information helps us make a faster decision.

For searches such as appliance pickup, appliance removal, appliance recycling, washer pickup, refrigerator pickup or appliance removal near me in Newport Beach, send the exact address or ZIP, appliance condition and HOA/property access details so the current 909 route can be reviewed.

Qualification: fully working appliances may qualify for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. One nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify.

Condo, HOA & Coastal Access Details

Newport Beach has many condos, gated communities and properties where access matters as much as appliance condition. Include gate instructions, elevator availability, loading-zone rules, garage clearance and whether management approval is required. For upper-floor appliances, tell us the exact floor and number of stair flights.

For narrow kitchens or laundry closets, send a photo of the route from the appliance to the exit. If door removal may be needed, mention it before scheduling.

Appliance Categories

Laundry equipment

Working washers and dryers are a strong priority. Describe cycle performance, noise, leaks and whether cords or hoses are included.

Cold-storage appliances

For refrigerators and freezers, confirm cooling performance and provide interior photos when possible.

Cooking appliances

Stoves, ranges and ovens can be reviewed when condition, fuel type and access are clearly stated.

Items With Extra Limits

Microwaves are not accepted alone and must come with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are reviewed only when included with two or more major working appliances. Appliances needing minor repair may still be submitted for review, but free pickup is not guaranteed.

Do not place an appliance outside solely because you submitted the form. Wait until the request is accepted and a pickup plan is confirmed.

Newport Beach Bulky-Item Alternative

If your appliance does not qualify for our service, the City of Newport Beach lists refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers and other large household items in its bulky-item program for qualifying residential customers. Current limits and scheduling are published by the city.
