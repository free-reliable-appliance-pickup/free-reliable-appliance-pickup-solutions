Fresno Refrigeration Disposal Gap

Fresno's Operation Clean Up page specifically excludes refrigerators and freezers, making refrigeration equipment a distinct local search and disposal problem. A working freezer may fit a reuse-focused route; a nonqualifying unit should go through an appropriate refrigerant-safe recycling or disposal option.

Keep the sealed system intact. Do not cut refrigerant lines, remove the compressor or dismantle the appliance before review.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can one working freezer qualify for free pickup in Fresno?

Yes. A fully working chest or upright freezer can be reviewed as a single appliance. Condition, access, local demand and route availability still determine acceptance.

Does Fresno Operation Clean Up take freezers?

The City of Fresno states that refrigerators and freezers are excluded from its Operation Clean Up appliance list.

Can a broken freezer be picked up free?

A single nonworking freezer does not automatically qualify for free pickup. Submit the actual condition and photos so the request can be reviewed accurately.

Should the freezer stay plugged in?

When practical and safe, keeping it plugged in helps confirm that it is freezing correctly. Remove food and loose contents before the confirmed pickup.

Do you review freezers in garages or sheds?

Yes. Include the size, freezing condition and the full route to the loading area, including gates, steps and long carries.
