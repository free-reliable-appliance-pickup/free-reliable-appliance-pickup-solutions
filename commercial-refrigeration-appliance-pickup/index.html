Free pickup may be available for qualifying working or reusable appliances. Final service depends on equipment condition, load mix, safe access, exact location and route availability.

Who This Pickup Review Is For

Restaurants, markets, hospitality properties, property operators, facilities teams and businesses replacing movable refrigeration equipment can submit one-time or recurring appliance replacement work for review. The goal is to collect enough operating detail to decide whether the load fits a current pickup route before anyone schedules service.

Refrigeration requests need clear cooling-condition and size information. End-of-life refrigerant-containing equipment may require an approved recovery or recycling path when reuse is not practical.

What to Include Before We Schedule

Include equipment type, dimensions, door count, approximate weight if known, cooling status, whether refrigerant-containing equipment is intact, loading access, floor level and disconnection status.

Also list each appliance category, how many units are ready, whether each unit works, whether photos are available and whether the request is one-time, recurring or part of a larger replacement project.

Appliance Categories We Review

Washers & Dryers

Working laundry equipment and complete sets receive strong priority. Include basic test results when available.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Include cooling status, size, interior/exterior photos and safe removal access.

Stoves, Ranges & Ovens

Identify gas or electric service, working functions and whether the unit is safely disconnected.

Mixed Loads

For replacement projects, list the full appliance mix so the working/reusable share can be reviewed accurately.

Not every appliance automatically qualifies for free pickup. Nonworking, incomplete, unsafe or difficult-access equipment may need a different removal or recycling option.

Refrigeration Equipment Details

Reach-In & Display Units

List door count, width, height, depth, casters, shelves, condition and whether the unit is movable.

Undercounter & Prep Units

Include built-in status, drains or water connections, footprint and the path from equipment to loading.

Walk-In or Fixed Systems

Fixed panels, remote compressors, condensers and large systems require separate project review and may not fit standard appliance pickup.

One Site or Multiple Locations

A single inquiry can cover one property, several sites or a broader service area. For multi-site work, list each city/property, typical appliance count, access differences and expected frequency. This makes it possible to review the work as one operating program without pretending every location already has confirmed service.

Commercial Refrigeration Appliance Pickup FAQ

What commercial refrigeration equipment can be reviewed?

Reach-ins, display cases, prep units, upright and chest freezers and other movable refrigeration equipment can be reviewed depending on condition, dimensions and access.

What refrigeration details should be provided?

Include dimensions, casters, shelves, built-in status, drain or water connections, refrigerant or service status when known and the full loading path.

Do walk-in or fixed systems qualify automatically?

No. Walk-in, fixed or integrated systems require separate review because disassembly, refrigerant handling, utility work and site conditions can change the scope.
