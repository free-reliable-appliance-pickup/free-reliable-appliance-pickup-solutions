What Qualifies for Free Pickup in Modesto?

Qualifying fully working appliances may receive free pickup in Modesto. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work. Minor repairs can be considered, but a nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup.

Working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. The final review also considers your Modesto location, access, local demand and available pickup coverage.

Dishwashers and microwaves: dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance.

Refrigerators

Standard, side-by-side, bottom-freezer and French-door refrigerators. Include an interior photo and cooling condition.

Freezers

Chest and upright household freezers. Tell us whether the unit freezes properly and send an interior photo.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric stoves, ranges and ovens may qualify. Tell us whether the burners and oven operate normally.

Pickup Access in Homes, Garages & Apartments

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, kitchen, laundry room, first floor, upstairs apartment, storage area or business. Include stair flights, narrow hallways, doorway concerns, gates, elevators and parking or loading restrictions.

If the appliance is inside a rental or managed property, include any required entry instructions. For a large refrigerator, mention if the exit looks tight or door removal may be necessary.

Modesto Appliance Pickup FAQ

Is appliance pickup free in Modesto?

Qualifying working appliances and eligible mixed loads may receive free pickup after condition, access, location, demand and coverage are reviewed.

Do you take a broken appliance by itself for free?

No. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for free pickup. Minor repairs may be considered, and mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the appliances work.

Can I submit a washer and dryer together?

Yes. Working laundry sets are a priority category. Tell us the condition of both machines and whether the dryer is gas or electric.

Can I request pickup from an apartment?

Yes. Include the floor level, stairs, elevator access and any building or parking restrictions before scheduling.

How do I request pickup?

Use the online request form or call or text 310-774-4304 with appliance photos, condition, Modesto ZIP code and access details.
