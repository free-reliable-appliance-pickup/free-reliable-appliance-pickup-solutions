Refrigerator cooling and removal information

A refrigerator is most useful for reuse when it still cools properly. Let us know whether the fresh-food section and freezer are cooling and whether there are known compressor, seal, water-dispenser or ice-maker problems.

For Eugene apartments and duplexes, narrow hallways and stair landings can determine whether an otherwise reusable refrigerator is practical to move. Include a photo of the exit if space is limited.

Important: Free pickup is conditional. A single nonworking machine normally does not qualify. For mixed major-appliance loads, we review how many items work, local reuse demand, safe access and the actual route. No appointment is guaranteed until confirmed.

Prepare your refrigerator pickup request

Measurements matter. Refrigerators can be hard to move through tight doors or around kitchen islands. Include an approximate width, height and depth, doorway constraints, floor level and photos of the front and inside. Do not cut refrigerant lines or attempt unsafe removal.

  • Share type, approximate dimensions, brand and model
  • Describe recent fridge and freezer cooling performance
  • Report stairs, tight doors or kitchen-island access
  • Empty food and discuss safe unplugging with the pickup contact

If you are moving, updating a rental, or replacing a still-working laundry or kitchen appliance, photos and a recent working test help us identify possible reuse options.

How qualification and pickup review work

  1. Send your city/ZIP, contact number, appliance photos and tested condition.
  2. Include property type, floor, stairs, door access and a safe pickup/loading location.
  3. We check free-pickup qualification and whether a local direct or partner route is available.
  4. If the request qualifies, the pickup details are confirmed before a visit. Otherwise, you can review other removal options.

Microwaves are not collected alone. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances. For nonqualifying items, check the applicable local waste or retailer take-back options; we do not guarantee free disposal.

Eugene refrigerator pickup questions

Can a regular top-freezer refrigerator qualify?

Yes. A standard top-freezer refrigerator can be reviewed for free pickup when it is working and the location and removal route qualify.

Is pickup automatically free?

No. We first review appliance working condition, actual location, safe access and current route or partner availability. A request is not a confirmed appointment.
