Working and reusable refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers, stoves and ranges receive priority. Fully working major appliances may qualify for free pickup; mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Sun City Palm Desert Appliance Removal FAQ

Do working appliances receive priority?

Yes. Reusable appliances receive the strongest priority because the goal is to keep useful machines in service when practical.

Can a high-end appliance needing repair still be worth submitting?

Yes. A high-end or desirable appliance with a clear repair issue can still be a useful request when the brand, model, photos and condition are provided.

Should I send several appliances in one request?

Yes. Package loads are easier to evaluate when all appliances and conditions are submitted together.
