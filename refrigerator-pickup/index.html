Looking for Refrigerator Pickup or Appliance Recycling Near Me?

Customers may search for free refrigerator pickup, fridge removal, appliance haul-away, refrigerator recycling or appliance pickup near me. Start with a pickup review when the refrigerator is still working and reusable. If it does not qualify, use the local disposal alternatives provided on many city pages.

Working refrigerators receive priority. A single nonworking refrigerator does not automatically qualify for free pickup. Exact condition, local demand, access and current route availability are reviewed before scheduling.

Which Refrigerators Receive Priority?

Top-freezer refrigerators

Standard working top-freezer models can be useful, especially when complete and reasonably clean.

Bottom-freezer models

Tell us whether both compartments maintain temperature and whether drawers and shelves are complete.

Side-by-side refrigerators

Include cooling status, approximate width and whether water or ice features work when known.

French-door refrigerators

Large models require accurate dimensions and doorway information because removal can involve door disassembly or tight turns.

How to Describe Refrigerator Condition

Tell us whether the refrigerator section is cold and whether the freezer is actually freezing. Mention unusual compressor noise, heavy frost, warm temperatures, damaged seals, missing shelves or drawers, broken handles, dispenser problems or a unit that runs continuously without cooling correctly.

When practical and safe, keep a working refrigerator plugged in until its operating condition has been confirmed. If it has already been unplugged, tell us how long it has been off and whether it was cooling normally beforehand.

Photos, Measurements and Doorway Clearance

Send a clear front photo, an interior photo and the model/serial label when possible. Approximate width, depth and height are especially useful for side-by-side and French-door models. Measure the narrowest doorway and tell us whether refrigerator doors or home doors may need to be removed.

Also include stairs, elevators, narrow halls, tight turns, gates, parking or loading restrictions, and whether the refrigerator is in a kitchen, garage, basement, apartment or outside area.

Interior Condition and Preparation

Remove all food, magnets and personal items before pickup. Disclose strong odors, mold, insects, broken glass or contamination during the initial review. Photograph shelves, drawers and door bins so missing or damaged parts are visible.

Do not place a working refrigerator at the curb or expose it to weather before pickup is confirmed. Keeping a reusable unit protected helps preserve its condition.

Refrigerator Pickup FAQ

Do I need to include my city when searching?

No. Use the main request form and provide the actual pickup address. Approved local pages are available for many priority markets, but routing is based on the location you submit.

Can a refrigerator with an ice maker qualify?

Yes. Describe the cooling condition and whether the ice maker or dispenser works when known. The refrigerator is reviewed as a complete appliance.

Can a nonworking refrigerator be picked up free?

A single nonworking refrigerator does not automatically qualify. If you have a mixed load, it may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work.

Can you remove a refrigerator from inside?

Inside pickup may be reviewed. Include floor level, stairs, doorway width, tight turns and whether appliance doors may need to be removed.

Free Pickup Rules

Fully working appliances receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

Submitting a request does not guarantee free pickup. Appliance condition, access, location, local demand and route availability are reviewed before scheduling.
