What receives priority?

Refrigerators that cool correctly, have intact shelves and drawers, close properly and are reasonably clean receive the strongest priority. Top-freezer, bottom-freezer, side-by-side and French-door models can all be submitted.

What photos help?

Send a clear front photo, an interior photo when possible and the model/serial label. Mention approximate width, whether the refrigerator is currently plugged in and cooling, and any dispenser, seal or temperature problems.

What access details matter?

Tell us about stairs, floor level, narrow doors, tight turns, gates and whether doors may need to be removed. Accurate access information helps prevent scheduling problems.

Do nonworking refrigerators qualify?

A single nonworking refrigerator does not automatically qualify for free pickup. Working and reusable units receive priority. Mixed loads may qualify when approximately 80% are working.

What happens after I submit?

We review cooling condition, size, age, photos, access and local demand before confirming whether free pickup is available. Accurate information matters because refrigerators vary greatly in weight and doorway clearance. If the refrigerator is working, keep it plugged in when practical until the condition has been confirmed, and remove food and personal items before the scheduled pickup.

How to describe refrigerator condition

Cooling and freezer performance

Tell us whether the refrigerator section is cold and whether the freezer is actually freezing. If the unit has been unplugged, say how long it has been off and whether it was working before disconnection. Mention unusual compressor noise, heavy frost, warm temperatures or a unit that runs continuously without cooling correctly.

Doors, shelves and dispensers

Note missing shelves, broken drawers, torn door gaskets, damaged handles or doors that do not close correctly. For side-by-side and French-door models, mention whether the water or ice dispenser works when known. Cosmetic dents can be acceptable, but clear photos help us judge overall reusable condition.

Measurements and doorway access

Approximate width, depth and height are useful, especially for larger side-by-side and French-door refrigerators. Tell us whether appliance doors or home doors may need to be removed to clear the exit. Include stairs, elevators, narrow halls, tight turns, gates and whether the refrigerator is in a kitchen, garage, basement or outside area.

Before pickup, remove food, magnets and personal items. Keep the path clear and avoid moving a working refrigerator to a harder-to-access location.
