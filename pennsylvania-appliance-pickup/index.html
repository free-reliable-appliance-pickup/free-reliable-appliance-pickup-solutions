What qualifies for a potential free pickup review?

Fully working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers and stoves are prioritized. Working laundry sets and mostly-working multi-appliance loads can be stronger candidates for reuse; broken or difficult-access appliances must be reviewed individually. A separate paid removal quote is optional and requires approval.

Official recycling and disposal alternatives

Philadelphia public disposal: The City's residential bulky-item appointment program has separate pickup details and preparation rules for large appliances. It is a municipal service, not free private reuse pickup through us. Do not remove refrigerator doors for a reuse inquiry; city collection preparation is separate.

Pittsburgh refrigerant appliances: The City's waste disposal resource list describes independent refrigerated-appliance recycling and some utility programs subject to current terms. They are not our private pickup partners.

Residents who cannot qualify for reuse should check with their municipality or county recycling program, a local licensed recycler, or a retailer's separate haul-away service. Appliance handling and fees vary by program. Never cut refrigerant lines or handle connected gas fittings yourself.
