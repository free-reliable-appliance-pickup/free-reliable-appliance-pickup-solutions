Oregon City Appliance Pickup Review

Our Portland-metro network reviews Oregon City requests for major appliances that are working, reusable or reasonable to evaluate for pickup. We prefer accurate condition details before a route is promised so customers know whether the request fits our free service.

Free pickup is conditional. Fully working appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A single nonworking appliance does not qualify for free pickup.

What Helps Us Review Your Request

Current condition

Tell us what works today. Refrigerators and freezers should include cooling condition. Laundry requests should describe the washer and dryer separately.

Current location

Tell us whether the appliance is in a garage, driveway, first floor, upper floor, basement or outside. Include stairs and tight doorways.

Pickup access

Describe parking, gates, building rules, narrow hallways or a long carry from the appliance to the truck area. Photos of difficult access are useful.

Appliances We Consider in Oregon City

We review washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves, ranges and qualifying multiple-appliance loads. Working laundry equipment is a priority, especially complete sets. Refrigerator and freezer requests should clearly state whether the appliance maintains temperature.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

South Metro Route Planning

Oregon City can fit routes connecting the southern Portland metro and nearby Clackamas County areas. Exact approval depends on the address, the appliance mix, current demand and access. A city name by itself is not a pickup guarantee.

For rentals or property cleanouts, list every appliance and its condition in one request. If a refrigerator is still working, keeping it available for condition verification can help the review. Remove food and personal items before the scheduled pickup time.

Metro South Disposal Alternative

If an appliance does not qualify for our service, Metro South Transfer Station is located in Oregon City. Metro publishes separate handling rules for coolant appliances such as refrigerators and freezers and also lists metal washers, dryers, stoves and ovens among accepted appliance categories. Confirm current fees, hours and item rules directly with Metro before visiting.

Oregon City Pickup FAQs

Can I request a washer and dryer together?

Yes. A complete working laundry set is a strong request. Describe both machines separately.

What if the appliance is downstairs?

Include the number of stair flights, turns and doorway restrictions. Access can affect whether a pickup is practical.

Is pickup automatically same-day?

No. We confirm qualification and route availability before giving a pickup time.
