Which appliances may qualify?

Fully working refrigerators, washers, dryers, freezers and stoves are prioritized for possible reuse, including matching laundry sets and mostly-working batches. Nonworking appliances, upstairs units, difficult access and optional paid quotes need individual evaluation and agreement.

Independent Wisconsin disposal and recycling alternatives

Milwaukee's separate public program: The City of Milwaukee recycling directory lists appliances including washers, dryers, stoves, refrigerators and freezers as accepted at city Drop Off Centers and not accepted curbside. City drop-off is not our pickup service.

Madison's separate public program: Madison's large-item program requires a work order for eligible curbside appliance collection and does not serve properties with more than eight units under its regular program. Fees and property pickup details depend on the city's rules. Municipal collection is not scheduled through this private pickup form.

Wisconsin appliance recycling: Wisconsin DNR's recycling rules identify major appliances as materials barred from landfill disposal. The DNR Refrigerant Recovery Program explains registered salvage and certified transport requirements for discarded refrigerated appliances. These requirements apply separately from an unconfirmed private reuse-pickup inquiry.

City services are separate from the private pickup inquiry. Refrigerants must be handled by appropriately qualified providers.
