How South Pasadena Pickup Review Works

1. Send the appliance details

Tell us the appliance type, tested condition and whether it is already disconnected and movable.

2. Describe the access

3. Confirm qualification and route

Free pickup is qualification-based. A request is reviewed before pickup or route availability is confirmed.

Appliances and Free-Pickup Qualification

Working and reusable major appliances are the strongest candidates for free pickup. Mixed loads may qualify when most major appliances are working. Microwaves require another qualifying major appliance, and dishwashers require at least two other major working appliances.
