Centennial Appliance Pickup for Working Major Appliances

Centennial is part of our Denver/Aurora service cluster. We review working washers, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, stoves and ranges for possible free pickup. Condition, route availability and the actual removal path are reviewed together before a time is confirmed.

A completely nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for our free service. Minor repair situations may be considered, and mixed loads can be reviewed when roughly 80% of the major appliances are working. In Colorado, working laundry equipment and freezers are especially useful categories to submit with complete details.

Best request: photos, tested functions, floor level, stairs and driveway or parking access.

What We Need to Know About the Appliance

Washers

Tell us whether the washer fills, drains and spins. Mention leaks, unusual noise, door-lock problems and error codes.

Dryers

State whether it tumbles and heats and whether it is gas or electric. Submit the washer too when you have a working pair.

Refrigerators & Freezers

Tell us whether cooling is normal now. Include interior photos and note missing shelves, bins or major cosmetic damage.

Stoves & Ranges

Identify gas or electric, describe burner and oven operation and tell us whether the appliance is already disconnected.

Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

HOAs, Private Trash Providers and Property Access

Centennial does not operate one citywide trash collection service, and private provider arrangements can vary by address or HOA. For our pickup review, focus on the appliance and access: tell us whether it is in a garage, kitchen, basement, upper floor or outside, and list any stairs, elevators, gates or loading restrictions.

For HOA or managed properties, include any move-hour limits and parking rules. For refrigerators, mention tight doors and turns. For stacked laundry, tell us whether the machines are still installed in a closet or alcove and whether there are stairs between the appliance and the vehicle.

Centennial Recycling Resources Are the Backup When Reuse Does Not Fit

If an appliance does not qualify for our free reuse-based pickup, the City of Centennial explains that trash and recycling are provided by private companies and directs residents to current recycling resources. The City's recycling page also points residents to tools for finding a recycler for specific materials.

For a washer or dryer, describe the actual tested cycle functions. For a refrigerator or freezer, tell us whether it is currently cold and how long it has been plugged in. Send a full exterior image and an interior image for cooling appliances when practical.

Centennial Appliance Pickup FAQ

Does Centennial have one city trash hauler?

No. The City states that trash and recycling services are provided by private companies, so backup disposal options can depend on the property.

Can you review an HOA property pickup?

Yes. Include gates, parking, loading rules, stairs or elevators and any move-hour restrictions.

Are working freezers useful in Colorado?

Yes. Working freezers are a strong category. Tell us whether the unit freezes correctly and is empty and accessible.
