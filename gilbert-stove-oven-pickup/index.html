Gilbert Stove Pickup, Range Removal & Oven Review

Gilbert stove and oven requests are reviewed based on real reuse potential—not simply the age of the appliance. Working range burners, reliable oven heat, complete parts and accessible removal can make a request easier to evaluate for no-cost pickup.

This page is for Gilbert homeowners, rentals and remodelers replacing usable kitchen cooking appliances. For a washer or dryer rather than a cooking appliance, use the Gilbert washer and dryer pickup page. For refrigerators, freezers and mixed appliance loads, see Gilbert general appliance pickup.

Free-pickup qualification: fully working or reusable major appliances receive priority. A single nonworking stove generally does not qualify. We must review the exact pickup address, appliance condition and safe access before offering a confirmed collection.

What to Tell Us About Your Gilbert Stove

Working condition

Do the cooktop burners, oven heat, controls and doors work? Describe any missing parts or known problems.

Gas or electric range

Describe the appliance as a freestanding range, slide-in stove or separate oven, and state whether it uses gas or electricity. Share burner and oven test results, photos and any stairs, narrow doorways or removal obstacles.

Stairs, loading and access

For a Gilbert rental turnover, provide the installation or move-out date only as planning information; no pickup time is reserved until coverage is confirmed.

Send photographs showing the entire stove and cooktop. Include the ZIP code, floor/stairs and a clear path for removal. Do not disconnect a gas line or hardwired range without a qualified professional.

Gilbert City Appliance Recycling Alternative

Gilbert provides an appliance-pickup service by request, with fees that vary according to scheduling; appliances are not automatically included in normal bulk trash. The town's program is a separate alternative if a reuse pickup cannot be approved.
