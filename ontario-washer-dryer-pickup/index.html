Ontario Washer & Dryer Pickup on the Inland Empire Route

Ontario requests connect the Montclair/Pomona side of the corridor with Upland, Rancho Cucamonga and Fontana. Because pickup conditions can vary, we review working condition together with the actual route from the laundry area to the loading point before confirming service.

Working washers, dryers and complete sets receive the strongest consideration. A single working machine can also be reviewed. Repairable or nonworking equipment is considered only when current local demand and route capacity make pickup practical.

For faster review: include clear photos, brand/model if known, tested functions, gas or electric dryer type, floor level, stairs or elevator, gates, parking or driveway access and carrying distance.

Ontario Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Can one working washer or dryer be reviewed?

Yes. Single working machines can be reviewed, while complete working sets generally receive stronger priority.

Should I include access photos?

They are useful when stairs, elevators, gates, tight doorways, longer carries or parking limitations are involved.

Can nonworking equipment qualify?

Sometimes, depending on condition and current local demand, but free pickup is not guaranteed for nonworking equipment.
