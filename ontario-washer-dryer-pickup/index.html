Ontario Washer & Dryer Pickup FAQ

Can one working washer or dryer be reviewed?

Yes. A fully working individual machine can be reviewed, while complete working sets usually receive stronger reuse priority.

What access details matter most in Ontario?

Send floor level, stairs or elevator, gate access, garage versus interior location, parking/loading limits and carrying distance. Apartment and condo requests should include property or association access rules.

What if our free route does not accept the machines?

Ontario's current bulky-item program allows single-family residents up to four appointments per calendar year, with up to five large items per appointment. Apartment and condo residents are directed to their property or association manager. The City's cleanup events also list appliances among accepted bulky items.

Is our free pickup guaranteed?

No. Working condition, reuse value, access, local demand and route availability must be confirmed.
