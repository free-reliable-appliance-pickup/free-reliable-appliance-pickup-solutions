Regional Appliance Pickup Route

This hub connects city-specific appliance pickup pages instead of treating a large part of Southern California as one undifferentiated market. Use the city page that matches the actual pickup address whenever one is available.

Qualifying fully working major appliances may receive free pickup. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances are working and reusable. A completely nonworking single appliance generally does not qualify for free pickup.

Exact location matters. The corridor crosses Los Angeles and San Bernardino County markets, so city, ZIP and access details are needed before routing.

Major Appliances We Review

Refrigerators & Freezers

Working cooling appliances receive priority. Send exterior, interior and model-label photos when possible and say whether the unit is cooling.

Washers & Dryers

For washers, report fill, drain and spin. For dryers, report gas or electric, tumble and heat. Complete working sets receive strong priority.

Stoves & Ranges

Gas and electric ranges can be reviewed when major cooking functions are working.

Mixed Loads

List every appliance and its condition. Mixed loads may qualify when most major appliances are working and reusable.

Access Changes Across the Corridor

Pickup access can range from flat suburban garages to apartments, hillside properties, gated communities, long carries and narrow interior routes. Include floor level, stairs, gates, parking, driveway access and carrying distance before pickup is confirmed.
