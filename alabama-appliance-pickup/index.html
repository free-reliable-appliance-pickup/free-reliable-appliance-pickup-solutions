How Free Appliance Pickup Requests Work

  1. Tell us the number of appliances and working condition.
  2. Provide city, ZIP, available photos, floor, stairs and loading access.
  3. We review reuse potential and check for a genuinely accepting operator.
  4. Any appointment or separately quoted fee must be confirmed.

Working appliances and mostly working sets are prioritized. Nonworking appliances are reviewed case by case. Dishwashers are considered only with two or more working major appliances, and microwaves are not accepted alone.
