High Desert RV Appliance & Generator Route

The High Desert RV-equipment route is centered on Victorville, Hesperia, Adelanto, Apple Valley, Phelan and El Mirage. Because these communities can involve long distances, large lots, storage properties and off-street access, the pickup review depends on both the equipment and the actual loading conditions.

Victorville & Hesperia

These are the core High Desert routing markets. Removed RV refrigerators, compact laundry units, ranges, furnaces, water heaters and generators can be reviewed when they are already disconnected and safely accessible.

Apple Valley & Adelanto

Send driveway length, gate information and whether the equipment is in a garage, yard, storage area or already staged near the loading point. Working and reusable equipment receives the strongest priority.

RV Equipment Details That Speed Up Review

Include full photos, the model label when available, working condition, fuel or power type, approximate dimensions, and a photo of the access path. For generators, identify whether the unit is portable or already removed from a permanent mount. For refrigerators, state whether the unit is electric-only, propane-capable or another configuration when known.

Equipment must already be safely disconnected. We do not disconnect propane lines, fuel systems, hard-wired electrical equipment or refrigerant systems, and we do not perform RV repair or built-in appliance removal.

What Is Outside the RV Appliance Pickup Service

Whole RVs, propane cylinders, fuel tanks or fuel containers, active gas or electrical systems, built-in equipment that still requires disassembly, and major RV structural components are not part of this service. If the item is not already removable and accessible, use a qualified RV technician before requesting pickup.
