Westminster Appliance Pickup Review

Westminster sits within the broader Denver-area route we review for appliance pickups. Requests are strongest when the appliance is still working, the current location is clear and the exit path can be understood before scheduling. This helps us avoid promising a pickup that cannot be completed safely or economically.

How free pickup works: fully working major appliances may qualify. Mixed loads may also qualify when approximately 80% of the major appliances work. A nonworking appliance by itself does not qualify for our free pickup.

Apartment, Townhome & House Access

Westminster requests can come from apartments, attached housing, garages and detached homes. For multi-unit buildings, include elevator access, loading-zone rules and the distance from the unit to a safe truck position. For townhomes or homes with interior stairs, include the floor and number of stair flights.

If a refrigerator is wider than a doorway or a laundry set is stacked in a tight closet, send photos of the appliance and the route out. We use those details to decide whether the pickup fits the available crew and route.

Appliances We Prioritize

Laundry equipment

Working washers and dryers are priority categories. Complete sets are especially useful. Describe each machine separately and mention any leaks, error codes or heating issues.

Refrigerators & freezers

Tell us whether the unit maintains temperature and include interior and exterior photos when possible. Working freezers remain a valuable Colorado category.

Stoves & ranges

Major cooking appliances can be reviewed when condition and access are clear. Include whether the unit is gas or electric in the request.

What We Do Not Promise

We do not promise free pickup merely because an appliance is old or unwanted. Route availability, local demand, working condition and access all matter. Microwaves are accepted only with another qualifying major appliance, and dishwashers are considered only with two or more major working appliances.

The request form starts a review. Pickup is confirmed only after the Colorado team accepts the request and gives you a scheduling window.

Westminster Large-Item Alternatives

The City of Westminster states that it does not currently provide a municipal large-item pickup program. The City directs residents to their trash hauler or listed junk-hauling options and also publishes recycling resources. If your appliance does not qualify for our service, confirm the current city guidance before arranging disposal.

Westminster Pickup FAQs

Can I submit several appliances from a rental?

Yes. List every appliance, condition and unit location so the working share of the complete load can be reviewed.

Do you pick up freezers?

Yes, working chest and upright freezers are important Colorado requests. Include size and cooling condition.

What if parking is far from the building?

Describe the loading situation and carrying distance in the access notes. That information can affect route approval.
